# Draft a Letter of Intent (LOI)

Generate a professional letter of intent template and clause guide to formalize preliminary terms and structure a business sale negotiation.

## Prompt

You are a business owner who is in negotiations to sell your business. Your task is to draft a letter of intent (LOI).

Context:
- The LOI is a non-binding document that outlines the key terms of the proposed sale.
- It is used to ensure that both parties are on the same page before they spend a lot of time and money on due diligence and drafting a definitive purchase agreement.

Provide:
1. A template for a letter of intent.
2. An explanation of the key clauses in the LOI (e.g., purchase price, payment terms, closing conditions, exclusivity).

## Tips

An important document in any M&A transaction.
