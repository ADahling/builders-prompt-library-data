# Develop a Content Moderation Policy for an Online Forum

Establish a comprehensive content moderation framework featuring prohibited categories, enforcement protocols, and a structured appeals process.

## Prompt

You are a community manager. Your task is to develop a content moderation policy for a new online forum.

Context:
- The forum is for a specific hobby and has a diverse user base.
- The policy should be clear, fair, and easy for moderators to enforce.

Provide:
1. A list of prohibited content (e.g., hate speech, spam, personal attacks).
2. A description of the moderation process, including warnings, content removal, and user bans.
3. An appeals process for users who believe their content was unfairly moderated.

## Tips

A crucial document for maintaining a healthy and safe online community.
