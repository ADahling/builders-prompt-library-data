# Compare Blue-Green and Canary Deployment Strategies

Evaluate blue-green versus canary deployments to select the optimal strategy based on risk, cost, and specific workload requirements.

## Prompt

You are a solutions architect. Write a document comparing and contrasting blue-green and canary deployment strategies. 

Instructions:
1. Explain the core concepts of each strategy.
2. Discuss the pros and cons of each in terms of risk, cost, and complexity.
3. Provide example scenarios where one strategy would be a better fit than the other.
4. Create a decision matrix to help teams choose the right strategy.

## Tips

A conceptual prompt that helps in understanding and choosing the right deployment strategy.
