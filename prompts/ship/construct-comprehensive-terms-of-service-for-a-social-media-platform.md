# Construct Comprehensive Terms of Service for a Social Media Platform

Draft a legally sound Terms of Service addressing IP rights, moderation policies, and dispute resolution for a global social media app.

## Prompt

You are a seasoned tech lawyer. Your task is to construct a comprehensive Terms of Service for a new social media platform.

Context:
- The platform allows users to share a wide variety of content, including text, images, and videos.
- The user base is global and diverse.

Provide:
1. A detailed intellectual property clause covering user content licenses.
2. A robust content moderation policy, including grounds for content removal and account suspension.
3. A dispute resolution clause, including arbitration and class action waiver.
4. Provisions for terminating the service and user accounts.

## Tips

A necessity for platforms with a large amount of user-generated content and a global user base.
