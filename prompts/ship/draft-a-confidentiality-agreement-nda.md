# Draft a Confidentiality Agreement (NDA)

Generate a professional mutual NDA template with clause explanations to secure sensitive client data during freelance software projects.

## Prompt

You are a freelance developer. Your task is to draft a confidentiality agreement (NDA) to use with your clients.

Context:
- You often work on projects that involve sensitive client information.
- You need a standard NDA that you can use to protect this information.

Provide:
1. A template for a mutual NDA (where both parties are sharing confidential information).
2. An explanation of the key clauses in the NDA (e.g., definition of confidential information, obligations of the receiving party, term of the agreement).
3. A discussion of when it is appropriate to use an NDA.
