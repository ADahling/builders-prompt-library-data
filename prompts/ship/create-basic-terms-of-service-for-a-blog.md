# Create Basic Terms of Service for a Blog

Draft basic Terms of Service for a blog covering user conduct, content ownership, and liability disclaimers to ensure legal protection.

## Prompt

You are a paralegal. Your task is to draft a basic Terms of Service (ToS) for a personal blog.

Context:
- The blog allows users to post comments.
- The ToS should cover user conduct and content ownership.

Provide:
1. A clause on acceptable user behavior (e.g., no spam, no harassment).
2. A statement on the ownership of user-generated content.
3. A disclaimer of liability for the accuracy of the blog's content.

## Tips

A simple ToS for websites with user-generated content.
