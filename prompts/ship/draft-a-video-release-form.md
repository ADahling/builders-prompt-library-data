# Draft a Video Release Form

Generate a professional video release form template with clear explanations of essential legal clauses for commercial media production.

## Prompt

You are a videographer who is filming people. Your task is to draft a video release form.

Context:
- The video release form is a legal document that gives you the right to use the video for commercial purposes.
- It should be signed by the people in the video.

Provide:
1. A template for a video release form.
2. An explanation of the key clauses in the video release form.

## Tips

A legal document that is used to get permission to use someone's likeness in a video.
