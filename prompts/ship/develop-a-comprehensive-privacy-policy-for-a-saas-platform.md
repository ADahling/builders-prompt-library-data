# Develop a Comprehensive Privacy Policy for a SaaS Platform

Generate a legally compliant, globally applicable privacy policy for SaaS platforms handling sensitive financial and personal identification data.

## Prompt

You are a senior legal counsel. Your task is to create a comprehensive privacy policy for a new Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform that handles sensitive user data.

Context:
- The platform processes financial and personal identification information.
- The service is offered globally, requiring compliance with multiple regulations (GDPR, CCPA).

Provide:
1. A detailed explanation of data processing activities.
2. Specific measures for data security and breach notification.
3. A clear policy on data retention and deletion.
4. Information on international data transfers and the safeguards in place.

For SaaS companies handling sensitive data and operating in multiple jurisdictions.
