# Draft a CCPA-Compliant 'Do Not Sell My Personal Information' Page

Draft a CCPA-compliant privacy page that explains data opt-out rights, provides a request form, and outlines identity verification steps.

## Prompt

You are a web content strategist with legal expertise. Your task is to draft the content for a 'Do Not Sell My Personal Information' page as required by the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

Context:
- The company sells user data to third-party advertisers.
- The page must be clear and easy for consumers to use.

Provide:
1. A clear explanation of the consumer's right to opt-out of the sale of their personal information.
2. A simple, accessible form or link for submitting an opt-out request.
3. Information on how the company verifies the identity of the person making the request.

## Tips

A mandatory requirement for businesses subject to the CCPA that sell personal information.
