# A/B Testing vs. Canary Releasing

Distinguish between A/B testing and canary releases through non-technical explanations and analogies focused on business versus technical goals.

## Prompt

You are a product manager. Explain the difference between A/B testing and canary releasing to a non-technical audience.

Instructions:
1. Define each concept in simple terms.
2. Highlight the different goals of each practice (A/B testing is for business metrics, canary is for technical stability).
3. Explain how they can be used together.
4. Provide a real-world analogy for each.

## Tips

A common point of confusion, this prompt helps clarify the distinction between these two related practices.
