# Draft a Promissory Note

Create a legally enforceable promissory note template and explanation to document personal loans with clear repayment terms and clauses.

## Prompt

You are an individual who is lending money to a friend or family member. Your task is to draft a simple promissory note.

Context:
- You want to have a written record of the loan.
- The promissory note should be legally enforceable.

Provide:
1. A template for a simple promissory note.
2. An explanation of the key clauses in the promissory note (e.g., principal amount, interest rate, repayment schedule, late fees).

## Tips

A simple legal document that can be used to document a loan.
