# Compare Different CDN Providers

Evaluate and compare three CDN providers across performance, features, pricing, and infrastructure to select the optimal solution.

Variables: cdn_provider_1, cdn_provider_2, cdn_provider_3

## Prompt

You are a CTO. Your task is to choose a CDN provider for your company. Compare three major providers: `{{cdn_provider_1}}`, `{{cdn_provider_2}}`, and `{{cdn_provider_3}}`.

Instructions:
1. Compare them on performance (speed and latency).
2. Compare them on features (WAF, image optimization, etc.).
3. Compare them on price.
4. Compare them on their network size and points of presence (PoPs).
5. Make a recommendation based on your company's needs.

## Tips

A research-oriented prompt that requires gathering and analyzing data.
