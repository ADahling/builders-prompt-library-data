# Draft a Photo Release Form

Generate a comprehensive commercial photo release template and explanatory guide to secure legal usage rights from subjects.

## Prompt

You are a photographer who is taking pictures of people. Your task is to draft a photo release form.

Context:
- The photo release form is a legal document that gives you the right to use the photos for commercial purposes.
- It should be signed by the people in the photos.

Provide:
1. A template for a photo release form.
2. An explanation of the key clauses in the photo release form.

## Tips

A legal document that is used to get permission to use someone's likeness in a photo.
