# Establish Error Budgets for a SaaS Platform

Define, track, and manage error budgets to balance feature velocity with system reliability based on a 99.5% monthly uptime SLO.

Variables: platform_name

## Prompt

As a senior developer, you need to establish error budgets for a Software as a Service (SaaS) platform named {{platform_name}}. The platform has an SLO for 99.5% monthly uptime. Define the error budget in terms of acceptable downtime per month and discuss how this budget can be used to make informed decisions about feature releases and bug fixes. Consider the impact of exceeding the error budget on the development process and customer satisfaction. Detail the processes for tracking error budget consumption and the actions to take when the budget is exhausted. Include strategies for communicating error budget status to stakeholders and ensuring that the development team understands the importance of maintaining the error budget.

## Tips

Ideal for developers managing service reliability and balancing development priorities.
