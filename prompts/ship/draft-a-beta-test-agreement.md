# Draft a Beta Test Agreement

Generate a comprehensive beta testing agreement template featuring essential clauses for intellectual property protection and liability limitation.

## Prompt

You are a software company that is about to launch a new product. Your task is to draft a beta test agreement for your beta testers.

Context:
- You want to get feedback on your product from a limited number of users before you launch it to the public.
- You need to protect your intellectual property and limit your liability.

Provide:
1. A template for a beta test agreement.
2. Clauses on confidentiality, feedback, and intellectual property.
3. A disclaimer of warranties and limitation of liability.

## Tips

An important legal document for any company that runs a beta testing program.
