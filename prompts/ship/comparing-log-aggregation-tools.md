# Comparing Log Aggregation Tools

Get an ELK Stack, Graylog, and Splunk comparison covering scale, usability, integrations, cost, and support, with an organization-specific choice.

Variables: organization_name

## Prompt

Your organization is considering different log aggregation tools for centralizing log data from multiple sources. Prepare a comparison report that evaluates the features, pros, and cons of three popular log aggregation tools: ELK Stack, Graylog, and Splunk. Consider criteria such as scalability, ease of use, integration capabilities, cost, and community support. Provide a recommendation based on the needs of your organization, {{organization_name}}, and justify your choice.
