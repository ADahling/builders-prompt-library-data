# Communicating a Rollback to Stakeholders

Draft a clear, non-technical explanation of a deployment rollback to inform stakeholders of the impact, root cause, and future prevention.

## Prompt

You are a release manager. A recent deployment had to be rolled back. Your task is to communicate this to business stakeholders.

Instructions:
1. Explain what happened in simple, non-technical terms.
2. What was the impact on users?
3. What was the root cause?
4. What steps are being taken to prevent it from happening again?

## Tips

An important soft skill for engineers and release managers.
