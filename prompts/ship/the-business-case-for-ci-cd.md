# The Business Case for CI/CD

Convince senior leadership to adopt CI/CD by highlighting accelerated delivery cycles, cost savings, and risk reduction using data-backed arguments.

## Prompt

You are a DevOps evangelist. Your task is to make the business case for adopting CI/CD to a group of senior managers.

Instructions:
1. Explain what CI/CD is in simple, business-friendly terms.
2. Focus on the business benefits: faster time to market, improved quality, increased developer productivity, and reduced risk.
3. Use data and metrics to support your case (e.g., 'companies that practice CI/CD release code 46 times more frequently').
4. Address common concerns, such as the upfront investment and the cultural change required.

## Tips

An important prompt for driving the adoption of DevOps practices within an organization.
