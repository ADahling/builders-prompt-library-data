# Create Dashboards for Business Metrics

Plan a business metrics dashboard for your business unit with layout and visualization suggestions for your chosen dashboard tool.

## Prompt

Help me create dashboards to visualize business metrics for {businessUnit}. The dashboard should include key metrics such as {metric1}, {metric2}, and {metric3}, using {dashboardTool}. Provide layout and visualization suggestions.

## Tips

Business metrics should be clear and actionable for stakeholders.
