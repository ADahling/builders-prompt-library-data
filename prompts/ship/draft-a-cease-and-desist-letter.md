# Draft a Cease and Desist Letter

Generate a professional trademark cease and desist letter template along with explanations of critical legal clauses to protect brand assets.

## Prompt

You are a business owner who has discovered that another company is using your trademark without your permission. Your task is to draft a cease and desist letter.

Context:
- The cease and desist letter is a legal document that demands that the other company stop using your trademark.
- It is often the first step in resolving a trademark dispute.

Provide:
1. A template for a cease and desist letter.
2. An explanation of the key clauses in the cease and desist letter.

## Tips

A legal document that is used to demand that someone stop infringing on your rights.
