# Create a GDPR Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA)

Generate a documented GDPR Legitimate Interest Assessment to legally justify and balance marketing email processing for existing customers.

## Prompt

You are a data protection lawyer. Your task is to conduct and document a Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA) for a marketing campaign under GDPR.

Context:
- The company wants to send marketing emails to existing customers based on legitimate interest.
- The LIA is needed to justify this legal basis.

Provide:
1. Identification of the legitimate interest.
2. A necessity test: is the processing necessary for that interest?
3. A balancing test: do the individual’s interests, rights, and freedoms override the legitimate interest?

## Tips

A critical step for relying on legitimate interest as a legal basis for processing data under GDPR.
