# Create a Go/No-Go Launch Checklist

Generate a comprehensive, cross-functional Go/No-Go checklist to evaluate launch readiness across engineering, marketing, and support teams.

## Prompt

You are a release manager preparing for a major product launch. Create a go/no-go checklist that will be used in the final meeting before the launch.

Instructions:
1. The checklist should cover all aspects of the launch: engineering, QA, product, marketing, support.
2. Each item should be a question that can be answered with 'go' or 'no-go'.
3. For example: 'Have all critical bugs been fixed?', 'Is the support team trained on the new features?', 'Has the marketing announcement been scheduled?'.
4. The final launch decision will be based on the answers to these questions.

## Tips

A critical tool for making a final, informed decision about a launch.
