# Draft a Software Development Agreement

Draft a comprehensive, milestone-driven software development agreement to secure intellectual property rights and project timelines.

## Prompt

You are a business owner who is hiring a software development company to build a custom software application for your business. Your task is to draft a software development agreement.

Context:
- The project is complex and has a large budget.
- You need to ensure that the project is completed on time and on budget, and that you own the intellectual property in the software.

Provide:
1. A detailed statement of work (SOW) that outlines the scope of the project, the deliverables, and the timeline.
2. A payment schedule that is tied to project milestones.
3. Clauses on intellectual property ownership, warranties, and liability.

## Tips

A critical legal document for any custom software development project.
