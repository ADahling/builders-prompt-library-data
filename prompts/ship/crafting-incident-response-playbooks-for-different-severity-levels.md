# Crafting Incident Response Playbooks for Different Severity Levels

Get markdown incident playbooks by severity, with response steps, communication protocols, escalation paths, and logging and impact templates.

## Prompt

Create a series of incident response playbooks tailored to different severity levels, such as SEV-1, SEV-2, and SEV-3. Each playbook should include specific response steps, communication protocols, and escalation paths appropriate for its severity level. Include templates for logging incident details, assessing impact, and coordinating with stakeholders. Ensure the playbooks are structured for quick reference and can be easily updated as your systems and processes evolve. Deliver the playbooks in a clear, organized markdown format.

## Tips

Ideal for teams needing structured response guides across various incident types.
