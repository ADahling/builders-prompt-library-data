# Integrate Automated Testing into Your CI/CD Pipeline

Plan automated unit, integration, and deployment test phases for your project's CI/CD pipeline, with guidance on reporting results to your team.

Variables: project_name, testing_framework

## Prompt

Describe how to add automated testing to your CI/CD pipeline for {{project_name}}. Include phases for unit tests, integration tests, and deployment tests using {{testing_framework}} and how to report the results back to your team.

## Tips

Automated tests help catch issues early in the deployment process.
