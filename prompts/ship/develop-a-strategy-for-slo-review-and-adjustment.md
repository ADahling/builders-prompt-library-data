# Develop a Strategy for SLO Review and Adjustment

Plan regular mobile app SLO reviews using feedback and performance data, with cross-team input, change documentation, and stakeholder communication.

Variables: app_name

## Prompt

Create a strategy for the regular review and adjustment of SLOs for a mobile application, {{app_name}}, to ensure they remain relevant and aligned with user needs and business objectives. Detail the process for collecting and analyzing feedback from users and stakeholders, as well as performance data. Discuss how to use this information to adjust SLO targets appropriately. Include methods for involving cross-functional teams in the review process to gain diverse insights and foster collaboration. Provide guidelines for documenting changes and communicating them effectively to all stakeholders. Address how to handle resistance to change and ensure buy-in from key decision-makers.

## Tips

Promotes a proactive approach to maintaining effective SLOs through regular review.
