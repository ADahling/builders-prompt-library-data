# Draft a Simple Refund Policy

Create a clear, concise refund policy for an e-commerce craft store that defines return conditions, deadlines, and procedural steps.

## Prompt

You are a small business owner. Your task is to draft a simple refund policy for your online store.

Context:
- You sell handmade crafts and want to offer a fair refund policy to your customers.
- The policy should be easy to understand.

Provide:
1. The conditions under which a refund can be requested (e.g., damaged goods, wrong item shipped).
2. The timeframe for requesting a refund.
3. The process for returning the item and receiving the refund.
