# Draft a Basic Privacy Policy

Generate a clear, user-friendly privacy policy for a personal blog that outlines data collection limits and protects subscriber confidentiality.

## Prompt

You are a legal assistant. Your task is to draft a simple privacy policy for a new website that collects user names and email addresses. The policy should be easy to understand for a general audience.

Context:
- The website is a personal blog with a newsletter subscription form.
- The data collected is only used for sending the newsletter.

Provide:
1. A clear statement on what data is collected.
2. The purpose of collecting the data.
3. A statement that the data will not be shared with third parties.

## Tips

A starting point for websites with minimal data collection.
