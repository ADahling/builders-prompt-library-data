# Draft a Freelance Contract

Create a clear freelance writing contract template with simplified legal clauses and negotiation strategies to protect work ownership and pay.

## Prompt

You are a freelance writer. Your task is to draft a simple contract to use with your clients.

Context:
- You need a contract that covers the scope of work, payment terms, and ownership of the content you create.
- The contract should be easy for both you and your clients to understand.

Provide:
1. A template for a simple freelance writing contract.
2. An explanation of the key clauses in the contract.
3. Tips for negotiating the terms of the contract with your clients.

## Tips

A basic contract for freelancers to protect their interests.
