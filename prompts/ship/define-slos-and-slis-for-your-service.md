# Define SLOs and SLIs for Your Service

Help me define Service Level Objectives SLOs and Service Level Indicators SLIs for my service {serviceName}.

## Prompt

Help me define Service Level Objectives (SLOs) and Service Level Indicators (SLIs) for my service {serviceName}. The key metrics we want to monitor include {metric1}, {metric2}, and {metric3}. Please provide a structured approach to create meaningful SLOs that align with our business goals.

## Tips

Focus on identifying key metrics that reflect user experience and reliability.
