# Review a SaaS Agreement for a Small Business

Identify risky clauses and negotiate better terms in SaaS vendor contracts to protect business interests and data ownership.

## Prompt

You are a small business owner. Your task is to review a SaaS agreement from a vendor and identify any potential red flags.

Context:
- You are considering using a new CRM software for your business.
- You have been presented with a standard SaaS agreement from the vendor.

Provide:
1. A checklist of key clauses to look for in the agreement (e.g., data ownership, liability, termination).
2. An explanation of common red flags in SaaS agreements (e.g., automatic renewal with no notice, unilateral price increases).
3. A list of questions to ask the vendor before signing the agreement.

## Tips

Helps small business owners understand and negotiate SaaS agreements.
