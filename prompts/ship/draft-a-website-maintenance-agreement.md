# Draft a Website Maintenance Agreement

Produce a professional website maintenance contract featuring service scope, SLA tiers, and pricing models to secure recurring agency revenue.

## Prompt

You are a web development agency. Your task is to draft a website maintenance agreement to offer to your clients.

Context:
- You want to offer ongoing website maintenance services to your clients after you have built their websites.
- The agreement should cover the scope of services, response times, and payment terms.

Provide:
1. A template for a website maintenance agreement.
2. A list of services to include in the agreement (e.g., software updates, backups, security scans, content updates).
3. A tiered pricing structure for different levels of service.

## Tips

A way for web development agencies to generate recurring revenue.
