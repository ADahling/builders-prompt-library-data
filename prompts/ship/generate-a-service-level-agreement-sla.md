# Generate a Service Level Agreement (SLA)

Draft a comprehensive, Markdown-formatted Service Level Agreement outlining uptime, performance metrics, support response times, and penalties.

## Prompt

You are a service provider for a critical business application. You need to create a Service Level Agreement (SLA) to define the level of service you will provide to your customers.

Your task is to generate a comprehensive SLA that includes the following sections:

*   **Service Description:** A description of the service you are providing.
*   **Service Availability:** The percentage of time that the service will be available.
*   **Service Performance:** The performance metrics that you will use to measure the service.
*   **Support:** The hours of support that you will provide and the response times for different types of issues.
*   **Exclusions:** A list of the things that are not covered by the SLA.
*   **Penalties:** The penalties that will apply if you fail to meet the terms of the SLA.

Generate the complete SLA in Markdown format.
