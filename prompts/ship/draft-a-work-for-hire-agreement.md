# Draft a Work for Hire Agreement

Generate a comprehensive work-for-hire agreement template with clause explanations to secure intellectual property ownership rights.

## Prompt

You are a business owner who is hiring a freelancer to create a work for you. Your task is to draft a work for hire agreement.

Context:
- The work for hire agreement is a legal document that makes you the owner of the work that the freelancer creates.
- It is important to have a written agreement to avoid disputes about ownership.

Provide:
1. A template for a work for hire agreement.
2. An explanation of the key clauses in the work for hire agreement.

## Tips

A legal document that is used to transfer ownership of a copyrighted work.
