# Create a Privacy Policy for a Mobile App

Draft a mobile app privacy policy in Markdown covering data collection, use, sharing, user choices, security, and GDPR and CCPA requirements.

Variables: app_name

## Prompt

You have developed a new mobile app, {{app_name}}, and you need to create a privacy policy to inform users how you collect, use, and share their data.

Your task is to generate a comprehensive privacy policy that complies with relevant regulations, such as the GDPR and CCPA. The privacy policy should include the following sections:

*   **Information We Collect:** A description of the types of information you collect from users.
*   **How We Use Your Information:** An explanation of how you use the information you collect.
*   **How We Share Your Information:** A description of who you share user information with.
*   **Your Choices:** An explanation of the choices that users have regarding their information.
*   **Data Security:** A description of the measures you take to protect user data.
*   **Contact Us:** Information on how users can contact you with questions about the privacy policy.

Generate the complete privacy policy in Markdown format.
