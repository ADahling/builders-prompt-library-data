# Draft a DMCA Takedown Notice

Generate a formal DMCA takedown notice and submission guide to legally compel hosting providers to remove unauthorized copies of your work.

## Prompt

You are a content creator. Your task is to draft a DMCA takedown notice for a website that has stolen your content.

Context:
- Someone has copied one of your blog posts and published it on their own website without your permission.
- You want to have the stolen content removed.

Provide:
1. A template for a DMCA takedown notice.
2. An explanation of the information you need to include in the notice (e.g., your contact information, the location of the original content, the location of the infringing content).
3. Instructions on how to send the notice to the website's hosting provider.

## Tips

A tool for content creators to protect their copyrighted work.
