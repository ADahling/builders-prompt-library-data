# Customizing a Blameless Post-Mortem for a Specific Incident

Draft a blameless incident post-mortem in markdown for team review, with a timeline, root cause, impact, lessons, and action items.

## Prompt

Using the blameless post-mortem approach, customize a post-mortem for a recent incident that occurred in your system. Include specific details such as the incident timeline, root cause analysis, and impact assessment. Focus on the lessons learned and actionable improvements to prevent future occurrences. Use a structured format with sections for Incident Summary, Timeline, Root Cause, Impact, What Went Well, Improvements, and Action Items. Deliver the output in markdown format suitable for team review.

## Tips

Useful for incident retrospectives with a specific focus.
