# Creating an Alert Policy Document

Draft a project alert policy covering purpose, alert types, trigger conditions, recipients, response responsibilities, and review procedures.

Variables: project_name

## Prompt

Draft an alert policy document for {{project_name}}. This document should include the purpose of alerts, the types of alerts used, and the conditions under which each type is triggered. Additionally, outline the roles and responsibilities related to managing alerts, including who receives alerts and who is responsible for responding to them. Describe the procedures for reviewing and updating alert policies. The document should be structured into the following sections: 1) Purpose and Scope, 2) Alert Types and Conditions, 3) Roles and Responsibilities, 4) Review and Update Procedures.
