# Develop a Strategy for Log Retention

Draft a log retention strategy for your log source with retention periods by log type and a discussion of compliance considerations.

## Prompt

Draft a log retention strategy for our logs generated by {logSource}. Specify retention periods for various log types, such as {logType1} and {logType2}, and discuss compliance considerations.

## Tips

Retention policies should balance storage costs with data accessibility.
