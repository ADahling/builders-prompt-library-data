# Update Privacy Policy for GDPR Compliance

Revise an e-commerce privacy policy to include mandatory GDPR clauses, user rights, legal processing bases, and DPO contact information.

## Prompt

You are a data privacy consultant. Your task is to update an existing privacy policy to be compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Context:
- The company is a small e-commerce business that sells products to customers in the European Union.
- The current privacy policy does not mention user rights under GDPR.

Provide:
1. A section detailing user rights under GDPR (e.g., right to access, rectification, erasure).
2. Information on the legal basis for processing personal data.
3. Contact details for the Data Protection Officer (DPO).

## Tips

Essential for businesses with customers in the EU.
