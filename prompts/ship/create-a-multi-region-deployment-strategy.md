# Create a Multi-Region Deployment Strategy

Plan multi-region application deployment for high availability, covering data replication, traffic management, and cloud-based failover.

Variables: application_name, cloud_provider

## Prompt

Design a multi-region deployment strategy for your {{application_name}} to ensure high availability. Include considerations for data replication, traffic management, and failover mechanisms using {{cloud_provider}} services.

## Tips

Multi-region strategies can greatly enhance your application's reliability.
