# Create a Launch Checklist for a New Feature

Generate a comprehensive, time-bound launch plan covering pre-release prep, deployment steps, and risk mitigation for new feature rollouts.

Variables: feature_name

## Prompt

You are a product manager. Your task is to create a comprehensive launch checklist for a new feature, `{{feature_name}}`.

Instructions:
1. List all pre-launch activities, such as final testing, documentation updates, and marketing announcements.
2. Detail the steps for the launch day, including the deployment process and post-launch verification.
3. Outline post-launch monitoring and rollback procedures.
4. Assign owners and deadlines for each checklist item.

## Tips

Helps beginners in product and project management understand the importance of a structured launch process.
