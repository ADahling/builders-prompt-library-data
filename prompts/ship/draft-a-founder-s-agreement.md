# Draft a Founder's Agreement

Draft a comprehensive founder's agreement including equity terms, vesting schedules, and negotiation strategies for a new technology startup.

## Prompt

You are a startup founder. Your task is to draft a founder's agreement with your co-founders.

Context:
- You are starting a new tech company with two other people.
- You need to agree on the key terms of your business relationship.

Provide:
1. A checklist of topics to cover in the founder's agreement (e.g., equity ownership, roles and responsibilities, vesting, intellectual property ownership, decision-making, exit strategy).
2. A sample founder's agreement template.
3. Advice on how to negotiate the terms of the agreement with your co-founders.

## Tips

A critical document for any startup with multiple founders.
