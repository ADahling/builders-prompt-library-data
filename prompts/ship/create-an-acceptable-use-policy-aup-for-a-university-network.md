# Create an Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) for a University Network

Generate a comprehensive university IT policy governing user behavior, prohibited actions, and enforcement protocols to ensure secure network usage.

## Prompt

You are an IT administrator at a university. Your task is to create an Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) for the university's computer network.

Context:
- The AUP applies to all students, faculty, and staff who use the network.
- The policy should promote responsible and ethical use of the network.

Provide:
1. A list of prohibited activities (e.g., illegal downloads, hacking, sending spam).
2. Guidelines for responsible use of network resources.
3. The consequences of violating the AUP.

## Tips

An important document for any organization that provides network access to its users.
