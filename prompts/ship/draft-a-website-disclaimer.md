# Draft a Website Disclaimer

Draft a comprehensive legal disclaimer for a finance blog to mitigate liability by clarifying that content is for informational use only.

## Prompt

You are a blogger who writes about financial topics. Your task is to draft a disclaimer for your website.

Context:
- You are not a licensed financial advisor and your blog posts are for informational purposes only.
- You want to limit your liability in case someone makes a bad financial decision based on your content.

Provide:
1. A clear statement that you are not a financial advisor.
2. A disclaimer that the information on your website is not financial advice.
3. A recommendation that readers consult with a professional financial advisor before making any financial decisions.

## Tips

An important legal protection for bloggers and other content creators who write about professional topics.
