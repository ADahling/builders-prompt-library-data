# Draft a Simple Cookie Policy

Generate a concise cookie policy for small business websites that exclusively use essential session management cookies.

## Prompt

You are a junior web developer. Your task is to draft a simple cookie policy for a small business website.

Context:
- The website uses only essential cookies for session management.
- No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

Provide:
1. A brief explanation of what cookies are.
2. A statement that the website uses only essential cookies.
3. Information on how users can manage cookies in their browser settings.

## Tips

A basic cookie policy for websites with minimal cookie usage.
