# Develop a CCPA Training Program for Employees

Create a comprehensive CCPA compliance training program featuring regulatory overviews, data handling protocols, and interactive quizzes.

## Prompt

You are a corporate trainer specializing in data privacy. Your task is to develop a training program for employees on the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

Context:
- The company has many employees who handle customer data.
- The training needs to be comprehensive and engaging.

Provide:
1. An overview of the CCPA and its key requirements.
2. Specific guidance on how to handle consumer requests under the CCPA (e.g., access, deletion, opt-out).
3. Scenarios and quizzes to test employee understanding.

## Tips

Ensures that employees who handle personal information are aware of their responsibilities under the CCPA.
