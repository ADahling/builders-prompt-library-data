# Design a Compliance Framework for SLOs

Design an SLO compliance framework for a financial platform with policies, monitoring, reporting, audits, and feedback loops.

Variables: platform_name

## Prompt

Design a compliance framework to ensure adherence to defined SLOs for a financial services platform called {{platform_name}}. Discuss the importance of regulatory compliance and risk management in the context of financial services. Outline the components of the compliance framework, including policy development, performance monitoring, and reporting mechanisms. Include strategies for conducting regular audits and assessments to verify compliance. Explain how to incorporate feedback loops to continuously improve the compliance process. Provide examples of industry standards and best practices that should be considered when developing this framework. Highlight the role of compliance in maintaining trust with customers and regulatory bodies.

## Tips

Suitable for developers working in regulated industries needing robust compliance frameworks.
