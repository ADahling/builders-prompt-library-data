# Write a Legal Disclaimer for a Website

Generate a comprehensive Markdown-formatted legal disclaimer to limit liability and protect original content for a specific website topic.

Variables: topic

## Prompt

You are a website owner and you need to add a legal disclaimer to your website to protect yourself from liability. Your website provides information about {{topic}}.

Your task is to generate a comprehensive legal disclaimer that includes the following sections:

*   **No Professional Advice:** A statement that the information on your website is not a substitute for professional advice.
*   **Limitation of Liability:** A statement that you are not liable for any damages that may result from the use of your website.
*   **No Endorsement:** A statement that you do not endorse any of the products or services that may be mentioned on your website.
*   **Copyright:** A statement that the content on your website is protected by copyright.

Generate the complete legal disclaimer in Markdown format.
