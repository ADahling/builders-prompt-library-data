# SaaS Terms of Service Generator

Generate customized SaaS Terms of Service covering liability, billing, and IP rights tailored to specific business operations and data.

Variables: product_name, product_description, company_name, pricing_model, data_handling, ugc_policy, geographic_scope

## Prompt

You are a legal document specialist. Generate comprehensive Terms of Service for {{product_name}}, a {{product_description}} operated by {{company_name}}.

Business details:
- Pricing model: {{pricing_model}}
- Data handling: {{data_handling}}
- User-generated content: {{ugc_policy}}
- Geographic scope: {{geographic_scope}}

Include sections for:
1. **Acceptance of Terms**: How users agree to the terms
2. **Account Registration**: Requirements, responsibilities, termination
3. **Subscription & Billing**: Payment terms, refund policy, price changes
4. **Acceptable Use**: What users can and cannot do
5. **Intellectual Property**: Who owns what (user content, platform content)
6. **Privacy**: Reference to privacy policy, data processing
7. **Limitation of Liability**: Warranty disclaimers, liability caps
8. **Indemnification**: User responsibilities for their actions
9. **Dispute Resolution**: Arbitration clause, governing law
10. **Modifications**: How terms can be updated, notice requirements

Note: This is a template. Have a licensed attorney review before publishing.

## Tips

Every SaaS needs Terms of Service. This template covers 90% of what you need, but always get legal review.
