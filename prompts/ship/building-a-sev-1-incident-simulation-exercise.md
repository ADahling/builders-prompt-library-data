# Building a SEV-1 Incident Simulation Exercise

Get a Markdown SEV-1 training exercise with a scenario, roles, timeline, communication protocols, evaluation criteria, and debrief template.

## Prompt

Design a SEV-1 incident simulation exercise to train your team on handling critical incidents effectively. Outline the scenario, including the initial symptoms, expected impact, and potential root causes. Specify the roles of participants, the timeline of events, and the communication protocols to follow. Include evaluation criteria to assess team performance during the exercise and provide a debriefing template to discuss outcomes. Format the exercise plan in markdown for easy distribution and use in training sessions.

## Tips

To enhance team readiness for critical incident response.
