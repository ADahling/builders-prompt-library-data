# Draft Terms of Service for an E-commerce Website

Generate a legally structured Terms of Service agreement covering payment, shipping, and return policies for a consumer e-commerce platform.

## Prompt

You are a legal advisor. Your task is to draft the Terms of Service for an e-commerce website.

Context:
- The website sells physical goods directly to consumers.
- The ToS needs to cover payment, shipping, and returns.

Provide:
1. Terms related to payment processing and accepted payment methods.
2. The company's shipping and delivery policies.
3. A clear and fair return and refund policy.

## Tips

Crucial for any online store to manage customer expectations and legal risk.
