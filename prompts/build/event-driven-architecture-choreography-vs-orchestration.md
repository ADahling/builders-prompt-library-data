# Event-Driven Architecture: Choreography vs. Orchestration

Compare choreography and orchestration in event-driven systems to determine the best integration strategy through a structured trade-off analysis.

## Prompt

You are a software architect designing an event-driven system. You need to decide between a choreography-based approach and an orchestration-based approach.

**Your Task:**
Write a paper that compares and contrasts choreography and orchestration in the context of event-driven architectures.

**Instructions & Constraints:**
1.  **Choreography:**
    *   Explain the choreography-based approach.
    *   Discuss its pros and cons.

2.  **Orchestration:**
    *   Explain the orchestration-based approach.
    *   Discuss its pros and cons.

3.  **A Detailed Comparison:**
    *   Create a table that compares the two approaches on multiple criteria.

4.  **Recommendation:**
    *   Provide guidance on when to choose each approach.

**Output Format:**
Generate a paper in Markdown format:
1.  **Choreography vs. Orchestration in Event-Driven Architectures**
2.  **The Dance of Choreography: Pros and Cons**
3.  **The Conductor of Orchestration: Pros and Cons**
4.  **Making the Right Choice for Your System**
