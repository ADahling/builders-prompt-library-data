# Cloning a News Article Page with a Mobile-First Approach

Replicate a news article layout using mobile-first CSS to ensure optimal typography, vertical rhythm, and responsive multi-column scaling.

Variables: news_article_url

## Prompt

You are a front-end developer with some experience in responsive design. Your task is to clone a news article page using a mobile-first approach. The page should be highly readable on mobile devices, with a clean layout and optimized images.

Context:
- The news article page to clone is {{news_article_url}}.
- You must pay close attention to typography and vertical rhythm to ensure a comfortable reading experience on small screens.
- The layout should adapt to larger screens by introducing columns for related articles or advertisements.

Provide:
1. The HTML and CSS for the responsive news article page.
2. A write-up on how you optimized the reading experience for mobile users.

If you cannot open {{news_article_url}} yourself, say so before anything else and ask me for screenshots at each breakpoint or the page's HTML and CSS. Do not invent the design or describe it as verified.
If the full result does not fit in one reply, deliver it in parts in the order above, finish each part completely, and stop after each part so I can say continue.
