# Event-Driven Architecture: A Guide to Schema Management

Establish a robust schema management strategy for event-driven systems to ensure data consistency and seamless evolution across services.

## Prompt

You are a data architect for an event-driven system. You need to manage the schema of your events as they evolve over time.

**Your Task:**
Create a guide to schema management in an event-driven architecture.

**Instructions & Constraints:**
*   Discuss the importance of a schema registry.
*   Explain different schema evolution strategies (e.g., backward compatibility, forward compatibility).

**Output Format:**
Generate a guide in Markdown:
1.  **A Guide to Schema Management in an Event-Driven World**
2.  **The Schema Registry: Your Single Source of Truth for Event Schemas**
3.  **Evolving Your Schemas Without Breaking Your Consumers**
