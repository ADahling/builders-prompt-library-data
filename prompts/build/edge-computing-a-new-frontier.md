# Edge Computing: A New Frontier

Generate a structured technology article explaining the mechanics, drivers, and real-world applications of edge computing architecture.

## Prompt

You are a technology journalist writing an article about the rise of edge computing.

**Your Task:**
Write an article that explains what edge computing is, why it's important, and what its main use cases are.

**Instructions & Constraints:**
1.  **What is Edge Computing?**
    *   Explain the concept in simple terms.

2.  **Why is it Important?**
    *   Discuss the drivers behind the move to the edge (e.g., IoT, 5G, real-time AI).

3.  **Use Cases:**
    *   Provide examples of edge computing in action.

**Output Format:**
Generate an article in Markdown format:
1.  **Edge Computing: The Next Wave of Cloud Innovation**
2.  **Why the Future of Computing is at the Edge**
3.  **Edge Computing in Action: From Smart Factories to Autonomous Cars**
