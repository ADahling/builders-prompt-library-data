# Caching Strategies: A Deep Dive into Write-Through vs. Write-Back

Evaluate and compare write-through and write-back caching strategies to determine the optimal trade-off between data safety and performance.

## Prompt

You are a database architect designing a caching strategy. You need to decide between a write-through cache and a write-back cache.

**Your Task:**
Write a paper that compares and contrasts write-through and write-back caching.

**Instructions & Constraints:**
1.  **Write-Through Caching:**
    *   Explain how it works.
    *   Discuss its pros and cons.

2.  **Write-Back Caching:**
    *   Explain how it works.
    *   Discuss its pros and cons.

3.  **Recommendation:**
    *   Provide guidance on when to use each strategy.

**Output Format:**
Generate a paper in Markdown format:
1.  **Write-Through vs. Write-Back: A Tale of Two Caching Strategies**
2.  **Write-Through: The Safe and Simple Choice**
3.  **Write-Back: The High-Performance Option**
4.  **Making the Right Choice for Your Application**
