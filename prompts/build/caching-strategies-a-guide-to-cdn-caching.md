# Caching Strategies: A Guide to CDN Caching

Create a comprehensive Markdown guide explaining CDN mechanics, content selection, and optimization tactics to accelerate website performance.

## Prompt

You are a web developer. You want to use a Content Delivery Network (CDN) to cache your website's content.

**Your Task:**
Create a guide to CDN caching.

**Instructions & Constraints:**
*   Explain how a CDN works.
*   Discuss what content can and should be cached on a CDN.
*   Provide tips for optimizing your CDN caching strategy.

**Output Format:**
Generate a guide in Markdown:
1.  **A Developer's Guide to CDN Caching**
2.  **How a CDN Can Supercharge Your Website**
3.  **Optimizing Your CDN Caching Strategy: Tips and Tricks**
