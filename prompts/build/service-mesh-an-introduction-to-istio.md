# Service Mesh: An Introduction to Istio

Generate a Markdown presentation explaining Istio's service mesh concepts, core features, and architectural components for an organization.

## Prompt

You are a platform engineer introducing the Istio service mesh to your organization.

**Your Task:**
Create a presentation that explains what Istio is, its core features, and its architecture.

**Instructions & Constraints:**
1.  **What is Istio?**
    *   Explain the concept of a service mesh and the problems it solves.

2.  **Core Features:**
    *   Describe Istio's main features (traffic management, security, observability).

3.  **Architecture:**
    *   Explain the architecture of Istio (the control plane and the data plane).

**Output Format:**
Generate a presentation in Markdown format:
1.  **An Introduction to Istio: The Service Mesh for the Modern Era**
2.  **Unlocking Superpowers for Your Microservices: The Core Features of Istio**
3.  **A Look Under the Hood: The Istio Architecture**
