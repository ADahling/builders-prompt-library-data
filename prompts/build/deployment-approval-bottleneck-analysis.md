# Deployment Approval Bottleneck Analysis

Plan a deployment approval redesign with automated gates, risk-based routing, and emergency procedures to address approval delays.

## Prompt

Map out our deployment approval workflow and identify velocity killers:

Current state: [Describe approval chain, manual gates, average time from PR to production]

Pain points:
[Specific delays, who's the bottleneck, what approvals add little value]

Risk tolerance:
[Industries/compliance requirements, blast radius concerns]

Propose a "shift-left" approval redesign that maintains security and compliance while reducing deployment time by 50%.

Include automated gates, risk-based approval routing, and emergency deployment procedures.

## Tips

Most teams can cut deployment time in half by replacing manual gates with automated checks. Source: reddit.com/r/devops/comments/1qcu9ja
