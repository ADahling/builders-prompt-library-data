# Serverless Architecture: The Pros and Cons

Evaluate the strategic trade-offs of serverless architectures to determine technical feasibility for new product development.

## Prompt

You are a CTO of a startup considering a serverless architecture for your new product.

**Your Task:**
Create a balanced overview of the pros and cons of serverless architectures to help you make an informed decision.

**Instructions & Constraints:**
1.  **The Pros:**
    *   Discuss the advantages of serverless (e.g., cost, scalability, developer productivity).

2.  **The Cons:**
    *   Discuss the disadvantages of serverless (e.g., vendor lock-in, cold starts, observability challenges).

**Output Format:**
Generate a document in Markdown:
1.  **The Serverless Revolution: Should We Join?**
2.  **The Bright Side: The Advantages of Going Serverless**
3.  **The Dark Side: The Challenges of Serverless**
