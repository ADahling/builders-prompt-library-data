# Service Mesh: A Guide to mTLS

Implement mutual TLS within a service mesh to secure microservice communications through automated identity verification and encryption.

## Prompt

You are a security engineer. You want to use a service mesh to secure the communication between your microservices with mutual TLS (mTLS).

**Your Task:**
Create a guide to implementing mTLS with a service mesh.

**Output Format:**
Generate a guide in Markdown:
1.  **Securing Your Microservices with mTLS and a Service Mesh**
2.  **What is mTLS and Why Do You Need It?**
3.  **A Practical Guide to Enabling mTLS in Your Service Mesh**
