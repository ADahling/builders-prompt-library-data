# Distributed Systems: The CAP Theorem Explained

Explain the trade-offs of the CAP theorem by defining its core pillars and illustrating real-world system configurations for students.

## Prompt

You are a computer science professor explaining the CAP theorem to your students.

**Your Task:**
Create a clear and simple explanation of the CAP theorem.

**Instructions & Constraints:**
1.  **The Three Properties:**
    *   Explain Consistency, Availability, and Partition Tolerance.

2.  **The Trade-off:**
    *   Explain that a distributed system can only choose two of the three properties.

3.  **Real-World Examples:**
    *   Provide examples of CP, AP, and CA systems.

**Output Format:**
Generate a guide in Markdown:
1.  **The CAP Theorem: A Fundamental Law of Distributed Systems**
2.  **The Three Pillars: Consistency, Availability, and Partition Tolerance**
3.  **You Can't Have It All: The Inevitable Trade-off**
4.  **The CAP Theorem in the Wild: Real-World System Examples**
