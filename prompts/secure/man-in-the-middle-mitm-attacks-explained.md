# Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) Attacks Explained

Explain MitM attacks using simple analogies to persuade non-technical users to secure public Wi-Fi connections with a VPN.

## Prompt

You are explaining the concept of a Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) attack to a friend who is not a tech expert. You want to use a simple analogy to help them understand how this attack works and why using public Wi-Fi can be risky.

Context:
- Your friend often uses public Wi-Fi at cafes and airports.
- You want to convince them to use a VPN.

Provide:
1. A simple analogy to explain a MitM attack (e.g., someone intercepting and reading your mail before it reaches you).
2. An explanation of how an attacker could perform a MitM attack on a public Wi-Fi network.
3. A clear recommendation to use a VPN on public Wi-Fi and a simple explanation of how a VPN protects against MitM attacks.

## Tips

An important security concept explained in a simple and relatable way.
