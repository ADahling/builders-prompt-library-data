# What is a Deepfake?

Create an accessible, balanced tech article explaining deepfake mechanics, societal risks, and creative applications for a general audience.

## Prompt

You are a technology journalist writing an article about deepfakes. Your goal is to explain what a deepfake is, how it is created, and the potential societal impact of this technology in a way that is accessible to a general audience.

Context:
- The article is for a mainstream news publication.
- The tone should be balanced, covering both the creative and the malicious uses of deepfakes.

Provide:
1. A clear and simple definition of a deepfake.
2. A high-level explanation of the technology behind deepfakes (e.g., using generative adversarial networks or GANs).
3. A discussion of at least two potential risks of deepfakes (e.g., misinformation, blackmail) and one potential beneficial use (e.g., in entertainment, education).

## Tips

A timely and relevant prompt for explaining a powerful and potentially disruptive technology.
