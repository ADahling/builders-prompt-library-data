# Introduction to Penetration Testing Concepts

Onboard development teams to offensive security by explaining pentesting phases and defining expectations for the upcoming engagement.

Variables: testing_company, application_name

## Prompt

You are a security lead preparing your team for an upcoming penetration test. Your team has a strong development background but limited experience with offensive security. Your task is to create a document that introduces the core concepts of penetration testing and sets expectations for the engagement.

Context:
- The penetration test will be performed by {{testing_company}}.
- The scope of the test is the {{application_name}} application.

Provide:
1. A clear definition of penetration testing and how it differs from a vulnerability scan.
2. An overview of the typical phases of a penetration test (e.g., reconnaissance, scanning, exploitation, post-exploitation, reporting).
3. A list of key questions to ask the penetration testing company before the engagement begins.

## Tips

An excellent resource for development teams preparing for their first penetration test.
