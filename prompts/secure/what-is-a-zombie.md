# What is a Zombie?

Draft a non-technical podcast explanation of zombie computers and botnets using simple analogies to educate a general audience.

## Prompt

You are a cybersecurity expert who is being interviewed for a podcast. The host asks you to explain what a 'zombie computer' is. Your task is to provide a clear and concise explanation for a non-technical audience.

Context:
- The podcast is about technology and cybersecurity for a general audience.
- The host wants a simple analogy to help the listeners understand the concept.

Provide:
1. A simple and relatable analogy to explain what a zombie computer is (e.g., a puppet that is controlled by someone else).
2. An explanation of how a computer becomes a zombie (i.e., through a malware infection).
3. A description of what a zombie computer can be used for by an attacker, such as being part of a botnet to send spam or to launch DDoS attacks.

## Tips

A good prompt for explaining a common cybersecurity term in a simple and memorable way.
