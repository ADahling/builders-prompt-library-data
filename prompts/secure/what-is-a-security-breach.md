# What is a Security Breach?

Draft a comprehensive security breach response package including a customer notification email, regulatory checklists, and an inquiry plan.

Variables: company_type

## Prompt

You are a customer service manager who needs to communicate with your customers about a recent security breach. The breach involved the unauthorized access to customer names and email addresses. Your task is to draft a notification email to the affected customers.

Context:
- The company is a `{{company_type}}`.
- The breach did not involve any financial data or passwords.

Provide:
1. A draft of the security breach notification email. The email should be clear, concise, and empathetic. It should explain what happened, what information was involved, what the company is doing in response, and what the customers can do to protect themselves.
2. A list of the key elements that should be included in a security breach notification, according to regulations like GDPR.
3. A plan for how to handle customer inquiries about the breach.

## Tips

A practical and important prompt that deals with the communication aspect of incident response.
