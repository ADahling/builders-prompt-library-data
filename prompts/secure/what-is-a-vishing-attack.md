# What is a Vishing Attack?

Generate interactive security training materials, including scripts and red flags, to educate bank staff on identifying vishing attempts.

## Prompt

You are a security awareness officer who is creating a training session on vishing attacks. Vishing is a type of phishing attack that uses voice calls instead of emails. Your task is to create the content for this training session.

Context:
- The audience is employees of a financial institution.
- The training should be interactive and engaging.

Provide:
1. A clear definition of a vishing attack.
2. A role-playing script for a vishing attack. The script should have two roles: the attacker and the employee. The attacker should try to trick the employee into revealing sensitive information, such as a customer's account number.
3. A list of red flags to listen for in a phone call that might indicate a vishing attack (e.g., a sense of urgency, a request for confidential information, a threat of negative consequences).

## Tips

A practical and interactive way to train employees on how to recognize and to respond to vishing attacks.
