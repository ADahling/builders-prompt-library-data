# Security Policy Development

Draft a comprehensive Acceptable Use Policy and roll-out strategy tailored to a company’s specific industry and workforce size.

Variables: company_type, number_of_employees

## Prompt

You are a security manager who has been tasked with creating a new information security policy for your company. The first policy you need to write is an Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) for employees. Your task is to draft this policy.

Context:
- The company is a `{{company_type}}` with about `{{number_of_employees}}` employees.
- The AUP should be clear, concise, and easy for all employees to understand.

Provide:
1. A list of the key sections that should be included in an AUP (e.g., purpose, scope, acceptable uses, unacceptable uses, enforcement).
2. A draft of the 'Unacceptable Uses' section, with at least five specific examples of prohibited activities.
3. A plan for how to communicate the new policy to employees and get their acknowledgement.

## Tips

A practical prompt for security managers and anyone involved in creating security policies.
