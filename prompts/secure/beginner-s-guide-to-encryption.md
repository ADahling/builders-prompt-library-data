# Beginner's Guide to Encryption

Explain the fundamentals of symmetric and asymmetric encryption using practical analogies to help junior developers secure sensitive user data.

Variables: programming_language

## Prompt

You are a mentor to a junior developer who is new to the concept of encryption. Your task is to explain the basics of encryption in a simple and understandable way. You need to cover the difference between symmetric and asymmetric encryption and provide a real-world analogy for each.

Context:
- The junior developer is working on a feature that requires storing sensitive user data.
- The programming language is `{{programming_language}}`.

Provide:
1. A simple definition of encryption and why it is important.
2. An explanation of symmetric encryption with an analogy (e.g., a shared secret key for a locked box).
3. An explanation of asymmetric encryption with an analogy (e.g., a public mailbox with a private key to open it).

## Tips

A foundational prompt for anyone new to security and data protection.
