# What is a Security Policy?

Generate a concise one-page information security policy, implementation rationale, and employee communication plan tailored for small businesses.

Variables: number_of_employees

## Prompt

You are a small business owner who wants to create a simple information security policy for your company. You are not a security expert, so you need a simple template to get started. Your task is to create a one-page information security policy.

Context:
- The company has `{{number_of_employees}}` employees.
- The company handles some customer data, but it is not highly sensitive.

Provide:
1. A simple, one-page information security policy. The policy should be written in plain English and should cover the key areas of information security, such as acceptable use, data classification, access control, and incident response.
2. A brief explanation of why each section of the policy is important.
3. A plan for how to communicate the policy to your employees and to get their acknowledgement.

## Tips

A practical prompt for small business owners and managers who need to create their first security policy.
