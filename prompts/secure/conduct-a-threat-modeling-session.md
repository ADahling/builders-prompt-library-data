# Conduct a Threat Modeling Session

Get an architecture-based application threat model with vulnerabilities, impacts, a risk assessment, and mitigations for the top three threats.

Variables: application_name

## Prompt

Develop a threat model for the application {{application_name}}. Identify potential threats, vulnerabilities, and impacts based on the application's architecture. Provide a risk assessment and recommend mitigations for the top three identified threats.

## Tips

Useful for understanding the threat landscape of your application.
