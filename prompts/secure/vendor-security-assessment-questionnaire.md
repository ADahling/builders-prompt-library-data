# Vendor Security Assessment Questionnaire

Generate a comprehensive Markdown security questionnaire to evaluate a SaaS vendor's risk posture, compliance, and data protection practices.

Variables: company_name, vendor_name

## Prompt

You are a security analyst at {{company_name}}. Your company is considering using a new SaaS vendor, {{vendor_name}}, to process sensitive customer data. You need to conduct a security assessment of the vendor to ensure they meet your company's security requirements.

Create a comprehensive vendor security assessment questionnaire in Markdown. The questionnaire should be sent to the vendor to gather information about their security posture.

The questionnaire should cover the following areas:

1.  **Information Security Policies:** Does the vendor have a formal information security program?
2.  **Access Control:** How does the vendor control access to their systems and data?
3.  **Encryption:** How does the vendor encrypt data in transit and at rest?
4.  **Vulnerability Management:** How does the vendor scan for and remediate vulnerabilities?
5.  **Incident Response:** Does the vendor have an incident response plan? Have they had any recent security breaches?
6.  **Compliance:** Is the vendor compliant with any industry security standards (e.g., SOC 2, ISO 27001)?
7.  **Data Privacy:** How does the vendor handle customer data? What are their data retention and privacy policies?

For each question, provide a brief explanation of why the information is needed. Your questionnaire should be thorough and help you make an informed decision about the security of the vendor.
