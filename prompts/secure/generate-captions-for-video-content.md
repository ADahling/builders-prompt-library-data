# Generate Captions for Video Content

Generate synchronized, SRT-formatted captions with timecodes and sound descriptions to improve video accessibility and viewer engagement.

Variables: project_title, video_duration, language

## Prompt

You are tasked with creating accurate and synchronized captions for a video project. The project is titled {{project_title}} and has a duration of {{video_duration}} minutes. The video is in the {{language}} language and needs captions that are concise, grammatically correct, and accurately reflect the spoken content. Additionally, the captions should be synchronized with the video to enhance viewer comprehension. Please adhere to the following requirements: 

1. **Content Accuracy**: Ensure that all spoken words and sounds are accurately represented in the captions. 

2. **Simplicity and Clarity**: Use simple language and avoid jargon to ensure the captions are easily understood by a broad audience. 

3. **Synchronization**: Use timecodes to ensure the captions appear at the correct times during playback. 

4. **Formatting**: Use standard captioning format with a maximum of two lines of text per screen, each with a maximum of 32 characters. 

5. **Accessibility Considerations**: Include descriptions of non-verbal sounds that are essential to understanding the content. 

Output the captions in a standard subtitle format such as SRT, including timecodes for each caption entry. Ensure that the file is properly formatted for direct use in video editing software or video streaming platforms.

## Tips

Use this prompt when you need to generate captions for video content, ensuring accessibility and enhancing viewer engagement. This is particularly useful for educational content, corporate videos, or any project requiring accurate and accessible video captions.
