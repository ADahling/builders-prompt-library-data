# What is a Virus?

Explain computer viruses to non-technical users through simple analogies and provide actionable recovery steps after a detected threat.

## Prompt

You are a tech support specialist who is explaining to a customer what a computer virus is. The customer is worried because their antivirus software has detected a virus on their computer. Your task is to explain what a virus is and what to do about it in simple terms.

Context:
- The customer is not a technical person.
- The antivirus software has already quarantined the virus.

Provide:
1. A simple analogy to explain what a computer virus is (e.g., like a flu virus for your computer).
2. An explanation of how a computer virus spreads (e.g., through email attachments, infected downloads).
3. A clear instruction on what the customer should do next (e.g., let the antivirus software delete the virus, run a full system scan).

## Tips

A common and practical scenario for tech support, presented in a simple and clear way.
