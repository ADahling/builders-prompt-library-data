# What is an Indicator of Compromise (IOC)?

Define IOCs and research actionable threat intelligence data for a specific malware campaign using public security sources.

Variables: malware_campaign_name

## Prompt

You are a junior security analyst who is learning about threat intelligence. Your manager has asked you to find some Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) for a recent malware campaign. Your task is to research the malware campaign and to collect some IOCs.

Context:
- The malware campaign is `{{malware_campaign_name}}`.
- You will use public sources of threat intelligence, such as security blogs and threat reports.

Provide:
1. A clear definition of an Indicator of Compromise (IOC).
2. A list of at least three different types of IOCs (e.g., file hashes, IP addresses, domain names, registry keys).
3. A list of at least five IOCs that you have found for the specified malware campaign.

## Tips

A foundational concept in threat intelligence and security operations, presented as a practical research task.
