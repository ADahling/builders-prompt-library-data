# What is a Risk Assessment?

Generate a security risk assessment report featuring asset vulnerabilities, threat identification, and a qualitative risk matrix for a small business.

Variables: business_type, number_of_employees

## Prompt

You are a security consultant who has been hired by a small business to help them improve their security. The first thing you need to do is to conduct a security risk assessment. Your task is to explain what a risk assessment is and to create a simple risk assessment for the business.

Context:
- The business is a `{{business_type}}` with about `{{number_of_employees}}` employees.
- The business has a website, an office network, and employee laptops.

Provide:
1. A clear and simple definition of a security risk assessment.
2. A list of at least five potential security risks to the business. For each risk, you should identify the asset, the vulnerability, and the threat.
3. A simple risk matrix (e.g., a 3x3 matrix with likelihood and impact) and a qualitative assessment of each identified risk (e.g., high, medium, low).

## Tips

A foundational concept in information security management, presented as a practical task.
