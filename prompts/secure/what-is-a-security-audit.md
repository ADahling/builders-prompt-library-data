# What is a Security Audit?

Generate a structured security audit plan and compliance tests for a specific department to verify adherence to internal IT policies.

Variables: department_name

## Prompt

You are a junior IT auditor who has been tasked with conducting a security audit of a department in your company. This is your first audit, and you need to create an audit plan. Your task is to create a simple security audit plan.

Context:
- The department being audited is the `{{department_name}}` department.
- The scope of the audit is the department's compliance with the company's information security policy.

Provide:
1. An explanation of what a security audit is and its purpose.
2. A simple audit plan, with sections for the audit objectives, scope, methodology, and schedule.
3. A list of at least five audit tests you will perform. For each test, you should specify the control being tested and the evidence you will collect.
