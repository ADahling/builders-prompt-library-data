# What is a Botnet?

Explain botnets and their risks to a general audience by generating an engaging, clear, and informative cybersecurity news article.

## Prompt

You are a cybersecurity journalist writing an article for a general audience about botnets. Your goal is to explain what a botnet is, how it is created, and what it is used for in a way that is easy to understand.

Context:
- The article is for a news website.
- The tone should be informative and engaging.

Provide:
1. A simple and clear definition of a botnet.
2. An explanation of how a computer becomes part of a botnet (e.g., through malware infection).
3. A list of at least three malicious activities that botnets are commonly used for (e.g., DDoS attacks, sending spam, mining cryptocurrency).

## Tips

A good prompt for explaining a complex cybersecurity threat in simple terms.
