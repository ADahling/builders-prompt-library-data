# What is a DDoS Attack?

Train junior staff on DDoS fundamentals, attack categories, and mitigation strategies using clear analogies and technical overviews.

## Prompt

You are a network engineer explaining a Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attack to a junior colleague. You want to help them understand what a DDoS attack is, how it works, and the different types of DDoS attacks.

Context:
- Your company's website was recently the target of a DDoS attack.
- Your colleague is new to the field of network security.

Provide:
1. A simple analogy to explain a DDoS attack (e.g., a traffic jam on the highway to your website).
2. An explanation of the three main categories of DDoS attacks: volume-based, protocol-based, and application-layer attacks.
3. A high-level overview of common DDoS mitigation techniques (e.g., rate limiting, IP blacklisting, using a DDoS mitigation service).

## Tips

A foundational topic in network security, explained in a clear and structured way.
