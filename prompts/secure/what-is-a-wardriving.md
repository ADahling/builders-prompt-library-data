# What is a Wardriving?

Learn how to safely and legally map local wireless networks using open-source tools to identify common encryption vulnerabilities.

Variables: wardriving_tool

## Prompt

You are a security enthusiast who is interested in learning about Wi-Fi security. You have decided to try wardriving, the act of searching for Wi-Fi networks from a moving vehicle. Your goal is to map the Wi-Fi networks in your neighborhood and to identify any that are insecure.

Context:
- You have a laptop with a Wi-Fi card that supports monitor mode.
- You will use a free and open-source tool like `{{wardriving_tool}}` (e.g., Kismet, WiGLE).

Provide:
1. An explanation of what wardriving is and the legal and ethical considerations.
2. A step-by-step guide on how to set up your laptop and the chosen tool for wardriving.
3. A description of what to look for in the scan results to identify insecure Wi-Fi networks (e.g., networks with no encryption, networks using WEP).

## Tips

A fun and practical prompt for those who want to learn about Wi-Fi security in a hands-on way.
