# What is Ransomware?

Draft a concise security guide for non-technical staff explaining ransomware risks, delivery methods, and emergency response procedures.

## Prompt

You are a security awareness trainer creating a short guide on ransomware for employees. Your goal is to explain what ransomware is, how it spreads, and what to do in case of an infection.

Context:
- The audience is non-technical employees.
- The guide should be easy to read and actionable.

Provide:
1. A simple definition of ransomware and its impact.
2. A list of common ways ransomware spreads (e.g., phishing emails, malicious downloads).
3. Clear instructions on what to do if an employee suspects their computer is infected with ransomware, including who to contact immediately.

## Tips

An essential security awareness topic given the prevalence and impact of ransomware attacks.
