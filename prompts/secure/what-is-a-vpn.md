# What is a VPN?

Explain the core functions and benefits of a VPN using simple analogies to help non-technical users protect their privacy on public Wi-Fi.

## Prompt

You are explaining to a friend what a VPN is and why they might want to use one. Your friend is not a technical person, so you need to use simple terms and analogies.

Context:
- Your friend is concerned about their privacy online.
- They often use public Wi-Fi.

Provide:
1. A simple analogy to explain what a VPN does (e.g., a private tunnel for your internet traffic).
2. A list of at least three benefits of using a VPN (e.g., enhances privacy, improves security on public Wi-Fi, can bypass geo-restrictions).
3. A high-level guide on how to choose a reputable VPN provider.

## Tips

A great prompt for explaining a fundamental privacy and security tool to a non-technical audience.
