# What is a Spear Phishing Attack?

Analyze a targeted spear phishing attack on an executive to generate a detailed forensic report and strategic mitigation recommendations.

Variables: executive_title

## Prompt

You are a security analyst who is investigating a spear phishing attack that targeted a high-profile executive in your company. The attacker did a lot of research on the executive and crafted a very convincing email. Your task is to analyze the attack and to create a report.

Context:
- The executive is the `{{executive_title}}`.
- The goal of the attacker was to get the executive to click on a malicious link.

Provide:
1. An explanation of what a spear phishing attack is and how it differs from a regular phishing attack.
2. An analysis of the spear phishing email, pointing out the elements that made it so convincing (e.g., personalized greeting, reference to a recent event, spoofed sender address).
3. A list of recommendations for how to protect high-profile employees from spear phishing attacks (e.g., targeted training, email filtering, two-factor authentication).

## Tips

A practical prompt that deals with a more sophisticated and targeted type of phishing attack.
