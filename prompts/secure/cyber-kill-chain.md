# Cyber Kill Chain

Map observed security artifacts to the Cyber Kill Chain to analyze APT intrusions and optimize defensive response strategies.

## Prompt

You are a threat intelligence analyst who is using the Cyber Kill Chain framework to analyze a recent intrusion. You have collected a variety of artifacts from the incident, and your task is to map them to the different phases of the Cyber Kill Chain.

Context:
- The intrusion was a sophisticated attack by an Advanced Persistent Threat (APT) group.
- The artifacts you have collected include phishing emails, malware samples, C2 server IP addresses, and logs showing lateral movement.

Provide:
1. A brief explanation of the seven phases of the Cyber Kill Chain framework.
2. A table that maps the collected artifacts to the corresponding phases of the Cyber Kill Chain.
3. An explanation of how understanding the Cyber Kill Chain can help in developing a more effective defense strategy.

## Tips

An advanced prompt for security analysts on how to use a popular framework for analyzing intrusions.
