# What is Phishing?

Draft a clear security training segment to define phishing, list common red flags, and outline incident reporting procedures for staff.

## Prompt

You are a security awareness trainer creating a presentation for new employees. Your first topic is phishing, one of the most common ways attackers gain unauthorized access to systems. Your task is to create a simple and clear explanation of what phishing is and how to spot a phishing email.

Context:
- The audience is non-technical employees.
- The goal is to make them more resilient to phishing attacks.

Provide:
1. A simple definition of phishing.
2. A list of at least five common signs of a phishing email (e.g., sense of urgency, generic greetings, suspicious links or attachments).
3. A clear instruction on what employees should do if they suspect they have received a phishing email.
