# Descriptive Labels for Form Controls

Review webpage form controls for clear, descriptive labels and check that label references match their corresponding input IDs.

## Prompt

Ensure all form controls on your webpage, including inputs and buttons, have clear and descriptive labels. For example, if you have an input for {inputType}, use <label for='inputId'>Enter {inputType}:</label>. Also, check that the 'for' attribute matches the corresponding input's 'id'.

## Tips

Descriptive labels are essential for screen reader users.
