# What is a Whaling Attack?

Generate an executive-level training module to educate senior leadership on identifying and mitigating targeted high-value whaling attacks.

## Prompt

You are a CISO who is concerned about whaling attacks, a type of phishing attack that specifically targets senior executives. You want to create a special training session for your company's executives to make them more resilient to these attacks. Your task is to create the content for this training session.

Context:
- The audience is the C-suite of your company.
- The training should be short, to the point, and highly relevant to them.

Provide:
1. A clear definition of a whaling attack and how it differs from a regular phishing attack.
2. A few real-world examples of successful whaling attacks and their impact on the targeted companies.
3. A list of practical tips for executives on how to spot and to respond to a potential whaling attack. The tips should be tailored to their context and should include things like being suspicious of urgent requests for wire transfers or sensitive information.

## Tips

A practical and important prompt for protecting a company's most valuable targets.
