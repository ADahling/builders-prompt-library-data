# What is a Smart Contract?

Explain smart contracts using simple analogies and non-financial examples to help beginners grasp key technical properties and utility.

## Prompt

You are a blockchain enthusiast explaining the concept of a smart contract to a friend who is new to the space. You want to use a simple analogy to help them understand what a smart contract is and how it works.

Context:
- Your friend has heard about cryptocurrencies like Ethereum but does not understand the technology behind them.
- You want to explain the potential of smart contracts beyond just financial transactions.

Provide:
1. A simple analogy to explain what a smart contract is (e.g., a vending machine that automatically executes a contract when certain conditions are met).
2. An explanation of the key properties of a smart contract: it is self-executing, immutable, and transparent.
3. An example of a simple, non-financial use case for a smart contract (e.g., a decentralized voting system).

## Tips

A good prompt for explaining a core concept of blockchain technology in a simple and accessible way.
