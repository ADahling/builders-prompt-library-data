# What is a Trojan Horse?

Create engaging cybersecurity lessons for children that explain Trojan horse malware using simplified mythological analogies and relatable examples.

Variables: age_range

## Prompt

You are a cybersecurity educator who is creating a video for kids about online safety. You want to explain what a Trojan horse is in a way that is easy for them to understand. You decide to use the story of the Trojan horse from Greek mythology as an analogy.

Context:
- The video is for kids aged `{{age_range}}`.
- The tone should be fun and engaging.

Provide:
1. A short and simple retelling of the story of the Trojan horse.
2. An explanation of how a computer Trojan horse is similar to the Trojan horse from the story (i.e., it looks like something good, but it has something bad hidden inside).
3. An example of a computer Trojan horse that kids might encounter, such as a free game that is bundled with malware.

## Tips

A creative and effective way to explain a cybersecurity concept to a young audience.
