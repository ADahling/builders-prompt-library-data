# Introduction to Digital Signatures

Explain digital signatures to non-technical users using simple analogies to verify document authenticity and integrity in common software.

## Prompt

You are explaining the concept of a digital signature to a non-technical colleague. You want to help them understand how digital signatures can be used to verify the authenticity and integrity of a document.

Context:
- Your colleague has received a digitally signed document and is not sure what it means.
- You want to use a simple analogy.

Provide:
1. A simple analogy to explain what a digital signature is (e.g., a tamper-proof seal on a letter).
2. An explanation of the two main guarantees that a digital signature provides: authenticity (who sent it) and integrity (it has not been changed).
3. A high-level description of how to verify a digital signature using common software (e.g., Adobe Acrobat Reader).

## Tips

A foundational concept in digital security, explained in a simple and accessible way.
