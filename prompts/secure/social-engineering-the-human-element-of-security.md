# Social Engineering: The Human Element of Security

Educate a diverse audience on social engineering through relatable definitions, illustrative scenarios, and actionable defense strategies.

## Prompt

You are a security expert giving a talk on social engineering. You want to explain what social engineering is and provide a few examples of common social engineering techniques to make the audience more aware of these threats.

Context:
- The audience is a mix of technical and non-technical people.
- The talk should be engaging and memorable.

Provide:
1. A simple and relatable definition of social engineering.
2. At least three examples of social engineering techniques (e.g., pretexting, baiting, tailgating) with a brief story or scenario for each.
3. A key takeaway message for the audience on how to protect themselves from social engineering attacks.

## Tips

A crucial security awareness topic that focuses on the human side of security.
