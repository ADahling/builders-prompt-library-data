# Incident Response Plan for a Cloud Data Breach

Generate a NIST-aligned incident response plan to systematically detect, contain, and recover from cloud-based customer data breaches.

Variables: company_name, cloud_provider

## Prompt

You are the lead of the Security Incident Response Team (SIRT) at {{company_name}}. You need to create a detailed incident response plan for a data breach involving customer data stored in your {{cloud_provider}} environment.

Create a comprehensive incident response plan in Markdown. The plan should be actionable and follow the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover).

The plan must include the following sections:

1.  **Preparation:**
    *   Roles and responsibilities of the SIRT.
    *   Communication plan (internal and external).
    *   Tools and resources required for incident response.

2.  **Detection and Analysis:**
    *   How will a data breach be detected (e.g., through monitoring, alerts, or external notification)?
    *   What are the initial steps to take to analyze the breach and determine its scope?

3.  **Containment, Eradication, and Recovery:**
    *   What are the short-term and long-term steps to contain the breach and prevent further data loss?
    *   How will the root cause of the breach be eradicated?
    *   What is the process for recovering the affected systems and data?

4.  **Post-Incident Activity:**
    *   What is the process for conducting a post-mortem of the incident?
    *   How will the lessons learned be used to improve security controls?
    *   What are the legal and regulatory reporting requirements?

Your incident response plan should be a clear and detailed guide that will enable your team to respond effectively to a data breach.
