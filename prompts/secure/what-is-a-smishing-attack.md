# What is a Smishing Attack?

Generate a concise security awareness module defining smishing, identifying common red flags, and analyzing a real-world attack example.

## Prompt

You are a security awareness trainer who is creating a training module on smishing attacks. Smishing is a type of phishing attack that uses text messages (SMS) instead of emails. Your task is to create the content for this training module.

Context:
- The audience is all employees of the company.
- The training should be short and easy to understand.

Provide:
1. A clear definition of a smishing attack.
2. A list of at least three common signs of a smishing attack (e.g., a sense of urgency, a request for personal information, a suspicious link).
3. A real-world example of a smishing message and an explanation of why it is suspicious.

## Tips

A relevant and important security awareness topic, as smishing attacks are becoming more common.
