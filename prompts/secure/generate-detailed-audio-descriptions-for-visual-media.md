# Generate Detailed Audio Descriptions for Visual Media

Generate time-stamped, accessible audio descriptions that capture visual details, character actions, and scene dynamics for media projects.

Variables: project_title

## Prompt

You are tasked with creating detailed audio descriptions for a visual media project titled '{{project_title}}'. This project involves providing comprehensive and vivid verbal descriptions for visually impaired audiences. Your goal is to describe the visual content accurately and engagingly. Here are the specific requirements and instructions: 

1. **Contextual Information**: Start with a brief overview of the scene, setting, or event, highlighting key visual elements and their significance.

2. **Character Descriptions**: Include detailed descriptions of characters, focusing on their actions, expressions, clothing, and interactions. Mention any notable features that contribute to the scene's context or mood.

3. **Scene Dynamics**: Describe movement, transitions, and any visual changes that occur within the scene. Pay attention to camera angles, lighting, and color schemes that convey emotions or themes.

4. **Audio Integration**: Ensure that your descriptions are synchronized with existing audio elements. Avoid overlapping with dialogue or sound effects, allowing for a seamless narrative experience.

5. **Format**: Provide your descriptions in a structured format, with time stamps (e.g., [00:01:15]) for each descriptive segment.

For example, if describing a beach scene, you might say: '[00:02:10] The camera pans over a serene beach at sunrise. Gentle waves lap at the shore, creating a rhythmic sound. A lone seagull soars above, its wings catching the first light of dawn.'

Please adapt your descriptions to match the tone and style of the original media while ensuring they are accessible to all audiences.

## Tips

Use this prompt when you need to create audio descriptions for visual media projects aimed at visually impaired audiences. It is suitable for movies, TV shows, or any visual content requiring detailed verbal narration to enhance accessibility. Ensure that the descriptions are synchronized with existing audio tracks and provide a coherent narrative experience.
