# What is a Purple Team?

Design and execute a collaborative security exercise to synchronize offensive and defensive teams for improved threat detection.

## Prompt

You are a security manager who wants to improve the collaboration between your offensive security team (Red Team) and your defensive security team (Blue Team). You have decided to run a purple team exercise. Your task is to plan this exercise.

Context:
- The Red Team has a set of TTPs they will use to simulate an attack.
- The Blue Team is responsible for detecting and responding to the attack.
- The goal of the exercise is to improve the Blue Team's detection and response capabilities.

Provide:
1. An explanation of what a purple team exercise is and how it differs from a traditional Red Team engagement.
2. A plan for the purple team exercise, including the scope, the rules of engagement, and the communication plan between the Red Team and the Blue Team.
3. A list of the key metrics you will use to measure the success of the exercise (e.g., time to detection, time to remediation).

## Tips

A strategic prompt for security leaders on how to foster a more collaborative and effective security culture.
