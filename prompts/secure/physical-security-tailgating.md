# Physical Security: Tailgating

Create effective security awareness content to prevent unauthorized building access by educating employees on the risks of tailgating.

## Prompt

You are a security manager creating a security awareness poster about tailgating. The poster should be visual and easy to understand, with a clear message. Your task is to design the content for this poster.

Context:
- The poster will be placed near the entrance of the office building.
- The goal is to remind employees not to let unauthorized people follow them into the building.

Provide:
1. A catchy headline for the poster.
2. A simple and clear graphic or image that illustrates the concept of tailgating.
3. A short and direct message that tells employees what to do if someone tries to tailgate them (e.g., 'Don't hold the door for strangers. Ask them to use their own badge.').

## Tips

A simple but important aspect of physical security, presented in a creative format.
