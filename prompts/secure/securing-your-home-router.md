# Securing Your Home Router

Generate a beginner-friendly guide to securing a specific home router by updating administrative credentials and encryption settings.

Variables: router_brand

## Prompt

You are helping a friend secure their new home Wi-Fi router. The router is currently using the default settings, which is not secure. Your task is to guide your friend through the basic steps to secure their router.

Context:
- Your friend is not a technical person.
- The router is a `{{router_brand}}` model.

Provide:
1. A list of the top three most important things to do to secure a new router.
2. Step-by-step instructions for each of the three things, explained in simple, non-technical language. The three things should be: changing the default admin password, changing the Wi-Fi password (SSID and pre-shared key), and enabling WPA2 or WPA3 encryption.
3. A brief explanation of why each of these steps is important.

## Tips

A very practical and essential security guide for everyone.
