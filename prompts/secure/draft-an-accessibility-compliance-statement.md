# Draft an Accessibility Compliance Statement

Generate a formal web accessibility statement outlining compliance standards, commitment goals, and support channels for user feedback.

## Prompt

You are a web content manager. Your task is to draft an accessibility compliance statement for your company's website.

Context:
- The company is committed to making its website accessible to people with disabilities.
- The statement should be published on the website.

Provide:
1. A statement of commitment to accessibility.
2. A list of the accessibility standards the website aims to meet (e.g., WCAG 2.1 AA).
3. Contact information for users to provide feedback on accessibility issues.

## Tips

Demonstrates a commitment to accessibility and can help mitigate legal risk.
