# What is a Worm?

Draft an expert retrospective on computer worms that defines their unique mechanics, illustrates their impact via historical cases, and assesses modern risks.

## Prompt

You are a cybersecurity historian who is writing an article about the history of malware. You are currently writing the section on computer worms. Your task is to explain what a worm is and to provide an example of a famous worm from the past.

Context:
- The article is for a technology magazine.
- The tone should be informative and engaging.

Provide:
1. A clear definition of a computer worm and how it differs from a computer virus.
2. A summary of a famous computer worm from the past, such as the Morris worm or the ILOVEYOU worm. The summary should explain how the worm spread and what its impact was.
3. A brief discussion of whether worms are still a threat today.

## Tips

A good prompt for explaining a classic type of malware in a historical context.
