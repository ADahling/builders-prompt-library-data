# Wi-Fi Security: WPA2 vs. WPA3

Compare WPA2 and WPA3 security standards and configure a specific router model with the most secure encryption settings available.

Variables: router_model

## Prompt

You are a network security enthusiast who wants to secure your home Wi-Fi network. You have heard that WPA3 is more secure than WPA2. Your task is to research the differences between WPA2 and WPA3 and configure your router to use the most secure option available.

Context:
- Your home Wi-Fi router is a `{{router_model}}`.
- You want to understand the practical benefits of WPA3.

Provide:
1. A summary of the key security improvements in WPA3 compared to WPA2 (e.g., protection against offline dictionary attacks, improved forward secrecy).
2. Instructions on how to check if your router and devices support WPA3.
3. A step-by-step guide on how to configure your router to use WPA3. If WPA3 is not available, describe the most secure settings for WPA2.
