# Introduction to Firewalls

Explain firewall basics to non-technical users through simple analogies and configuration steps to secure their home network.

## Prompt

You are explaining the concept of a firewall to a family member who is not technical. You want to help them understand what a firewall is and why it is important for their home network's security.

Context:
- Your family member has a new home Wi-Fi router.
- You want to ensure they have the built-in firewall enabled.

Provide:
1. A simple analogy to explain what a firewall does (e.g., a security guard for your network).
2. An explanation of the difference between inbound and outbound traffic and how a firewall can control both.
3. Instructions on how to check if the firewall on their home router is enabled.

## Tips

A foundational security concept explained in a non-technical and relatable way.
