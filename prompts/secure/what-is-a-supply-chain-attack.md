# What is a Supply Chain Attack?

Educate stakeholders on software supply chain risks through a SolarWinds case study and actionable mitigation strategies.

## Prompt

You are a security consultant explaining the concept of a software supply chain attack to a client. You want to help them understand this type of attack and its potential impact. You decide to use the SolarWinds attack as a case study.

Context:
- Your client is a large enterprise that uses a lot of third-party software.
- They are concerned about the security of their software supply chain.

Provide:
1. A clear definition of a software supply chain attack.
2. A summary of the SolarWinds attack, explaining how the attackers compromised the build process of SolarWinds' Orion software to distribute a malicious update to its customers.
3. A list of at least three high-level recommendations for how an organization can mitigate the risk of software supply chain attacks (e.g., vetting third-party vendors, using software composition analysis, implementing a secure build process).

## Tips

A very relevant and important topic in cybersecurity, explained through a real-world example.
