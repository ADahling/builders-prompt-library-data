# Comprehensive List of AI Marketing Automation Platforms

Identify and evaluate twelve AI-driven marketing tools through structured profiles focusing on automation, personalization, and ROI.

## Prompt

I am a digital marketing manager for a growing e-commerce brand. I need to find an AI-powered marketing platform to help us scale our efforts. Generate a list of the top 12 AI marketing tools, focusing on campaign management, personalization, and analytics.

Structure the output in a Markdown document. For each tool, create a profile that includes:

- **Tool Name & Website:**
- **Core AI Focus:** (e.g., 'AI-driven email marketing', 'Predictive lead scoring', 'Ad campaign optimization', 'Customer journey personalization').
- **Key Features:** Provide a detailed, bulleted list of at least 5 features, such as:
    - AI-generated copy for emails and ads.
    - Predictive analytics for customer churn.
    - Automated budget allocation for ad spend.
    - Dynamic content personalization for websites.
    - AI-powered SEO recommendations.
- **Target Business Size:** (e.g., SMB, Mid-Market, Enterprise).
- **Pricing Summary:** A brief overview of their pricing tiers.

Include a mix of all-in-one platforms and specialized tools. The goal is to get a clear picture of the leading solutions that use AI to drive marketing ROI.
