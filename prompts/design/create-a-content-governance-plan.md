# Create a Content Governance Plan

Establish a structured framework for managing the complete content lifecycle through defined roles, workflows, and quality standards.

## Prompt

You are a content strategist. Your task is to create a content governance plan for our organization. The goal is to ensure that our content is consistent, high-quality, and effective over time. The plan should define roles, responsibilities, and processes for content creation, publication, and maintenance.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Define Content Roles and Responsibilities:** Who is responsible for what in the content lifecycle? (e.g., content strategist, writer, editor, designer, publisher, subject matter expert).

2.  **Create a Content Workflow:** Outline the step-by-step process for creating and publishing a new piece of content, from idea to promotion.

3.  **Establish Content Quality Standards:** What are the quality standards that all of our content must meet? (e.g., style guide, tone of voice, SEO best practices, legal compliance).

4.  **Create a Content Maintenance Plan:** How will you ensure that our content is kept up-to-date and accurate over time? (e.g., content audits, content refreshes, content archiving).

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Content Governance Plan**
    *   **Content Roles and Responsibilities:**
    *   **Content Workflow:**
    *   **Content Quality Standards:**
    *   **Content Maintenance Plan:**
