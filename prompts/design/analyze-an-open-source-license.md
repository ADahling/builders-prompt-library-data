# Analyze an Open Source License

Simplify the MIT License terms to understand permissions, obligations, and commercial compatibility for a software project.

## Prompt

You are a software developer. Your task is to analyze the MIT License and explain its key terms in simple language.

Context:
- You are considering using a library licensed under the MIT License in your project.
- You need to understand your obligations.

Provide:
1. A summary of the permissions granted by the license.
2. An explanation of the conditions and limitations.
3. A statement on whether the license is compatible with commercial use.

## Tips

Helps developers understand the implications of using open source software.
