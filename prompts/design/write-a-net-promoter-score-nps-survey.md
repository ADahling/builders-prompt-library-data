# Write a Net Promoter Score (NPS) Survey

Generate a comprehensive Net Promoter Score survey and email invitation package to measure and analyze brand loyalty across customer segments.

Variables: brand_name

## Prompt

You are a customer experience (CX) specialist. Your task is to write a Net Promoter Score (NPS) survey to measure our customers' loyalty to our brand, {{brand_name}}.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Write the Main NPS Question:** The main question should ask customers how likely they are to recommend your brand to a friend or colleague on a scale of 0-10.

2.  **Write Follow-up Questions:** Write two open-ended follow-up questions to understand the reason behind the customer's score. The questions should be different for promoters (9-10), passives (7-8), and detractors (0-6).

3.  **Design the Survey Invitation:** Write the copy for an email that we can use to invite customers to take the NPS survey.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **NPS Survey**
    *   **Main Question:**
    *   **Follow-up Questions:**
*   **Survey Invitation Email:**
