# Guide to AI-Powered Educational Tools for Learning and Teaching

Generates a categorized guide of 12 AI educational tools to help teachers evaluate adaptive platforms, grading aids, and student tutors.

## Prompt

As an educator and curriculum developer, I am interested in how AI can create more personalized and effective learning experiences. Generate a guide to the top 12 AI-powered educational tools for both students and teachers.

Organize the guide into three categories:
1.  **Personalized Learning Platforms:** Adaptive platforms that tailor content and assessments to individual student needs.
2.  **Teaching Assistants & Grading Tools:** AI tools that help teachers with administrative tasks, grading, and lesson planning.
3.  **AI Tutors & Study Aids:** Tools that provide students with on-demand help, practice problems, and feedback.

For each tool, provide:
- **Tool Name:**
- **Target Age Group/Level:** (e.g., K-12, Higher Education, Lifelong Learners).
- **Core AI Function:** Explain its main AI-driven feature (e.g., 'Adaptive learning paths', 'Automated essay scoring', 'AI-powered Socratic dialogue').
- **Subject(s):** The academic subjects it primarily covers (e.g., Math, Science, Languages, All).
- **How it Enhances Learning:** A short paragraph on its pedagogical benefits.

Your output should be a resource that helps educators discover and evaluate AI tools to implement in their classrooms.

## Tips

This prompt is well-suited for the EdTech sector and provides a practical framework for evaluating educational tools.
