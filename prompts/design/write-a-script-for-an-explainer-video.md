# Write a Script for an Explainer Video

Generate a structured two-column explainer video script featuring engaging voiceover and visual descriptions using a problem-solution framework.

Variables: product_name

## Prompt

You are a video scriptwriter. Your task is to write a 90-second script for an animated explainer video for our product, {{product_name}}. The video should explain what our product does, who it's for, and why it's valuable, in a simple and engaging way.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Follow the Problem-Solution-Benefit Framework:** The script should first introduce the problem that your target audience is facing, then present your product as the solution, and finally highlight the key benefits of using your product.

2.  **Write the Voiceover Script:** Write the complete voiceover script for the video. The tone should be friendly, upbeat, and easy to understand.

3.  **Describe the Visuals:** For each part of the script, describe the visuals that will be shown on screen. The visuals should be simple, clear, and help to illustrate the concepts being explained in the voiceover.

**Output Format:**

Please provide the complete script in a two-column format, with one column for the voiceover and the other for the visuals.
