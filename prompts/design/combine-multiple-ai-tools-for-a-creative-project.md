# Combine Multiple AI Tools for a Creative Project

Architect a multi-modal workflow that integrates specific AI tools to execute complex creative projects through a structured, step-by-step guide.

Variables: creative_project_description, tool_list

## Prompt

You are an AI artist and creative technologist. I want to create a {{creative_project_description}} by combining the capabilities of multiple AI tools. The tools I have access to are {{tool_list}}.

Brainstorm a creative concept and a workflow that leverages the unique strengths of each tool to produce a novel and compelling output. Provide a step-by-step guide on how to execute the project.

## Tips

A prompt for creatives who want to explore the artistic possibilities of combining different AI tools.
