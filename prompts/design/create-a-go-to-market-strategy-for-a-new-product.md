# Create a Go-to-Market Strategy for a New Product

Develop a comprehensive, markdown-formatted go-to-market strategy covering audience targeting, positioning, messaging, and launch logistics.

Variables: product_name

## Prompt

You are a product marketing manager for a new product, {{product_name}}. You need to create a go-to-market strategy to launch the product successfully.

Your task is to generate a comprehensive go-to-market strategy that includes the following sections:

*   **Target Audience:** A detailed description of your target audience.
*   **Positioning:** How you will position your product in the market.
*   **Messaging:** The key messages you will use to communicate the value of your product.
*   **Channels:** The marketing channels you will use to reach your target audience.
*   **Launch Plan:** A detailed plan for the product launch.
*   **Budget:** A budget for the go-to-market campaign.

Generate the complete go-to-market strategy in Markdown format.
