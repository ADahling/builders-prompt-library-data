# Create a Customer Case Study Video

Generates a comprehensive video production plan including storyboards, interview questions, and B-roll lists to showcase customer success stories.

Variables: customer_name, product_name, result

## Prompt

You are a video marketing strategist. Your task is to create a plan for a 3-minute customer case study video. The video will feature our customer, {{customer_name}}, and showcase how they used our product, {{product_name}}, to achieve {{result}}.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Develop the Storyboard:** Create a shot-by-shot storyboard for the video. The storyboard should include a description of the visuals, the audio (including interview snippets and voiceover), and any on-screen text or graphics.

2.  **Write the Interview Questions:** Write a list of 10-12 open-ended questions to ask the customer during the interview. The questions should be designed to elicit compelling soundbites that tell a story.

3.  **Create a B-Roll Shot List:** Create a list of B-roll shots that you will need to capture to visually enhance the video and make it more engaging.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Customer Case Study Video Plan**
    *   **Storyboard:**
    *   **Interview Questions:**
    *   **B-Roll Shot List:**
