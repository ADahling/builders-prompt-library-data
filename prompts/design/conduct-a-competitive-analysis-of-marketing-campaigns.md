# Conduct a Competitive Analysis of Marketing Campaigns

Analyze the content, social media, and paid advertising strategies of two rivals to identify competitive advantages and growth opportunities.

Variables: competitor_1, competitor_2

## Prompt

You are a marketing analyst. Your task is to conduct a competitive analysis of the marketing campaigns of our top two competitors, {{competitor_1}} and {{competitor_2}}. The goal is to understand their strategies and identify opportunities for our own marketing efforts.

**Instructions:**

For each competitor, investigate and analyze the following:

1.  **Content Marketing:** What kind of content are they creating (e.g., blog posts, videos, webinars)? What topics are they focusing on? How often are they publishing new content?

2.  **Social Media Marketing:** Which social media platforms are they most active on? What kind of content are they posting? What is their engagement rate like?

3.  **Paid Advertising:** Are they running ads on Google, Facebook, or other platforms? What kind of ad copy and creatives are they using?

4.  **Overall Strategy and Key Takeaways:** What do you think is their overall marketing strategy? What are the key takeaways for our own marketing team?

**Output Format:**

Please provide your analysis in a structured Markdown format, with a separate section for each competitor.

## Tips

This prompt guides users in conducting a thorough competitive analysis of their competitors' marketing campaigns.
