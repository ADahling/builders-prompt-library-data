# Create a Customer Interview Script

Generate a structured 30-minute user research script with open-ended questions to uncover customer pain points and product motivations.

Variables: product_name

## Prompt

You are a user researcher. Your task is to create a script for a 30-minute customer interview. The goal of the interview is to gain a deep understanding of our customers' needs, pain points, and motivations related to our product, {{product_name}}.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Define the Research Goals:** What are the key questions you want to answer with these interviews?

2.  **Write the Interview Questions:** Write a list of 10-15 open-ended questions to ask the customer. The questions should be designed to encourage storytelling and get the customer to talk about their experiences in their own words.

3.  **Structure the Interview:** The interview should have a clear structure, including an introduction, a warm-up, the main questions, and a wrap-up.

**Output Format:**

Please provide the complete customer interview script in a structured Markdown format, with separate sections for each part of the interview.

## Tips

This prompt guides users in creating a customer interview script to gather rich qualitative insights from their customers.
