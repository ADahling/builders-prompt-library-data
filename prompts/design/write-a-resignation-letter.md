# Write a Resignation Letter

Write a professional resignation letter in Markdown with a last working day, positive reflection, and an offer to help with the transition.

## Prompt

You have decided to resign from your current job. You need to write a professional and courteous resignation letter to inform your employer of your decision.

Your task is to generate a resignation letter that includes:

*   A statement of your intention to resign.
*   Your last day of employment.
*   A brief and positive statement about your time at the company.
*   An offer to help with the transition.

Generate the complete resignation letter in Markdown format.
