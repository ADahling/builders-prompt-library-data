# Develop a Custom AI Tool Evaluation Model

Create a tailored AI evaluation framework with custom metrics, weighted scoring, and a mathematical formula to assess tools for niche business needs.

Variables: industry, critical_business_function, unique_requirements

## Prompt

You are a principal AI scientist. My organization, a {{industry}} leader, requires a bespoke evaluation model for assessing highly specialized AI tools for {{critical_business_function}}. Off-the-shelf frameworks are insufficient due to our unique requirements, including {{unique_requirements}}.

Design a multi-dimensional evaluation model that incorporates our specific needs. The model should include custom metrics, a weighting system that reflects our priorities, and a process for conducting rigorous, hands-on testing. Provide a mathematical formula for the final scoring.

## Tips

A prompt for organizations with unique needs that require a custom-built evaluation model for AI tools.
