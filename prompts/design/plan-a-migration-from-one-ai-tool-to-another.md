# Plan a Migration from One AI Tool to Another

Generate a comprehensive migration roadmap and risk assessment to transition teams and data between AI platforms with minimal operational downtime.

Variables: current_tool_name, new_tool_name, user_count, project_count

## Prompt

You are an AI implementation consultant. My team is planning to migrate from {{current_tool_name}} to {{new_tool_name}}. We need to ensure a seamless transition with minimal disruption to our operations. We have {{user_count}} users and {{project_count}} projects on the current tool.

Create a detailed migration plan that covers all the key steps, from data migration and user training to post-migration support. The plan should also include a timeline and a list of potential risks and mitigation strategies.

## Tips

A prompt for teams that are switching from one AI tool to another and need a structured migration plan.
