# Develop a Governance Framework for Generative AI

Establish a comprehensive governance strategy with clear policies and review processes to ensure the ethical and secure use of generative AI.

Variables: key_concerns

## Prompt

You are a chief AI officer. My organization is increasingly using generative AI tools, and we need to establish a comprehensive governance framework to manage the risks and ensure responsible use. Our primary concerns are {{key_concerns}}.

Develop a governance framework for generative AI that covers acceptable use, data privacy, intellectual property, and ethical considerations. The framework should include policies, procedures, and a review process.
