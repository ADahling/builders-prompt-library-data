# Develop an AI System for Predicting Stock Market Trends

Architect an end-to-end AI forecasting system that analyzes multi-source data to generate and validate trading signals for specific sectors.

Variables: market_sector

## Prompt

You are a quantitative analyst. I want to develop an AI system to predict stock market trends. The system will analyze a variety of data sources, including financial news, social media sentiment, and economic indicators, to generate trading signals. The system will focus on the {{market_sector}} sector.

Design the AI system for stock market prediction. The design should specify the data ingestion pipeline, the predictive models, and the backtesting framework.
