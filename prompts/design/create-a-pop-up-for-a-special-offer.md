# Create a Pop-up for a Special Offer

Generate a conversion-optimized pop-up plan including timing triggers, persuasive copy, and visual design to boost lead generation.

Variables: offer_description

## Prompt

You are a conversion rate optimization specialist. Your task is to design a pop-up for our website to promote a special offer: {{offer_description}}. The goal of the pop-up is to generate leads or drive sales.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Choose a Pop-up Type:** Will it be a time-based pop-up, a scroll-based pop-up, or an exit-intent pop-up? Justify your choice.

2.  **Write the Pop-up Copy:** Write a compelling headline, a brief description of the offer, and a strong call-to-action for the pop-up.

3.  **Design the Pop-up:** Describe the visual design of the pop-up. What will it look like? Will it include an image? The design should be eye-catching but not intrusive.

4.  **Set the Targeting Rules:** Who should see the pop-up? Should it be displayed to all visitors, or only to new visitors or returning visitors? Should it be displayed on all pages or only on specific pages?

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Pop-up Design**
    *   **Pop-up Type:**
    *   **Pop-up Copy:**
    *   **Pop-up Design:**
    *   **Targeting Rules:**
