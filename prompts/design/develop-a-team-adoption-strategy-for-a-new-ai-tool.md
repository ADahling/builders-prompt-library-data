# Develop a Team Adoption Strategy for a New AI Tool

Create a comprehensive change management strategy to drive employee engagement and seamless integration of a new AI tool into daily workflows.

Variables: tool_name, team_size

## Prompt

You are a change management consultant. My company is planning to introduce a new AI tool, {{tool_name}}, to our team of {{team_size}} employees. We want to ensure a smooth and successful adoption of the tool.

Develop a comprehensive adoption strategy that includes a communication plan, training materials, and a support system. The strategy should be designed to get employees excited about the new tool and help them integrate it into their daily workflows.

## Tips

A prompt for organizations that are introducing new AI tools and want to drive user adoption.
