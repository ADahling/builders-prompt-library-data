# Create a Webinar Promotion Plan

Develop a comprehensive webinar marketing strategy including a multi-week timeline, email copy, social posts, and cross-channel tactics.

Variables: webinar_title, webinar_topic, target_audience

## Prompt

You are a webinar marketing expert. Your task is to create a promotion plan for our upcoming webinar, "{{webinar_title}}". The webinar is about {{webinar_topic}} and our target audience is {{target_audience}}.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Create a Promotion Timeline:** Outline a week-by-week promotion timeline leading up to the webinar. What promotional activities will you carry out each week?

2.  **Write an Email Invitation:** Write the copy for an email that we can send to our email list to invite them to the webinar.

3.  **Create Social Media Posts:** Write a set of social media posts (for Facebook, Twitter, and LinkedIn) to promote the webinar.

4.  **Suggest Other Promotion Channels:** Suggest at least three other channels we could use to promote the webinar (e.g., guest posts, influencer outreach, paid ads).

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Webinar Promotion Plan**
    *   **Promotion Timeline:**
    *   **Email Invitation:**
    *   **Social Media Posts:**
    *   **Other Promotion Channels:**

## Tips

This prompt helps users create a comprehensive promotion plan to maximize attendance for their webinar.
