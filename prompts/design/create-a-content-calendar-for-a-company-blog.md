# Create a Content Calendar for a Company Blog

Generate a structured one-month editorial calendar with strategic themes and catchy headlines tailored to a specific niche and audience.

Variables: blog_topic, target_audience

## Prompt

You are a content marketing manager. Your task is to create a one-month content calendar for our company blog. Our blog focuses on {{blog_topic}} and our target audience is {{target_audience}}.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Define Content Themes:** Identify two to three content themes that are relevant to our blog topic and target audience.

2.  **Brainstorm Blog Post Ideas:** For each theme, brainstorm four blog post ideas (a total of 8-12 ideas). For each idea, write a catchy headline and a brief description.

3.  **Create the Content Calendar:** Organize the blog post ideas into a one-month content calendar. You can use a simple table format. The calendar should specify the publication date and the blog post title for each entry.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Content Themes:**
*   **Blog Post Ideas:**
*   **One-Month Content Calendar:**
