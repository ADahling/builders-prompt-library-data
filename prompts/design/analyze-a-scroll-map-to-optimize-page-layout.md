# Analyze a Scroll Map to Optimize Page Layout

Evaluate user drop-off points on a scroll map to provide prioritized layout optimizations that increase page depth and content engagement.

Variables: scroll_map_image_url

## Prompt

You are a UX analyst. You have been provided with a scroll map of our landing page, which can be found at {{scroll_map_image_url}}. The scroll map shows how far down the page users are scrolling.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Analyze the Scroll Map:** Carefully examine the scroll map. At what point on the page do most users drop off? Are there any sections of the page that are being completely ignored?

2.  **Identify Key Insights:** Based on your analysis, what are the three most important insights you can draw about the page's layout and content hierarchy?

3.  **Formulate Actionable Recommendations:** For each insight, propose a specific, actionable recommendation for improving the page layout. Your recommendations should be aimed at encouraging users to scroll further down the page and engage with more of the content.
