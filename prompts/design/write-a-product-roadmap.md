# Write a Product Roadmap

Generate a structured quarterly product roadmap in Markdown to align stakeholders on strategic themes, feature releases, and key objectives.

## Prompt

You are a product manager for a software product. You need to create a product roadmap to communicate your product strategy and plans to your team and stakeholders.

Your task is to generate a product roadmap that includes the following for each quarter:

*   **Themes:** The high-level themes that you will be focusing on.
*   **Features:** The specific features that you will be building.
*   **Goals:** The goals that you hope to achieve.

Generate the product roadmap in a Markdown table format.
