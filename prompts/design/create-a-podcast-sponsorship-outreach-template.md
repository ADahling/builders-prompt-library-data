# Create a Podcast Sponsorship Outreach Template

Generate professional email outreach templates to secure podcast sponsorship deals by pitching products and requesting media kits.

Variables: product_name

## Prompt

You are a marketing manager. Your task is to create an email template for reaching out to podcast hosts to sponsor their show. We want to promote our product, {{product_name}}, to their audience.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Write a Personalized Subject Line:** The subject line should be catchy and relevant to the podcast.

2.  **Write the Email Body:** The body of the email should:
    *   Introduce yourself and our product.
    *   Explain why you think their podcast would be a good fit for a sponsorship.
    *   Propose a specific sponsorship package (e.g., a pre-roll ad, a mid-roll ad, a dedicated episode).
    *   Clearly state your budget or ask for their media kit.
    *   End with a clear call-to-action.

**Output Format:**

Please provide the complete email template in a single block of text, with placeholders for the podcast name, host's name, and other details.

## Tips

This prompt provides a ready-to-use email template for reaching out to podcasters for sponsorship opportunities.
