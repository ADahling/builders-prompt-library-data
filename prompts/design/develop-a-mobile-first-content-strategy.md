# Develop a Mobile-First Content Strategy

Get a mobile-first content strategy document for a new website, covering content priorities, clear delivery, and mobile search optimization.

Variables: website_name, target_audience

## Prompt

You are a content strategist. Your task is to develop a mobile-first content strategy for a new website, {{website_name}}. You need to start by identifying the most important content for mobile users, and then create a plan for how to deliver that content in a clear, concise, and engaging way. You should also consider how to optimize the content for search engines on mobile devices. The output should be a detailed content strategy document. Use {{website_name}} and {{target_audience}} as variables.

## Tips

This is an advanced prompt for developing a content strategy that is optimized for mobile users.
