# Product Hunt Launch Playbook

Plan a Product Hunt launch with pre-launch assets, outreach templates, launch-day copy and engagement tasks, plus post-launch follow-up.

## Prompt

Create a complete Product Hunt launch plan for [describe product]:

PRE-LAUNCH (2-4 weeks before):
1. Upcoming Page: Tagline (60 chars), description, subscribe link. Build 200+ subscribers before launch.
2. Hunter: Find a top hunter to post you (or self-post if no connection). Outreach template included.
3. Assets: Logo (240x240), gallery images (6 screenshots with captions), maker video (optional, 1-2 min).
4. Launch Copy: First comment template — tell your story (problem, why you built it, what makes it different). Be personal, not corporate.
5. Support Network: List of 20+ people to notify on launch day. Draft DM/email asking for support (upvote + comment).

LAUNCH DAY:
6. Timing: Post at 12:01 AM PST for maximum visibility window.
7. First Comment: Post within 5 minutes of launch. Personal story, not marketing speak.
8. Engagement: Respond to EVERY comment within 30 minutes. Be genuine and helpful.
9. Social: 3 social media posts spaced throughout the day (morning, noon, evening).
10. Do NOT ask for upvotes directly (against PH rules). Ask people to "check it out and share feedback."

POST-LAUNCH:
11. Follow-up: Thank supporters, share results, offer launch-day discount.
12. Leverage: Add "Featured on Product Hunt" badge to site. Share metrics in future marketing.

## Tips

Product Hunt can drive 1000+ signups in a day if done right. Preparation is everything.
