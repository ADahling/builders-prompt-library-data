# Directory of AI Tools for Academic and Scientific Research

Generate an organized directory of AI-powered tools categorized by research phase to streamline literature discovery, analysis, and writing.

## Prompt

I am a postgraduate researcher working on my thesis. I need a comprehensive list of AI tools that can assist with the academic research process. Generate a directory of tools that help with literature discovery, data analysis, and writing.

Please structure the list into the following phases of the research lifecycle:

1.  **Literature Review & Discovery:** Tools that help find, summarize, and organize relevant academic papers.
2.  **Research Data Analysis:** AI-powered software for analyzing complex datasets (statistical, qualitative, etc.).
3.  **Academic Writing & Citation:** Tools that assist in drafting papers, checking for plagiarism, and managing citations.

For each tool (aim for 4-5 per category), provide:
- **Tool Name:**
- **Specific Function:** A clear, one-sentence description of what it does.
- **Key AI Features:** A bulleted list of its AI capabilities (e.g., 'Semantic search for research papers', 'AI-powered summarization of articles', 'Automated generation of literature maps', 'AI writing assistant trained on academic prose').
- **Freemium/Paid:** Indicate if a useful free version is available.

Your response should serve as a practical toolkit for a researcher looking to leverage AI to improve their workflow and productivity.

## Tips

Structuring the list by the 'research lifecycle' makes the output highly intuitive and actionable for its target audience.
