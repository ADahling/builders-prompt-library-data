# Develop an AI System for Optimizing Supply Chains

Architect a full-stack AI supply chain solution to automate global demand forecasting, inventory control, and logistics routing.

Variables: number

## Prompt

You are a supply chain analyst. I want to develop an AI system to optimize the supply chain for a global manufacturing company. The system should be able to forecast demand, manage inventory, and optimize logistics. The company has {{number}} of factories and {{number}} of distribution centers.

Design the AI system for supply chain optimization. The design should specify the data inputs, the optimization algorithms, and the dashboard for visualizing the supply chain performance.
