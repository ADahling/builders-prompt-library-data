# Write a Press Release for a New Product Launch

Generate a professional, structured press release in Markdown format to announce a new product and secure media coverage.

Variables: product_name

## Prompt

You are a public relations manager for a technology startup. You are about to launch a new product, {{product_name}}, and you need to write a press release to generate media coverage.

Your task is to write a professional and compelling press release that announces the launch of your new product. The press release should include:

*   **Headline:** A catchy and informative headline.
*   **Dateline:** The city, state, and date of the release.
*   **Introduction:** A brief overview of the new product and its key features.
*   **Body:** A more detailed explanation of the product, its benefits, and its target market.
*   **Quote:** A quote from a company executive about the new product.
*   **About Us:** A brief description of your company.
*   **Contact Information:** The name, title, email, and phone number of your media contact.

Generate the complete press release in Markdown format.
