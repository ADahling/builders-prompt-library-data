# Write a Letter of Recommendation for a Colleague

Write a Markdown recommendation letter for a colleague's job application, highlighting accomplishments, skills, character, and endorsement.

Variables: colleague_name, number

## Prompt

A colleague of yours, {{colleague_name}}, is applying for a new job and has asked you to write a letter of recommendation for them. You have worked with this colleague for {{number}} years and have a high opinion of their work.

Your task is to write a persuasive letter of recommendation that highlights your colleague's skills, accomplishments, and character. The letter should include:

*   Your relationship to the colleague and how long you have known them.
*   Specific examples of their accomplishments and contributions.
*   Your assessment of their skills and abilities.
*   Your personal endorsement of them for the new role.

Generate the complete letter of recommendation in Markdown format.
