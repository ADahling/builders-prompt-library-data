# Troubleshoot an Issue with an AI Tool

Resolve technical errors in specific AI tools through a structured diagnostic plan and targeted troubleshooting steps.

Variables: tool_name, issue_description, troubleshooting_steps_taken

## Prompt

You are an AI support engineer. I am encountering an issue with {{tool_name}}. The issue is that {{issue_description}}. I have already tried {{troubleshooting_steps_taken}}.

Provide a systematic approach to troubleshooting this issue. The approach should include a list of potential causes and a series of steps to diagnose and resolve the problem.

## Tips

A prompt for users who are facing a technical issue with an AI tool and need help with troubleshooting.
