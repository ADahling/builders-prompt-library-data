# Create a Customer Satisfaction Survey (CSAT)

Generates a primary rating question, qualitative follow-up prompts, and an invitation email to measure and improve product satisfaction.

Variables: product_name

## Prompt

You are a customer experience (CX) specialist. Your task is to create a customer satisfaction (CSAT) survey to measure how satisfied our customers are with our product, {{product_name}}.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Write the Main CSAT Question:** The main question should ask customers to rate their overall satisfaction with the product on a scale (e.g., 1-5 or 1-10).

2.  **Write Follow-up Questions:** Write two to three open-ended follow-up questions to gather more detailed feedback. These questions should ask customers what they like most about the product and what they would like to see improved.

3.  **Design the Survey Invitation:** Write the copy for an email that we can use to invite customers to take the survey.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **CSAT Survey**
    *   **Main Question:**
    *   **Follow-up Questions:**
*   **Survey Invitation Email:**

## Tips

This prompt helps users create a simple but effective CSAT survey to gather valuable customer feedback.
