# Create a Brand Voice and Tone Guide

Develop a comprehensive brand voice and tone guide, defining personality traits and communication standards to ensure cross-channel consistency.

Variables: company_name

## Prompt

You are a brand strategist. Your task is to create a brand voice and tone guide for our company, {{company_name}}. The guide should define our brand's personality and provide clear guidelines for how to communicate with our audience across all channels.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Define Your Brand's Personality:** Choose 3-5 adjectives that describe your brand's personality (e.g., friendly, witty, authoritative, inspiring).

2.  **Create a Voice and Tone Spectrum:** For each personality trait, create a spectrum that shows what your brand voice is and what it is not. For example, if your brand is "witty", the spectrum might be "witty vs. sarcastic".

3.  **Provide Examples of Your Brand Voice in Action:** Write a few examples of how your brand voice would be applied in different situations, such as a social media post, a customer support email, and a blog post introduction.

**Output Format:**

Please provide the brand voice and tone guide in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Brand Voice and Tone Guide**
    *   **Brand Personality:**
    *   **Voice and Tone Spectrum:**
    *   **Examples in Action:**
