# Develop an Advanced Email Capture Strategy

Architect an advanced lead generation system by designing interactive opt-ins, high-value content upgrades, and high-conversion exit copy.

Variables: website_url, blog_post_title

## Prompt

You are a lead generation expert. Your task is to develop an advanced email capture strategy for our website, {{website_url}}. We want to move beyond simple newsletter sign-up forms and implement more sophisticated techniques to grow our email list.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Propose Three Advanced Email Capture Techniques:** Suggest three email capture techniques that go beyond a basic sign-up form. Examples could include content upgrades, multi-step pop-ups, gamified opt-in forms, or exit-intent pop-ups. For each technique, explain why it would be effective for our website and target audience.

2.  **Design a Content Upgrade:** Choose one of the techniques you proposed and design a specific content upgrade for one of our most popular blog posts, "{{blog_post_title}}" (you can imagine the content of the blog post). A content upgrade is a bonus piece of content that is offered to users in exchange for their email address.

3.  **Write the Copy for an Exit-Intent Pop-up:** Write the copy for an exit-intent pop-up that will be triggered when a user is about to leave our website. The pop-up should offer a compelling reason for the user to stay and subscribe to our email list.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Advanced Email Capture Strategy**
    *   **Proposed Techniques:**
    *   **Content Upgrade Design:**
    *   **Exit-Intent Pop-up Copy:**
