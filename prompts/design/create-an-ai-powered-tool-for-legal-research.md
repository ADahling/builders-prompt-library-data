# Create an AI-Powered Tool for Legal Research

Design the core interface, summarization features, and citation analysis tools for a jurisdiction-specific AI legal research platform.

Variables: jurisdiction

## Prompt

You are a legal technologist. I want to create an AI-powered tool to help lawyers with legal research. The tool should be able to search through a large database of case law and statutes to find relevant precedents and legal arguments. The tool will be used by lawyers in the {{jurisdiction}} jurisdiction.

Design the core features of the legal research tool. The design should include the natural language search interface, the document summarization feature, and the citation analysis tool.
