# Design a Custom AI-Powered Chatbot

Architect a custom AI chatbot strategy by mapping conversation flows, knowledge bases, and personality traits for specific web domains.

Variables: website_url, topic, personality_description

## Prompt

You are a chatbot developer. I want to design a custom chatbot for my website, {{website_url}}, to handle customer inquiries about {{topic}}. The chatbot should have a {{personality_description}} personality.

Outline the key steps to design and build the chatbot. The outline should cover conversation flow design, knowledge base creation, and integration with a chatbot platform.

## Tips

A prompt for users who want to create a custom chatbot for their website.
