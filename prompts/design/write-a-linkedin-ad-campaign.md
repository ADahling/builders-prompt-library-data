# Write a LinkedIn Ad Campaign

Generate a comprehensive LinkedIn advertising strategy for B2B SaaS products including audience targeting, creative assets, and budget plans.

Variables: saas_product_name, target_audience, campaign_goal, budget

## Prompt

You are a LinkedIn advertising expert. Your task is to create a LinkedIn ad campaign to promote our B2B SaaS product, {{saas_product_name}}, to our target audience of {{target_audience}}.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Choose a Campaign Objective:** Select the most appropriate campaign objective for our goal of {{campaign_goal}} (e.g., Lead Generation, Website Traffic, Brand Awareness).

2.  **Define the Target Audience:** Specify the targeting criteria you will use to reach our target audience on LinkedIn. This should include job titles, industries, company sizes, and any other relevant attributes.

3.  **Create the Ad Creative:** Write the ad copy (headline and body text) and suggest an image or video creative for the ad. The ad should be compelling and relevant to the target audience.

4.  **Set the Budget and Schedule:** Recommend a daily budget and a campaign duration, keeping in mind our overall budget of {{budget}}.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **LinkedIn Ad Campaign Plan**
    *   **Campaign Objective:**
    *   **Target Audience:**
    *   **Ad Creative:**
        *   **Headline:**
        *   **Body Text:**
        *   **Creative Suggestion:**
    *   **Budget and Schedule:**

## Tips

This prompt guides users in creating a targeted and effective LinkedIn ad campaign to reach their B2B audience.
