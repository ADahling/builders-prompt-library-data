# Write a LinkedIn Sponsored InMail Campaign

Generate a high-converting B2B LinkedIn InMail campaign including targeted audience segments, persuasive messaging, and strategic bidding.

Variables: webinar_title, target_audience

## Prompt

You are a B2B marketing expert. Your task is to write a LinkedIn Sponsored InMail campaign to promote our upcoming webinar, "{{webinar_title}}", to a highly targeted audience of {{target_audience}}.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Define the Target Audience:** Specify the precise targeting criteria you will use for the campaign, including job titles, industries, company sizes, and skills.

2.  **Write the InMail Message:** Write a personalized and compelling InMail message that will grab the recipient's attention and persuade them to register for the webinar. The message should be concise, value-driven, and have a clear call-to-action.

3.  **Set the Budget and Bidding Strategy:** Recommend a budget for the campaign and a bidding strategy (e.g., cost-per-send, cost-per-click).

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **LinkedIn Sponsored InMail Campaign**
    *   **Target Audience:**
    *   **InMail Message:**
    *   **Budget and Bidding Strategy:**

## Tips

This prompt guides users in creating a highly targeted and effective LinkedIn Sponsored InMail campaign to generate leads for a webinar.
