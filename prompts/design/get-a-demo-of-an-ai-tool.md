# Get a Demo of an AI Tool

Request a product demonstration tailored to specific business objectives to understand the tool's core features and consultation process.

Variables: tool_name, business_objective

## Prompt

You are a sales engineer. I am interested in {{tool_name}} and I would like to see a demo of the product. My team is focused on achieving {{business_objective}}.

Explain how I can request a demo of the tool and what I should expect to see in the demonstration.

## Tips

A prompt for users who want to see an AI tool in action before making a purchase decision.
