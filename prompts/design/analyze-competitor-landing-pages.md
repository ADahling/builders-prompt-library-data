# Analyze Competitor Landing Pages

Benchmark rival landing pages against your own to uncover strategic design gaps and unique differentiation opportunities.

Variables: competitor_1_url, competitor_2_url, competitor_3_url, our_landing_page_url

## Prompt

You are a competitive intelligence analyst. Your task is to analyze the landing pages of our top three competitors: {{competitor_1_url}}, {{competitor_2_url}}, and {{competitor_3_url}}. Our own landing page is at {{our_landing_page_url}}. The goal is to identify their strengths and weaknesses and find opportunities for us to differentiate ourselves.

**Instructions:**

For each competitor's landing page, analyze the following elements:

1.  **Headline and Sub-headline:** What is their main value proposition? Is it clear and compelling?

2.  **Call-to-Action (CTA):** What is their primary CTA? Is it prominent and persuasive?

3.  **Social Proof:** What types of social proof are they using (e.g., testimonials, case studies, logos)?

4.  **Overall Design and User Experience:** Is the page well-designed and easy to navigate?

5.  **Key Strengths and Weaknesses:** What are the one or two things they are doing particularly well, and what are their biggest weaknesses?

**Output Format:**

Please provide your analysis in a structured Markdown format, with a separate section for each competitor.

## Tips

This prompt guides users in conducting a thorough analysis of their competitors' landing pages to gain a competitive advantage.
