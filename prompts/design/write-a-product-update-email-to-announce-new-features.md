# Write a Product Update Email to Announce New Features

Generate a professional product update email that highlights three new features and their benefits to drive user engagement and adoption.

Variables: feature_1, feature_2, feature_3

## Prompt

You are a product marketing manager. Your task is to write a product update email to our customers to announce a set of new features that we have just released. The new features are: {{feature_1}}, {{feature_2}}, and {{feature_3}}.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Write a Catchy Subject Line:** The subject line should be exciting and clearly communicate that this is a product update.

2.  **Write the Email Body:** The body of the email should:
    *   Start with a brief and enthusiastic introduction.
    *   For each new feature, provide a concise description of what it is and how it benefits the customer. Use bullet points and bold text to make the email easy to scan.
    *   Include a clear call-to-action for customers to try out the new features.
    *   End with a friendly closing and a link to our help documentation or a blog post with more details.

**Output Format:**

Please provide the complete email in a single block of text, with placeholders for any links.

## Tips

This prompt provides a ready-to-use template for a product update email to keep customers informed and engaged.
