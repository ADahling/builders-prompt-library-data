# Write a Go-to-Market Strategy for a New Product

Develop a comprehensive, multi-component go-to-market strategy to launch a new product and achieve product-market fit.

Variables: product_name

## Prompt

You are a product marketing manager. Your task is to create a go-to-market (GTM) strategy for our new product, {{product_name}}. The GTM strategy should outline how you will launch the product and achieve your business goals.

**Instructions:**

Create a GTM strategy that includes the following components:

1.  **Market and Competitive Analysis:** Who is your target market? Who are your main competitors? What is your unique value proposition?

2.  **Product-Market Fit:** How does your product solve a problem for your target market? What is the evidence that there is a demand for your product?

3.  **Pricing and Packaging:** How will you price your product? What are the different pricing tiers or packages?

4.  **Marketing and Sales Strategy:** How will you generate awareness and demand for your product? How will you sell it to customers?

5.  **Launch Plan:** What are the key activities and milestones for the product launch?

**Output Format:**

Please provide the GTM strategy in a structured Markdown format, with a separate section for each component.
