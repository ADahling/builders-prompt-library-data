# Optimize a Sales Funnel for a Digital Product

Analyze conversion barriers across digital product sales stages to implement targeted UX improvements and validated A/B testing strategies.

Variables: digital_product_name, funnel_stages

## Prompt

You are a sales funnel optimization expert. Your task is to analyze and optimize the sales funnel for our digital product, {{digital_product_name}}. The funnel consists of the following stages: {{funnel_stages}} (e.g., Landing Page -> Checkout Page -> Thank You Page).

**Instructions:**

1.  **Identify Potential Drop-off Points:** For each stage of the funnel, identify the most likely reasons why users might drop off and not proceed to the next stage.

2.  **Propose Optimization Strategies:** For each potential drop-off point, propose a specific optimization strategy to reduce the drop-off rate. These strategies could involve changes to the copy, design, or user experience.

3.  **Suggest A/B Tests:** For each optimization strategy, suggest a specific A/B test that could be run to validate its effectiveness.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format, with a separate section for each stage of the funnel.

## Tips

This prompt helps users identify and address weak points in their sales funnel to increase conversions for their digital product.
