# Develop a Referral Program to Drive Word-of-Mouth Marketing

Design a structured referral program for a specific product including cost-effective incentives, a frictionless user flow, and ready-to-use promotional copy.

Variables: product_name

## Prompt

You are a marketing manager specializing in referral programs. Your task is to design a referral program for our product, {{product_name}}, to encourage our existing customers to refer their friends and colleagues.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Define the Incentives:** What will you offer to both the referrer and the referred friend as an incentive to participate in the program? The incentives should be attractive and cost-effective.

2.  **Outline the Referral Process:** Describe the step-by-step process for a customer to refer a friend. It should be as simple and frictionless as possible.

3.  **Create Promotional Materials:** Write the copy for an email and a social media post that we can use to promote the referral program to our existing customers.
