# Create a Crisis Communication Plan

Develop a comprehensive crisis management framework and pre-approved messaging to proactively mitigate corporate PR risks and data breaches.

Variables: company_name

## Prompt

You are a public relations and crisis management expert. Your task is to create a crisis communication plan for our company, {{company_name}}. The plan should outline the steps we will take to respond to a potential PR crisis, such as a data breach, a product recall, or negative social media backlash.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Form a Crisis Communication Team:** Identify the key roles and responsibilities of the crisis communication team.

2.  **Develop a Crisis Monitoring System:** How will you monitor for potential crises? What tools and processes will you use?

3.  **Create a Crisis Communication Protocol:** Outline the step-by-step process for responding to a crisis, from initial assessment to public statements and media outreach.

4.  **Prepare Holding Statements:** Write a set of pre-approved holding statements that can be used in the initial hours of a crisis to acknowledge the situation and provide reassurance to stakeholders.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Crisis Communication Plan**
    *   **Crisis Communication Team:**
    *   **Crisis Monitoring System:**
    *   **Crisis Communication Protocol:**
    *   **Holding Statements:**

## Tips

This prompt guides users in creating a crisis communication plan to protect their brand's reputation in the event of a PR crisis.
