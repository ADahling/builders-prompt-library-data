# Create a Social Media Content Strategy for Instagram

Develop a comprehensive Instagram marketing plan by defining core content pillars, engagement formats, posting schedules, and hashtag strategies.

Variables: company_name, products, target_audience

## Prompt

You are a social media strategist. Your task is to create a content strategy for our company's Instagram account. Our company, {{company_name}}, sells {{products}} and our target audience is {{target_audience}}.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Define Content Pillars:** Identify three to five content pillars that will be the foundation of our Instagram content. These should be topics that are relevant to our brand and interesting to our target audience.

2.  **Suggest Content Formats:** For each content pillar, suggest two to three different content formats we could use (e.g., single image posts, carousels, Reels, Stories).

3.  **Create a Posting Schedule:** Recommend a posting frequency (e.g., how many times per week) and a schedule for when to post.

4.  **Propose a Hashtag Strategy:** Suggest a set of relevant hashtags to use in our posts to increase their visibility.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Instagram Content Strategy**
    *   **Content Pillars:**
    *   **Content Formats:**
    *   **Posting Schedule:**
    *   **Hashtag Strategy:**
