# Create a Growth Hacking Strategy for a New Product Launch

Develop a multi-phase growth hacking roadmap to maximize product visibility, user acquisition, and retention on a restricted budget.

Variables: product_name, budget, timeline

## Prompt

You are a seasoned growth hacker. Your task is to devise a growth hacking strategy for the launch of our new product, {{product_name}}. We have a limited budget of {{budget}} and a timeline of {{timeline}}.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Pre-Launch Strategy:** Outline a strategy to build buzz and a waitlist before the product is officially launched. This should include at least three tactics.

2.  **Launch Day Strategy:** Describe the key activities to be executed on launch day to maximize visibility and initial user acquisition.

3.  **Post-Launch Strategy:** Propose a plan for sustaining growth in the weeks and months following the launch. Include at least three long-term growth tactics.

4.  **Key Metrics for Success:** Define the key metrics you will track to measure the success of this growth hacking strategy.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Growth Hacking Strategy: {{product_name}} Launch**

    *   **Post-Launch Strategy:**
    *   **Key Metrics for Success:**
