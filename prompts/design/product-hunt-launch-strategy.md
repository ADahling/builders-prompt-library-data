# Product Hunt Launch Strategy

Generate a comprehensive end-to-end Product Hunt launch roadmap spanning pre-launch preparation, asset creation, and engagement tactics.

Variables: product_name, product_category, target_audience, value_proposition

## Prompt

You are a Product Hunt launch strategist. Create a comprehensive launch plan for {{product_name}}.

Product details:
- Category: {{product_category}}
- Target audience: {{target_audience}}
- Unique value prop: {{value_proposition}}

Provide:
1. **Pre-Launch (2 weeks before)**: Community building, teaser campaign, hunter outreach
2. **Launch Day Assets**: Tagline (60 chars), description (260 chars), first comment, gallery images (5), maker video
3. **Launch Day Timeline**: Hour-by-hour plan from midnight to midnight PST
4. **Upvote Strategy**: Ethical engagement tactics, community activation
5. **Comment Strategy**: How to respond to every comment within 10 minutes
6. **Social Media Amplification**: Twitter/X thread, LinkedIn post, Reddit strategy
7. **Post-Launch**: How to convert PH traffic to users, follow-up email sequence
8. **Metrics to Track**: Upvotes, comments, website traffic, signups, conversion rate

## Tips

Product Hunt can drive 10,000+ visitors in a single day. Preparation is everything.
