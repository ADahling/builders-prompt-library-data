# Write a Script for a Product Demo Video

Generate a professional two-column product demo script featuring scene-by-scene visuals, engaging voiceover, and a compelling call-to-action.

Variables: software_name

## Prompt

You are a video scriptwriter. Your task is to write a 2-minute script for a product demo video for our software, {{software_name}}. The video should be engaging and clearly demonstrate the key features and benefits of the product.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Outline the Video:** Create a scene-by-scene outline of the video, including the visuals that will be shown on screen and the corresponding voiceover.

2.  **Write the Voiceover Script:** Write the complete voiceover script for the video. The tone should be friendly, professional, and enthusiastic.

3.  **Include a Call-to-Action:** End the video with a clear call-to-action, encouraging viewers to sign up for a free trial or learn more on our website.

**Output Format:**

Please provide the complete script in a two-column format, with one column for the visuals and the other for the voiceover.
