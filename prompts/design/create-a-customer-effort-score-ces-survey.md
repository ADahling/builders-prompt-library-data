# Create a Customer Effort Score (CES) Survey

Generate a professional Customer Effort Score survey with optimized questions and strategic delivery timing to measure service friction.

## Prompt

You are a customer experience (CX) specialist. Your task is to create a Customer Effort Score (CES) survey to measure how easy it is for our customers to interact with our company (e.g., to get a support issue resolved, to make a purchase, to find information on our website).

**Instructions:**

1.  **Write the Main CES Question:** The main question should ask customers to rate the ease of their interaction on a scale (e.g., from "Very Difficult" to "Very Easy").

2.  **Write a Follow-up Question:** Write an open-ended follow-up question to understand why the customer gave that score and what could be improved.

3.  **Determine When to Send the Survey:** When is the best time to send the CES survey? (e.g., immediately after a specific interaction).

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **CES Survey**
    *   **Main Question:**
    *   **Follow-up Question:**
*   **Survey Timing:**

## Tips

This prompt helps users create a CES survey to measure and reduce customer effort, which is a key driver of customer loyalty.
