# SEO Content Strategy for SaaS

Plan a SaaS SEO strategy with keywords, a content calendar, landing pages, templates, distribution, and KPIs prioritized by traffic and conversion.

## Prompt

Create an SEO content strategy for [describe SaaS product]:

1. Keyword Research: 20 target keywords grouped by intent (informational, comparison, transactional).
2. Content Calendar: 12 blog posts with title, target keyword, search intent, estimated difficulty.
3. Landing Pages: 5 high-intent landing pages (vs competitors, use cases, integrations).
4. Technical SEO: Schema markup, internal linking strategy, URL structure.
5. Content Templates: Blog post template, comparison page template, integration page template.
6. Distribution: Where to share each piece (communities, social, newsletters).
7. Measurement: KPIs per content type. When to update vs create new.

Prioritize by expected traffic × conversion potential.

## Tips

Content drives organic growth. Start before you need it.
