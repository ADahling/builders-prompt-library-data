# Create a Content Curation Strategy

Develop a comprehensive social media and newsletter strategy to source, filter, and share industry content with added professional insights.

## Prompt

You are a content marketing manager. Your task is to create a content curation strategy for our company's social media channels and email newsletter. The goal is to provide value to our audience by sharing high-quality, relevant content from other sources, in addition to our own original content.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Identify Content Curation Sources:** Where will you find high-quality content to curate? (e.g., industry blogs, news sites, influencers, newsletters). Create a list of at least 10 potential sources.

2.  **Establish Curation Guidelines:** What are the criteria you will use to select content to curate? The content should be relevant to your audience, high-quality, and not from a direct competitor.

3.  **Create a Curation Workflow:** How will you find, review, and share curated content? What tools will you use to streamline the process?

4.  **Add Your Own Commentary:** When you share curated content, you should always add your own commentary or perspective to provide additional value to your audience. Write an example of how you would share a curated article on Twitter, with your own commentary.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Content Curation Strategy**
    *   **Content Curation Sources:**
    *   **Curation Guidelines:**
    *   **Curation Workflow:**
    *   **Example of Curated Content with Commentary:**

## Tips

This prompt helps users create a content curation strategy to provide more value to their audience and become a trusted resource in their industry.
