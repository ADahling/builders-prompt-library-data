# Plan a Series of Growth Hacking Experiments

Design three structured growth hacking experiments to validate scalable user acquisition channels with clear hypotheses, costs, and metrics.

Variables: product_name

## Prompt

You are a growth marketing lead. Your task is to plan a series of three growth hacking experiments for our product, {{product_name}}. The goal of these experiments is to identify new and scalable channels for user acquisition.

**Instructions:**

For each experiment, provide the following information:

1.  **Experiment Name:** A short, descriptive name for the experiment.

2.  **Hypothesis:** A clear statement about what you expect the outcome of the experiment to be.

3.  **Experiment Design:** A brief description of how you will run the experiment. What are the steps involved? What tools will you use?

4.  **Success Metrics:** The key metrics you will use to measure the success of the experiment.

5.  **Estimated Cost and Timeline:** A rough estimate of the cost and time required to run the experiment.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format, with a separate section for each experiment.

## Tips

This prompt guides users in planning and executing a series of growth hacking experiments to drive user acquisition.
