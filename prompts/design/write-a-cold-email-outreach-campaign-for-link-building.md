# Write a Cold Email Outreach Campaign for Link Building

Generate a structured three-part cold email sequence designed to secure backlinks by pitching blog content to relevant website owners.

Variables: blog_post_title, blog_post_url

## Prompt

You are an SEO outreach specialist. Your task is to write a 3-part cold email outreach campaign to acquire backlinks for our blog post, "{{blog_post_title}}" ({{blog_post_url}}). The goal is to get other websites to link to our blog post in their content.

**Instructions:**

For each of the three emails in the sequence, provide the following:

1.  **Subject Line:** A personalized and intriguing subject line that gets the recipient to open the email.

2.  **Email Body:** The full body copy of the email. The tone should be friendly, professional, and value-driven.

**Email 1 (initial outreach):** Should introduce yourself, compliment their website, and pitch your blog post as a valuable resource for their audience.
**Email 2 (sent 3-5 days after initial outreach):** Should be a gentle reminder and provide additional context or a different angle on why your content is a good fit.
**Email 3 (sent 7-10 days after initial outreach):** Should be a final, polite follow-up and a last attempt to get a response.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format, with a separate section for each email.

## Tips

This prompt provides a ready-to-use cold email outreach campaign for link building, which is a crucial off-page SEO tactic.
