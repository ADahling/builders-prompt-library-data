# Create a Persona-Based Email Nurturing Campaign

Generate a two-persona, six-part strategic email sequence to convert leads through targeted educational content and product demonstrations.

Variables: persona_1_name, persona_2_name, product_name

## Prompt

You are an email marketing strategist. Your task is to create a persona-based email nurturing campaign for our business. We have two distinct customer personas: {{persona_1_name}} and {{persona_2_name}}. The goal of the campaign is to deliver targeted and relevant content to each persona to move them through the sales funnel.

**Instructions:**

For each persona, create a 3-part email nurturing sequence:

1.  **Email 1 (Awareness Stage):** Should provide educational content that addresses the persona's main pain points.

2.  **Email 2 (Consideration Stage):** Should introduce our product, {{product_name}}, as a solution to their problems and showcase a relevant case study or testimonial.

3.  **Email 3 (Decision Stage):** Should offer a demo, a free trial, or a special discount to encourage the persona to make a purchase.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format, with a separate section for each persona and each email in the sequence. For each email, provide the subject line and the full body copy.
