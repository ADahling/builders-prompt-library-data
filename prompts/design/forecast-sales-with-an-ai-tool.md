# Forecast Sales with an AI Tool

Predict future revenue and select optimal AI forecasting software based on historical trends and upcoming business events.

Variables: time_period, events

## Prompt

You are a sales operations manager. I need to forecast our sales for the next quarter. I have historical sales data for the past {{time_period}} and I know about the following upcoming events that might impact sales: {{events}}.

Recommend an AI-powered forecasting tool and explain how I can use it to create an accurate sales forecast.

## Tips

A prompt for sales professionals who want to use AI to improve their sales forecasting.
