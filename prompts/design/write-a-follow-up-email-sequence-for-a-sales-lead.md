# Write a Follow-Up Email Sequence for a Sales Lead

Generate an automated three-part SDR email sequence to re-engage white paper leads using value-driven messaging and a soft breakup closing.

## Prompt

You are a sales development representative (SDR). Your task is to write a 3-part follow-up email sequence for a sales lead who has downloaded a white paper from our website but has not yet responded to our initial outreach.

**Instructions:**

For each of the three emails in the sequence, provide the following:

1.  **Subject Line:** A compelling subject line that stands out in a crowded inbox.

2.  **Email Body:** The full body copy of the email. The tone should be helpful and persistent, but not pushy.

**Email 1 (sent 2 days after initial outreach):** Should provide additional value and a soft call-to-action.
**Email 2 (sent 4 days after initial outreach):** Should reference the white paper they downloaded and ask a thought-provoking question.
**Email 3 (sent 7 days after initial outreach):** Should be a final, friendly check-in and a breakup email if there is no response.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format, with a separate section for each email.

## Tips

This prompt provides a ready-to-use follow-up email sequence to nurture sales leads and increase response rates.
