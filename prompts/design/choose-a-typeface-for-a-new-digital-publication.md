# Choose a Typeface for a New Digital Publication

Select and justify a cohesive typography system for a digital magazine based on its name and niche to ensure visual brand alignment.

Variables: magazine_name, magazine_topic

## Prompt

You are a digital art director. Your task is to choose a primary typeface for a new online magazine, {{magazine_name}}, that covers the topic of {{magazine_topic}}. You need to research and evaluate a variety of typefaces, considering factors such as readability, personality, and licensing costs. You should also choose a secondary typeface for headings and other display text. The output should be a presentation that showcases your chosen typefaces and explains why they are a good fit for the magazine. Use {{magazine_name}} and {{magazine_topic}} as variables.

## Tips

This prompt is for designers who want to develop their skills in typography and learn how to choose the right typeface for a project.
