# Create a Customer Testimonial Request Template

Generate a professional email sequence with guided questions and incentives to solicit high-quality customer testimonials for marketing use.

## Prompt

You are a customer marketing manager. Your task is to create an email template for requesting a testimonial from a satisfied customer. The goal is to get a high-quality testimonial that we can use on our website and in our marketing materials.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Write a Subject Line:** The subject line should be friendly and personal, and it should clearly state the purpose of the email.

2.  **Write the Email Body:** The body of the email should:
    *   Thank the customer for their business.
    *   Briefly explain why you are requesting a testimonial.
    *   Provide clear instructions on how to submit the testimonial (e.g., a link to a form, or simply by replying to the email).
    *   (Optional) Offer a small incentive for providing a testimonial, such as a discount on their next purchase or a gift card.

3.  **Suggest Questions to Guide the Testimonial:** To help the customer write a great testimonial, suggest three to five questions they can answer. These questions should prompt them to talk about their experience with your product or service, the results they achieved, and why they would recommend you to others.

**Output Format:**

Please provide the complete email template in a single block of text, with placeholders for the customer's name and other personal details.

## Tips

This prompt provides a ready-to-use email template for requesting customer testimonials, which are a powerful form of social proof.
