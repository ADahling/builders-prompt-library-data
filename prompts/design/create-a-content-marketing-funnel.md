# Create a Content Marketing Funnel

Develop a strategic full-funnel content plan with targeted goals, formats, and distribution tactics to convert leads across the buyer journey.

Variables: target_audience

## Prompt

You are a content marketing strategist. Your task is to create a content marketing funnel for our business. The funnel should guide potential customers through the different stages of the buyer's journey, from awareness to consideration to decision.

**Instructions:**

For each stage of the funnel (Top of Funnel, Middle of Funnel, Bottom of Funnel), provide the following:

1.  **Goal of the Stage:** What is the primary goal of the content in this stage?

2.  **Content Formats:** What are the best content formats for this stage? (e.g., blog posts, ebooks, webinars, case studies).

3.  **Content Ideas:** Brainstorm three specific content ideas for this stage that would be relevant to our target audience, {{target_audience}}.

4.  **Distribution Channels:** How will you promote the content in this stage to reach your target audience?

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format, with a separate section for each stage of the funnel.

## Tips

This prompt helps users create a strategic content marketing funnel to nurture leads and drive conversions.
