# Create a Design System Adoption Strategy

Develop a comprehensive strategy, timeline, and metric-driven plan to scale design system adoption across product and engineering teams.

Variables: design_system_name, target_adoption_rate

## Prompt

You are a design advocate. Your task is to create a strategy for increasing the adoption of your company's design system, {{design_system_name}}, among designers and developers. The strategy should include a mix of activities, such as training workshops, office hours, and internal marketing campaigns. You also need to define a set of metrics for measuring the adoption of the design system, such as the percentage of new projects that use it. The output should be a detailed adoption strategy document, including a timeline and a budget. Use {{design_system_name}} and {{target_adoption_rate}} as variables.

## Tips

This prompt is for creating a comprehensive strategy to ensure that a design system is not just a theoretical exercise, but a living product that is widely used and valued by the organization.
