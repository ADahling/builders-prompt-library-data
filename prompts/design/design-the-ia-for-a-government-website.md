# Design the IA for a government website

Generate a comprehensive sitemap and rationale for a government website tailored to diverse user needs and accessibility standards.

Variables: website_name, user_needs

## Prompt

You are an information architect. Your task is to design the information architecture (IA) for a government website, {{website_name}}. Government websites have a unique set of challenges, such as a large and diverse audience, a large amount of content, and a need to be accessible to people with disabilities. You need to design an IA that is clear, concise, and easy to use for all users. The output should be a sitemap for the government website, along with a report that explains the rationale behind your decisions. Use {{website_name}} and {{user_needs}} as variables.

## Tips

This is an advanced prompt for designing the information architecture for a government website, which requires a deep understanding of the needs of a diverse audience.
