# Create a Viral Marketing Campaign for a Mobile App

Develop a comprehensive viral marketing strategy for a mobile app, encompassing shared hooks, mechanics, distribution channels, and KPIs.

Variables: app_name, app_functionality

## Prompt

You are a viral marketing expert. Your task is to brainstorm a viral marketing campaign for our new mobile app, {{app_name}}. The app allows users to {{app_functionality}}.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Identify the Viral Hook:** What is the core element of the app or the campaign that will make people want to share it? This could be a unique feature, a contest, a humorous video, or something else.

2.  **Outline the Campaign Mechanics:** Describe how the campaign will work. How will users participate? How will they be encouraged to share it with their friends?

3.  **Choose the Right Channels:** Which social media platforms or other channels will be most effective for spreading this campaign? Justify your choices.

4.  **Define Success Metrics:** How will you measure the success of the campaign? What are the key metrics to track?

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Viral Marketing Campaign: {{app_name}}**
    *   **The Viral Hook:**
    *   **Campaign Mechanics:**
    *   **Distribution Channels:**
    *   **Success Metrics:**
