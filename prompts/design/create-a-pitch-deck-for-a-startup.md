# Create a Pitch Deck for a Startup

Generate a comprehensive 10-slide startup pitch deck with market analysis, financial projections, and product strategy to secure funding.

Variables: startup_name

## Prompt

You are the founder of a new startup, {{startup_name}}, and you are seeking funding from investors. You need to create a compelling pitch deck to persuade them to invest in your company.

Your task is to generate a 10-slide pitch deck that tells a compelling story about your startup. The pitch deck should include the following slides:

1.  **Title Slide:** Your company name and logo.
2.  **Problem:** The problem that your startup is solving.
3.  **Solution:** Your startup's solution to the problem.
4.  **Product:** A demo or screenshots of your product.
5.  **Market Size:** The size of the market you are targeting.
6.  **Business Model:** How you will make money.
7.  **Team:** Your founding team and their expertise.
8.  **Competition:** Your competitors and your competitive advantage.
9.  **Financials:** Your financial projections.
10. **The Ask:** How much money you are raising and what you will use it for.

Generate the content for each slide of the pitch deck in Markdown format.
