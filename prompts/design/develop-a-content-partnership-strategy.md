# Develop a Content Partnership Strategy

Create a comprehensive content partnership plan including partner selection criteria, collaborative project ideas, and outreach templates.

Variables: niche

## Prompt

You are a business development manager. Your task is to develop a content partnership strategy to expand our brand's reach and authority. We want to collaborate with other companies or publications in our niche, {{niche}}, to create and promote content together.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Identify Potential Partners:** How would you identify potential content partners? What criteria would you use to evaluate them?

2.  **Propose Three Content Partnership Ideas:** Suggest three different types of content partnerships we could pursue. Examples include co-authoring a white paper, co-hosting a webinar, or creating a joint research report.

3.  **Create an Outreach Template:** Write an email template for reaching out to potential partners to propose a collaboration.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Content Partnership Strategy**
    *   **Identifying Potential Partners:**
    *   **Content Partnership Ideas:**
    *   **Outreach Template:**

## Tips

This prompt helps users develop a strategy for building content partnerships to grow their audience and brand authority.
