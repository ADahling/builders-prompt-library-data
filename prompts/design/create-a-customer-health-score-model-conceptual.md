# Create a Customer Health Score Model (Conceptual)

Design a conceptual SaaS customer health score model to proactively mitigate churn and identify expansion opportunities through actionable playbooks.

## Prompt

You are a customer success manager. Your task is to conceptually design a customer health score model for our SaaS business. The goal is to proactively identify customers who are at risk of churning and customers who are prime candidates for upselling.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Identify Key Health Indicators:** What are the key data points (health indicators) that you would use to measure customer health? (e.g., product usage data, support tickets, survey responses, billing information).

2.  **Create a Scoring System:** How would you combine these health indicators into a single customer health score? (e.g., a weighted average, a point system).

3.  **Define Health Score Tiers:** How would you categorize customers based on their health score? (e.g., "Healthy", "At-Risk", "Critical").

4.  **Develop Playbooks for Each Tier:** What actions will you take for customers in each health score tier? (e.g., for at-risk customers, you might schedule a check-in call; for healthy customers, you might offer an upsell).

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

    *   **Key Health Indicators:**
    *   **Scoring System:**
    *   **Health Score Tiers:**
    *   **Playbooks:**
