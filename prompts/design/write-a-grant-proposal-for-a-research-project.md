# Write a Grant Proposal for a Research Project

Generate a comprehensive, structured grant proposal including methodology, budget, and objectives to secure funding for a research project.

Variables: project_title, research_area, funding_agency

## Prompt

You are a researcher seeking funding for a new research project. You need to write a compelling grant proposal to persuade a funding agency to support your work.

Your task is to generate a comprehensive grant proposal that includes the following sections:

*   **Project Summary:** A concise and compelling summary of your research project.
*   **Introduction:** A detailed background of the research problem, including a review of the relevant literature.
*   **Research Objectives:** A clear statement of the specific aims and objectives of your research.
*   **Methodology:** A detailed description of the research methods and procedures you will use.
*   **Expected Outcomes:** A discussion of the expected outcomes of your research and their potential impact.
*   **Budget:** A detailed budget for the project, including personnel, equipment, and other expenses.

Here is some information about your research project:

*   **Project Title:** {{project_title}}
*   **Research Area:** {{research_area}}
*   **Funding Agency:** {{funding_agency}}

Generate the complete grant proposal in Markdown format. The proposal should be well-written, persuasive, and scientifically sound.
