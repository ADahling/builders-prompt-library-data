# Create a Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) Page

Generate a comprehensive, categorized FAQ page in Markdown to streamline customer support and explain product features, billing, and troubleshooting.

Variables: product_name, feature_1, feature_2, feature_3, pricing_model

## Prompt

You are a product manager for a new SaaS product, {{product_name}}. You need to create a Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) page to address common customer questions and reduce the load on your support team.

Your task is to generate a list of at least 15 common questions and their corresponding answers for the FAQ page. The questions should cover a range of topics, including:

*   **Product Features:** Questions about what the product does and how it works.
*   **Pricing and Billing:** Questions about pricing plans, payment methods, and billing cycles.
*   **Account Management:** Questions about creating an account, managing users, and changing settings.
*   **Troubleshooting:** Common issues that users may encounter.

For each question, provide a clear and concise answer. The tone should be helpful and customer-friendly.

Here is some information about the product:

*   **Product Name:** {{product_name}}
*   **Key Features:** {{feature_1}}, {{feature_2}}, {{feature_3}}
*   **Pricing Model:** {{pricing_model}}

Generate the complete FAQ page content in Markdown format. The questions should be organized into logical categories.
