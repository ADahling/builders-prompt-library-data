# Evaluate AI Tool's Ease of Use

Generate a tailored UX evaluation checklist to assess the onboarding, navigation, and accessibility of software for specific user profiles.

Variables: tool_name, user_profile

## Prompt

You are a UX analyst. I need to evaluate the ease of use of {{tool_name}}. My team consists of {{user_profile}} with varying technical skills.

Provide a checklist of criteria to assess the tool's user-friendliness. The checklist should cover aspects like onboarding, navigation, and overall user experience.

## Tips

A prompt for users who want to assess the usability of an AI tool before committing to it.
