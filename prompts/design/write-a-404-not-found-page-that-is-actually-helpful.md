# Write a '404 Not Found' Page That Is Actually Helpful

Generate empathetic and solution-oriented 404 page copy that reduces user bounce rates by providing clear navigation and helpful next steps.

## Prompt

You are a UX writer. Your task is to write the copy for a '404 Not Found' page that is not only apologetic but also helpful to the user. The goal is to turn a frustrating experience into a positive one.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Write a Friendly and Apologetic Headline:** The headline should acknowledge that something went wrong, but in a friendly and human way.

2.  **Explain What Happened in Simple Terms:** Briefly explain what a 404 error is in non-technical terms.

3.  **Provide Helpful Next Steps:** What should the user do next? Provide a few helpful options, such as:
    *   A search bar to help them find what they were looking for.
    *   Links to your homepage, blog, or other popular pages on your website.
    *   A link to your contact page or customer support.

4.  **(Optional) Add a Touch of Humor or Personality:** Depending on your brand's voice, you can add a touch of humor or personality to the page to make it more memorable.

**Output Format:**

Please provide the complete copy for the 404 page in a single block of text, using Markdown for formatting.

## Tips

This prompt guides users in writing a 404 page that is both user-friendly and on-brand.
