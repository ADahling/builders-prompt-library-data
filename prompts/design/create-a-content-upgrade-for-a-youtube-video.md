# Create a Content Upgrade for a YouTube Video

Generate lead-generating content upgrades and scripted calls-to-action to convert YouTube viewers into email subscribers.

Variables: video_title, video_topic

## Prompt

You are a content marketer. Your task is to create a content upgrade for our YouTube video, "{{video_title}}". The video is about {{video_topic}}. The goal is to convert YouTube viewers into email subscribers.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Brainstorm Content Upgrade Ideas:** Brainstorm three different ideas for a content upgrade that would be a valuable and relevant bonus to the video. Examples include a checklist, a worksheet, a template, or a resource guide related to the video's topic.

2.  **Choose the Best Idea and Create the Content:** Choose the most compelling idea and create the content for the content upgrade. The content should be high-quality and provide real value to the user.

3.  **Write the Call-to-Action for the Video:** Write a script for a call-to-action that can be included in the YouTube video (e.g., in the description, as a pinned comment, or as a verbal CTA in the video itself) to direct viewers to the content upgrade.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Content Upgrade for YouTube Video**
    *   **Content Upgrade Ideas:**
    *   **Content Upgrade Content:**
    *   **Call-to-Action Script:**
