# Develop a Lead Magnet to Capture Email Subscribers

Generate creative lead magnet concepts, a detailed content outline, and high-converting opt-in copy to grow a niche email subscriber list.

Variables: website_topic, target_audience

## Prompt

You are a content marketing strategist. Your task is to brainstorm and outline a compelling lead magnet to attract and capture email subscribers for our website, which is about {{website_topic}}. Our target audience is {{target_audience}}.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Brainstorm Lead Magnet Ideas:** Generate three distinct lead magnet ideas that would be highly valuable to our target audience. For each idea, provide a brief description and explain why it would be effective.

2.  **Select and Outline the Best Idea:** Choose the most promising lead magnet idea from your brainstorm and create a detailed outline for it. The outline should include the main sections, key points to be covered, and any specific examples or resources to be included.

3.  **Write the Opt-in Copy:** Write the copy for the opt-in form that will be used to capture email addresses. This includes the headline, a brief description of the lead magnet, and the call-to-action button copy.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Lead Magnet Strategy**
    *   **Lead Magnet Ideas:**
        *   **Idea 1:**
        *   **Idea 2:**
        *   **Idea 3:**
    *   **Selected Lead Magnet Outline:**
    *   **Opt-in Form Copy:**
        *   **Headline:**
        *   **Description:**
        *   **CTA Button Copy:**
