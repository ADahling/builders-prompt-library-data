# AI Tool for Social Media Management

Identify and select AI-driven management platforms to automate post scheduling, engagement analytics, and mention tracking for specific goals.

Variables: social_media_platform, social_media_goal

## Prompt

You are a social media manager. I need an AI tool to help me schedule posts, analyze engagement, and track mentions on {{social_media_platform}}. My goal is to {{social_media_goal}}.

Recommend an AI-powered social media management tool that can help me achieve my goals.

## Tips

A prompt for social media managers looking to automate and optimize their workflows.
