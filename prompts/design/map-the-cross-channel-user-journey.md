# Map the Cross-Channel User Journey

Visualize the seamless transition of a specific persona across digital and physical touchpoints to identify friction and optimize brand consistency.

Variables: brand_name, customer_persona

## Prompt

You are a customer experience manager. Your task is to map the cross-channel user journey for a customer who is interacting with a brand, {{brand_name}}, across multiple touchpoints, such as the website, mobile app, social media, and physical store. You need to identify how the customer moves between these different channels and how their experience is connected across them. The output should be a cross-channel user journey map that highlights opportunities to create a more seamless and consistent customer experience. Use {{brand_name}} and {{customer_persona}} as variables.

## Tips

This is an advanced prompt for mapping the complex user journeys that are common in today's multi-channel world.
