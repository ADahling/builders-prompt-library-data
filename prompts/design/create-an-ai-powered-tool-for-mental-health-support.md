# Create an AI-Powered Tool for Mental Health Support

Design a regulation-compliant AI therapeutic assistant featuring empathetic conversational agents, evidence-based exercises, and mood tracking.

Variables: mental_health_condition, regulations

## Prompt

You are a clinical psychologist and AI researcher. I want to create an AI-powered tool to provide mental health support to individuals struggling with {{mental_health_condition}}. The tool should offer a safe and confidential space for users to express their feelings and receive guidance. The tool must be compliant with {{regulations}}.

Design the core features of the mental health support tool. The design should include the conversational AI agent, the therapeutic exercises, and the mood tracking functionality.
