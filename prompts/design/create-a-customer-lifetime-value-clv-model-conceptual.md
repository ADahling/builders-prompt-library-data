# Create a Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) Model (Conceptual)

Architect a conceptual customer lifetime value model to optimize e-commerce acquisition, retention data analysis, and growth strategies.

## Prompt

You are a marketing analyst. Your task is to conceptually design a customer lifetime value (CLV) model for our e-commerce business. The goal is to understand the long-term value of our customers so that we can make better decisions about customer acquisition and retention.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Choose a CLV Formula:** There are several ways to calculate CLV. Choose a formula that is appropriate for our business and explain why you chose it.

2.  **Identify the Key Inputs:** What are the key data points (inputs) that you would need to calculate CLV? (e.g., average order value, purchase frequency, customer lifespan).

3.  **Outline the Data Collection and Analysis Process:** How would you collect the necessary data? How would you use it to calculate CLV for different customer segments?

4.  **Suggest Ways to Increase CLV:** Based on your understanding of the CLV model, suggest three strategies for increasing the lifetime value of our customers.

    *   **Strategies to Increase CLV:**
