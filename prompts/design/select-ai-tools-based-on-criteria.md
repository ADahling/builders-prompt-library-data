# Select AI Tools Based on Criteria

Get an AI tool selection checklist and justified shortlist for your project based on budget, team skills, integration, and support.

Variables: project_context

## Prompt

Identify the key selection criteria for AI tools that will be used in {{project_context}}. Create a checklist that includes factors like budget, team expertise, integration capabilities, and long-term support. Then, shortlist AI tools that fit these criteria and justify your selections.

## Tips

This structured approach will help make informed decisions regarding tool selection.
