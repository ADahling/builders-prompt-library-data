# Optimize a Google Ads Campaign for a Local Business

Analyze performance metrics and generate a structured optimization plan to improve CTR and conversions for a localized Google Ads campaign.

Variables: business_name, service_name, city, impressions, clicks, ctr, cpc, conversions, conversion_rate

## Prompt

You are a Google Ads specialist. Your task is to optimize a Google Ads campaign for a local business, {{business_name}}, which provides {{service_name}} in {{city}}. The campaign has been running for a month, but the results are not satisfactory.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Analyze the Campaign Performance:** Based on the following data, what are the key areas for improvement?
    *   **Impressions:** {{impressions}}
    *   **Clicks:** {{clicks}}
    *   **CTR:** {{ctr}}
    *   **CPC:** {{cpc}}
    *   **Conversions:** {{conversions}}
    *   **Conversion Rate:** {{conversion_rate}}

2.  **Propose Optimization Strategies:** Propose at least three specific optimization strategies to improve the campaign's performance. These could include changes to the keywords, ad copy, landing page, or targeting.

3.  **Suggest a New Ad Group:** Suggest a new ad group that could be created to target a specific sub-service or customer segment.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Google Ads Campaign Optimization Plan**
    *   **Performance Analysis:**
    *   **Optimization Strategies:**
    *   **New Ad Group Suggestion:**
