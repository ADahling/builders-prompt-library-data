# Write a Product Hunt Launch Strategy

Generate a comprehensive end-to-end Product Hunt launch plan to maximize community engagement, homepage visibility, and user conversions.

Variables: product_name

## Prompt

You are a product launch specialist. Your task is to create a launch strategy for our new product, {{product_name}}, on Product Hunt. The goal is to get to the top of the homepage and drive a significant amount of traffic and sign-ups.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Pre-Launch Activities:** Outline the key activities to be completed in the weeks leading up to the launch, such as building a landing page, preparing your Product Hunt assets (e.g., images, video, description), and building a list of supporters.

2.  **Launch Day Execution:** Describe the step-by-step plan for launch day, from the moment you post your product to how you will engage with the Product Hunt community throughout the day.

3.  **Post-Launch Follow-up:** What will you do in the days following the launch to capitalize on the momentum and continue to engage with new users?

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Product Hunt Launch Strategy**
    *   **Pre-Launch Activities:**
    *   **Launch Day Execution:**
    *   **Post-Launch Follow-up:**

## Tips

This prompt provides a comprehensive guide to launching a product on Product Hunt, which can be a powerful channel for early-stage startups.
