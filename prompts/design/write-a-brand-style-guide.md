# Write a Brand Style Guide

Generate a comprehensive, markdown-formatted brand style guide defining visual identity, typography, color palettes, and editorial voice.

Variables: company_name

## Prompt

You are a brand strategist and designer. Your task is to create a brand style guide for our company, {{company_name}}. The style guide should provide clear guidelines for how our brand should be represented across all marketing materials.

**Instructions:**

Create a brand style guide that includes the following sections:

1.  **Logo Usage:** Rules for how to use our logo, including minimum size, clear space, and incorrect usage examples.

2.  **Color Palette:** Our primary and secondary brand colors, with hex codes and RGB values.

3.  **Typography:** Our brand fonts for headings and body text, with specifications for size and weight.

4.  **Tone of Voice:** A description of our brand's personality and tone of voice (e.g., friendly, professional, witty).

5.  **Imagery Style:** Guidelines for the type of images and photography that should be used to represent our brand.

**Output Format:**

Please provide the brand style guide in a structured Markdown format, with a separate section for each element.

## Tips

This prompt helps users create a brand style guide to ensure brand consistency across all marketing channels.
