# Write Product Marketing Copy Package

Get product marketing copy for your audience: a one-liner, pitch, store description, five social posts, and a three-email welcome sequence.

## Prompt

Write marketing copy for [describe product]:

1. One-liner: Single sentence for bios and headers.
2. Elevator Pitch: 30-second version (3-4 sentences).
3. App Store / Product Hunt Description: 150 words, discovery-optimized.
4. 5 Social Media Posts: LinkedIn/X. Mix: insight, problem/solution, behind-the-scenes, social proof, CTA.
5. Email Sequence: 3-email welcome for new signups. Email 1: Welcome + quick start. Email 2: Key feature. Email 3: Social proof + upgrade CTA.

Tone: [professional / conversational / bold]. Audience: [describe].

## Tips

Customize tone and audience before running.
