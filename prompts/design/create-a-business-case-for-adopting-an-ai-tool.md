# Create a Business Case for Adopting an AI Tool

Generate a persuasive, data-driven business case to justify investment in a specific AI tool based on targeted organizational goals.

Variables: tool_name, business_goal

## Prompt

You are a business analyst. I need to create a compelling business case to convince my company's leadership to invest in {{tool_name}}. The tool is expected to help us achieve {{business_goal}}.

Write a business case that outlines the problem, the proposed solution, the expected benefits, and the financial justification for the investment. The business case should be persuasive and data-driven.

## Tips

A prompt for individuals who need to champion the adoption of a new AI tool within their organization.
