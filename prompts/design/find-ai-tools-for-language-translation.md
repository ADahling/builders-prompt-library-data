# Find AI Tools for Language Translation

Identify and select high-accuracy AI translation tools tailored to specific languages and industry-specific localization requirements.

Variables: source_language, target_language, industry

## Prompt

You are a localization manager. I need an AI tool to translate our website content from {{source_language}} to {{target_language}}. Accuracy is critical for our {{industry}}.

Recommend a reliable AI translation tool that is known for its accuracy and supports these languages.

## Tips

A prompt for users who need to translate content and require a high level of accuracy.
