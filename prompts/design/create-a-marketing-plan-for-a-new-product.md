# Create a Marketing Plan for a New Product

Generate a comprehensive marketing plan and launch strategy for a new product including objectives, budget, and a tactical execution calendar.

Variables: product_name

## Prompt

You are a marketing manager for a new product, {{product_name}}. You need to create a comprehensive marketing plan to launch the product successfully.

Your task is to generate a detailed marketing plan that includes the following sections:

*   **Marketing Objectives:** The specific goals of the marketing campaign.
*   **Target Audience:** A detailed description of your target audience.
*   **Marketing Strategy:** The overall marketing strategy, including the marketing mix (product, price, place, promotion).
*   **Marketing Tactics:** The specific marketing tactics you will use to execute the strategy.
*   **Marketing Budget:** A budget for the marketing campaign.
*   **Marketing Calendar:** A calendar of marketing activities.

Generate the complete marketing plan in Markdown format.
