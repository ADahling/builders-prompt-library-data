# Write a Creative Brief for a New Marketing Campaign

Generate a comprehensive marketing creative brief with SMART goals, audience profiles, and clear deliverables to align campaign execution.

Variables: product_name

## Prompt

You are a marketing manager. Your task is to write a creative brief for our internal creative team or an external agency for a new marketing campaign for our product, {{product_name}}.

**Instructions:**

Create a creative brief that includes the following sections:

1.  **Background:** Why are we running this campaign? What is the business context?

2.  **Objectives:** What are the specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) goals of the campaign?

3.  **Target Audience:** Who are we trying to reach with this campaign? (Provide a detailed description of the target audience). 

4.  **Key Message:** What is the single most important thing we want to communicate to our target audience?

5.  **Deliverables:** What are the specific creative assets that need to be produced? (e.g., a 30-second video, a set of social media ads, a landing page).

6.  **Budget and Timeline:** What is the budget for the campaign? What are the key deadlines?

**Output Format:**

Please provide the creative brief in a structured Markdown format, with a separate section for each element.

## Tips

This prompt guides users in writing a clear and comprehensive creative brief to ensure that everyone involved in a marketing campaign is on the same page.
