# Optimize a Marketing Campaign with an AI Tool

Select an AI tool and execute a data-driven optimization strategy to boost performance metrics for a live multi-channel marketing campaign.

Variables: channel, product_or_service, time_period, performance_metrics

## Prompt

You are a digital marketing manager. I am running a marketing campaign on {{channel}} to promote {{product_or_service}}. The campaign has been running for {{time_period}} and the current performance is {{performance_metrics}}.

Recommend an AI tool for optimizing marketing campaigns and provide a plan for using it to improve the performance of my campaign.

## Tips

A prompt for marketers who want to use AI to optimize their marketing campaigns.
