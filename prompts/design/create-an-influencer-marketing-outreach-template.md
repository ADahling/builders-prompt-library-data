# Create an Influencer Marketing Outreach Template

Generate a professional, customizable email template to recruit influencers in specific niches for brand collaborations and product promotions.

Variables: brand_name, niche

## Prompt

You are a social media manager. Your task is to create an email template for reaching out to potential influencers to collaborate with our brand, {{brand_name}}. We are looking for influencers in the {{niche}} niche.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Write a Personalized Subject Line:** The subject line should be catchy and personalized to the influencer.

2.  **Write the Email Body:** The body of the email should:
    *   Introduce yourself and our brand.
    *   Explain why you think they would be a good fit for a collaboration.
    *   Propose a specific collaboration idea (e.g., a sponsored post, a product review, a giveaway).
    *   Clearly state what you are offering in return (e.g., payment, free products).
    *   End with a clear call-to-action (e.g., asking if they are interested in learning more).

**Output Format:**

Please provide the complete email template in a single block of text, with placeholders for the influencer's name and other personal details.

## Tips

This prompt provides a ready-to-use email template for reaching out to influencers for marketing collaborations.
