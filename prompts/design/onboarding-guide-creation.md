# Onboarding Guide Creation

Draft an onboarding guide for new team members covering company culture, the tools they will use, and a week-one checklist.

Variables: company_name, tools

## Prompt

Draft an onboarding guide for new team members at {{company_name}}. Include sections on company culture, tools used (like {{tools}}), and a week one checklist that new hires should complete.

## Tips

Make it friendly and engaging to encourage new hires.
