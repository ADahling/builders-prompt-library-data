# Create a Micro-interaction Prototype for a Mobile App

Design and prototype a functional micro-interaction for a specific mobile app trigger to improve user feedback and interface delight.

Variables: app_name, micro_interaction_trigger

## Prompt

You are a UI/UX designer. Your task is to design and prototype a single, delightful micro-interaction for a mobile app. The micro-interaction should provide feedback to the user after they complete a specific action, such as adding an item to their cart, liking a post, or completing a task. You can use any prototyping tool that supports animation, such as Principle, ProtoPie, or Figma's Smart Animate. The output should be a short video or GIF of the micro-interaction and a brief explanation of the design choices you made, including the timing, easing, and visual effects. Use {{app_name}} and {{micro_interaction_trigger}} as variables.

## Tips

This prompt is for focusing on the small details that can make a big difference in the user experience. Micro-interactions can make an app feel more polished and engaging.
