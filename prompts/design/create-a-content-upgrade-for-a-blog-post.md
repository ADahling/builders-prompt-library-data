# Create a Content Upgrade for a Blog Post

Generate high-value lead magnets, associated content, and opt-in copy to convert blog readers into email subscribers.

Variables: blog_post_title

## Prompt

You are a content marketer. Your task is to create a content upgrade for our blog post, "{{blog_post_title}}". A content upgrade is a bonus piece of content that is offered to readers in exchange for their email address. The goal is to convert blog readers into email subscribers.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Brainstorm Content Upgrade Ideas:** Brainstorm three different ideas for a content upgrade that would be a valuable and relevant bonus to the blog post. Examples include a checklist, a cheat sheet, a template, or a resource guide.

2.  **Choose the Best Idea and Create the Content:** Choose the most compelling idea and create the content for the content upgrade. The content should be high-quality and provide real value to the user.

3.  **Write the Opt-in Copy:** Write the copy for the opt-in form that will be used to offer the content upgrade within the blog post. This includes the headline, a brief description of the content upgrade, and the call-to-action button copy.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Content Upgrade**
    *   **Content Upgrade Ideas:**
    *   **Content Upgrade Content:**
    *   **Opt-in Copy:**

## Tips

This prompt helps users create a content upgrade to convert their blog traffic into email subscribers.
