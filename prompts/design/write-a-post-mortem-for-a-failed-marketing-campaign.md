# Write a Post-Mortem for a Failed Marketing Campaign

Generate a structured marketing post-mortem to analyze failed campaigns, identify root causes, and develop actionable improvement strategies.

## Prompt

You are a marketing manager. Your team has just completed a marketing campaign that did not meet its goals. Your task is to write a post-mortem for the campaign to understand what went wrong and how you can improve in the future. The goal is to learn from your mistakes, not to place blame.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Summarize the Campaign Goals and Results:** Briefly summarize the goals of the campaign and how the actual results compared to those goals.

2.  **Analyze What Went Wrong:** What were the main reasons why the campaign failed? Was it the offer, the targeting, the messaging, the channels, or something else? Be honest and specific.

3.  **Identify What Went Well:** Even in a failed campaign, there are usually some things that went well. What were the bright spots?

4.  **Create a List of Actionable Takeaways:** What are the key lessons learned from this campaign? What will you do differently next time? Create a list of at least three specific and actionable takeaways.

**Output Format:**

Please provide the post-mortem in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Marketing Campaign Post-Mortem**
    *   **Campaign Summary:**
    *   **What Went Wrong:**
    *   **What Went Well:**
    *   **Actionable Takeaways:**

## Tips

This prompt helps users conduct a constructive post-mortem for a failed marketing campaign to foster a culture of learning and continuous improvement.
