# Optimize the Cost of Using AI Tools

Reduce AI expenditure by generating specific, actionable cost-saving strategies tailored to your team's toolset and usage patterns.

Variables: tool_list, usage_pattern

## Prompt

You are an AI cost optimization consultant. My team is using a suite of AI tools, including {{tool_list}}, and our monthly bill is getting expensive. We need to find ways to reduce our costs without sacrificing performance. Our usage pattern is {{usage_pattern}}.

Analyze our usage of these AI tools and provide a set of actionable recommendations for optimizing our costs. The recommendations should be specific and easy to implement.

## Tips

A prompt for teams that want to manage and reduce their spending on AI tools.
