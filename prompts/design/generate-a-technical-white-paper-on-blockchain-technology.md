# Generate a Technical White Paper on Blockchain Technology

Create a comprehensive technical white paper outlining the architecture, protocols, and use cases for blockchain-based decentralized identity.

## Prompt

You are a blockchain expert and technical writer. Your task is to write a comprehensive technical white paper on the topic of 'Decentralized Identity Management using Blockchain'. The white paper should be targeted at a technical audience of software developers and architects.

Your white paper should cover the following key areas:

*   **Introduction to Decentralized Identity:** Explain the current challenges with centralized identity management systems and introduce the concept of self-sovereign identity.
*   **Blockchain as a Solution:** Describe how blockchain technology can be used to build a decentralized identity management system. Explain the role of public-key cryptography, digital signatures, and distributed ledgers.
*   **Architecture:** Propose a high-level architecture for a decentralized identity management system. Describe the key components, such as decentralized identifiers (DIDs), verifiable credentials, and the blockchain network.
*   **Use Cases:** Discuss potential use cases for decentralized identity, such as secure login, KYC/AML compliance, and healthcare.
*   **Challenges and Future Work:** Discuss the challenges and limitations of blockchain-based identity management and suggest areas for future research and development.

Generate the complete white paper in Markdown format. The content should be technically accurate, well-structured, and persuasive.
