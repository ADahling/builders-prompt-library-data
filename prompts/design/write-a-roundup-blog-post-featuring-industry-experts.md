# Write a Roundup Blog Post Featuring Industry Experts

Generate a high-authority expert roundup blog post including outreach templates, structured industry insights, and SEO-optimized copy.

Variables: topic

## Prompt

You are a content marketer. Your task is to write a 2,000-word roundup blog post featuring insights from 10-15 industry experts on the topic of {{topic}}. The goal is to create a highly valuable and shareable piece of content that will attract backlinks and social shares.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Create an Outreach Template:** Write an email template for reaching out to industry experts to ask them to contribute to the roundup post.

2.  **Compile and Organize the Contributions:** Once you have received the contributions from the experts, organize them into a coherent and well-structured blog post. You can group the contributions by sub-topic or present them as a list.

3.  **Write an Introduction and Conclusion:** Write a compelling introduction to the blog post that sets the context and a conclusion that summarizes the key takeaways.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Roundup Blog Post**
    *   **Outreach Template:**
    *   **Blog Post Structure and Content:**
    *   **Introduction and Conclusion:**
