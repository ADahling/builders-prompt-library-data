# Create a SWOT Analysis for a Business

Generate a structured Markdown SWOT analysis for a business strategist to evaluate internal capabilities and external market factors.

## Prompt

You are a business strategist for a company. You need to create a SWOT analysis to identify your company's strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.

Your task is to generate a SWOT analysis in a four-quadrant format. For each quadrant, provide at least three bullet points.

Generate the complete SWOT analysis in Markdown format.
