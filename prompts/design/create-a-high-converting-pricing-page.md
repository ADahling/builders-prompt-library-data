# Create a High-Converting Pricing Page

Structure a high-converting SaaS pricing page with tier-based feature comparisons, persuasive CTAs, and an objection-handling FAQ section.

Variables: saas_product_name, tier_1_name, tier_2_name, tier_3_name

## Prompt

You are a pricing strategy and landing page expert. Your task is to design a high-converting pricing page for our SaaS product, {{saas_product_name}}. We have three pricing tiers: {{tier_1_name}}, {{tier_2_name}}, and {{tier_3_name}}.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Pricing Table Layout:** Design a clear and easy-to-understand pricing table. You can describe the layout in detail or create a simple text-based representation of the table. The table should clearly display the features included in each tier and the price.

2.  **Feature Highlighting:** For each pricing tier, identify the key features that should be highlighted to justify the price and appeal to the target user for that tier. Explain your reasoning.

3.  **Call-to-Action (CTA) for Each Tier:** Write a compelling CTA for each pricing tier. The CTAs should be tailored to the specific tier and encourage users to sign up.

4.  **Address Common Objections:** Identify three common objections or questions users might have about our pricing and write concise answers to address them. This could be in the form of an FAQ section below the pricing table.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Pricing Page Design**
    *   **Pricing Table Layout:**
    *   **Feature Highlighting:**
        *   **{{tier_1_name}}:**
        *   **{{tier_2_name}}:**
        *   **{{tier_3_name}}:**
    *   **Calls-to-Action:**
        *   **{{tier_1_name}} CTA:**
        *   **{{tier_2_name}} CTA:**
        *   **{{tier_3_name}} CTA:**
    *   **Addressing Objections (FAQ):**
