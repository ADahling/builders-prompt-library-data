# Write a Crisis Communication Plan

Develop a comprehensive Markdown-formatted crisis communication plan covering team roles, risk scenarios, messaging strategies, and media protocols.

## Prompt

You are a public relations manager for a company. You need to create a crisis communication plan to prepare for a potential crisis.

Your task is to generate a comprehensive crisis communication plan that includes the following sections:

*   **Crisis Communication Team:** The members of the crisis communication team and their roles.
*   **Crisis Scenarios:** A list of potential crisis scenarios.
*   **Communication Strategy:** The overall communication strategy for each crisis scenario.
*   **Key Messages:** The key messages that will be communicated to the media, customers, and employees.
*   **Spokespeople:** The designated spokespeople for the company.
*   **Media Monitoring:** A plan for monitoring the media for coverage of the crisis.

Generate the complete crisis communication plan in Markdown format.
