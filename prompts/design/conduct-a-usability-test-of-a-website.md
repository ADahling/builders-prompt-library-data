# Conduct a Usability Test of a Website

Generate a structured usability test plan and comprehensive findings report to identify and resolve user experience issues on a website.

Variables: website_url, test_tasks

## Prompt

You are a UX researcher. Your task is to conduct a usability test of a website, {{website_url}}, with 5 participants. You need to create a test plan that outlines the tasks that the participants will perform on the website. During the test, you need to observe the participants and take notes on any usability issues they encounter. After the test, you need to analyze the data and create a report that summarizes your findings and provides recommendations for improvement. The output should be the test plan and the usability test report. Use {{website_url}} and {{test_tasks}} as variables.

## Tips

This prompt is for learning how to conduct a usability test to identify and fix usability issues on a website.
