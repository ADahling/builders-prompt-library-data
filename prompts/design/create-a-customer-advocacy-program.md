# Create a Customer Advocacy Program

Design a structured customer advocacy program to identify, engage, and reward loyal users as influential brand ambassadors.

## Prompt

You are a customer marketing manager. Your task is to design a customer advocacy program to turn your most passionate customers into a powerful marketing force for your brand.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Identify and Recruit Advocates:** How will you identify your most passionate customers? What are the criteria for becoming a customer advocate? How will you recruit them?

2.  **Define the Advocate Activities:** What will you ask your customer advocates to do? (e.g., write reviews, provide testimonials, participate in case studies, refer new customers, share content on social media).

3.  **Create a Rewards and Recognition System:** How will you reward and recognize your customer advocates for their contributions? (e.g., exclusive access to new features, company swag, gift cards, a private online community).

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Customer Advocacy Program Design**
    *   **Advocate Recruitment:**
    *   **Advocate Activities:**
    *   **Rewards and Recognition:**
