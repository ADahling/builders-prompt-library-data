# Design an Affiliate Marketing Program

Create a comprehensive affiliate marketing program strategy including commission structures, tracking policies, and creator onboarding.

Variables: product_name

## Prompt

You are a marketing manager. Your task is to design an affiliate marketing program for our product, {{product_name}}. The goal of the program is to incentivize bloggers, influencers, and other content creators to promote our product in exchange for a commission on sales.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Define the Commission Structure:** What percentage of each sale will you offer to your affiliates? Will you offer a one-time commission or a recurring commission for subscription products?

2.  **Set the Cookie Duration:** How long will the tracking cookie last? This is the period of time after a user clicks on an affiliate's link during which the affiliate will be credited for a sale.

3.  **Create Promotional Materials for Affiliates:** What kind of promotional materials will you provide to your affiliates to help them promote your product? This could include banners, email templates, and social media posts.

4.  **Outline the Affiliate Onboarding Process:** How will you onboard new affiliates and provide them with the information and resources they need to be successful?

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:
