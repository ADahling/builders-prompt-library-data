# Analyze User Feedback to Identify Pain Points

Categorize and prioritize user feedback to generate a structured report of product pain points and actionable mitigation recommendations.

Variables: product_name, feedback_sources

## Prompt

You are a product manager. Your task is to analyze user feedback from a variety of sources, such as customer support tickets, app store reviews, and social media comments, to identify the most common pain points that users are experiencing with a product, {{product_name}}. You need to categorize the feedback and prioritize the pain points based on their severity and frequency. The output should be a report that summarizes your findings and provides recommendations for how to address the pain points. Use {{product_name}} and {{feedback_sources}} as variables.

## Tips

This prompt is for learning how to analyze user feedback to identify and prioritize user pain points.
