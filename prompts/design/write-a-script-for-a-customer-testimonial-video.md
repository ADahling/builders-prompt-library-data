# Write a Script for a Customer Testimonial Video

Generate a comprehensive 2-minute testimonial video script including interview questions, B-roll directions, and a compelling call-to-action.

Variables: customer_name

## Prompt

You are a video producer. Your task is to write a script for a 2-minute customer testimonial video featuring one of our satisfied customers, {{customer_name}}. The video should be authentic, engaging, and persuasive.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Outline the Video:** Create a scene-by-scene outline of the video, including the questions you will ask the customer and the B-roll footage you will use to supplement their interview.

2.  **Write the Interview Questions:** Write a list of 5-7 open-ended questions to ask the customer during the interview. The questions should prompt them to talk about their challenges before using your product, their experience with the product, and the results they have achieved.

3.  **Write the Intro and Outro:** Write a brief intro to set the context for the video and an outro with a clear call-to-action.

**Output Format:**

Please provide the complete script in a structured Markdown format, with separate sections for the outline, interview questions, and intro/outro.

## Tips
