# Create a Customer Exit Survey

Generate an actionable SaaS exit survey that identifies churn drivers through conditional follow-up questions and provides retention incentives.

Variables: saas_product_name

## Prompt

You are a customer retention specialist. Your task is to create a customer exit survey to understand why customers are canceling their subscriptions to our SaaS product, {{saas_product_name}}. The goal is to gather actionable feedback to reduce churn.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Write the Main Question:** The main question should be a multiple-choice question that asks the customer for the primary reason they are canceling. Provide a list of common reasons (e.g., "It's too expensive", "I'm not using it enough", "I switched to a competitor") and an "Other" option with a text field.

2.  **Write Follow-up Questions:** Based on the customer's answer to the main question, ask a few follow-up questions to get more details. For example, if they say "It's too expensive", you could ask what they would consider a fair price.

3.  **Offer an Incentive to Stay:** At the end of the survey, offer the customer an incentive to stay, such as a discount on their subscription for the next few months.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Customer Exit Survey**
    *   **Main Question:**
    *   **Follow-up Questions:**
    *   **Incentive to Stay:**

## Tips
