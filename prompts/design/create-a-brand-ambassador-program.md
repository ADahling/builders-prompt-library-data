# Create a Brand Ambassador Program

Design a comprehensive brand ambassador program including recruitment criteria, incentive structures, application forms, and onboarding workflows.

Variables: company_name

## Prompt

You are a marketing manager. Your task is to design a brand ambassador program for our company, {{company_name}}. The goal of the program is to recruit and empower a group of passionate customers to promote our brand to their networks.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Define the Ideal Brand Ambassador:** What are the qualities and characteristics of your ideal brand ambassador? Where would you find them?

2.  **Outline the Program Structure:** What will you ask your brand ambassadors to do? (e.g., post on social media, create content, refer friends). What will you offer them in return? (e.g., free products, exclusive discounts, commissions, early access to new products).

3.  **Create an Application Form:** Create a short application form to screen potential brand ambassadors.

4.  **Develop an Onboarding Process:** How will you onboard new brand ambassadors and provide them with the training and resources they need to be successful?

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Brand Ambassador Program Design**
    *   **Ideal Brand Ambassador Profile:**
    *   **Program Structure:**
    *   **Application Form:**
    *   **Onboarding Process:**
