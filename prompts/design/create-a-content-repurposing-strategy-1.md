# Create a Content Repurposing Strategy

Develop a structured plan to identify, transform, and distribute high-performing assets across multiple formats to maximize content ROI.

Variables: blog_post_title

## Prompt

You are a content marketing strategist. Your task is to create a content repurposing strategy to get more mileage out of our existing content. We have a library of blog posts, videos, and webinars that are not being fully utilized.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Identify High-Performing Content:** Explain how you would identify our most popular and evergreen pieces of content. These are the best candidates for repurposing.

2.  **Propose Three Content Repurposing Ideas:** For a high-performing blog post titled "{{blog_post_title}}", propose three ways it could be repurposed into different content formats. For example, you could turn it into an infographic, a series of social media posts, or a short video.

3.  **Create a Distribution Plan:** For each repurposed piece of content, suggest the best channels for distributing it to reach a wider audience.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Content Repurposing Strategy**
    *   **Identifying High-Performing Content:**
    *   **Content Repurposing Ideas:**
    *   **Distribution Plan:**

## Tips

This prompt helps users develop a strategy for repurposing their existing content to maximize its reach and impact.
