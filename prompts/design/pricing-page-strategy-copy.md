# Pricing Page Strategy & Copy

Design a SaaS pricing page with a recommended model, three tiers, a feature comparison, value-focused copy, FAQs, and pricing tactics.

## Prompt

Design a pricing page for [describe SaaS product]:

1. Pricing Model: Recommend the best model for this product type:
   - Freemium (free tier + paid)
   - Free trial (14 or 30 days)
   - Usage-based
   - Flat-rate tiers
   - Per-seat
   Justify your recommendation.

2. Tier Design: Create 3 tiers:
   - Free/Starter: What's included to hook users. Enough value to get started, limits that create upgrade pressure.
   - Pro/Growth: The "most popular" tier. Sweet spot pricing. Key unlock features.
   - Enterprise/Team: High-value features (SSO, audit logs, SLA, priority support).

3. Feature Comparison Table: List 12-15 features across tiers. Use checkmarks, limits, and "contact us" strategically.

4. Pricing Copy:
   - Headline that addresses value, not cost
   - Per-tier taglines (who each tier is for)
   - Annual vs monthly toggle (show savings)
   - FAQ section addressing objections (refunds, switching tiers, cancellation)

5. Psychological Tactics:
   - Anchor pricing (show enterprise first or highlight middle tier)
   - Social proof per tier ("Used by 500+ teams")
   - Money-back guarantee placement
   - "Most Popular" badge on recommended tier

6. Anti-Patterns to Avoid:
   - Too many tiers (max 4)
   - Hidden fees
   - Confusing feature names
   - No free option for SaaS (kills organic growth)

## Tips

Pricing is the most impactful page on your site. Get it right before launch.
