# Organic & Natural UI Design Style

Design an app page with earth tones, soft typography, flowing layouts, and natural textures for a warm, organic visual style.

Variables: page_type, app_name

## Prompt

Design a {{page_type}} for {{app_name}} using an organic and natural visual style. Apply these design principles:

**Color Palette**: Use earth tones — warm browns, sage greens, terracotta, sand, and cream. Avoid pure digital colors. Reference nature: forest, desert, ocean, stone.
**Typography**: Use humanist sans-serifs or soft serifs. Avoid geometric or technical fonts. Handwritten accents for labels or callouts.
**Layout**: Use flowing, non-rigid layouts. Rounded containers, organic blob shapes as backgrounds, and wave-style section dividers.
**Imagery**: Use natural photography — plants, textures, landscapes. Apply warm color filters. Use circular image crops.
**Textures**: Add subtle paper grain, watercolor washes, or botanical illustrations as decorative elements.
**Icons**: Use hand-drawn or organic-style icons. Rounded strokes, imperfect lines, natural feel.

Key vocabulary: earthy, organic, sustainable, handcrafted, botanical, natural, warm, wholesome, grounded.

The design should feel like a farmer's market — warm, authentic, and connected to the earth.
