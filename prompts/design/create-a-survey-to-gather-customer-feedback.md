# Create a Survey to Gather Customer Feedback

Generate a comprehensive customer feedback survey in Markdown featuring diverse question types to identify user pain points and feature needs.

## Prompt

You are a product manager for a software product. You need to create a survey to gather feedback from your customers about their experience with your product.

Your task is to generate a survey with at least 10 questions that will help you understand your customers' needs and pain points. The survey should include a mix of question types, such as:

*   Multiple choice questions.
*   Rating scale questions.
*   Open-ended questions.

The survey should cover topics such as:

*   Customer satisfaction.
*   Product usability.
*   Feature requests.

Generate the complete survey in Markdown format.
