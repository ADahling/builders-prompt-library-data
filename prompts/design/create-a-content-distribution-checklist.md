# Create a Content Distribution Checklist

Generate a comprehensive multi-channel distribution checklist to maximize content reach across owned, earned, and paid media platforms.

## Prompt

You are a content marketing manager. Your task is to create a content distribution checklist that we can use every time we publish a new piece of content (e.g., a blog post, a video, a podcast episode). The goal is to ensure that we are maximizing the reach of our content.

**Instructions:**

Create a checklist of at least 15 content distribution tactics, organized into the following categories:

1.  **Owned Media:** (e.g., email newsletter, social media channels, website pop-ups)
2.  **Earned Media:** (e.g., outreach to influencers and journalists, guest posting, social sharing)
3.  **Paid Media:** (e.g., social media ads, search engine marketing, content discovery platforms)

**Output Format:**

Please provide the checklist in a structured Markdown format, with a separate section for each category.

## Tips

This prompt provides a comprehensive checklist to help users distribute their content effectively and reach a wider audience.
