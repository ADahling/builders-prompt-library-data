# Develop a Data-Driven Color Palette for a New Brand

Generate a comprehensive, data-backed brand color palette and style guide optimized for industry psychology and competitive positioning.

Variables: brand_name, industry

## Prompt

You are a brand designer. Your task is to develop a new color palette for a fictional brand, {{brand_name}}, that operates in the {{industry}} industry. Instead of relying on subjective preferences, you must use a data-driven approach to create the palette. This involves researching the color palettes of competitors, analyzing the psychological associations of different colors in your target market, and using tools to generate harmonious color combinations. The output should be a detailed presentation that explains your process, justifies your color choices, and showcases the final color palette with hex codes and usage guidelines. Use {{brand_name}} and {{industry}} as variables.

## Tips

This prompt is for designers who want to create color palettes that are not only beautiful but also effective and backed by research.
