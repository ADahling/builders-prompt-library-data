# Write a Welcome Email Series for New Customers

Generate a three-part automated onboarding email sequence to engage new buyers, minimize churn, and maximize product adoption.

Variables: product_name

## Prompt

You are an email marketing specialist. Your task is to write a 3-part welcome email series for new customers who have just purchased our product, {{product_name}}. The goal of the series is to onboard new customers, reduce buyer's remorse, and encourage them to get the most out of their purchase.

**Instructions:**

For each of the three emails in the series, provide the following:

1.  **Subject Line:** A compelling subject line that encourages the customer to open the email.

2.  **Email Body:** The full body copy of the email. The tone should be friendly, helpful, and reassuring.

**Email 1 (sent immediately after purchase):** Should confirm the purchase, provide access to the product, and set expectations.
**Email 2 (sent 2-3 days after purchase):** Should provide tips and resources to help the customer get started with the product.
**Email 3 (sent 5-7 days after purchase):** Should check in with the customer, ask for feedback, and offer further support.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format, with a separate section for each email.

## Tips

This prompt guides users in creating a welcome email series to onboard new customers and build a strong customer relationship from the start.
