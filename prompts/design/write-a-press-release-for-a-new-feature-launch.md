# Write a Press Release for a New Feature Launch

Generate a professional, media-ready press release to announce a new software feature and its benefits to the public.

Variables: feature_name, software_name, feature_benefit

## Prompt

You are a public relations (PR) professional. Your task is to write a professional press release for the launch of a new feature, {{feature_name}}, for our software, {{software_name}}. The feature allows users to {{feature_benefit}}.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Write a Compelling Headline:** The headline should be attention-grabbing and clearly announce the new feature.

2.  **Write the Body of the Press Release:** The body should provide details about the new feature, its benefits for users, and a quote from a company spokesperson (e.g., the CEO or Head of Product).

3.  **Include Boilerplate and Contact Information:** Include a standard boilerplate about the company and provide contact information for media inquiries.

**Output Format:**

Please provide the complete press release in a professional format, ready to be sent to media outlets.

## Tips

This prompt guides users in writing a professional press release to announce a new feature and generate media coverage.
