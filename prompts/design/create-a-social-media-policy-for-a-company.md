# Create a Social Media Policy for a Company

Draft a comprehensive, professional social media policy in Markdown format to define employee usage standards and protect brand integrity.

## Prompt

You are a human resources manager for a company. You need to create a social media policy to provide guidelines for employees on how to use social media in a way that is consistent with the company's brand and values.

Your task is to generate a comprehensive social media policy that includes the following sections:

*   **Introduction:** An overview of the policy and its purpose.
*   **Guidelines for Personal Use:** Guidelines for employees on how to use social media for personal purposes.
*   **Guidelines for Professional Use:** Guidelines for employees on how to use social media for professional purposes.
*   **Confidentiality:** A reminder to employees not to share confidential company information on social media.
*   **Consequences of Violation:** The consequences of violating the social media policy.

Generate the complete social media policy in Markdown format.
