# Write a Meeting Agenda

Generate a structured Markdown meeting agenda featuring schedules, attendee lists, time-boxed topics, and prerequisite reading materials.

Variables: meeting_topic

## Prompt

You are organizing a meeting to discuss {{meeting_topic}}. You need to write a clear and concise meeting agenda to ensure that the meeting is productive and stays on track.

Your task is to generate a meeting agenda that includes:

*   The date, time, and location of the meeting.
*   The attendees of the meeting.
*   The topics to be discussed, with a time allocation for each topic.
*   Any materials that attendees should review before the meeting.

Generate the complete meeting agenda in Markdown format.
