# Creating a Product Roadmap

Plan a product roadmap with timelines, milestones, dependencies, and feature rationale tied to user needs and business goals.

Variables: product_name

## Prompt

Develop a product roadmap for {{product_name}}. Include timelines, milestones, and dependencies. Explain the rationale behind each feature and how it aligns with user needs and business goals.

## Tips

Aim for clarity and strategic alignment.
