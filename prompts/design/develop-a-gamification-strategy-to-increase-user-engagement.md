# Develop a Gamification Strategy to Increase User Engagement

Design a tailored gamification framework for a mobile app to boost retention through strategic mechanics, milestones, and a badge system.

Variables: app_name

## Prompt

You are a user engagement and gamification expert. Your task is to develop a gamification strategy for our mobile app, {{app_name}}. The goal is to increase user engagement and retention.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Identify Key User Actions to Gamify:** What are the key actions within the app that you want to encourage users to perform more often? (e.g., completing a task, inviting a friend, making a purchase).

2.  **Propose Three Gamification Mechanics:** Suggest three gamification mechanics that could be used to incentivize these actions. Examples include points, badges, leaderboards, and progress bars.

3.  **Design a Badge System:** For one of the mechanics you proposed, design a system of badges that users can earn for achieving certain milestones within the app. For each badge, provide a name, a description, and the criteria for earning it.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Gamification Strategy**
    *   **Key User Actions to Gamify:**
    *   **Gamification Mechanics:**
    *   **Badge System Design:**

## Tips

This prompt helps users develop a gamification strategy to make their app more engaging and fun to use.
