# Create a Contribution Model for a Design System

Establish a structured contribution workflow and governance model to scale your design system through collaborative component development.

Variables: design_system_name, contribution_guidelines

## Prompt

You are a design systems manager. Your task is to create a contribution model for your company's design system, {{design_system_name}}. The goal is to encourage designers and developers from different teams to contribute to the design system, while maintaining its quality and consistency. You need to define the process for proposing new components, a review and approval workflow, and guidelines for documenting and testing contributions. The output should be a document that clearly explains the contribution model and provides templates for contribution proposals. Use {{design_system_name}} and {{contribution_guidelines}} as variables.

## Tips

This prompt is for creating a sustainable and collaborative design system that can evolve with the needs of the organization.
