# Create a Customer Feedback Loop

Establish a structured system to capture, evaluate, and implement user insights to drive continuous product improvement and engagement.

## Prompt

You are a product manager. Your task is to design a customer feedback loop for our company. The goal is to systematically collect, analyze, and act on customer feedback to improve our product and customer experience.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Choose Feedback Channels:** What channels will you use to collect customer feedback? (e.g., surveys, in-app feedback widgets, customer interviews, social media monitoring).

2.  **Design a Feedback Analysis Process:** How will you analyze the feedback you collect? What tools will you use to identify trends, patterns, and actionable insights?

3.  **Create a Process for Acting on Feedback:** How will you prioritize and act on the feedback you receive? How will you communicate back to customers to let them know you are listening and taking their feedback seriously?

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Customer Feedback Loop Design**
    *   **Feedback Channels:**
    *   **Feedback Analysis Process:**
    *   **Process for Acting on Feedback:**

## Tips

This prompt helps users design a customer feedback loop to systematically improve their product and customer satisfaction.
