# Write a 'Coming Soon' Landing Page for a New Product

Generate high-converting landing page copy to build pre-launch anticipation and capture early-bird leads via email signups.

Variables: product_name

## Prompt

You are a marketing copywriter. Your task is to write the copy for a 'Coming Soon' landing page for our new product, {{product_name}}. The goal of the page is to build anticipation and capture email addresses from people who are interested in being notified when the product launches.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Write a Teaser Headline:** The headline should be intriguing and create a sense of mystery and excitement about the upcoming product.

2.  **Briefly Describe the Product's Value Proposition:** Without giving away too much detail, briefly describe the main benefit of the product and who it is for.

3.  **Create a Compelling Call-to-Action:** The call-to-action should be to sign up for the email list to be the first to know when the product launches. You can also offer an exclusive early-bird discount to people who sign up.

4.  **Include Social Sharing Buttons:** Encourage visitors to share the 'Coming Soon' page with their friends to help spread the word.

**Output Format:**

Please provide the complete landing page copy in a single block of text, using Markdown for formatting.

## Tips

This prompt guides users in writing effective copy for a 'Coming Soon' landing page to build a pre-launch email list.
