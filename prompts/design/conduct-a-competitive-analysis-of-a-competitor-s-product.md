# Conduct a Competitive Analysis of a Competitor's Product

Analyze a competitor's product to identify strengths and weaknesses and generate strategic differentiation recommendations for your own.

Variables: competitor_product_url, your_product_name

## Prompt

You are a product designer. Your task is to conduct a competitive analysis of a competitor's product, {{competitor_product_url}}. You need to analyze the competitor's product in terms of its features, design, and user experience. You should also identify the competitor's strengths and weaknesses. The output should be a detailed report that summarizes your findings and provides recommendations for how your own product, {{your_product_name}}, can differentiate itself from the competition. Use {{competitor_product_url}} and {{your_product_name}} as variables.

## Tips

This prompt is for learning how to conduct a competitive analysis to gain insights into the market and the competition.
