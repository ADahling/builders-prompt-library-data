# Analyze the Use of Color in a Competitor's Product

Evaluate competitor color strategies and accessibility to improve your own product's visual hierarchy, emotional impact, and UX design.

Variables: competitor_product_url, your_product_name

## Prompt

You are a product designer. Your task is to analyze the use of color in a competitor's product, {{competitor_product_url}}. You need to identify the primary, secondary, and accent colors used in the product, and analyze how they are used to create a visual hierarchy, convey meaning, and evoke emotions. You also need to assess the accessibility of the color palette. The output should be a detailed report that summarizes your findings and provides recommendations for how your own product, {{your_product_name}}, can use color more effectively. Use {{competitor_product_url}} and {{your_product_name}} as variables.

## Tips

This prompt is for conducting a competitive analysis of a competitor's use of color, which can provide valuable insights for your own design process.
