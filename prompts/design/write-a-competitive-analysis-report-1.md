# Write a Competitive Analysis Report

Generate a comprehensive report comparing your company against top rivals with a SWOT analysis and actionable strategic recommendations.

Variables: company_name, competitor_1, competitor_2, competitor_3

## Prompt

You are a marketing analyst. Your task is to write a competitive analysis report that compares our company, {{company_name}}, to our top three competitors: {{competitor_1}}, {{competitor_2}}, and {{competitor_3}}. The report should provide a clear overview of the competitive landscape and identify our strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats (SWOT analysis).

**Instructions:**

Create a competitive analysis report that includes the following sections:

1.  **Executive Summary:** A brief overview of the key findings of the report.

2.  **Competitive Landscape:** An overview of the market and the key players.

3.  **Competitor Profiles:** A detailed profile of each competitor, including their products, pricing, marketing strategies, and strengths and weaknesses.

4.  **SWOT Analysis:** A SWOT analysis of our own company in relation to the competition.

5.  **Recommendations:** A list of actionable recommendations based on your analysis.

**Output Format:**

Please provide the competitive analysis report in a structured Markdown format, with a separate section for each element.

## Tips

This prompt helps users create a comprehensive competitive analysis report to inform their business and marketing strategy.
