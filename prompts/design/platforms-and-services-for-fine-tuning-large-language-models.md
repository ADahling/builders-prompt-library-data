# Platforms and Services for Fine-Tuning Large Language Models

Compare five leading open-source LLM fine-tuning platforms to select the best infrastructure, workflow, and pricing model for your project.

Variables: budget, dataset_size

## Prompt

I am a developer with a specific dataset and I want to fine-tune an open-source LLM to create a specialized model for my application. Generate a guide to the top 5 platforms and services that facilitate the fine-tuning process.

For each platform, provide a detailed profile covering:

- **Platform Name:**
- **Supported Base Models:** Which open-source models can be fine-tuned on the platform? (e.g., Llama 4, Mixtral, Gemma).
- **Fine-Tuning Process:** Describe the workflow. Is it a no-code UI, a Python SDK, or do you need to manage the infrastructure yourself?
- **Data Handling:** How does a user upload and manage their training data? What formats are supported?
- **Deployment:** Once fine-tuned, how is the model deployed? Do they provide a managed API endpoint?
- **Cost of Fine-Tuning & Hosting:** Explain the pricing. Is it a one-time cost for the tuning job? What is the ongoing cost for hosting the fine-tuned model?

Your response should be a practical guide for a developer who is new to fine-tuning and wants to understand the landscape of available services. Conclude with a summary paragraph recommending a starting point for a developer with a budget of {{budget}} and a dataset of {{dataset_size}} records.
