# Automate Scientific Research with an AI Lab Assistant

Architect a custom hardware and software framework for an AI lab assistant to automate specific research workflows and equipment controls.

Variables: field, tasks_to_automate, equipment_list

## Prompt

You are a research scientist in the field of {{field}}. I want to develop an AI lab assistant to automate repetitive and time-consuming tasks in my research workflow, such as {{tasks_to_automate}}. My lab uses the following equipment: {{equipment_list}}.

Design the AI lab assistant. The design should specify the hardware and software components, the control system for the lab equipment, and the user interface for interacting with the assistant.
