# Orchestrate a Multi-Cloud AI Workflow

Architect a resilient, multi-cloud AI infrastructure to optimize data processing, model training, and deployment across diverse providers.

Variables: cloud_providers, data_type, purpose

## Prompt

You are a cloud architect specializing in AI/ML. I need to design a resilient and cost-effective AI workflow that leverages services from multiple cloud providers, specifically {{cloud_providers}}. The workflow will process {{data_type}} data for {{purpose}}.

Design a multi-cloud architecture that orchestrates the entire AI workflow, from data ingestion and preprocessing to model training and deployment. The design should address data sovereignty, security, and interoperability challenges.
