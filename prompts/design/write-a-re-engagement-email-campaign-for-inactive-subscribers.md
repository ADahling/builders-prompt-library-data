# Write a Re-engagement Email Campaign for Inactive Subscribers

Generate a targeted three-part re-engagement sequence to win back inactive subscribers and optimize list hygiene through personalized copy.

## Prompt

You are an email marketing specialist. Your task is to write a 3-part re-engagement email campaign for subscribers who have not opened or clicked an email from us in the last 90 days. The goal of the campaign is to win back their attention or remove them from our list to improve our email deliverability.

**Instructions:**

For each of the three emails in the sequence, provide the following:

1.  **Subject Line:** A compelling subject line that piques their curiosity and encourages them to open the email.

2.  **Email Body:** The full body copy of the email. The tone should be friendly and personal.

**Email 1 (sent to inactive subscribers):** Should be a gentle nudge, reminding them of the value we provide and asking if they still want to hear from us.
**Email 2 (sent 3-5 days after email 1 if no response):** Should offer a special incentive, such as a discount or a free resource, to encourage them to re-engage.
**Email 3 (sent 7-10 days after email 2 if no response):** Should be a final, friendly goodbye, letting them know that we will be removing them from our list unless they take action.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format, with a separate section for each email.

## Tips

This prompt provides a ready-to-use re-engagement email campaign to clean up an email list and improve email marketing performance.
