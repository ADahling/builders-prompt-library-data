# Write a Technical Vision for a Product

Create a structured technical vision and architectural roadmap to align engineering teams and define core development principles.

## Prompt

You are the CTO of a startup. You need to write a technical vision for your product to align your engineering team and guide your product's technical direction.

Your task is to generate a comprehensive technical vision that includes the following sections:

*   **Introduction:** A brief overview of the product and its goals.
*   **Guiding Principles:** The key principles that will guide your technical decisions.
*   **Target Architecture:** A high-level description of the target architecture for the product.
*   **Technology Stack:** The technology stack that will be used to build the product.
*   **Key Challenges:** The key technical challenges that you will need to overcome.

Generate the complete technical vision in Markdown format.
