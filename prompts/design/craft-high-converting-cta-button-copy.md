# Craft High-Converting CTA Button Copy

Generate five conversion-optimized CTA variations with psychological rationales and color suggestions to maximize click-through rates.

Variables: product_name, desired_action, target_audience, current_cta_copy

## Prompt

You are a world-class direct-response copywriter. Your task is to generate five high-converting call-to-action (CTA) button copy variations for our product, {{product_name}}. The primary goal of the CTA is to get users to {{desired_action}}.

**Context:**
*   **Product:** {{product_name}}
*   **Target Audience:** {{target_audience}}
*   **Desired Action:** {{desired_action}}
*   **Current CTA Copy:** "{{current_cta_copy}}"

**Instructions:**

1.  **Analyze the Goal:** Briefly explain your understanding of the user's goal and the psychological drivers that will motivate the target audience to click the CTA.

2.  **Generate Variations:** Create five distinct CTA copy variations. For each variation, provide a brief rationale explaining the copywriting principle behind it (e.g., benefit-driven, urgency, command, etc.).

3.  **Consider Button Design:** While you are not designing the button, suggest a color for each CTA copy variation that would be most effective in drawing attention and conveying the right emotion, based on color psychology.
