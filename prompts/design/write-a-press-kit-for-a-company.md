# Write a Press Kit for a Company

Generate a professional, structured press kit in Markdown to provide media outlets with essential company background, bios, and product facts.

Variables: company_name

## Prompt

You are a public relations professional. Your task is to create a press kit for our company, {{company_name}}. The press kit should provide journalists and media outlets with all the information they need to write about our company.

**Instructions:**

Create a press kit that includes the following components:

1.  **Company Overview:** A brief overview of our company, including our mission, vision, and history.

2.  **Fact Sheet:** A one-page fact sheet with key information about our company, such as founding date, location, number of employees, and key milestones.

3.  **Founder Bios:** Short bios and headshots of our founders.

4.  **Product Information:** A description of our products or services, with high-resolution images.

5.  **Press Releases:** Links to our most recent press releases.

6.  **Media Mentions:** Links to any previous media coverage we have received.

7.  **Contact Information:** Contact information for media inquiries.

**Output Format:**

Please provide the press kit as a structured Markdown document, with a separate section for each component. You can use placeholders for images and links.

## Tips

This prompt guides users in creating a professional press kit to make it easy for journalists to write about their company.
