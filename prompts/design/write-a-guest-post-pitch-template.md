# Write a Guest Post Pitch Template

Generate a professional guest post outreach template featuring personalized subject lines, expert introductions, and tailored content proposals.

Variables: niche

## Prompt

You are a content marketer and outreach specialist. Your task is to create an email template for pitching a guest post to a high-authority blog in our niche, {{niche}}.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Write a Personalized Subject Line:** The subject line should be catchy and relevant to the blog you are pitching to.

2.  **Write the Email Body:** The body of the email should:
    *   Introduce yourself and your expertise in the niche.
    *   Explain why you want to write for their blog and how it would benefit their audience.
    *   Propose three unique and compelling guest post ideas that are tailored to their blog's content.
    *   Include links to a few of your previously published articles to showcase your writing style and quality.
    *   End with a clear call-to-action.

**Output Format:**

Please provide the complete email template in a single block of text, with placeholders for the blog name, editor's name, and other details.

## Tips

This prompt provides a ready-to-use email template for pitching guest posts to build backlinks and authority.
