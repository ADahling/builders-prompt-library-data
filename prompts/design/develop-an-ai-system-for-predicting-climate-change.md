# Develop an AI System for Predicting Climate Change

Architect a regional climate prediction system that integrates specific data sources and modeling pipelines to project long-term environmental impacts.

Variables: region, data_sources

## Prompt

You are a climate scientist. I want to develop an AI system to predict the long-term impacts of climate change on a specific region, {{region}}. The system should use data from {{data_sources}} to model the climate system and generate projections for various climate variables, such as temperature and precipitation.

Design the AI system for climate change prediction. The design should specify the data processing pipeline, the climate models to be used, and the visualization tools for presenting the results.
