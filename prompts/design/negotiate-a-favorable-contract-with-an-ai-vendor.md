# Negotiate a Favorable Contract with an AI Vendor

Identify strategic negotiation leverage points for AI tool procurement to optimize pricing, security, and service level agreements.

Variables: tool_name, usage_volume

## Prompt

You are a procurement manager. I am about to negotiate a contract with the vendor of {{tool_name}}. I want to ensure that we get the best possible terms and pricing. Our expected usage is {{usage_volume}}.

Provide a list of key negotiation points to discuss with the vendor. The list should cover pricing, service-level agreements (SLAs), data ownership, and security.

## Tips

A prompt for individuals who are responsible for negotiating contracts with AI vendors.
