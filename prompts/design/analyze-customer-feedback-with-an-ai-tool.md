# Analyze Customer Feedback with an AI Tool

Select and implement an AI tool to categorize sentiment and extract actionable product insights from multilingual customer feedback.

Variables: feedback_sources, language

## Prompt

You are a product manager. I have a large volume of customer feedback from various sources, such as {{feedback_sources}}. I need to analyze this feedback to identify common themes, sentiment, and actionable insights. The feedback is in {{language}}.

Recommend an AI tool for analyzing customer feedback and provide a guide on how to use it to extract valuable insights.
