# Write a White Paper to Generate B2B Leads

Produce a comprehensive technical white paper and its corresponding lead-capture landing page to position a product as a solution.

Variables: topic, target_audience, product_name

## Prompt

You are a B2B content marketer. Your task is to write a 2,000-word white paper on the topic of {{topic}}. The white paper should be a deep dive into a specific problem that our target audience, {{target_audience}}, is facing, and it should subtly position our product, {{product_name}}, as the solution. The goal is to generate high-quality leads for our sales team.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Create a Detailed Outline:** Create a detailed outline for the white paper, including the introduction, the main sections, and the conclusion.

2.  **Write the White Paper:** Write the full white paper, following the outline you created. The tone should be authoritative, well-researched, and educational. Use data, charts, and graphs to support your points.

3.  **Design a Landing Page for the White Paper:** Write the copy for a landing page where users can download the white paper in exchange for their contact information. The landing page should have a compelling headline, a brief description of the white paper, and a clear call-to-action.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **White Paper**
    *   **Outline:**
    *   **Full Text:**
*   **Landing Page Copy:**
