# Optimize a Landing Page for Different Traffic Sources

Tailor landing page content and conversion strategies to align with the specific intent and metrics of multiple distinct traffic sources.

Variables: landing_page_url, traffic_source_1, traffic_source_2, traffic_source_3

## Prompt

You are a digital marketing expert specializing in landing page optimization. Your task is to create a strategy for optimizing our landing page, {{landing_page_url}}, for three different traffic sources: {{traffic_source_1}}, {{traffic_source_2}}, and {{traffic_source_3}} (e.g., Google Ads, Facebook Ads, Organic Search).

**Instructions:**

For each traffic source, answer the following questions:

1.  **What is the user's intent?** What is the user looking for when they arrive on the landing page from this specific source?

2.  **How should the landing page be customized?** What changes should be made to the landing page to make it more relevant to the user's intent? This could include changes to the headline, copy, images, or call-to-action.

3.  **What is the primary success metric?** What is the most important metric to track to measure the performance of the landing page for this traffic source?

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format, with a separate section for each traffic source.

## Tips

This prompt helps users understand how to tailor their landing pages to different traffic sources to improve conversion rates.
