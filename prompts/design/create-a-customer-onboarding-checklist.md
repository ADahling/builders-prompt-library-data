# Create a Customer Onboarding Checklist

Generate a structured SaaS onboarding roadmap with actionable milestones, specific setup tasks, and integrated in-app guidance suggestions.

Variables: saas_product_name

## Prompt

You are a customer success manager. Your task is to create a customer onboarding checklist for new customers of our SaaS product, {{saas_product_name}}. The checklist should guide them through the key steps they need to take to get set up and start seeing value from the product as quickly as possible.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Define the Onboarding Milestones:** Break down the onboarding process into key milestones (e.g., "Complete your profile", "Invite your team", "Create your first project").

2.  **Create the Checklist:** For each milestone, create a list of specific action items that the customer needs to complete. The checklist should be clear, concise, and easy to follow.

3.  **Suggest In-App Guidance:** For each action item, suggest how we could provide in-app guidance to help the customer complete it (e.g., a tooltip, a product tour, a help doc link).

**Output Format:**

Please provide the customer onboarding checklist in a structured Markdown format, with a separate section for each milestone.

## Tips

This prompt helps users create a customer onboarding checklist to improve user activation and reduce churn.
