# Create a Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) Program

Design a comprehensive customer satisfaction framework including survey methodologies, question sets, and systematic feedback action plans.

## Prompt

You are a customer experience (CX) manager. Your task is to design a customer satisfaction (CSAT) program to systematically measure and improve customer satisfaction over time.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Choose a CSAT Survey Methodology:** Will you use a 1-5 scale, a 1-10 scale, or something else? When will you send the survey (e.g., after a purchase, after a customer support interaction)?

2.  **Design the CSAT Survey:** Write the survey questions, including the main CSAT question and any follow-up questions to gather more context.

3.  **Create a Process for Analyzing and Acting on Feedback:** How will you analyze the CSAT data? How will you identify trends and areas for improvement? How will you close the loop with customers who provide feedback?

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **CSAT Program Design**
    *   **Survey Methodology:**
    *   **Survey Design:**
    *   **Feedback Analysis and Action Plan:**
