# Write a Brand Manifesto

Draft a bold, inspiring brand manifesto that captures your company's core purpose and values in a powerful call to action for your audience.

Variables: company_name

## Prompt

You are a brand strategist and storyteller. Your task is to write a brand manifesto for our company, {{company_name}}. The manifesto should be a bold and inspiring declaration of our purpose, values, and beliefs. It should be a rallying cry for our employees, customers, and community.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Start with a Big Idea:** What is the big idea or belief that is at the core of your brand? This should be something that is bigger than just your product or service.

2.  **Use Strong and Emotive Language:** The manifesto should be written in a powerful and persuasive tone. Use strong verbs, vivid imagery, and emotional language to make your message resonate.

3.  **Keep it Short and Memorable:** A brand manifesto should be concise and easy to remember. Aim for 100-300 words.

**Output Format:**

Please provide the complete brand manifesto in a single block of text.

## Tips

This prompt helps users write a powerful brand manifesto to inspire and align their team and community.
