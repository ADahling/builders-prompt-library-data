# Create a Customer Advisory Board

Develop a comprehensive strategy to recruit, manage, and leverage a customer advisory board for actionable product feedback and growth.

## Prompt

You are a customer marketing manager. Your task is to create a plan for establishing a customer advisory board (CAB) for our company. A CAB is a group of customers that you meet with on a regular basis to get feedback on your product and strategy.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Define the Goals of the CAB:** What do you want to achieve with your CAB? (e.g., get feedback on your product roadmap, validate new ideas, build stronger customer relationships).

2.  **Recruit CAB Members:** How will you recruit members for your CAB? What are the criteria for membership?

3.  **Plan the CAB Meetings:** How often will you meet with your CAB? What will be the format of the meetings? What topics will you discuss?

4.  **Manage the CAB:** How will you manage the CAB and keep the members engaged over time?

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Customer Advisory Board Plan**
    *   **Goals:**
    *   **Recruitment:**
    *   **Meetings:**
    *   **Management:**

## Tips

This prompt guides users in creating a customer advisory board to get strategic feedback from their most important customers.
