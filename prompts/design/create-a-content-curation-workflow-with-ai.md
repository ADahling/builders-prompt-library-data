# Create a Content Curation Workflow with AI

Design an AI-driven workflow to discover, filter, and schedule curated content for social media to achieve specific marketing goals.

Variables: topic, social_media_platform, content_curation_goal

## Prompt

You are a content marketing manager. I want to create a workflow to automatically curate and share relevant articles and news about {{topic}} with our audience on {{social_media_platform}}. My goal is to {{content_curation_goal}}.

Design a content curation workflow that uses AI tools to discover, filter, and schedule content for sharing.

## Tips

A prompt for marketers who want to automate their content curation efforts.
