# Create a Customer Avatar for a Niche Market

Construct a comprehensive, data-driven customer persona with psychographics and pain points to refine marketing strategies for niche markets.

Variables: product_name, niche_market

## Prompt

You are a marketing strategist specializing in niche markets. Your task is to create a detailed customer avatar for our product, {{product_name}}, which serves the {{niche_market}} market. The avatar should be a fictional representation of our ideal customer.

**Instructions:**

Create a customer avatar that includes the following details:

1.  **Name and Photo:** (You can use a placeholder for the photo)
2.  **Demographics:** (e.g., age, gender, location, job title, income)
3.  **Psychographics:** (e.g., personality, values, interests, lifestyle)
4.  **Goals and Motivations:** What are their biggest goals and aspirations, both personal and professional?
5.  **Pain Points and Challenges:** What are their biggest struggles and frustrations?
6.  **Watering Holes:** Where do they hang out online and offline? (e.g., blogs, forums, social media groups, conferences)
7.  **A Day in the Life:** Write a short narrative that describes a typical day in the life of your customer avatar.

**Output Format:**

Please provide the customer avatar in a structured Markdown format, with a separate section for each element.

## Tips

This prompt guides users in creating a detailed customer avatar to gain a deep understanding of their target audience in a niche market.
