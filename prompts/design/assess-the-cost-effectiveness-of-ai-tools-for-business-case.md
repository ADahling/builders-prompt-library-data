# Assess the Cost-Effectiveness of AI Tools

Compare AI tool costs, expected ROI, and projected efficiency gains for a business case, including alignment with strategic objectives.

Variables: business_case

## Prompt

Conduct an analysis of the cost-effectiveness of implementing AI tools for {{business_case}}. Include initial costs, ongoing expenses, and expected ROI. Compare this with projected efficiency gains and how these tools align with strategic business objectives.

## Tips
