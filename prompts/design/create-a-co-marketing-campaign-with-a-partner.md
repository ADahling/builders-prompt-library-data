# Create a Co-Marketing Campaign with a Partner

Design a comprehensive lead-generation campaign plan that defines joint offers, partner duties, and cross-channel promotional strategies.

Variables: partner_company_name, target_audience

## Prompt

You are a partnership marketing manager. Your task is to create a co-marketing campaign with a partner company, {{partner_company_name}}. Our two companies have a complementary target audience, {{target_audience}}. The goal of the campaign is to generate leads for both companies.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Brainstorm a Co-Marketing Offer:** What will you create together? (e.g., a joint webinar, a co-authored ebook, a joint research report). The offer should be valuable to both of your audiences.

2.  **Define the Roles and Responsibilities:** Who will be responsible for what? Create a clear division of labor for creating the offer, promoting it, and following up with the leads.

3.  **Create a Promotion Plan:** How will you promote the co-marketing offer? The plan should include promotional activities on both companies' channels (e.g., email, social media, blog).

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Co-Marketing Campaign Plan**
    *   **Co-Marketing Offer:**
    *   **Roles and Responsibilities:**
    *   **Promotion Plan:**

## Tips

This prompt helps users plan a co-marketing campaign with a partner to expand their reach and generate leads.
