# Create a Customer Referral Program for a SaaS Business

Design a comprehensive SaaS referral program featuring double-sided incentives, friction-free workflows, and ready-to-use marketing copy.

Variables: saas_product_name

## Prompt

You are a marketing manager for a SaaS business. Your task is to design a customer referral program for our product, {{saas_product_name}}. The goal is to incentivize our existing customers to refer new customers and drive viral growth.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Define the Referral Incentives (Double-Sided):** What will you offer to both the referrer and the referred friend? The incentives should be attractive and aligned with your business model (e.g., a discount on their subscription, a credit to their account, a free month of service).

2.  **Outline the Referral Process:** Describe the step-by-step process for a customer to refer a friend. It should be as simple and frictionless as possible. How will you track the referrals?

3.  **Create Promotional Materials:** Write the copy for an email and an in-app notification that we can use to promote the referral program to our existing customers.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **SaaS Referral Program Design**
    *   **Referral Incentives:**
    *   **Referral Process:**
    *   **Promotional Materials:**
