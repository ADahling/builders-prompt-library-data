# Create a Chatbot for Lead Generation

Select an ideal chatbot platform and implement a lead qualification workflow to streamline the transition from visitor to sales prospect.

Variables: qualifying_questions

## Prompt

You are a marketing technologist. I want to create a chatbot for our website to capture leads and qualify them before passing them to our sales team. The chatbot should ask the following qualifying questions: {{qualifying_questions}}.

Recommend a chatbot platform that is suitable for lead generation and provide a guide on how to build and deploy the chatbot.

## Tips

A prompt for marketers who want to use a chatbot to automate lead generation.
