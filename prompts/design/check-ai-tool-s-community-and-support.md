# Check AI Tool's Community and Support

Evaluate a software tool's support ecosystem and community resources to ensure long-term project stability and reliable assistance.

Variables: tool_name, reasoning

## Prompt

You are a community manager. I am considering using {{tool_name}} and I want to know about its community and support channels. This is important for me because {{reasoning}}.

Provide information on the tool's community forums, documentation, and customer support options.

## Tips

A prompt for users who value community and support when choosing an AI tool.
