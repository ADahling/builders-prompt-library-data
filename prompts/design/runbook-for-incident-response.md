# Runbook for Incident Response

Write a system incident response runbook with diagnosis, mitigation, resolution steps, escalation paths, and stakeholder communication protocols.

Variables: system_name, stakeholders

## Prompt

Develop a runbook for incident response related to {{system_name}}. Outline the steps to diagnose, mitigate, and resolve incidents. Include escalation paths and communication protocols with {{stakeholders}}.

## Tips

Ensure clarity for quick understanding during incidents.
