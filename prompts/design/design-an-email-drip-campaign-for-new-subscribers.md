# Design an Email Drip Campaign for New Subscribers

Generate a complete 5-day automated onboarding sequence including strategic goals, subject lines, and a high-converting welcome email draft.

## Prompt

You are an email marketing specialist. Your task is to design a 5-day email drip campaign for new subscribers to our newsletter. The goal of the campaign is to welcome new subscribers, build a relationship with them, and introduce them to our brand and products.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Outline the Campaign:** For each of the 5 days, specify the goal of the email and the main content to be included.

2.  **Write the Subject Lines:** Write a compelling subject line for each of the 5 emails.

3.  **Write the Body Copy for the First Email:** Write the full body copy for the first email in the sequence (the welcome email).

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **Email Drip Campaign Outline:**
*   **Subject Lines:**
*   **Welcome Email (Day 1) Body Copy:**
