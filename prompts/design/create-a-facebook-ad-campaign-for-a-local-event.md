# Create a Facebook Ad Campaign for a Local Event

Generate a comprehensive Facebook advertising strategy to drive ticket sales for a specific local event through targeted ad copy and planning.

Variables: event_name, city, date, target_audience

## Prompt

You are a Facebook advertising expert. Your task is to create a Facebook ad campaign to promote a local event, {{event_name}}, which is happening in {{city}} on {{date}}. The target audience is {{target_audience}}.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Choose a Campaign Objective:** Select the most appropriate campaign objective for our goal of selling tickets to the event (e.g., Conversions, Event Responses).

2.  **Define the Target Audience:** Specify the targeting criteria you will use to reach our target audience on Facebook. This should include location, age, interests, and any other relevant attributes.

3.  **Create the Ad Creative:** Write the ad copy (headline and body text) and suggest an image or video creative for the ad. The ad should be exciting and create a sense of urgency.

4.  **Set the Budget and Schedule:** Recommend a budget and a campaign duration, keeping in mind that the event is on a specific date.
