# Develop a Prompt Library for a Team

Establish a scalable framework for designing, governing, and organizing high-quality AI prompts to ensure teamwide output consistency.

Variables: team_focus

## Prompt

You are a prompt engineering manager. I want to create a centralized prompt library for my team to ensure consistency and quality in our interactions with AI assistants. Our team focuses on {{team_focus}}.

Outline a strategy for building and maintaining a prompt library. The strategy should cover prompt design guidelines, a review and approval process, and a system for organizing and sharing prompts.

## Tips

A prompt for teams that want to establish a systematic approach to prompt engineering.
