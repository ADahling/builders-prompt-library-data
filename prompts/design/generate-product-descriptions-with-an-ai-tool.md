# Generate Product Descriptions with an AI Tool

Identify optimal AI tools and draft customized copywriting prompts to create high-converting product descriptions for specific target audiences.

Variables: product_category, target_audience, selling_points

## Prompt

You are an e-commerce copywriter. I need to generate compelling product descriptions for a new line of products in the {{product_category}} category. The target audience is {{target_audience}} and the key selling points are {{selling_points}}.

Recommend an AI-powered writing tool and provide a prompt that I can use to generate high-quality product descriptions.

## Tips

A prompt for e-commerce professionals who want to use AI to write product descriptions.
