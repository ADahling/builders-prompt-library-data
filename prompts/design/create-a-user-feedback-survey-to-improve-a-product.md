# Create a User Feedback Survey to Improve a Product

Generate a comprehensive customer survey and engagement email to extract actionable qualitative and quantitative insights for product growth.

Variables: product_name

## Prompt

You are a product manager. Your task is to create a user feedback survey to gather insights from our existing customers on how we can improve our product, {{product_name}}.

**Instructions:**

1.  **Define the Survey Goals:** What are the key questions you want to answer with this survey? What are the most important areas of the product you want to get feedback on?

2.  **Write the Survey Questions:** Write a mix of multiple-choice, rating scale, and open-ended questions to gather both quantitative and qualitative feedback. The survey should not be too long (aim for 5-10 questions).

3.  **Design the Survey Invitation:** Write the copy for an email that we can use to invite our customers to take the survey. The email should be personal and explain why their feedback is important.

**Output Format:**

Please provide your response in a structured Markdown format with the following sections:

*   **User Feedback Survey**
    *   **Survey Goals:**
    *   **Survey Questions:**
*   **Survey Invitation Email:**
