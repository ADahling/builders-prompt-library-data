# Customize an AI Tool for a Specific Use Case

Design and implement a structured configuration strategy to tailor specific AI tools to your team's unique operational workflows.

Variables: tool_name, use_case_description, customization_options

## Prompt

You are an AI solutions architect. I need to customize {{tool_name}} to better fit my team's specific workflow for {{use_case_description}}. The tool offers the following customization options: {{customization_options}}.

Provide a step-by-step guide on how to customize the tool to meet our requirements. The guide should include best practices for customization and tips for maintaining the customized solution.

## Tips

A prompt for users who want to tailor an AI tool to their specific needs.
