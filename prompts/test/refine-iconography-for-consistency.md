# Refine Iconography for Consistency

Get an audit of icon style, size, and color inconsistencies on a page, plus a proposed icon style guide aligned with your branding.

Variables: page_name, icon_elements

## Prompt

Inspect the iconography used across the {{page_name}}. Identify inconsistencies in style, size, or color. Propose a cohesive icon style guide for {{icon_elements}}, ensuring all icons align with the overall design and branding.

## Tips

Consistent iconography enhances visual coherence.
