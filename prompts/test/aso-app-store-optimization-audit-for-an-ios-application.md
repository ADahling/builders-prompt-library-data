# ASO (App Store Optimization) Audit for an iOS Application

Conduct a comprehensive App Store Optimization audit to identify ranking weaknesses and receive a prioritized action plan for growth.

Variables: project_name, project_description

## Prompt

As an ASO specialist, I need you to perform a comprehensive ASO audit for our iOS application, `{{project_name}}`. The application is a {{project_description}} and our goal is to improve our visibility in the App Store and increase downloads.

Your audit should cover all aspects of ASO, including:

1.  **Keyword Research and Optimization:**
    *   Analyze the keywords we are currently targeting and identify opportunities for improvement.
    *   Research new keywords that we should be targeting.

2.  **App Title and Subtitle:**
    *   Analyze our app title and subtitle for keyword usage and effectiveness.

3.  **App Description:**
    *   Review our app description for clarity, persuasiveness, and keyword usage.

4.  **App Icon and Screenshots:**
    *   Assess the quality and effectiveness of our app icon and screenshots.

5.  **App Previews:**
    *   Review our app previews for quality and effectiveness.

6.  **Ratings and Reviews:**
    *   Analyze our ratings and reviews and provide recommendations for improving them.

**Output Format:**

Please provide an ASO audit report in a Markdown file with the following structure:

*   **ASO Health Score:** A summary of our app's overall ASO health, with scores for each of the audited areas.
*   **Audit Findings:** A detailed list of all identified issues, categorized by the areas mentioned above. For each issue, provide:
    *   **Issue ID:** A unique identifier.
    *   **Severity:** (High, Medium, Low)
    *   **Description:** A clear explanation of the issue and its impact on ASO.
    *   **Recommendation:** Specific and actionable steps to fix the issue.
*   **ASO Action Plan:** A prioritized list of recommendations to improve our app's ASO.
