# Design Clear Call-to-Action Buttons

Review a page's call-to-action button visibility and get suggested changes to color, size, and positioning to help users notice and interact with them.

Variables: page_name, button_elements

## Prompt

Review the call-to-action buttons on the {{page_name}}. Identify if they stand out enough for users to notice. Suggest design changes for {{button_elements}}, including color, size, and positioning, to enhance visibility and encourage user interaction.

## Tips

Prominent call-to-action buttons can significantly increase conversion rates.
