# UI/UX Audit for a Design System

Conduct a comprehensive heuristic evaluation and UI/UX audit of a design system to identify usability gaps and generate a structured roadmap.

Variables: design_system_name

## Prompt

As a UI/UX designer, I need to conduct a UI/UX audit for our design system, `{{design_system_name}}`. This design system is used by multiple teams across our organization, and we want to ensure that it is easy to use, consistent, and promotes a high-quality user experience.

Your audit should be based on a heuristic evaluation using Jakob Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics. For each heuristic, please evaluate the design system and provide a score from 1 to 5 (1 = major usability issue, 5 = no usability issues). In addition to the heuristic evaluation, your audit should also cover:

1.  **Visual Design and Branding:**
    *   Assess the consistency of the visual design, including colors, typography, and iconography.
    *   Evaluate how well the design reflects our brand identity.

2.  **Component Library:**
    *   Review the component library for completeness, consistency, and ease of use.
    *   Identify any missing components or variations.

3.  **Documentation:**
    *   Review the documentation for clarity, completeness, and accuracy.
    *   Ensure that the documentation provides clear examples of how to use the design system.

4.  **Onboarding and Support:**
    *   Evaluate the onboarding process for new users of the design system.
    *   Assess the effectiveness of the support channels for the design system.

**Output Format:**

Please provide a UI/UX audit report in a Markdown file with the following structure:

*   **Overall Usability Score:** An average score based on the heuristic evaluation.
*   **Heuristic Evaluation:** A detailed breakdown of the heuristic evaluation, with a score and comments for each heuristic.
*   **UI/UX Issues and Recommendations:** A prioritized list of all identified UI/UX issues. For each issue, provide:
    *   **Issue ID:** A unique identifier.
    *   **Severity:** (High, Medium, Low)
    *   **Description:** A clear explanation of the issue, with screenshots to illustrate the problem.
    *   **Recommendation:** A specific and actionable recommendation for fixing the issue, with mockups or wireframes if necessary.
*   **UI/UX Improvement Plan:** A high-level plan for improving the UI/UX of the design system.

Your report should provide our team with a clear understanding of the usability issues in our design system and a roadmap for addressing them.
