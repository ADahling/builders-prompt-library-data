# Refine Tooltip Content Clarity

Get a review of unclear or redundant tooltips on a page, with suggested content revisions for concise, informative user guidance.

Variables: page_name, tooltip_elements

## Prompt

Evaluate the content of tooltips used on the {{page_name}}. Identify any tooltips that provide unclear or redundant information. Suggest content revisions for {{tooltip_elements}} to ensure they are concise, informative, and effectively assist users.

## Tips

Clear and concise tooltips enhance usability and information delivery.
