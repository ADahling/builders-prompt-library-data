# Adjust Card Hover Effects

Review card hover feedback and get proposed styles, animations, or color changes for cards that need clearer interaction cues.

Variables: page_name, card_elements

## Prompt

Review the hover effects for the cards on the {{page_name}}. Determine if the current hover effects provide clear feedback to users. Propose new hover styles for {{card_elements}}, including animations or color changes, to enhance interactivity.

## Tips

Effective hover effects can engage users and encourage exploration.
