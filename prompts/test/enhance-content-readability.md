# Enhance Content Readability

Review a page's content layout for hard-to-read fonts, sizes, and colors, and get suggested changes to improve readability.

Variables: page_name, content_elements

## Prompt

Examine the content layout on the {{page_name}}. Identify any areas where the content may be difficult to read due to font choices, sizes, or colors. Propose changes to {{content_elements}} to enhance readability and ensure a pleasant reading experience.

## Tips

Readable content keeps users engaged and informed.
