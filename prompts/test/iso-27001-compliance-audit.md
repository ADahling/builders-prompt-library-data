# ISO 27001 Compliance Audit

Conduct a comprehensive ISO 27001 audit to identify security gaps and generate a prioritized roadmap for organizational compliance.

Variables: project_name, project_description

## Prompt

As a compliance officer, I need you to perform a compliance audit of our application, `{{project_name}}`, against the ISO 27001 standard. The application is a {{project_description}} and handles sensitive customer data. We need to ensure that our application and processes are fully compliant with the requirements of ISO 27001.

Your audit should be a comprehensive assessment of our compliance posture, covering all relevant aspects of the ISO 27001. This includes, but is not limited to:

1.  **Information Security Policies:**
    *   Review our information security policies to ensure they are aligned with the requirements of ISO 27001.

2.  **Organization of Information Security:**
    *   Assess our organizational structure and roles and responsibilities for information security.

3.  **Human Resource Security:**
    *   Review our HR security policies and procedures.

4.  **Asset Management:**
    *   Assess our asset management processes.

5.  **Access Control:**
    *   Review our access control policies and procedures.

6.  **Cryptography:**
    *   Assess our use of cryptography.

7.  **Physical and Environmental Security:**
    *   Review our physical and environmental security controls.

8.  **Operations Security:**
    *   Assess our operations security procedures.

9.  **Communications Security:**
    *   Review our communications security controls.

10. **System Acquisition, Development and Maintenance:**
    *   Assess our system acquisition, development, and maintenance processes.

11. **Supplier Relationships:**
    *   Review our supplier relationship management processes.

12. **Information Security Incident Management:**
    *   Assess our incident management processes.

13. **Information Security Aspects of Business Continuity Management:**
    *   Review our business continuity management processes.

14. **Compliance:**
    *   Assess our compliance with legal and contractual requirements.

**Output Format:**

Please provide a compliance audit report in a Markdown file with the following structure:

*   **Compliance Status:** A high-level overview of our compliance status, with a clear statement on whether we are compliant with ISO 27001.
*   **Audit Findings:** A detailed list of all identified compliance gaps and areas of non-compliance. For each finding, provide:
    *   **Finding ID:** A unique identifier.
    *   **Requirement:** The specific requirement of ISO 27001 that is not being met.
    *   **Description:** A clear explanation of the compliance gap.
    *   **Risk:** The potential risk to our organization as a result of the compliance gap.
    *   **Recommendation:** Specific and actionable steps to address the compliance gap.
*   **Compliance Roadmap:** A prioritized plan for achieving full compliance with ISO 270al.
