# Manual QA Checklist Before Launch

Get a checkable pre-launch QA list for your app covering pages, forms, auth, data persistence, mobile usability, and loading states.

## Prompt

Generate a manual QA checklist for [describe app]:

1. Every page loads without errors.
2. Every form submits successfully with valid data.
3. Every form shows clear errors with invalid data.
4. Every button does what it says.
5. Every link goes to the right destination.
6. Auth: signup, login, logout, password reset all work.
7. Protected pages redirect to login when not authenticated.
8. Data persists across page reloads and sessions.
9. Mobile: all pages usable at 375px width.
10. No console errors in production.
11. Loading states on all async operations.
12. Empty states on all lists/tables.
13. Favicon, page titles, and meta tags set.

Checkable list format.

## Tips

Check every item before publishing.
