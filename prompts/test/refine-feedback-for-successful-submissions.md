# Refine Feedback for Successful Submissions

Review form submission success feedback for clarity and encouragement of further interaction, with proposed changes to the messaging.

Variables: form_name, page_name, success_feedback

## Prompt

Evaluate the feedback provided after a successful submission on the {{form_name}} on {{page_name}}. Determine if the feedback is clear and encourages further interaction. Propose changes to {{success_feedback}} to enhance user satisfaction.

## Tips

Positive feedback encourages users to continue interacting with your site.
