# Refine Footer Item Organization

Get a review of confusing or hard-to-find footer items and suggested categories for reorganizing a page's footer.

Variables: page_name, footer_items

## Prompt

Review the organization of items in the footer on the {{page_name}}. Identify any items that may confuse users or be hard to find. Suggest reorganizing {{footer_items}} into clearer categories to enhance overall usability.

## Tips

A well-organized footer improves user experience.
