# Adjust Form Instruction Visibility

Get a review of unclear or hard-to-see form instructions, with design suggestions and CSS styles to make them more prominent.

Variables: form_name, instruction_elements

## Prompt

Evaluate the form instructions on the {{form_name}} for clarity and visibility. Identify any instructions that are not easily seen or understood. Suggest design changes to the {{instruction_elements}} to make them clearer and more prominent, including CSS styles.

## Tips

Clear form instructions can guide users to complete forms successfully.
