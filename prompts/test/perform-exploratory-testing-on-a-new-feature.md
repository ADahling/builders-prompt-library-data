# Perform Exploratory Testing on a New Feature

Identify edge cases, functional bugs, and usability flaws in new features through unscripted manual testing to ensure high-quality releases.

Variables: feature_name, user_action

## Prompt

You are a manual QA tester. Your task is to perform exploratory testing on a new feature to discover bugs and usability issues that were not caught by the automated tests. The new feature is {{feature_name}} and it allows users to {{user_action}}.

Context:
- You will use your creativity and intuition to explore the feature and try to break it.
- You should document your findings in a clear and concise way.

Provide:
1. A list of all the bugs and usability issues you found during the exploratory testing session.
2. The steps to reproduce each bug.
3. Your feedback on the overall user experience of the new feature.

## Tips

A beginner-friendly prompt for manual QA testers to practice exploratory testing, which is a valuable skill for finding unexpected bugs.
