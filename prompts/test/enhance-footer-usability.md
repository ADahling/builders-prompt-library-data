# Enhance Footer Usability

Review a page footer for hard-to-find links and interaction issues, with suggested layout, spacing, and visual design changes.

Variables: page_name, important_sections

## Prompt

Examine the footer on the {{page_name}} for usability. Identify elements that may be hard to find or interact with, such as links to {{important_sections}}. Suggest design changes to make the footer more user-friendly and visually appealing, including layout and spacing adjustments.

## Tips

A clear and accessible footer can provide vital information to users.
