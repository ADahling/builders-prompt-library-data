# Optimize Filter Usability

Get a review of content filters with suggestions for clearer labels and easier interactions when filtering feels confusing.

Variables: content_type, page_name, filter_elements

## Prompt

Review the filter options for the {{content_type}} on the {{page_name}}. Identify any usability issues, such as unclear labels or difficult interactions. Propose adjustments to {{filter_elements}} to enhance usability and clarity.

## Tips

Optimized filters improve content discoverability.
