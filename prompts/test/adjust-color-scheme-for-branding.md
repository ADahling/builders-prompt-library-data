# Adjust Color Scheme for Branding

Review a page's color scheme for mismatches with brand identity and get proposed color adjustments for selected brand elements.

Variables: page_name, brand_elements

## Prompt

Evaluate the color scheme used on the {{page_name}}. Identify any colors that may not align with the brand identity. Propose color adjustments for {{brand_elements}} to ensure a cohesive and recognizable brand presence.

## Tips

A consistent color scheme reinforces brand identity.
