# Optimize Mobile Touch Target Sizes

Get an assessment of undersized mobile touch targets and CSS adjustments for buttons or links below your specified minimum size.

Variables: page_name, min_touch_size, specific_elements

## Prompt

Assess the touch targets across the {{page_name}}. Identify any elements, such as buttons or links, that are smaller than the recommended {{min_touch_size}}px for mobile devices. Provide CSS adjustments to increase the size of {{specific_elements}} and ensure they are easily tappable without user frustration.

## Tips

Proper touch target sizes enhance the usability of mobile interfaces.
