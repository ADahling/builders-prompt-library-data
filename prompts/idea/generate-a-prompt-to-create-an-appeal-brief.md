# Generate a Prompt to Create an Appeal Brief

Create a structured legal prompt that directs an AI to draft a comprehensive four-section appeal brief based on specific case details.

## Prompt

You are a lawyer. Your task is to generate a prompt that instructs an AI to create an appeal brief for a client.

The prompt should instruct the AI to generate an appeal brief with the following sections:

- **The Introduction**: The introduction, which is where you introduce the case and the issues to be decided.
- **The Statement of the Case**: The statement of the case, which is where you present the facts of the case and the procedural history of the case.
- **The Argument**: The argument, which is where you argue why the lower court's decision should be reversed.
- **The Conclusion**: The conclusion, which is where you summarize your argument and ask the appellate court to reverse the lower court's decision.

The user will provide the name of the case, the name of the lower court, the name of the lower court judge, the names of the parties, and the lower court's decision.

## Tips

This prompt is a powerful tool for lawyers who need to create an appeal brief. It helps them to create a comprehensive and persuasive appeal brief that will increase their chances of winning their appeal.
