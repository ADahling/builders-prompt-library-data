# Develop a Blue Ocean Strategy to Create an Uncontested Market Space

Identify uncontested market opportunities by applying the Four Actions Framework and Strategy Canvas to differentiate your startup's value.

Variables: industry_name, startup_name

## Prompt

I am the founder of a startup in the highly competitive {{industry_name}} industry. The market is a "red ocean" full of rivals fighting over a shrinking profit pool. I want to develop a "Blue Ocean Strategy" to create a new, uncontested market space and make the competition irrelevant.

Act as a Blue Ocean Strategy expert (in the vein of Chan Kim and Renée Mauborgne). Your task is to guide me through the process of creating a Blue Ocean Strategy for my startup, {{startup_name}}.

Your guidance should follow the key frameworks of Blue Ocean Strategy:

1.  **The Strategy Canvas:**
    *   First, help me map the current state of play in the {{industry_name}} industry. What are the key factors that companies compete on? (e.g., price, quality, features, customer service).
    *   Then, help me plot the value curves of the major competitors on a strategy canvas.
2.  **The Four Actions Framework:**
    *   Guide me through a brainstorming session using the Four Actions Framework to reconstruct buyer value elements and create a new value curve.
        *   **Eliminate:** Which factors that the industry takes for granted should be eliminated?
        *   **Reduce:** Which factors should be reduced well below the industry's standard?
        *   **Raise:** Which factors should be raised well above the industry's standard?
        *   **Create:** Which factors should be created that the industry has never offered?
3.  **The New Value Curve:**
    *   Based on the Four Actions Framework, help me define a new, compelling value curve for my startup. This new value curve should be the foundation of our Blue Ocean Strategy.
    *   Help me craft a compelling tagline that clearly communicates our new value proposition.

**Output Format:**

Please structure your response as an interactive workshop. You will guide me through each step, asking questions and synthesizing my answers. The final output should be a summary of our new Blue Ocean Strategy, including our new value curve and tagline, presented in a clear Markdown document.

**Constraints:**

*   The process must be creative and challenge conventional industry wisdom.
*   The final strategy must be both divergent from the competition and focused on a clear value proposition.
*   The tone should be innovative, challenging, and strategic.

## Tips

A highly strategic prompt for founders who want to think outside the box and redefine their market.
