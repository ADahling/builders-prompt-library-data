# Implement the Lean Startup Methodology for a New Venture

Generate a structured, capital-efficient roadmap to validate startup hypotheses and iterate through the Build-Measure-Learn feedback loop.

Variables: startup_idea

## Prompt

I am an entrepreneur with an idea for a new tech startup, {{startup_idea}}. I want to avoid the common pitfall of building a product that nobody wants. I want to apply the Lean Startup methodology (as popularized by Eric Ries) to build my venture in a capital-efficient and customer-centric way.

Act as a Lean Startup coach. Your task is to provide me with a step-by-step roadmap for implementing the Build-Measure-Learn feedback loop.

The roadmap should cover:

1.  **Phase 1: Define a Leap-of-Faith Hypothesis:**
    *   Help me articulate my core business hypotheses using a "Lean Canvas" (a one-page business plan).
    *   Identify the riskiest assumptions that need to be tested first (both the value hypothesis and the growth hypothesis).
2.  **Phase 2: Build a Minimum Viable Product (MVP):**
    *   Guide me in defining the smallest possible MVP that I can build to test my riskiest assumption.
    *   Brainstorm different types of MVPs I could use, such as a landing page MVP, a "Wizard of Oz" MVP, or a concierge MVP.
3.  **Phase 3: Measure and Learn:**
    *   Help me define the key metrics and actionable analytics to track for my MVP.
    *   Explain the concept of "innovation accounting" and how to use cohort analysis to get a clear picture of our progress.
4.  **Phase 4: Pivot or Persevere:**
    *   Describe how to use the data from our MVP experiment to make a "pivot or persevere" decision.
    *   Provide examples of different types of pivots (e.g., zoom-in pivot, customer segment pivot, technology pivot).

**Output Format:**

Please generate a detailed roadmap in Markdown. The roadmap should be a practical guide that I can follow as I build my startup. Include links to relevant resources and templates (like the Lean Canvas).

**Constraints:**

*   The advice must be practical and actionable for a solo founder or small team.
*   The roadmap must emphasize speed and learning over perfection.
*   The tone should be encouraging, pragmatic, and focused on validated learning.
