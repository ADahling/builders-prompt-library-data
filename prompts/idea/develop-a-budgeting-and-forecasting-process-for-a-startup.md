# Develop a Budgeting and Forecasting Process for a Startup

Establish a lean financial framework for seed-stage startups to manage cash burn through annual budgets, rolling forecasts, and BvA reviews.

Variables: startup_name

## Prompt

I am the founder of a seed-stage startup, {{startup_name}}. We have just raised our first round of funding, and I need to establish a more formal budgeting and forecasting process to manage our cash burn and report to our new investors.

Act as a startup CFO. Your task is to create a lightweight budgeting and forecasting process for my startup.

The process should include:

1.  **The Annual Budget:**
    *   A guide to creating our first annual budget. This should be a collaborative process involving the whole leadership team.
    *   The budget should be a detailed financial plan for the year, including a hiring plan, a sales forecast, and an expense budget.

2.  **The Rolling Forecast:**
    *   Explain the concept of a rolling forecast. Instead of just having a static annual budget, we will update our forecast every month or every quarter.
    *   How do we create a 12-month rolling forecast for our P&L and cash flow?

3.  **The Budget vs. Actuals (BvA) Review:**
    *   At the end of each month, we will compare our actual financial results to our budget.
    *   Create a template for a monthly BvA report. The report should highlight the key variances and provide an explanation for why we were over or under budget.

4.  **The Tools:**
    *   What tools should we use for budgeting and forecasting? Can we start with Google Sheets or Excel, or should we use a dedicated financial planning and analysis (FP&A) tool?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the budgeting and forecasting process as a detailed guide in Markdown. The guide should be a practical playbook for a startup founder who is not a finance expert.

**Constraints:**

*   The process must be lightweight and appropriate for an early-stage startup.
*   The guide must be easy to understand and implement.
*   The tone should be that of a pragmatic and experienced startup CFO.

## Tips

Good financial hygiene is critical for any startup. This prompt provides a guide to establishing a solid budgeting and forecasting process.
