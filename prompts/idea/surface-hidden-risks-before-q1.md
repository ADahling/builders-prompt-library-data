# Surface Hidden Risks Before Q1

Surface the risks hiding in a quarter before it starts: key-person dependencies, cash flow, revenue concentration, and operational gaps.

## Prompt

Identify hidden risks going into Q1:

1. People: Key person dependencies, burnout, departures, skill gaps.
2. Financial: Cash flow, large expenses, revenue concentration, late receivables.
3. Technical: Tech debt, expiring licenses, infrastructure limits.
4. Market: Competitive threats, regulatory changes, customer concentration.
5. Operational: Single points of failure, vendor dependencies, compliance deadlines.
6. For each: likelihood, impact, mitigation, owner.
7. Top 5 to address in first two weeks of Q1.

Be thorough. Better to surface now.
