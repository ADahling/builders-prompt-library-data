# Design an Internal AI Assistant

Plan a small team's internal AI assistant with tasks, knowledge sources, guardrails, an interface, five prompt templates, rollout steps, and metrics.

## Prompt

Design a lightweight internal AI assistant for my [business/team]:

1. Purpose: 3-5 specific tasks it handles.
2. Knowledge Base: What docs/FAQs/data it needs. How to keep updated.
3. Guardrails: What it should never do.
4. Interface: Where people use it (Slack, Teams, web, email).
5. Prompt Templates: 5 ready-to-use templates for employees.
6. Rollout Plan: Introduction, training, feedback loop.
7. Success Metrics: How to measure time savings.

Keep simple and practical for a small team.
