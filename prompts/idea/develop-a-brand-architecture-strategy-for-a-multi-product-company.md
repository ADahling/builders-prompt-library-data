# Develop a Brand Architecture Strategy for a Multi-Product Company

Strategize and implement a cohesive brand architecture model to unify diverse product portfolios and streamline cross-promotional equity.

Variables: company_name, number_of_products, company_description

## Prompt

I am the CMO of {{company_name}}, a company that has grown through acquisitions and now has a portfolio of {{number_of_products}} products. Our brand architecture is confusing for customers, and there is little cross-promotion between our products. I need to develop a clear brand architecture strategy.

Act as a brand strategist. Your task is to guide me in choosing and implementing a brand architecture model for our company.

Your guidance should cover:

1.  **Brand Architecture Models Explained:**
    *   Explain the three main brand architecture models:
        *   **Branded House (e.g., Google, FedEx):** A strong master brand with descriptive sub-brands.
        *   **House of Brands (e.g., P&G, Unilever):** A portfolio of distinct, individual product brands.
        *   **Hybrid (e.g., Microsoft, Coca-Cola):** A mix of the two models.
    *   Discuss the pros and cons of each model.
2.  **Choosing the Right Model:**
    *   Based on our company's situation ({{company_description}}), recommend the most appropriate brand architecture model for us.
    *   Justify your recommendation, considering factors like our brand equity, target audiences, and long-term business strategy.
3.  **Implementation Plan:**
    *   Outline a phased plan for migrating to the new brand architecture. This should include:
        *   **Brand Naming & Identity:** A system for naming new products and aligning visual identities.
        *   **Brand Hierarchy:** A clear visual representation of the relationship between the corporate brand and the product brands.
        *   **Internal & External Communication:** A plan for communicating the new brand architecture to employees and customers.

**Output Format:**

Please generate a brand architecture strategy document in Markdown. The document should be a persuasive and practical guide for my leadership team.

**Constraints:**

*   The recommendation must be well-reasoned and tailored to our specific context.
*   The implementation plan must be realistic and consider the potential challenges of a brand migration.
*   The tone should be strategic, clear, and authoritative.

## Tips

A highly strategic prompt for marketing leaders in complex organizations with multiple products or brands.
