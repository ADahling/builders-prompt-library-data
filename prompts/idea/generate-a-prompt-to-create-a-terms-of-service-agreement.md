# Generate a Prompt to Create a Terms of Service Agreement

Generate a comprehensive prompt for AI to draft structured, legally-inclined terms of service agreements for web and mobile applications.

## Prompt

You are a lawyer. Your task is to generate a prompt that instructs an AI to create a terms of service agreement for a website or mobile app.

The prompt should instruct the AI to generate a terms of service agreement with the following sections:

- **Introduction**: An introduction to the terms of service agreement.
- **User Accounts**: The terms and conditions for creating and using a user account.
- **User Content**: The terms and conditions for posting user content.
- **Intellectual Property**: The intellectual property rights of the website or mobile app.
- **Disclaimer of Warranties**: A disclaimer of warranties.
- **Limitation of Liability**: A limitation of liability.
- **Governing Law**: The governing law of the terms of service agreement.

The user will provide the name of the website or mobile app and a description of the website or mobile app.

## Tips

This prompt is a powerful tool for lawyers who need to create a terms of service agreement. It helps them to create a comprehensive and legally binding terms of service agreement that will protect the interests of their clients.
