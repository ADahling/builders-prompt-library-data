# Develop a Freemium to Paid Conversion Strategy

Create a data-driven growth strategy to optimize upgrade triggers, lifecycle marketing, and in-app onboarding to boost freemium conversions.

Variables: product_name, number_of_free_users, conversion_rate

## Prompt

I am the Head of Growth for a SaaS product, {{product_name}}, that has a freemium model. We have a large base of {{number_of_free_users}} free users, but our conversion rate to our paid plan is a dismal {{conversion_rate}}%. I need to develop a strategy to increase this conversion rate.

Act as a growth marketing expert specializing in freemium models. Your task is to create a comprehensive strategy to convert more free users into paying customers.

The strategy should include:

1.  **Analyze User Behavior:**
    *   How can we identify the free users who are most likely to convert? What are the behavioral signals of purchase intent? (e.g., frequent usage, hitting a usage limit, using a specific feature).

2.  **Optimize the Upgrade Triggers:**
    *   Where in the product experience should we place the upgrade prompts? (e.g., when a user tries to access a paid feature, when they hit a usage quota).
    *   How can we make these upgrade prompts more persuasive and context-aware?

3.  **Lifecycle Email Marketing:**
    *   Design a series of automated lifecycle emails to nurture free users and educate them on the value of the paid plan.
    *   This should include a welcome series, a series that highlights the benefits of paid features, and a series that triggers when a user shows high engagement.

4.  **In-App Messaging & Onboarding:**
    *   How can we use in-app messages and guided tours to onboard free users effectively and lead them to the "aha!" moment where they experience the core value of the product?

5.  **Limited-Time Offers & Promotions:**
    *   Should we use discounts or limited-time offers to encourage conversion? If so, how can we do this without devaluing our product?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the conversion strategy as a detailed plan in Markdown. The plan should be full of actionable tactics and best practices for freemium conversion.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be data-driven and focused on user behavior.
*   The recommendations should be practical and easy to implement for a growth team.
*   The tone should be persuasive, analytical, and results-oriented.

## Tips

For any freemium business, converting free users to paid is the name of the game. This prompt provides a playbook for doing just that.
