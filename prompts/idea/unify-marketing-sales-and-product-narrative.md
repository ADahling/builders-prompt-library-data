# Unify Marketing, Sales, and Product Narrative

Audit what marketing, sales, and product each claim, then write the one core narrative all three teams build from.

## Prompt

Teams telling different stories. Unify:

1. Current Audit: What each team says. Where messages diverge.
2. Core Narrative: ONE story (3-4 paragraphs) all teams build from.
3. Messaging Framework: Key message, 3 supporting points, proof points.
4. Adaptation Guide: How each team adapts for their audience.
5. Differentiation: ONE thing no competitor can claim.
6. One-Pager: Reference document for all teams.
7. Consistency Check: Monthly review to catch drift.

Based on: [product, audience, current messaging].
