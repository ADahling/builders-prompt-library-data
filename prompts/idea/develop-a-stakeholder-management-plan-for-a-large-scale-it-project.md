# Develop a Stakeholder Management Plan for a Large-Scale IT Project

Generate a comprehensive stakeholder management plan for complex IT projects, featuring power/interest matrices and targeted communication.

Variables: project_name, project_description, list_of_departments

## Prompt

I am the project manager for a new IT project, {{project_name}}, which involves {{project_description}}. The project will impact multiple departments, including {{list_of_departments}}. We have a wide range of stakeholders with different levels of interest and influence.

Act as a senior project management consultant. Your task is to create a comprehensive stakeholder management plan to ensure the project's success. The plan should be a practical guide for me and my team.

The plan should include the following sections:

1.  **Stakeholder Identification:**
    *   Guide me in brainstorming a list of all potential stakeholders, both internal and external.
2.  **Stakeholder Analysis:**
    *   Create a stakeholder analysis matrix (Power/Interest Grid) to categorize stakeholders based on their level of power and interest in the project.
    *   For each stakeholder, define their key interests, expectations, and potential influence.
3.  **Communication Plan:**
    *   Develop a communication plan that specifies the frequency, channel, and content of communication for each stakeholder group.
    *   Provide templates for different types of communication, such as project status updates, meeting agendas, and risk notifications.
4.  **Engagement Strategy:**
    *   Recommend strategies for engaging with each stakeholder group to gain their support and manage their expectations.
    *   Create a plan for managing and resolving conflicts that may arise between stakeholders.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the stakeholder management plan as a detailed Markdown document. Use tables and matrices to visualize the stakeholder analysis and communication plan.

**Constraints:**

*   The plan must be tailored to a large-scale IT project with complex stakeholder relationships.
*   The recommendations should be actionable and easy to implement.
*   The tone should be professional, structured, and strategic.
