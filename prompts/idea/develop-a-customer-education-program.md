# Develop a Customer Education Program

Design a comprehensive customer education strategy and curriculum to accelerate B2B SaaS adoption, improve retention, and scale onboarding.

Variables: company_name, product_name

## Prompt

I am the Head of Customer Success at a B2B SaaS company, {{company_name}}. Our product, {{product_name}}, is powerful but has a steep learning curve. I believe that a strong customer education program could significantly improve our customer onboarding, adoption, and retention. I want to create a "{{company_name}} Academy".

Act as a customer education and instructional design expert. Your task is to create a plan for developing a customer education program.

The plan should cover:

1.  **Curriculum Design:**
    *   What are the key topics we need to teach our customers to be successful with our product?
    *   Structure the curriculum into a logical learning path, from beginner to advanced.

2.  **Content Formats:**
    *   Recommend a mix of content formats for our education program:
        *   **On-demand courses:** (e.g., video tutorials, interactive exercises).
        *   **Live training:** (e.g., webinars, workshops).
        *   **Written content:** (e.g., knowledge base articles, user guides).

3.  **The Learning Management System (LMS):**
    *   Should we use a dedicated LMS platform to host our academy? What are the key features to look for in an LMS?

4.  **Certification Program:**
    *   Should we offer a certification program for customers who complete our training? What are the benefits of doing this?

5.  **Promotion & Engagement:**
    *   How will we promote our academy to our customers and encourage them to participate?

6.  **Measuring Success:**
    *   How will we measure the impact of our customer education program on our business goals? (e.g., faster onboarding, higher feature adoption, lower churn).

**Output Format:**

Please generate the customer education program plan as a detailed and structured Markdown document. The plan should be a complete guide to building a successful customer academy.

**Constraints:**

*   The curriculum must be focused on helping customers achieve their desired outcomes, not just on teaching product features.
*   The recommendations should be practical and scalable.
*   The tone should be educational, strategic, and customer-centric.

## Tips

Customer education is a powerful lever for customer success. This prompt provides a framework for building a strategic customer education program.
