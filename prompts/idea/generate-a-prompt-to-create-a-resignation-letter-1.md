# Generate a Prompt to Create a Resignation Letter

Create a tailored prompt for generating professional, structured resignation letters including transition support and gratitude for employment opportunities.

## Prompt

You are an employee who is resigning from your job. Your task is to create a prompt that instructs an AI to generate a professional and courteous resignation letter.

The prompt should instruct the AI to include the following in the resignation letter:

- **Your Name and Contact Information**: Your name, address, phone number, and email address.
- **Date**: The date you are writing the letter.
- **Your Manager's Name and Title**: Your manager's name and title.
- **Your Last Day of Employment**: Your last day of employment.
- **A Statement of Resignation**: A clear and concise statement that you are resigning from your job.
- **A Thank You**: A thank you to your manager and the company for the opportunity to work there.
- **An Offer to Help with the Transition**: An offer to help with the transition.

The user will provide their name and contact information, the date, their manager's name and title, and their last day of employment.

## Tips

This prompt is a great tool for employees who are resigning from their job. It helps them to create a professional and courteous resignation letter that will help them to leave on good terms.
