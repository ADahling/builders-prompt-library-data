# Competitor Playbook Analyzer

Get a competitor map, feature and pricing comparisons, messaging analysis, and three strategic moves for your product or industry.

## Prompt

Analyze competitive landscape for [product/industry]:

1. Competitor Map: Top 5-7. Name, positioning, audience, pricing, differentiator.
2. Feature Comparison: Table vs top 3 across key features.
3. Messaging Analysis: How each describes themselves. What language resonates.
4. Pricing Intelligence: How they price, tiers, market gaps.
5. Weakness Spotting: Reviews, complaints, missing features. Opportunities.
6. Strategic Recommendations: 3 specific moves to make.
7. Monitoring Plan: What to watch (features, pricing, funding).
