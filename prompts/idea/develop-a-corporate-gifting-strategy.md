# Develop a Corporate Gifting Strategy

Design a personalized B2B gifting strategy that identifies key milestones, creative gift ideas, and ROI metrics to build client loyalty.

Variables: company_name

## Prompt

I am the Head of Marketing at a B2B company, {{company_name}}. I want to develop a corporate gifting strategy to build stronger relationships with our customers and prospects. I want to move beyond sending generic gift baskets and create a more thoughtful and memorable gifting experience.

Act as a corporate gifting expert. Your task is to create a corporate gifting strategy for my company.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **Gifting Occasions:**
    *   When should we send gifts? (e.g., to thank a new customer, to celebrate a customer milestone, to apologize for a service issue, to get a meeting with a key prospect).

2.  **The Gifts:**
    *   What kind of gifts should we send? Brainstorm a list of creative and personalized gift ideas that go beyond the usual swag. The gifts should be a reflection of our brand.

3.  **The Gifting Platform:**
    *   Should we use a corporate gifting platform (like Sendoso or Reachdesk) to automate the process of sending and tracking gifts?

4.  **The Budget:**
    *   How much should we budget for corporate gifting?

5.  **Measuring ROI:**
    *   How will we measure the return on investment (ROI) of our gifting strategy? (e.g., by tracking the impact on deal cycle times, customer retention, and brand sentiment).

**Output Format:**

Please generate the corporate gifting strategy as a detailed plan in Markdown. The plan should be a complete guide to using gifting as a powerful marketing and sales tool.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be thoughtful, personalized, and authentic.
*   The recommendations should be practical and scalable.
*   The tone should be creative, generous, and relationship-focused.

## Tips

Corporate gifting can be a powerful way to cut through the noise and build real relationships. This prompt provides a guide to doing it strategically.
