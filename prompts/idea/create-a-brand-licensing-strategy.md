# Create a Brand Licensing Strategy

Develop a comprehensive brand licensing roadmap that balances revenue growth with brand equity protection through structured category and partner selection.

Variables: brand_name

## Prompt

I am the owner of a well-known consumer brand, {{brand_name}}. Our brand has strong brand equity and a loyal following. I want to explore the possibility of licensing our brand to other companies to put on their products. I need to create a brand licensing strategy.

Act as a brand licensing expert. Your task is to create a brand licensing strategy for my brand.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **The Business Case for Licensing:**
    *   What are the potential benefits of brand licensing for us? (e.g., new revenue stream, increased brand awareness).
    *   What are the potential risks? (e.g., brand dilution, loss of control).

2.  **The Licensing Strategy:**
    *   **Product Categories:** What product categories should we license our brand into? The categories should be a good fit for our brand's image and values.
    *   **Ideal Licensee Profile:** What kind of companies should we partner with as our licensees?

3.  **The Licensing Agreement:**
    *   What are the key terms of a brand licensing agreement? This should include:
        *   **The Royalty Rate:** What percentage of sales will we receive as a royalty?
        *   **The Term:** How long will the agreement last?
        *   **The Territory:** What geographic regions does the agreement cover?
        *   **Quality Control:** How will we ensure that the licensed products meet our brand's quality standards?

4.  **Managing the Licensing Program:**
    *   How will we manage our licensing program and our relationships with our licensees?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the brand licensing strategy as a detailed and structured report in Markdown. The report should be a complete guide to launching and managing a successful brand licensing program.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be focused on protecting and enhancing our brand equity.
*   The recommendations must be commercially sound and legally prudent.
*   The tone should be strategic, professional, and brand-conscious.

## Tips

Brand licensing can be a lucrative business, but it's also fraught with risk. This prompt provides a framework for a strategic and disciplined approach to licensing.
