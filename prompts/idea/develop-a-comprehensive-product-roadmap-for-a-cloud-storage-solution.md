# Develop a Comprehensive Product Roadmap for a Cloud Storage Solution

Plan an enterprise cloud storage roadmap with short- and long-term frameworks, customer feedback strategies, and methods for adapting milestones.

Variables: enterprise_clients

## Prompt

Your task is to develop a comprehensive product roadmap for a cloud storage solution catering to {{enterprise_clients}}.

Context:
- The solution aims to provide secure and scalable storage options for enterprises.
- Aligning roadmap milestones with business objectives and technological advancements is crucial for success.

Provide:
1. Frameworks for structuring long-term and short-term roadmap components.
2. Strategies for incorporating customer feedback and market analysis into the roadmap.
3. Methods for adapting the roadmap based on changing customer needs, competitive landscape, and technology shifts.

## Tips

Utilize this prompt to create a roadmap that guides product development in alignment with enterprise clients' evolving requirements.
