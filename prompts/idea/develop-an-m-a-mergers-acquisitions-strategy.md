# Develop an M&A (Mergers & Acquisitions) Strategy

Construct a comprehensive M&A framework including strategic rationale, target scorecards, valuation methods, and post-merger integration plans.

Variables: company_name

## Prompt

I am the Head of Corporate Development at a large, publicly-traded company, {{company_name}}. We have a strong balance sheet and are looking to use M&A as a way to accelerate our growth. I need to develop a formal M&A strategy.

Act as an M&A advisor from a top investment bank. Your task is to create an M&A strategy for my company.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **The Strategic Rationale for M&A:**
    *   Why are we doing M&A? What are our goals? (e.g., acquire new technology, enter a new market, consolidate the industry, buy revenue).

2.  **The Acquisition Thesis:**
    *   What is our investment thesis? What types of companies are we looking to acquire? (e.g., what size, what industry, what business model).
    *   Create a scorecard with a set of criteria for evaluating potential acquisition targets.

3.  **The M&A Process:**
    *   Outline the end-to-end M&A process, from target identification and sourcing to due diligence, valuation, negotiation, and integration.

4.  **Valuation Methodology:**
    *   What valuation methodologies will we use to determine the price we are willing to pay for a target? (e.g., DCF, comparable company analysis, precedent transactions).

5.  **Due Diligence:**
    *   Create a comprehensive due diligence checklist covering all key areas: financial, legal, commercial, technology, and cultural.

6.  **Integration Strategy:**
    *   How will we approach post-merger integration? This is where most deals fail. What is our philosophy on integration?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the M&A strategy as a detailed and formal Markdown document. The document should be a complete guide to building a successful corporate M&A function.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be tightly aligned with our overall corporate strategy.
*   The advice must be financially sound and demonstrate a deep understanding of the M&A process.
*   The tone should be strategic, disciplined, and analytical.

## Tips

M&A is a high-stakes game. This prompt provides a framework for developing a disciplined and strategic approach to acquisitions.
