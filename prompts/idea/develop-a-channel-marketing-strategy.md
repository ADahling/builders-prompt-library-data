# Develop a Channel Marketing Strategy

Design a scalable B2B channel marketing plan to enable partners, manage MDF programs, and drive revenue through co-branded campaigns.

Variables: company_name, number_of_partners

## Prompt

I am the channel marketing manager at a B2B company, {{company_name}}. We have a network of {{number_of_partners}} channel partners who resell our product. I need to develop a channel marketing strategy to help our partners be more effective at marketing and selling our product.

Act as a channel marketing expert. Your task is to create a channel marketing strategy.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **Partner Enablement Content:**
    *   What marketing and sales content do we need to provide to our partners? (e.g., co-brandable data sheets, sales decks, email templates, case studies).

2.  **To-Partner Marketing:**
    *   How do we market *to* our partners to keep them engaged and informed? (e.g., a partner newsletter, a partner portal, partner-only webinars).

3.  **Through-Partner Marketing:**
    *   How do we help our partners market *through* to their end customers? This could include:
        *   **Marketing Development Funds (MDF):** A program to provide funding for our partners' marketing activities.
        *   **Co-branded Campaigns:** Running joint marketing campaigns with our top partners.
        *   **Turnkey Campaigns-in-a-Box:** Providing our partners with a complete, ready-to-run marketing campaign.

4.  **Partner Tiers:**
    *   Should we offer different levels of marketing support to different tiers of partners? (e.g., our Gold partners get more MDF and co-marketing support).

5.  **Measuring Success:**
    *   How will we measure the success of our channel marketing efforts? (e.g., partner engagement, partner-sourced pipeline, ROI of MDF).

**Output Format:**

Please generate the channel marketing strategy as a detailed and structured plan in Markdown. The plan should be a complete guide to building a successful channel marketing function.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be designed to make our partners successful.
*   The plan must be scalable and manageable.
*   The tone should be collaborative, supportive, and results-oriented.

## Tips

Channel marketing is a specialized discipline that is critical for any company with an indirect sales model. This prompt provides a framework for a comprehensive channel marketing strategy.
