# Optimize Budget for ROI

Review a budget line by line for next quarter: ROI per line, cut candidates, what to protect, and where to reinvest.

## Prompt

Current budget: [describe]. Optimize for next quarter:

1. Current ROI per budget line.
2. Cut Candidates: Lowest ROI or no longer justified.
3. Protect: Outsized returns, maintain or increase.
4. Reallocation: Where cut dollars generate most return.
5. New Investments: Unfunded areas deserving budget.
6. Risk: What happens if I cut each? Hidden dependencies?
7. Final Recommendation: Specific dollar amounts and expected impact.

Be specific with numbers.
