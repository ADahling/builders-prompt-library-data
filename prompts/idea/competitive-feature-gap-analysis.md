# Competitive Feature Gap Analysis

Get a competitor feature matrix, market gaps, MVP essentials, and positioning guidance for evaluating and differentiating an app idea.

## Prompt

Analyze the competitive landscape for [describe your app idea] and produce a feature gap analysis:

1. Competitors: List the top 5 competitors or alternatives. For each: name, URL, pricing, primary audience, core value proposition.
2. Feature Matrix: Create a table comparing features across all competitors and your planned product. Mark each as has it, partial, or missing.
3. Gaps & Opportunities: Identify 3-5 features or capabilities that NO competitor does well. These are your differentiation opportunities.
4. Table Stakes: Features every competitor has that you MUST include in your MVP (non-negotiable).
5. Positioning Statement: Based on the gaps, write a one-sentence positioning statement: "For [audience] who [need], [product] is the [category] that [differentiator], unlike [competitors] which [limitation]."
6. GTM Angle: How to message your differentiation in marketing copy and landing pages.

Be specific — use real competitor names and real features.

## Tips

Run before finalizing your PRD to ensure you're building something differentiated.
