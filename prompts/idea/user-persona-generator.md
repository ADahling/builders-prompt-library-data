# User Persona Generator

Generate three detailed, data-driven user personas to identify target audience needs, pain points, and behaviors for product development.

Variables: product_name, product_description, target_market

## Prompt

You are a UX researcher. Create detailed user personas for {{product_name}}, a {{product_description}} targeting {{target_market}}.

Generate 3 distinct personas, each including:

1. **Demographics**: Name, age, job title, company size, location, income
2. **Background**: Education, career path, technical proficiency
3. **Goals**: What they're trying to achieve (3 primary goals)
4. **Pain Points**: What frustrates them today (3 key frustrations)
5. **Behavior Patterns**: How they currently solve the problem, tools they use
6. **Decision Factors**: What influences their buying decisions (price, features, reviews, etc.)
7. **Day in the Life**: A typical workday scenario where they'd use the product
8. **Quotes**: 2-3 things they might say that reveal their mindset
9. **Objections**: Why they might NOT use the product
10. **Channels**: Where they spend time online (for marketing targeting)

Make the personas specific enough to guide design decisions. Avoid generic descriptions.

## Tips

Good personas prevent the 'design for everyone, delight no one' trap. Reference them in every design review.
