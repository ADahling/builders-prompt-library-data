# Develop a Downsell and Churn Prevention Strategy

Design a comprehensive B2B SaaS retention framework featuring tiered downsell offers, automated cancellation flows, and CSM scripts.

## Prompt

I am the Head of Customer Success at a B2B SaaS company. When our customers want to cancel their subscriptions, our only option is to let them go. I want to develop a more sophisticated strategy to prevent churn by offering a "downsell" option - a lower-cost plan that keeps them as a customer.

Act as a customer retention expert. Your task is to create a downsell and churn prevention strategy.

The strategy should include:

1.  **The Downsell Offer:**
    *   What should our downsell plan look like? It should be a significantly lower-cost plan with reduced features or usage limits.
    *   The goal is to provide a viable option for customers who are struggling with budget or who are not using all the features of their current plan.

2.  **The Cancellation Flow:**
    *   When a customer clicks the "cancel account" button, what should happen?
    *   Design a cancellation flow that first tries to understand why the customer is leaving (with a short survey) and then presents the downsell offer as an alternative to cancelling.

3.  **The Script for CSMs:**
    *   When a customer tells their Customer Success Manager (CSM) that they want to cancel, what should the CSM do?
    *   Provide a script for the CSM to use to probe for the reason for the cancellation and to offer the downsell plan.

4.  **The "Pause" Option:**
    *   Should we also offer an option to "pause" the subscription for a few months? When is this a good idea?

5.  **Re-engagement Strategy:**
    *   For customers who do downsell to the lower-cost plan, what is our strategy to re-engage them and eventually upsell them back to a higher plan?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the downsell and churn prevention strategy as a detailed plan in Markdown. The plan should include the design of the downsell offer, the cancellation flow, and the scripts for CSMs.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be customer-centric and designed to be helpful, not just to make it hard to cancel.
*   The downsell offer must be financially viable for the business.
*   The tone should be empathetic, proactive, and focused on retaining the customer relationship.

## Tips

A downsell strategy is a powerful tool for reducing churn and maximizing customer lifetime value. This prompt provides a guide to creating one.
