# Create a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

Generate a formal, legally sound performance improvement plan template featuring objective goals, support resources, and clear consequences.

Variables: company_name, employee_name, number_of_months

## Prompt

I am a manager at {{company_name}}. I have a team member, {{employee_name}}, who has been underperforming for the last {{number_of_months}}. I have had several informal conversations with them, but their performance has not improved. I now need to put them on a formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

Act as an experienced HR manager. Your task is to create a PIP for this employee. The PIP should be fair, clear, and legally sound.

The PIP document should include:

1.  **Employee Information:**
    *   Employee Name, Title, Department, Manager Name, Date.

2.  **Statement of Concern:**
    *   A clear and objective description of the performance issues. Provide specific, factual examples of where the employee has not met expectations. Reference the specific job responsibilities that are not being met.

3.  **Expected Level of Performance:**
    *   A clear description of the level of performance that is expected. What does "good" look like?

4.  **The Improvement Plan:**
    *   A set of specific, measurable, and time-bound goals that the employee must achieve during the PIP period (e.g., the next 30-60 days).
    *   A list of the support and resources that the company will provide to help the employee succeed (e.g., training, coaching, more frequent check-ins with the manager).

5.  **Consequences of Not Meeting the Plan:**
    *   A clear statement that failure to meet the requirements of the PIP may result in further disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.

6.  **Signatures:**
    *   A section for the employee and the manager to sign and date the document.

**Output Format:**

Please generate a professional and comprehensive PIP template in Markdown. The template should be easy for a manager to fill out.

**Constraints:**

*   The language in the PIP must be objective, factual, and non-emotional.
*   The plan must be designed to give the employee a genuine opportunity to improve.
*   The document must be legally sound and protect the company from potential legal risk.

## Tips

A PIP is a difficult but sometimes necessary management tool. This prompt provides a template for creating a fair and effective PIP.
