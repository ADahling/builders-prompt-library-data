# Create a Go-to-Market Strategy for a SaaS Product Entering a New Vertical

Develop a comprehensive, cross-functional go-to-market playbook to successfully launch and scale a horizontal SaaS product into a new vertical.

Variables: company_name, product_name, new_vertical

## Prompt

I am the Head of Marketing for a successful horizontal SaaS company, {{company_name}}. Our product, {{product_name}}, is used by a wide range of industries. We have identified the {{new_vertical}} industry as a major growth opportunity, and we want to create a dedicated go-to-market (GTM) strategy to win this vertical.

Act as a vertical marketing expert. Your task is to create a GTM strategy for entering the {{new_vertical}} industry.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **Market Analysis & ICP:**
    *   A deep dive into the {{new_vertical}} industry. What are the unique needs, challenges, and workflows of this industry?
    *   Define the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) and key personas within this vertical.

2.  **Product & Solution:**
    *   Do we need to build any vertical-specific features or integrations for our product?
    *   How do we package and message our product as a "solution" for the {{new_vertical}} industry?

3.  **Vertical-Specific Messaging & Content:**
    *   Develop a value proposition and key messaging that will resonate with the target audience in this vertical.
    *   Create a content marketing plan with topics and formats that are tailored to the vertical (e.g., case studies, whitepapers, webinars featuring industry experts).

4.  **Sales & Marketing Channels:**
    *   What are the most effective channels for reaching this vertical? (e.g., industry publications, trade shows, partnerships with industry associations).
    *   Should we build a dedicated vertical sales team?

5.  **Building Credibility:**
    *   How do we build credibility and be seen as an expert in this new vertical? (e.g., by getting our first few lighthouse customers and turning them into case studies).

**Output Format:**

Please generate the vertical GTM strategy as a detailed and structured plan in Markdown. The plan should be a complete playbook for dominating a new industry vertical.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be deeply tailored to the specific needs and language of the new vertical.
*   The plan must be a cross-functional effort, involving product, marketing, and sales.
*   The tone should be that of an industry insider who speaks the language of the customer.

## Tips

Winning a vertical requires a dedicated and focused GTM strategy. This prompt provides a framework for creating one.
