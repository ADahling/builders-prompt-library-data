# Develop a Pricing Strategy for a Tiered SaaS Product

Plan pricing for a three-tier B2B SaaS launch with value metrics, feature limits, competitor analysis, pricing models, and suggested price points.

Variables: product_name, target_customers, competitor_1, competitor_2

## Prompt

As the CEO of a startup launching a new B2B SaaS product, {{product_name}}, we need to define a robust pricing strategy. The product has three tiers: Basic, Pro, and Enterprise. Each tier offers a different level of features and support. Our target customers are {{target_customers}}.

I need you to act as a pricing consultant. Your task is to help me develop a comprehensive pricing strategy. Please guide me through the following steps:

1.  **Value Metric Identification:** Help me identify the core value metric for our product. What is the primary unit of value that our customers derive from our service? (e.g., per user, per project, per GB of storage).
2.  **Tiered Feature Definition:** For each tier (Basic, Pro, Enterprise), help me define the specific features and limitations. The goal is to create clear differentiation between the tiers and encourage upgrades.
3.  **Competitor Pricing Analysis:** Analyze the pricing of our main competitors ({{competitor_1}}, {{competitor_2}}). What are their pricing models and price points? How can we position our pricing relative to theirs?
4.  **Pricing Model Selection:** Recommend a pricing model (e.g., flat-rate, usage-based, per-user) and justify your recommendation based on our product and target market.
5.  **Price Point Determination:** Suggest specific price points for each tier. Provide a rationale for your suggestions, considering our costs, the value we provide, and the competitive landscape.

**Output Format:**

Please structure your response as a pricing strategy document in Markdown. The document should have clear sections for each of the steps above. For the final price points, present them in a table format.

**Constraints:**

*   The advice must be tailored to a B2B SaaS business model.
*   The strategy should be designed to maximize both customer acquisition and long-term revenue.
*   The final document should be a practical guide that I can present to my co-founders and investors.
