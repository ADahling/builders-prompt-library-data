# Develop a Financial Model for a Marketplace Business

Generate a comprehensive three-year financial projection and KPI dashboard for a two-sided marketplace to secure venture capital funding.

Variables: marketplace_name, provider_side, customer_side, take_rate

## Prompt

I am the founder of a new online marketplace, {{marketplace_name}}, that connects {{provider_side}} with {{customer_side}}. Our business model is to take a {{take_rate}}% commission on each transaction.

Act as a venture-backed marketplace founder and financial modeling expert. Your task is to create a 3-year financial projection model for my marketplace business.

The model needs to capture the unique dynamics of a two-sided marketplace:

1.  **Key Assumptions:**
    *   **Supply-Side Growth:** Monthly acquisition of new providers.
    *   **Demand-Side Growth:** Monthly acquisition of new customers.
    *   **Marketplace Liquidity:** Key assumptions about how supply and demand will interact. For example, what is the average number of transactions per customer per month? What is the average transaction value (ATV)?
    *   **Take Rate:** Our commission percentage.
    *   **CAC:** Separate Customer Acquisition Costs for both the supply side and the demand side.
    *   **Operating Expenses:** Salaries, marketing spend, technology costs, etc.

2.  **Financial Statements:**
    *   **Gross Merchandise Value (GMV):** A projection of the total value of all transactions flowing through the marketplace.
    *   **Net Revenue:** GMV multiplied by our take rate.
    *   **Income Statement (P&L):** Showing our path to profitability.
    *   **Cash Flow Statement:** Showing our cash burn and funding requirements.

3.  **Marketplace-Specific KPIs:**
    *   A dashboard that tracks critical marketplace metrics like:
        *   GMV Growth Rate
        *   Buyer-to-Seller Ratio
        *   LTV for both buyers and sellers
        *   Contribution Margin per transaction

**Output Format:**

Please generate the financial model as a detailed set of interconnected Markdown tables, clearly explaining the formulas and the logic behind them. The output should be a sophisticated model that I can use to pitch to investors.

**Constraints:**

*   The model must accurately reflect the two-sided nature of a marketplace business.
*   The formulas must be transparent and easy to follow.
*   The final output should be investor-ready.

## Tips

Marketplace models are notoriously complex. This prompt provides a structure for building a robust financial model that captures the key dynamics.
