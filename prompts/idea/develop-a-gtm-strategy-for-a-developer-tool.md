# Develop a GTM Strategy for a Developer Tool

Create a comprehensive developer-first go-to-market plan focused on bottom-up adoption, technical community building, and DevRel strategy.

Variables: tool_name, tool_functionality

## Prompt

I am the founder of a startup that has built a new developer tool, {{tool_name}}, which helps developers {{tool_functionality}}. I need to develop a go-to-market (GTM) strategy that is tailored to a developer audience.

Act as a developer marketing expert. Your task is to create a GTM strategy for my developer tool.

The strategy should be built around a developer-first, bottom-up adoption model:

1.  **Product & Documentation:**
    *   The product itself is the most important part of the marketing. How do we create an amazing developer experience?
    *   How do we create world-class documentation that is clear, comprehensive, and easy to use?

2.  **Community & Content:**
    *   Where do our target developers hang out online? (e.g., Hacker News, Reddit, Stack Overflow, specific Discords).
    *   What kind of content should we create to attract and educate developers? (e.g., tutorials, blog posts, technical deep dives).

3.  **Developer Relations (DevRel):**
    *   What is the role of a DevRel team? Should we hire developer advocates?
    *   How can we engage with the developer community in an authentic way? (e.g., speaking at conferences, contributing to open-source projects).

4.  **The Bottom-Up Funnel:**
    *   How do we get individual developers to try our tool?
    *   How do we then get them to adopt it within their teams and companies?
    *   This is the classic "land and expand" strategy.

5.  **Pricing & Packaging:**
    *   Should we have a free tier for individual developers?
    *   How should we price our team and enterprise plans?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the GTM strategy as a detailed plan in Markdown. The plan should be a complete guide to marketing and selling to developers.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be authentic and avoid traditional marketing tactics that don't work with a developer audience.
*   The plan must be built around a great product and a strong community.
*   The tone should be technical, helpful, and credible.

## Tips

Marketing to developers is a unique challenge. This prompt provides a playbook for a modern, developer-first GTM strategy.
