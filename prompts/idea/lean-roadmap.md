# Build a lean product roadmap

Get an outcome-based product roadmap with evidence targets, small tests, product work, owners, dependencies, and exit criteria.

## Prompt

Build a lean roadmap for {{product}} over {{timeframe}}. Organize work by outcome, not feature. For each outcome include evidence target, smallest test, product work, owner type, dependency, and exit criterion.
