# Develop a Compensation Philosophy and Strategy

Establish a comprehensive, market-aligned compensation framework and formal policy to ensure equitable pay and talent retention at scale.

Variables: startup_name

## Prompt

I am the Head of People at a fast-growing startup, {{startup_name}}. As we scale, I need to create a more formal compensation philosophy and strategy to ensure that we are paying our employees fairly and competitively.

Act as a compensation consultant. Your task is to help me create a compensation philosophy and strategy.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **The Compensation Philosophy:**
    *   A high-level statement that defines our approach to compensation. What is our goal? (e.g., to attract and retain the best talent). How do we want to position our pay relative to the market? (e.g., at the 75th percentile).

2.  **The Market Data:**
    *   Where will we get our market data to benchmark our salaries? (e.g., by using compensation survey data from providers like Radford or Levels.fyi).

3.  **The Compensation Structure:**
    *   **Job Leveling:** How will we define the different levels for our roles? (e.g., a career ladder with levels from 1 to 5).
    *   **Salary Bands:** For each role and level, we will create a salary band with a minimum, midpoint, and maximum. How do we create these bands?

4.  **The Components of Compensation:**
    *   What are the different components of our compensation package? This should include:
        *   **Base Salary:**
        *   **Variable Pay/Bonus:** (if applicable).
        *   **Equity:** (e.g., stock options). What is our equity philosophy?

5.  **Pay Transparency:**
    *   How transparent will we be about our compensation strategy? Will we share the salary bands with our employees?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the compensation philosophy and strategy as a detailed and formal Markdown document. The document should be a complete guide to our company's approach to compensation.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be fair, equitable, and competitive.
*   The process for determining pay must be clear and transparent.
*   The tone should be professional, thoughtful, and fair.

## Tips

A clear compensation strategy is essential for scaling a company and attracting top talent. This prompt provides a framework for creating one.
