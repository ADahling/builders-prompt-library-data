# Develop a Technology and Intellectual Property (IP) Strategy

Formulate a comprehensive strategy to protect, audit, and monetize deep-tech innovations through patents, trade secrets, and FTO analysis.

Variables: startup_name, technology_description

## Prompt

I am the CTO of a deep-tech startup, {{startup_name}}, that has developed a novel {{technology_description}}. Our technology is our core competitive advantage, and I need to develop a strategy to protect and leverage it as a strategic asset.

Act as an IP strategist and patent lawyer. Your task is to help me create a comprehensive technology and intellectual property (IP) strategy.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **IP Audit:**
    *   Guide me in conducting an audit of our current technology to identify all potential forms of IP we are creating (e.g., inventions, code, data, brand names, designs).

2.  **IP Protection Strategy:**
    *   For each type of IP, recommend the best form of protection:
        *   **Patents:** For novel inventions. What is the process and cost of filing a patent?
        *   **Copyrights:** For our source code, documentation, and marketing materials.
        *   **Trademarks:** For our company name, product names, and logos.
        *   **Trade Secrets:** For confidential information (like algorithms or customer lists) that we don't want to disclose publicly.
    *   Discuss the pros and cons of each form of protection.

3.  **Freedom to Operate (FTO) Analysis:**
    *   Explain the importance of conducting an FTO analysis to ensure that our technology does not infringe on the existing patents of other companies.

4.  **IP as a Business Asset:**
    *   How can we use our IP to create business value beyond just protection? Discuss options like:
        *   **Licensing:** Licensing our technology to other companies for a royalty.
        *   **Strategic Partnerships:** Using our IP as a bargaining chip in partnership negotiations.
        *   **Fundraising:** Using our patent portfolio to make our company more attractive to investors.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the IP strategy as a detailed report in Markdown. The report should be a practical guide for a technology startup to manage its most valuable assets.

**Constraints:**

*   The advice must be legally sound but also commercially pragmatic.
*   The strategy must be tailored to a deep-tech startup where IP is central to the business.
*   The tone should be expert, strategic, and forward-looking.

## Tips

For any tech startup, IP is a critical asset. This prompt provides a comprehensive framework for developing a strategy to protect and leverage it.
