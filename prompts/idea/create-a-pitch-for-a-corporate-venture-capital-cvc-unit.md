# Create a Pitch for a Corporate Venture Capital (CVC) Unit

Generate a comprehensive strategic pitch deck outline to secure executive approval and funding for a new Corporate Venture Capital unit.

Variables: large_corporation_name, industry

## Prompt

I am a senior innovation leader at {{large_corporation_name}}, a large company in the {{industry}} industry. I want to propose the creation of a new Corporate Venture Capital (CVC) unit. I need to create a compelling pitch to our CEO and board of directors to get approval and funding for this initiative.

Act as a CVC expert and corporate strategist. Your task is to help me build the internal pitch deck for creating a CVC unit.

The pitch deck should have the following slides:

1.  **The Strategic Imperative:** Why do we need a CVC now? (e.g., to combat disruption, to access external innovation, to accelerate our digital transformation).
2.  **What is CVC?** A brief, educational slide explaining what CVC is and how it differs from traditional VC.
3.  **The Strategic Goals of Our CVC:** What are the primary objectives of our CVC? (e.g., financial returns, strategic insights into new technologies, potential M&A pipeline, partnerships with startups).
4.  **Investment Thesis:** What is our investment thesis? (e.g., what stages, sectors, and geographies will we invest in? What is our check size?). The thesis must be tightly aligned with our corporate strategy.
5.  **The CVC Model:** Propose a model for our CVC. Will it be a separate legal entity? How will the investment team be structured? How will the investment committee be composed?
6.  **The Team:** Who will lead the CVC? What is the ideal profile for our CVC investment professionals?
7.  **The Financial Commitment:** What is the proposed fund size for the first 3-5 years? What is the expected annual operating budget?
8.  **Measuring Success:** How will we measure the success of the CVC? (This should be a mix of financial metrics like IRR and strategic metrics like the number of successful partnerships).
9.  **The Ask:** A clear summary of what you are asking for (e.g., approval to form the CVC, a specific capital commitment).

**Output Format:**

Please generate a detailed outline for the pitch deck in Markdown. For each slide, provide the key talking points and the key visuals or data to include.

**Constraints:**

*   The pitch must be framed in the language of corporate strategy, not just venture capital.
*   The proposal must be financially sound and demonstrate a clear understanding of the risks and rewards of CVC.
*   The tone should be visionary, yet pragmatic and credible.

## Tips

Launching a CVC is a major strategic move for a large corporation. This prompt provides a comprehensive guide to building the internal case for it.
