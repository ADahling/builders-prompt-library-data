# Create a 'Voice of the Customer' (VoC) Program

Plan a Voice of the Customer program with multichannel feedback collection, analysis methods, actionable insights, and a monthly report template.

Variables: company_name

## Prompt

As the Director of Customer Experience at {{company_name}}, I want to establish a formal 'Voice of the Customer' (VoC) program to systematically gather, analyze, and act on customer feedback. Our goal is to make customer feedback a central driver of our product development and business strategy.

Act as a VoC program expert. Your task is to create a comprehensive plan for designing and launching our VoC program.

The plan should include:

1.  **Feedback Collection Strategy:**
    *   Recommend a multi-channel strategy for collecting customer feedback. This should include a mix of:
        *   **Surveys:** (e.g., Net Promoter Score (NPS), Customer Satisfaction (CSAT), Customer Effort Score (CES)).
        *   **Direct Feedback:** (e.g., user interviews, focus groups, feedback forums).
        *   **Indirect Feedback:** (e.g., social media listening, online review monitoring).
        *   **Internal Feedback:** (e.g., insights from sales, customer support, and success teams).
2.  **Feedback Analysis & Synthesis:**
    *   Suggest a process and tools for aggregating and analyzing all the feedback from these different sources.
    *   How can we use techniques like sentiment analysis and topic modeling to identify key themes and trends?
3.  **Insight Generation & Reporting:**
    *   How do we turn the raw feedback into actionable insights?
    *   Create a template for a monthly VoC report that can be shared with the leadership team. The report should highlight key findings, customer quotes, and prioritized recommendations.
4.  **Closing the Loop:**
    *   Outline a process for "closing the loop" with customers who provide feedback. How do we let them know their voice has been heard and what actions we are taking?
5.  **Governance & Ownership:**
    *   Define the roles and responsibilities for the VoC program. Who is responsible for collecting, analyzing, and acting on the feedback?

**Output Format:**

Please generate a detailed VoC program plan in Markdown. The plan should be a practical guide that I can use to get buy-in from my leadership team and launch the program.

**Constraints:**

*   The program design must be scalable and sustainable.
*   The focus should be on driving action, not just collecting data.
*   The tone should be customer-centric, data-driven, and strategic.

## Tips

This prompt provides a structured approach for companies that want to become more customer-centric by systematically listening to and acting on customer feedback.
