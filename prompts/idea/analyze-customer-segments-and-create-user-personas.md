# Analyze Customer Segments and Create User Personas

Turn e-commerce customer CSV data into named segments and detailed personas with demographics, motivations, pain points, and contact preferences.

Variables: company_name, products, csv_columns

## Prompt

As the Head of Marketing for a growing e-commerce business, {{company_name}}, I need to gain a deeper understanding of our customer base to personalize our marketing campaigns and improve our product offerings. We sell {{products}} online.

I need you to act as a data analyst and marketing strategist. Your task is to analyze our customer data and create detailed user personas. I will provide you with a CSV file containing the following data for our customers: {{csv_columns}}.

Your process should be as follows:

1.  **Data Analysis:** Analyze the provided customer data to identify distinct customer segments based on demographics, purchase history, and behavior.
2.  **Segment Identification:** Describe the key characteristics of each customer segment you identify. Give each segment a descriptive name (e.g., 'Budget-Conscious Students', 'High-Value Loyalists').
3.  **Persona Creation:** For each customer segment, create a detailed user persona. Each persona should include:
    *   A fictional name and photo (you can use a placeholder for the photo).
    *   Demographics (age, location, occupation, income).
    *   Goals & Motivations (what are they trying to achieve with our products?).
    *   Pain Points & Frustrations (what challenges do they face?).
    *   Communication Preferences (how do they prefer to be contacted?).
    *   A brief narrative that brings the persona to life.

**Output Format:**

Please provide a report in Markdown format. The report should first describe the customer segments you identified. Then, for each segment, present the detailed user persona in a structured format.

**Constraints:**

*   You will need to ask me to provide the CSV data.
*   The personas should be based on the data analysis, not just generic archetypes.
*   The final output should be a practical tool that my team can use to guide their marketing and product development efforts.

## Tips

This is an advanced prompt that requires the user to provide data, and the AI to perform analysis and synthesis to create data-driven personas.
