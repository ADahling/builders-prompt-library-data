# Develop a Leadership Development Program

Design a comprehensive, multi-month blended learning curriculum to transition individual contributors into effective people managers.

Variables: company_name

## Prompt

I am the VP of People at a fast-growing company, {{company_name}}. We are promoting a lot of individual contributors into management roles for the first time, and many of them are struggling. I need to create a leadership development program to equip our new managers with the skills they need to be successful.

Act as a leadership development and HR consultant. Your task is to design a leadership development program for new managers.

The program should be a multi-month program that includes a mix of training, coaching, and practical application. The curriculum should cover key management skills:

1.  **The Manager's Role & Mindset Shift:**
    *   The transition from individual contributor to manager.

2.  **Core Management Skills:**
    *   **Setting Goals & Expectations:** (e.g., how to set SMART goals).
    *   **Delegating Effectively:**
    *   **Giving & Receiving Feedback:**
    *   **Coaching & Developing Your Team:**
    *   **Running Effective 1:1s and Team Meetings:**

3.  **Hiring & Onboarding:**
    *   How to interview and hire the right people.
    *   How to onboard new team members effectively.

4.  **Performance Management:**
    *   How to handle underperformance and have difficult conversations.

5.  **Leading with Emotional Intelligence:**
    *   Self-awareness, empathy, and communication skills.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the leadership development program as a detailed curriculum in Markdown. For each module, provide the learning objectives, the key topics to be covered, and suggested activities or exercises.

**Constraints:**

*   The program must be practical and focused on real-world management challenges.
*   The program should be a blended learning experience, not just a series of lectures.
*   The tone should be supportive, practical, and empowering.

## Tips

Investing in new managers is one of the highest-leverage things a company can do. This prompt provides a template for a comprehensive leadership development program.
