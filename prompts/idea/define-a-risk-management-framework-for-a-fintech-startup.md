# Define a Risk Management Framework for a Fintech Startup

Design a fintech risk framework with risk categories, impact and likelihood scoring, early warning indicators, and mitigation controls.

Variables: startup_name, fintech_service

## Prompt

As the Chief Risk Officer of a new Fintech startup, {{startup_name}}, that offers {{fintech_service}}, I need to establish a comprehensive risk management framework. We are operating in a highly regulated environment and need to be prepared for a variety of risks.

Act as a seasoned risk management professional with deep experience in the financial services industry. Your task is to create a risk management framework tailored to our Fintech business.

The framework should include:

1.  **Risk Universe Identification:**
    *   Guide me in identifying the full range of potential risks across different categories:
        *   **Credit Risk:** (e.g., default risk from borrowers)
        *   **Market Risk:** (e.g., interest rate risk, currency risk)
        *   **Operational Risk:** (e.g., fraud, technology failure, cyber-attacks)
        *   **Compliance & Regulatory Risk:** (e.g., AML/KYC regulations, data privacy laws)
        *   **Strategic Risk:** (e.g., competition, business model disruption)

2.  **Risk Assessment & Measurement:**
    *   For each identified risk, create a system for assessing its potential impact and likelihood. This could be a risk matrix (e.g., a 5x5 grid).
    *   Suggest key risk indicators (KRIs) that we can track to get early warnings of increasing risk levels.

3.  **Risk Mitigation & Control:**
    *   For our top risks, brainstorm specific mitigation strategies and internal controls. For example, for operational risk, this could include implementing multi-factor authentication and regular security audits.

4.  **Risk Governance & Reporting:**
    *   Define the roles and responsibilities for risk management across the organization.
    *   Create a template for a quarterly risk report to be presented to the board of directors.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the risk management framework as a detailed and structured Markdown document. The document should be a practical guide that we can implement immediately.

**Constraints:**

*   The framework must be specific to a Fintech company and the unique risks it faces.
*   The advice must be practical and actionable for a startup.
*   The tone should be thorough, prudent, and authoritative.

## Tips

Essential for any Fintech company. This prompt helps create a robust framework to manage the significant risks inherent in the financial industry.
