# Craft a Product Roadmap for a Sustainability Tracking App

Plan a sustainability app roadmap with feature and visualization frameworks, activist feedback strategies, and adaptation to policy changes.

Variables: environmental_activists

## Prompt

Craft a strategic product roadmap for a sustainability tracking app that aims to empower {{environmental_activists}} with real-time eco-friendly data insights.

Context:
- The app integrates data analytics and sustainability metrics to drive informed environmental actions.
- Aligning roadmap milestones with environmental advocacy trends and user needs is essential for app relevance.

Provide:
1. Frameworks for structuring sustainability features and data visualization tools in the roadmap.
2. Strategies for incorporating feedback from environmental activists to enhance data accuracy and usability.
3. Methods for adapting the roadmap to address emerging environmental challenges and policy changes.

## Tips

Utilize this prompt to create a roadmap that supports environmental activism and fosters sustainable behavioral change through technology.
