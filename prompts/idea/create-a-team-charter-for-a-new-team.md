# Create a Team Charter for a New Team

Generate a collaborative workshop template for establishing a new team's mission, goals, roles, and communication norms in a shared document.

Variables: team_name, number_of_people, team_mission

## Prompt

I am the manager of a newly formed team, the {{team_name}} team. The team is made up of {{number_of_people}} people from different departments, and our mission is to {{team_mission}}. I want to create a team charter to align the team on our purpose, goals, and ways of working.

Act as a team effectiveness coach. Your task is to help me create a team charter.

The team charter should be a document that is created and agreed upon by the whole team. It should include:

1.  **Our Purpose & Mission:**
    *   Why does our team exist? What is our unique contribution to the company?

2.  **Our Goals & Metrics:**
    *   What are our specific, measurable goals for the next quarter/year? How will we measure success?

3.  **Our Roles & Responsibilities:**
    *   Who is on the team? What is each person's role and responsibility?

4.  **Our Ways of Working (Team Norms):**
    *   How will we work together? This should cover:
        *   **Communication:** How will we communicate with each other? (e.g., Slack for quick questions, email for formal updates).
        *   **Meetings:** What is our meeting cadence? How will we run our meetings?
        *   **Decision-Making:** How will we make decisions? (e.g., consensus, majority vote, manager decides).
        *   **Giving Feedback:** How will we give each other feedback?

5.  **Our Stakeholders:**
    *   Who are our key stakeholders outside the team? How will we communicate and collaborate with them?

**Output Format:**

Please generate a team charter template in Markdown. The template should be a set of questions and prompts that I can use to facilitate a team chartering workshop with my new team.

**Constraints:**

*   The process of creating the charter should be collaborative and inclusive.
*   The charter should be a living document that the team can refer to and update over time.
*   The tone should be collaborative, clarifying, and empowering.

## Tips

A team charter is a powerful tool for aligning a new team and setting it up for success. This prompt provides a template for creating one.
