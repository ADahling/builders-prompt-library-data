# Create a Go-to-Market Strategy for a B2C Mobile App

Develop a comprehensive, three-phase go-to-market strategy to launch a mobile app and drive viral growth on a startup budget.

Variables: app_name, app_functionality, target_audience, launch_quarter, social_media_platform_1, social_media_platform_2

## Prompt

I am the founder of a new B2C mobile app, {{app_name}}, which helps users {{app_functionality}}. Our target audience is {{target_audience}}. We are planning to launch on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store in {{launch_quarter}}.

Act as a mobile app marketing expert. Your task is to create a comprehensive Go-to-Market (GTM) strategy for our app launch.

The GTM plan should cover the entire launch lifecycle:

1.  **Pre-Launch (Building Buzz):**
    *   **Landing Page:** What should be on our pre-launch landing page to capture email sign-ups?
    *   **Social Media:** How can we start building a community on platforms like {{social_media_platform_1}} and {{social_media_platform_2}} before the app is live?
    *   **PR & Influencers:** How do we identify and pitch to relevant journalists and influencers in our niche to get early coverage?

2.  **Launch Day (Making a Splash):**
    *   **App Store Optimization (ASO):** What are the key elements of ASO (app name, icon, keywords, screenshots) that we need to nail for both the App Store and Google Play?
    *   **Launch Day Promotions:** What tactics can we use to drive a surge of downloads on launch day (e.g., a Product Hunt launch, a limited-time offer for early users)?

3.  **Post-Launch (Sustaining Growth):**
    *   **User Acquisition:** What are the most effective paid user acquisition channels for a B2C app (e.g., Apple Search Ads, Facebook Ads, TikTok Ads)?
    *   **Engagement & Retention:** What strategies should we use to keep users coming back (e.g., push notifications, in-app messaging, email marketing)?
    *   **Referral Program:** How can we design a viral loop or referral program to encourage users to invite their friends?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the GTM strategy as a detailed, phased plan in Markdown. The plan should be full of actionable tactics and creative ideas.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be tailored to a B2C mobile app.
*   The recommendations should be practical for a startup with a limited marketing budget.
*   The tone should be energetic, creative, and results-driven.
