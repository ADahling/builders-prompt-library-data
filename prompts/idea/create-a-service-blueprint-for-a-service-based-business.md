# Create a Service Blueprint for a Service-Based Business

Map the end-to-end customer journey and internal operations into a structured service blueprint to optimize efficiency and experience.

Variables: business_name, service_description

## Prompt

I am the owner of a service-based business, {{business_name}}, that provides {{service_description}}. I want to map out our entire service delivery process to improve its quality, efficiency, and customer experience. I want to use a service blueprint for this.

Act as a service design consultant. Your task is to guide me in creating a service blueprint for my business.

The blueprint should be a diagram that visualizes the service delivery process from the customer's perspective. It should include the following lanes:

1.  **Customer Actions:**
    *   The step-by-step actions that a customer takes when interacting with our service.

2.  **Onstage (Frontstage) Actions:**
    *   The actions and interactions that are visible to the customer. This includes interactions with our employees (e.g., a phone call) or our technology (e.g., our website).

3.  **Backstage Actions:**
    *   The actions that our employees take behind the scenes to support the onstage actions. These are invisible to the customer.

4.  **Support Processes:**
    *   The internal processes and systems that support our employees in delivering the service.

5.  **Physical Evidence:**
    *   The tangible elements that the customer is exposed to during the service (e.g., our office, our website, our invoices).

**Output Format:**

Please generate a service blueprint template as a Markdown table. The columns should represent the stages of the service (e.g., Discovery, Booking, Service Delivery, Follow-up), and the rows should be the lanes described above. The template should be pre-filled with questions to guide me in mapping out my service.

After the template, provide a brief analysis of how to use the completed blueprint to identify pain points (e.g., where the customer journey breaks down) and opportunities for innovation.

**Constraints:**

*   The blueprint must provide a holistic view of the service, connecting the customer experience to the underlying operational processes.
*   The template must be easy to use for a non-designer.
*   The tone should be analytical, customer-centric, and process-oriented.

## Tips

A service blueprint is like a customer journey map but with the added dimension of the operational processes that deliver the experience. It's a powerful tool for service businesses.
