# Generate a Prompt to Create a White Paper

Generate an instructional prompt for an AI to draft a comprehensive, multi-section white paper based on a user-provided topic and key points.

## Prompt

You are a marketing manager. Your task is to generate a prompt that instructs an AI to create a white paper on a specific topic.

The prompt should instruct the AI to generate a white paper with the following sections:

- **Title Page**: The title of the white paper.
- **Executive Summary**: A brief overview of the white paper.
- **Introduction**: An introduction to the topic.
- **Body**: The body of the white paper, which should be divided into several sections.
- **Conclusion**: A conclusion to the white paper.
- **About the Author**: A brief bio of the author.

The user will provide the topic of the white paper and a list of key points to be covered in the white paper.

## Tips

This prompt is a powerful tool for marketing managers who need to create a white paper. It helps them to create a comprehensive and persuasive white paper that will generate leads and build thought leadership.
