# Develop a Turnaround Strategy for a Struggling Business

Get a phased 100-day turnaround plan for a struggling business, covering immediate triage, communication, strategic review, and cost restructuring.

Variables: company_name, industry

## Prompt

I have been hired as the new CEO of {{company_name}}, a company that was once a market leader in the {{industry}} industry but is now struggling with declining revenues, eroding margins, and low employee morale.

Act as a seasoned turnaround management consultant. Your task is to create a 100-day turnaround plan to stabilize the business and set it on a path to recovery.

The plan should be structured in three phases:

1.  **Phase 1: Stabilize (Days 1-30):**
    *   **Triage:** What are the immediate actions we need to take to stop the bleeding? (e.g., secure short-term financing, implement a hiring freeze, cut non-essential spending).
    *   **Communication:** How do we communicate with employees, customers, and investors to restore confidence?
    *   **Leadership:** How do we establish a new leadership team and governance structure?

2.  **Phase 2: Reset (Days 31-70):**
    *   **Strategic Review:** Conduct a rapid assessment of the business to identify the root causes of the decline. What are our core strengths that we can build on? Which parts of the business are non-core and should be divested?
    *   **Cost Restructuring:** Implement a more significant cost reduction program. This may include layoffs, facility closures, or renegotiating contracts with suppliers.
    *   **Revenue Generation:** Launch quick-win initiatives to generate revenue, such as a pricing review or a promotional campaign for our core products.

3.  **Phase 3: Grow (Days 71-100 and beyond):**
    *   **New Strategic Plan:** Develop a new, focused strategic plan for the business. What is our new vision and value proposition?
    *   **Investment:** Reallocate capital to the most promising growth areas.
    *   **Culture Change:** Launch initiatives to rebuild the company culture around performance, accountability, and innovation.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the 100-day turnaround plan as a detailed, phased plan in Markdown. The plan should be a high-impact, action-oriented guide for a new CEO in a crisis situation.

**Constraints:**

*   The plan must be bold and decisive.
*   The recommendations must be practical and executable in a short timeframe.
*   The tone should be that of a calm, experienced leader who is in control of the situation.

## Tips

A high-stakes prompt for a very challenging business situation. This provides a structured and proven approach to corporate turnarounds.
