# Develop a Community-Led Growth Strategy

Create a comprehensive community-led growth plan to drive B2B SaaS retention, product innovation, and cost-effective customer acquisition.

Variables: company_name, target_audience

## Prompt

I am the founder of a B2B SaaS company, {{company_name}}, that sells a product for {{target_audience}}. I believe that building a strong community around our product could be a powerful engine for growth. I want to develop a community-led growth (CLG) strategy.

Act as a community-led growth expert. Your task is to create a strategy for building and leveraging a community to drive our business.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **The Business Case for Community:**
    *   How will a community help us achieve our business goals? (e.g., reduce customer acquisition costs, increase retention, drive product innovation).

2.  **The Community Platform:**
    *   Where will our community live? (e.g., a Slack group, a Discourse forum, a dedicated community platform like Circle or Tribe).
    *   What are the pros and cons of each option?

3.  **Community Content & Programming:**
    *   What kind of content and programming will we create to engage our community members? (e.g., expert Q&As, member spotlights, virtual events, user groups).

4.  **The Community Flywheel:**
    *   How do we design a "community flywheel" where the community drives value back into the business? For example:
        *   **Community for Product:** How can we use the community to source product feedback and ideas?
        *   **Community for Marketing:** How can we empower our community members to become advocates and create user-generated content?
        *   **Community for Support:** How can we enable peer-to-peer support within the community to reduce the load on our support team?

5.  **Measuring Community ROI:**
    *   How will we measure the return on investment (ROI) of our community efforts? What are the key metrics to track?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the community-led growth strategy as a detailed plan in Markdown. The plan should be a practical guide for a company that is new to community building.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be authentic and focused on creating genuine value for community members.
*   The plan must show a clear link between community activities and business outcomes.
*   The tone should be collaborative, empathetic, and strategic.

## Tips

Community-led growth is a powerful, modern GTM strategy. This prompt provides a framework for building a successful community from the ground up.
