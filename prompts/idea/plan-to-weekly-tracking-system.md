# Plan to Weekly Tracking System

Convert a plan into weekly milestones with deliverables, a repeatable check-in template, and a way to see slippage early.

## Prompt

Here is my plan: [paste plan]. Turn into weekly tracking:

1. Break each goal into weekly milestones with deliverables.
2. Weekly check-in template: accomplished, blocked, next.
3. Leading indicators (actions) and lagging indicators (results).
4. Dashboard: goal, status (on track/at risk/behind), % complete, next milestone, owner.
5. Automated weekly summary prompt for Friday status updates.
6. Flag goals at risk based on pace needed.

Practical for solo operator or small team.
