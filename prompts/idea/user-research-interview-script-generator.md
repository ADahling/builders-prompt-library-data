# User Research Interview Script Generator

Write a user interview script the way a senior UX researcher would: warm-up, non-leading questions, and probes that surface real behavior.

## Prompt

Role: Act as a senior UX researcher.
Task: Generate a user interview script to uncover deep insights.
Context: The script is for a new [product type, e.g., mobile banking app] targeting [user demographic, e.g., millennials (25-35)]. The goal is to understand their [specific area of interest, e.g., current financial habits, frustrations with existing apps, and attitudes toward saving].
Format: Structure the output with a friendly introduction, 5 warm-up questions, 10 deep-dive questions, and 3 wrap-up questions.

## Tips

Good interviews uncover what users do, not what they say they do. Source: miro.com/ai/prompts/ux-design-prompts
