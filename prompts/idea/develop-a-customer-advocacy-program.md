# Develop a Customer Advocacy Program

Design and implement a structured customer advocacy program to identify brand champions, define reward tiers, and scale social proof.

Variables: company_name, program_name

## Prompt

I am the Head of Customer Marketing at {{company_name}}. We have a lot of happy customers, but we are not doing a good job of activating them as advocates for our brand. I want to create a formal customer advocacy program.

Act as a customer marketing expert. Your task is to create a customer advocacy program.

The program should be designed to identify, nurture, and reward our customer advocates. The program should include:

1.  **Identifying Advocates:**
    *   How do we identify our most passionate and successful customers? (e.g., by looking at NPS scores, product usage data, or by asking our CSMs).

2.  **The Advocacy Program ("{{program_name}}"):**
    *   Give the program a name (e.g., "The {{company_name}} Insiders").
    *   What are the benefits of being a member of the program? (e.g., exclusive access to our product team, invitations to special events, swag).

3.  **The "Asks":**
    *   What do we want our advocates to do? This should be a menu of options, not a set of requirements:
        *   **Write a review:** (on G2, Capterra, etc.).
        *   **Serve as a reference:** (for a prospective customer).
        *   **Participate in a case study:** (written or video).
        *   **Speak at a webinar or event:**
        *   **Share our content on social media:**

4.  **Rewards & Recognition:**
    *   How will we reward and recognize our advocates for their contributions? (e.g., a points system, gift cards, public recognition).

5.  **The Technology:**
    *   What technology should we use to manage our advocacy program? (e.g., a dedicated customer advocacy platform like Influitive or a simpler system built with our CRM).

**Output Format:**

Please generate the customer advocacy program plan as a detailed and structured Markdown document. The plan should be a complete guide to launching and managing a successful program.

**Constraints:**

*   The program must be a win-win for both the company and the customer advocates.
*   The program must be authentic and based on genuine customer relationships.
*   The tone should be appreciative, collaborative, and exclusive.

## Tips

Your happiest customers can be your most powerful marketing channel. This prompt provides a framework for building a customer advocacy program to activate them.
