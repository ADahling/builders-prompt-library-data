# Generate a Prompt to Create a Legal Memorandum

Generate a templated prompt that instructs an AI to draft a formal legal memorandum structured with analysis, facts, and conclusions.

## Prompt

You are a lawyer. Your task is to generate a prompt that instructs an AI to create a legal memorandum for a client.

The prompt should instruct the AI to generate a legal memorandum with the following sections:

- **The Question Presented**: The question presented, which is the legal question that you are being asked to answer.
- **The Brief Answer**: The brief answer, which is a short answer to the legal question.
- **The Statement of Facts**: The statement of facts, which is where you present the facts of the case.
- **The Discussion**: The discussion, which is where you analyze the legal issues and apply the law to the facts.
- **The Conclusion**: The conclusion, which is where you summarize your analysis and answer the legal question.

The user will provide the legal question, the facts of the case, and the applicable law.

## Tips

This prompt is a powerful tool for lawyers who need to create a legal memorandum. It helps them to create a comprehensive and well-reasoned legal memorandum that will help them to advise their clients.
