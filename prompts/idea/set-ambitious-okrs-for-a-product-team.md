# Set Ambitious OKRs for a Product Team

Generate ambitious, outcome-driven OKRs and strategic initiatives that align product team milestones with high-level company objectives.

Variables: company_name, quarter, company_goal, team_size

## Prompt

I am the Head of Product at {{company_name}}, and I need to set the Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) for our product team for the upcoming quarter, {{quarter}}. Our company's main goal for the quarter is to {{company_goal}}.

Act as an experienced product leader and OKR coach. Your task is to help me draft a set of ambitious and measurable OKRs for my team of {{team_size}} product managers and designers. The OKRs should align with the company's overall goal.

Your process should be as follows:

1.  **Objective Setting:**
    *   Help me craft 1-3 high-level, qualitative, and inspirational Objectives for the product team. The Objectives should describe what we want to achieve.
    *   For each Objective, explain why it's a good objective and how it connects to the company goal.
2.  **Key Result Definition:**
    *   For each Objective, help me define 3-5 specific, measurable, and time-bound Key Results. The Key Results should measure progress towards the Objective.
    *   The Key Results should be outcome-focused (e.g., "Increase user activation rate from 20% to 30%") rather than output-focused (e.g., "Launch 3 new features").
3.  **Initiatives Brainstorming:**
    *   For each Key Result, brainstorm a list of potential initiatives or projects that the team could work on to achieve it.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the OKRs in a structured Markdown format. For each Objective, list the corresponding Key Results and potential Initiatives.

**Example Structure:**

**Objective 1:** [Objective Title]
*   **Key Result 1.1:** [Description of KR]
*   **Key Result 1.2:** [Description of KR]
*   **Initiatives:**
    *   [Initiative A]
    *   [Initiative B]

**Constraints:**

*   The OKRs must be well-defined and follow the best practices of the OKR methodology.
*   The Key Results must be quantifiable and challenging.
*   The tone should be strategic, collaborative, and motivating.

## Tips

This prompt guides users through the process of setting high-quality OKRs, a critical skill for any product leader.
