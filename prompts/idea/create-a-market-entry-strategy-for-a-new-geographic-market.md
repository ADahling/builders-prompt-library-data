# Create a Market Entry Strategy for a New Geographic Market

Generate a comprehensive, localized international expansion plan covering market analysis, entry modes, and financial projections for new regions.

Variables: company_name, company_type, home_market, new_market

## Prompt

I am the Head of International Expansion for {{company_name}}, a successful {{company_type}} in our home market of {{home_market}}. We are planning to enter a new geographic market: {{new_market}}. I need to create a comprehensive market entry strategy.

Act as an international business strategist. Your task is to create a market entry strategy for {{new_market}}.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **Market Analysis:**
    *   A brief analysis of the market size, growth rate, and competitive landscape in {{new_market}}.

2.  **Market Entry Mode:**
    *   Recommend the best mode of entry for us. Discuss the pros and cons of different options:
        *   **Exporting:** Selling our products directly from our home country.
        *   **Licensing/Franchising:** Granting a local company the right to produce and sell our product.
        *   **Joint Venture:** Partnering with a local company to create a new entity.
        *   **Direct Investment:** Acquiring a local company or setting up our own subsidiary.

3.  **Product Localization:**
    *   What changes do we need to make to our product to meet the needs and preferences of customers in {{new_market}}? (e.g., language, features, pricing).

4.  **Marketing & Sales Strategy:**
    *   How will we market and sell our product in the new market? What channels should we use?

5.  **Operational Plan:**
    *   What are the key operational considerations? (e.g., legal & regulatory compliance, supply chain, customer support).

6.  **Financial Projections:**
    *   A high-level financial model showing the projected revenues, costs, and profitability of our expansion into {{new_market}}.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the market entry strategy as a detailed and structured report in Markdown. The report should be a complete business plan for our international expansion.

**Constraints:**

*   The recommendation for the market entry mode must be well-justified.
*   The strategy must be tailored to the specific characteristics of {{new_market}}.
*   The tone should be strategic, analytical, and comprehensive.

## Tips

International expansion is a major undertaking. This prompt provides a structured framework for creating a comprehensive market entry strategy.
