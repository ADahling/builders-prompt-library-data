# Craft a Product Roadmap for a Digital Marketing Automation Platform

Plan a marketing automation platform roadmap with feature release guidance, user feedback strategies, and methods to adapt to market changes.

Variables: marketing_campaigns

## Prompt

Craft a product roadmap for a digital marketing automation platform that aims to streamline {{marketing_campaigns}} for businesses.

Context:
- The platform combines AI algorithms with marketing automation tools to optimize campaign performance.
- Aligning roadmap milestones with industry trends and customer feedback is crucial for platform success.

Provide:
1. Structured approach to aligning feature releases with marketing automation best practices.
2. Strategies for incorporating user feedback to enhance platform functionality and usability.
3. Methods for adapting the roadmap based on changing market dynamics and competitive analysis.

## Tips

Use this prompt to drive efficient feature development and deployment in a competitive digital marketing landscape.
