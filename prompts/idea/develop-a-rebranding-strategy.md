# Develop a Rebranding Strategy

Generate a comprehensive rebranding strategy that covers business objectives, brand identity, and a multi-channel launch plan.

Variables: company_name, number_of_years

## Prompt

I am the CMO of {{company_name}}. Our brand has been around for {{number_of_years}} years, and it's starting to feel dated and out of touch with our target audience. I believe it's time for a rebrand. I need to develop a comprehensive rebranding strategy.

Act as a brand strategist from a top branding agency. Your task is to create a rebranding strategy for my company.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **The Business Case for Rebranding:**
    *   Why are we rebranding? What are the business goals we are trying to achieve?

2.  **Brand Research:**
    *   A plan for conducting research to understand our current brand perception among customers, employees, and the market.

3.  **The New Brand Strategy:**
    *   This is the core of the work. It should include:
        *   **A new brand positioning statement.**
        *   **A new brand mission and vision.**
        *   **A new set of brand values.**
        *   **A new brand personality, voice, and tone.**

4.  **The New Brand Identity:**
    *   The creative expression of the brand strategy. This includes:
        *   **A new company name (if necessary).**
        *   **A new logo.**
        *   **A new visual identity (colors, fonts, imagery).**

5.  **The Rebrand Launch Plan:**
    *   A detailed plan for launching the new brand to the world. This should be a coordinated "lightning strike" across all channels (PR, advertising, social media, website, etc.).
    *   How will we communicate the rebrand to our employees and get them excited about it?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the rebranding strategy as a detailed and structured plan in Markdown. The plan should be a complete guide to managing a complex and high-stakes rebranding project.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be based on research and a clear understanding of the business goals.
*   The plan must be comprehensive and cover all aspects of the rebrand, from strategy to execution.
*   The tone should be strategic, creative, and bold.

## Tips

A rebrand is a major undertaking. This prompt provides a comprehensive framework for a strategic and successful rebranding process.
