# Create a Competitive Analysis Using Porter's Five Forces

Conduct a comprehensive strategic analysis of a specific sector's profit potential and competitive landscape using the Five Forces framework.

Variables: industry_name

## Prompt

I am a business strategist analyzing the attractiveness of the {{industry_name}} industry. I want to use Porter's Five Forces framework to understand the competitive dynamics of this industry and its profit potential.

Act as a strategy consultant in the style of Michael Porter. Your task is to conduct a Five Forces analysis for the {{industry_name}} industry.

For each of the five forces, you need to:

1.  **Explain the Force:** Briefly describe what the force is.
2.  **Assess the Force's Strength:** Analyze the strength of the force in this industry (e.g., high, medium, or low). Provide a justification for your assessment.

**The Five Forces:**

*   **Threat of New Entrants:**
    *   How easy or difficult is it for new companies to enter this industry? (Consider barriers to entry like economies of scale, capital requirements, brand identity, government policy).

*   **Bargaining Power of Buyers:**
    *   How much power do customers have to drive down prices in this industry? (Consider factors like buyer concentration, switching costs, the availability of substitutes).

*   **Bargaining Power of Suppliers:**
    *   How much power do suppliers have to drive up their prices? (Consider factors like supplier concentration, the importance of the supplier's input, switching costs).

*   **Threat of Substitute Products or Services:**
    *   How likely are customers to switch to a different type of product or service that meets the same need? (e.g., video conferencing as a substitute for air travel).

*   **Rivalry Among Existing Competitors:**
    *   How intense is the competition among the existing players in the industry? (Consider factors like the number of competitors, industry growth rate, product differentiation).

**Conclusion:**

After analyzing all five forces, provide an overall assessment of the industry's attractiveness. Is this an industry where companies can earn high, sustainable profits?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the Five Forces analysis as a detailed and structured report in Markdown. The report should be a comprehensive strategic analysis of the industry.

**Constraints:**

*   The analysis must be specific to the {{industry_name}} industry.
*   The assessment of each force must be well-reasoned and based on industry knowledge.
*   The tone should be analytical, structured, and expert.

## Tips

Porter's Five Forces is a classic and powerful framework for industry analysis. This prompt provides a structured way to apply it.
