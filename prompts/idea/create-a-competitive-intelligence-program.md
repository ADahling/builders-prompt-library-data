# Create a Competitive Intelligence Program

Establish a robust framework for tracking competitors, leveraging automation tools, and delivering actionable strategic insights via kill sheets.

Variables: company_name

## Prompt

I am the Head of Product Marketing at {{company_name}}. Our competitors are moving faster than ever, and we are often caught by surprise by their new product launches and marketing campaigns. I need to establish a formal competitive intelligence program to systematically track and analyze our competition.

Act as a competitive intelligence expert. Your task is to create a plan for building a competitive intelligence program.

The plan should include:

1.  **Define Key Competitors:**
    *   Who are our tier 1 and tier 2 competitors?

2.  **Information Sources:**
    *   What are the key sources of information we should monitor for each competitor? This should include:
        *   **Their Website:** (e.g., pricing changes, new feature announcements, blog posts).
        *   **Their Product:** (e.g., by using their free trial).
        *   **Their Customers:** (e.g., by reading reviews on G2 and Capterra).
        *   **Their Employees:** (e.g., by monitoring LinkedIn for new hires in key roles).
        *   **Their Marketing:** (e.g., their social media, their ads, their webinars).

3.  **Tools & Technology:**
    *   What tools can we use to automate the process of tracking our competitors? (e.g., Crayon, Kompyte, or simpler tools like Google Alerts).

4.  **Analysis & Synthesis:**
    *   How do we move beyond just collecting data to generating actionable insights? What frameworks can we use to analyze our competitors' strategies?

5.  **Dissemination & Communication:**
    *   How will we share our competitive intelligence with the rest of the organization?
    *   Create a template for a monthly competitive intelligence report to be shared with the leadership team.
    *   How can we create battle cards for our sales team?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the competitive intelligence program plan as a detailed and structured Markdown document. The plan should be a practical guide to setting up this critical function.

**Constraints:**

*   The program must be scalable and sustainable.
*   The focus should be on generating actionable insights, not just collecting data.
*   The tone should be proactive, analytical, and strategic.

## Tips

In a competitive market, knowing your competition is table stakes. This prompt provides a framework for building a systematic competitive intelligence program.
