# Create a Social Impact Report

Generate a comprehensive, B Corp-aligned annual social impact report template featuring CEO narratives, metric tracking, and storytelling.

Variables: company_name

## Prompt

I am the Head of Social Impact at {{company_name}}, a B Corp. I need to create our annual social impact report to share with our stakeholders (customers, employees, investors, and the community). The report should be transparent, authentic, and inspiring.

Act as a social impact and sustainability reporting expert. Your task is to create a template for our annual social impact report.

The report should be structured around our key impact areas and should include:

1.  **A Letter from the CEO:**
    *   A personal letter from our CEO reflecting on our social and environmental impact over the past year.

2.  **Our Mission & Theory of Change:**
    *   A clear explanation of our company's mission and our theory of change - how our business model creates positive impact.

3.  **Our Impact Metrics:**
    *   For each of our key impact areas (e.g., environmental footprint, community giving, employee well-being, diversity & inclusion), we need to report on our key metrics.
    *   The report should show our performance against the goals we set for the year and our progress over time.

4.  **Stories of Impact:**
    *   The report should not just be data. It should also include powerful stories and testimonials that bring our impact to life.

5.  **Our Goals for Next Year:**
    *   A clear statement of our social and environmental goals for the upcoming year.

6.  **Third-Party Verification:**
    *   A statement from our third-party certifier (e.g., B Lab) to provide credibility.

**Output Format:**

Please generate a detailed template for the social impact report in Markdown. The template should have clear headings for each section and should include prompts and questions to guide me in filling it out.

**Constraints:**

*   The report must be transparent and authentic, including both our successes and our challenges.
*   The report must be a balance of quantitative data and qualitative storytelling.
*   The tone should be passionate, credible, and inspiring.

## Tips

A social impact report is a critical tool for any mission-driven company to communicate its impact and build trust with its stakeholders. This prompt provides a template for creating one.
