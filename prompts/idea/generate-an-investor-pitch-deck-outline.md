# Generate an Investor Pitch Deck Outline

Create a comprehensive 10-12 slide pitch deck outline with visual recommendations and key messaging to secure seed funding.

Variables: startup_name, product_description

## Prompt

I am the founder of an early-stage startup, {{startup_name}}, seeking seed funding. We are developing {{product_description}}. I need to create a compelling investor pitch deck to present to potential investors.

Act as an experienced venture capitalist and startup advisor. Your task is to generate a detailed outline for a 10-12 slide investor pitch deck. For each slide, you should specify:

*   **Slide Title:** (e.g., "The Problem")
*   **Key Content:** What information should be on this slide? What is the key message to convey?
*   **Design & Visuals:** Suggest how to present the information visually (e.g., use a graph, a customer quote, a product screenshot).

The pitch deck outline should cover the following classic slides:

1.  **Company Purpose / Vision**
2.  **The Problem**
3.  **The Solution**
4.  **Market Size (TAM, SAM, SOM)**
5.  **The Product**
6.  **Business Model**
7.  **Go-to-Market Strategy**
8.  **Competitive Landscape**
9.  **The Team**
10. **Financial Projections**
11. **The Ask & Use of Funds**

**Output Format:**

Please generate the pitch deck outline in a clear, slide-by-slide format in Markdown. Use headings for each slide title.

**Constraints:**

*   The advice should reflect the current expectations of venture capitalists.
*   The outline should be structured to tell a compelling story about our startup.
*   The tone should be professional, direct, and insightful.

## Tips

An essential prompt for any founder who is starting to fundraise and needs a solid pitch deck structure.
