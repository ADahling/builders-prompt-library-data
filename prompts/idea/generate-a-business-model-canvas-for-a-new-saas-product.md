# Generate a Business Model Canvas for a New SaaS Product

Develop a comprehensive SaaS business model canvas through an interactive, strategic dialogue that defines and validates all nine core blocks.

Variables: product_name, product_description, target_market

## Prompt

As a startup founder, I need to develop a comprehensive Business Model Canvas for my new SaaS product, '{{product_name}}'. The product is a {{product_description}}. Our target market is {{target_market}}.

I need you to act as an experienced business strategist and guide me through the process of creating a detailed Business Model Canvas. For each of the nine building blocks (Customer Segments, Value Propositions, Channels, Customer Relationships, Revenue Streams, Key Activities, Key Resources, Key Partnerships, and Cost Structure), I want you to:

1.  **Ask me targeted questions** to help me brainstorm and define each block. The questions should be specific to a SaaS business model.
2.  **Provide examples and best practices** for each block, relevant to the SaaS industry.
3.  **Synthesize my responses** into a concise and coherent summary for each block.
4.  **Challenge my assumptions** and suggest alternative approaches where appropriate.

**Output Format:**

Please structure your response in a clear, organized manner. For each of the nine blocks, provide:

*   **Block Name:** (e.g., Customer Segments)
*   **Your Questions:** (A list of questions for me to answer)
*   **Examples & Best Practices:** (Relevant examples for a SaaS business)
*   **My Summary:** (A placeholder for me to fill in my answers)

After we have gone through all nine blocks, I want you to generate a final, complete Business Model Canvas in a markdown table format. The table should have two columns: "Building Block" and "Description".

**Constraints:**

*   The tone should be professional, encouraging, and strategic.
*   The advice should be practical and actionable for an early-stage startup.
*   The final output should be a complete Business Model Canvas that I can use in my investor pitch deck and strategic planning documents.

## Tips

This prompt is designed to be an interactive, step-by-step guide for founders who are new to the Business Model Canvas.
