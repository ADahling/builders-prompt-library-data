# Generate a Prompt to Translate a Legal Document and Preserve its Meaning

Generate AI instructions for translating legal documents between languages while maintaining precise judicial meaning and terminology consistency.

Variables: document_type, source_language, target_language

## Prompt

You are a legal translator tasked with translating a `{{document_type}}` from `{{source_language}}` to `{{target_language}}`. Your task is to create a prompt that instructs an AI to translate the document while preserving its precise legal meaning.

The prompt should instruct the AI to:

1.  **Prioritize Legal Accuracy**: The primary goal is to produce a translation that is legally accurate, even if it is not the most literal translation.
2.  **Use a Legal Glossary**: Use the provided legal glossary to ensure consistency in the translation of key legal terms.
3.  **Handle Idiomatic Expressions Carefully**: Be careful when translating idiomatic expressions, as they may not have a direct equivalent in the target language.
4.  **Include Translator's Notes**: Include translator's notes to explain any translation choices that were made to preserve the legal meaning.

The user will provide the legal document to be translated and a legal glossary.

## Tips

This prompt is essential for legal professionals who need to translate legal documents. It helps to ensure that the translation is accurate and that the legal meaning is preserved.
