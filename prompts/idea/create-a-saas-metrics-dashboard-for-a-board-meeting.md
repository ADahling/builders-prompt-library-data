# Create a SaaS Metrics Dashboard for a Board Meeting

Generate a comprehensive, board-ready SaaS performance dashboard featuring key financial, customer, and efficiency metrics derived from raw quarterly data.

Variables: company_name, quarter, list_of_data_points

## Prompt

I am the CEO of a Series A SaaS company, {{company_name}}. I need to prepare a SaaS metrics dashboard for our upcoming board meeting. The dashboard should provide a clear and concise overview of our business performance over the last quarter, {{quarter}}.

Act as a SaaS industry expert and data analyst. Your task is to generate a comprehensive SaaS metrics dashboard. You should first ask me for the raw data for the quarter, including {{list_of_data_points}}.

Once I provide the data, the dashboard should include the following key metrics, with clear visualizations (you can describe the ideal chart for each metric):

1.  **Headline Metrics:**
    *   Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) - with MoM growth rate.
    *   Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR).
    *   Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).
    *   Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).
    *   LTV:CAC Ratio.
2.  **Customer Metrics:**
    *   Total Customers.
    *   New Customers Acquired.
    *   Customer Churn Rate (both logo and revenue churn).
3.  **Revenue Metrics:**
    *   MRR Growth (New MRR, Expansion MRR, Churn MRR, Net New MRR).
    *   Average Revenue Per Account (ARPA).
4.  **Efficiency Metrics:**
    *   CAC Payback Period.
    *   Sales & Marketing Efficiency.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the dashboard as a well-organized Markdown document. For each metric, provide the calculated value, a brief explanation of what it means, and a description of the recommended visualization (e.g., "a bar chart showing MRR growth over the last 6 months").

**Constraints:**

*   The dashboard must be easy for board members to understand, even if they are not SaaS experts.
*   The calculations must be accurate and based on industry-standard formulas.
*   The tone should be professional, data-driven, and transparent.

## Tips

An essential prompt for any SaaS CEO who needs to report on business performance to their board.
