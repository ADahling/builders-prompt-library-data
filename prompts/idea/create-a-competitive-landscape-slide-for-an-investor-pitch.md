# Create a Competitive Landscape Slide for an Investor Pitch

Generate a strategic competitive landscape slide by selecting the ideal framework, mapping competitors, and crafting a winning investment narrative.

Variables: startup_name, product_name, industry

## Prompt

I am a startup founder creating a pitch deck for my company, {{startup_name}}. I need to create a compelling and easy-to-understand 'Competitive Landscape' slide. Our product, {{product_name}}, operates in the {{industry}} space.

Act as a VC associate who reviews hundreds of pitch decks. Your task is to help me create a best-in-class competitive landscape slide. The slide should not just list our competitors; it should position our company as a winner in the market.

I need you to:

1.  **Choose the Right Framework:**
    *   Explain the two most common frameworks for a competitive landscape slide: the 'Gartner Magic Quadrant' (a 2x2 matrix) and the 'Feature Comparison Table'.
    *   Recommend the best framework for my specific business and justify your choice. For a 2x2 matrix, what should be the labels for the X and Y axes? The axes should be chosen to place my company in the top-right quadrant.

2.  **Identify Competitor Tiers:**
    *   Help me categorize our competitors into two tiers:
        *   **Direct Competitors:** Companies that solve the same problem for the same target audience.
        *   **Indirect Competitors & Alternatives:** Companies that solve the same problem with a different solution, or how customers are solving the problem today (e.g., using spreadsheets).

3.  **Design the Slide:**
    *   Based on the chosen framework, generate the content for the slide. 
    *   If it's a 2x2 matrix, tell me where to place the logos of our key competitors and our own company logo.
    *   If it's a feature table, list the key features down the side and the competitors across the top, using checkmarks to show how we stack up.

4.  **Craft the Narrative:**
    *   Provide a short, powerful narrative to accompany the slide. This narrative should explain why our chosen position on the slide gives us a unique and sustainable competitive advantage.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the output as a set of clear instructions in Markdown. The output should be a blueprint for creating the slide, including the visual layout and the verbal narrative.

**Constraints:**

*   The chosen framework and axes must be strategic and persuasive.
*   The slide must be visually clean and easy to understand in under 30 seconds.
*   The narrative must be confident and compelling.

## Tips

A very common and important slide in any pitch deck. This prompt helps founders get it right.
