# Uncover Customer Needs with the Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) Framework

Execute a comprehensive research process to uncover deep customer motivations and synthesize actionable product strategies using the JTBD framework.

Variables: product_name, product_function

## Prompt

I am a product manager for {{product_name}}, a product that helps users {{product_function}}. I want to move beyond focusing on demographics and product features and truly understand the underlying "job" that customers are "hiring" our product to do. I want to use the Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) framework for this.

Act as a JTBD expert and researcher (in the style of Clayton Christensen or Bob Moesta). Your task is to help me plan and conduct JTBD interviews to uncover deep customer insights.

The plan should include:

1.  **The JTBD Interview Script:**
    *   Provide a detailed script for conducting a JTBD interview. The interview should focus on the "story" of how a customer came to purchase and use our product.
    *   The script should include questions about the "First Thought" (the moment they realized they had a need), the "Struggling Moment," the "Passive Looking" phase, the "Active Looking" phase, and the "Decision Moment."
    *   The questions should be open-ended and designed to uncover the forces of progress (push and pull) and the forces of regress (anxiety and habit).
2.  **Recruiting Interview Participants:**
    *   Suggest criteria for recruiting customers for these interviews. (Hint: it should be recent purchasers).
3.  **Analyzing the Interviews:**
    *   Explain how to analyze the interview transcripts to identify the core job, the desired outcomes, and the hiring criteria.
    *   Guide me in synthesizing the findings into a "Job Story" with the format: "When _____, I want to _____, so I can _____."
4.  **Applying the Insights:**
    *   Discuss how to use the JTBD insights to improve our product roadmap, marketing messaging, and overall strategy.

**Output Format:**

Please generate a comprehensive guide to conducting JTBD research in Markdown. The guide should include the full interview script and a clear explanation of the analysis process.

**Constraints:**

*   The interview script must be designed to uncover the "why" behind customer behavior, not just the "what."
*   The methodology must be true to the principles of the JTBD framework.
*   The tone should be empathetic, curious, and analytical.

## Tips

This prompt provides a deep dive into the powerful JTBD framework, equipping product managers with a tool to uncover fundamental customer needs.
