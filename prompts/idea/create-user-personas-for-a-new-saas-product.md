# Create User Personas for a New SaaS Product

Get research methods, persona templates, and guidance for turning user insights into product decisions for a new SaaS product.

Variables: target_audience, specific_problem

## Prompt

You have been tasked with creating detailed user personas for a new SaaS product aimed at {{target_audience}}. 

Context:
- The product aims to solve {{specific_problem}} for the target audience.
- Understanding user needs and behaviors is crucial for designing a user-centric product.

Provide:
1. Research methodologies for gathering user information.
2. Persona templates to capture demographic and psychographic details.
3. Recommendations on translating user personas into actionable insights for product development.

## Tips

Use this prompt at the early stages of product ideation to ensure a user-focused approach.
