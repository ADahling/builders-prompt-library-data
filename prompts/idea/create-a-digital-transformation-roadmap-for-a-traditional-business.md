# Create a Digital Transformation Roadmap for a Traditional Business

Develop a multi-year, phased digital transformation roadmap that modernizes traditional business operations, culture, and customer engagement.

Variables: company_name, industry, number_of_years

## Prompt

I am the CEO of {{company_name}}, a traditional, family-owned business in the {{industry}} industry. We have been successful for {{number_of_years}} years, but I know we need to embrace digital transformation to stay competitive. I don't know where to start.

Act as a digital transformation consultant. Your task is to create a high-level digital transformation roadmap for my company. The roadmap should be a phased approach that we can implement over the next 1-3 years.

The roadmap should cover four key areas:

1.  **Customer Experience:**
    *   How can we use digital technologies to improve how we interact with our customers? (e.g., e-commerce, CRM, social media marketing, personalized customer service).
2.  **Operational Processes:**
    *   How can we use technology to make our internal operations more efficient and data-driven? (e.g., ERP systems, automation of manual tasks, supply chain optimization).
3.  **Business Model:**
    *   How can we leverage digital to create new revenue streams or new business models? (e.g., subscription services, data monetization, online platforms).
4.  **Culture & Capabilities:**
    *   What changes do we need to make to our company culture and employee skills to become a more digital-first organization? (e.g., digital literacy training, fostering a culture of experimentation, hiring new talent).

**Output Format:**

Please generate the digital transformation roadmap as a phased plan in Markdown. For each phase (e.g., Phase 1: Foundational, Phase 2: Growth, Phase 3: Innovation), outline the key initiatives in each of the four areas above. The roadmap should be easy for a non-technical CEO to understand.

**Constraints:**

*   The roadmap must be realistic and tailored to a traditional business with limited digital expertise.
*   The recommendations should focus on generating tangible business value, not just adopting technology for technology's sake.
*   The tone should be visionary, yet pragmatic and reassuring.

## Tips

A crucial prompt for leaders of traditional businesses who need a clear and structured path to navigate the complexities of digital transformation.
