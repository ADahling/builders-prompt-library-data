# Craft a Partnership Strategy to Enter a New Market

Plan local partnerships for entry into a new geographic market, with partner profiles, model comparisons, an outreach email, and management steps.

Variables: company_name, company_type, new_market

## Prompt

As the Head of Business Development for {{company_name}}, a {{company_type}} company, we are planning to enter a new geographic market: {{new_market}}. We believe that strategic partnerships will be key to our success in this new market.

Act as a business development expert. Your task is to create a partnership strategy for our market entry. The strategy should be a roadmap for identifying, approaching, and securing partnerships with local companies.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **Partnership Goals & Objectives:**
    *   What are our primary goals for forming partnerships in {{new_market}}? (e.g., market access, distribution, localization).
2.  **Ideal Partner Profile:**
    *   Define the characteristics of our ideal partner. What kind of companies should we target? (e.g., industry, size, reputation).
3.  **Partnership Models:**
    *   Propose different partnership models that we could offer, such as referral partnerships, reseller agreements, or joint ventures. Explain the pros and cons of each model.
4.  **Partner Identification & Outreach:**
    *   Suggest a process for identifying potential partners in {{new_market}}.
    *   Craft a compelling outreach email template that we can use to initiate conversations with potential partners.
5.  **Partnership Management:**
    *   Outline a plan for managing our partnerships to ensure mutual success. How will we support our partners and track our joint performance?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the partnership strategy as a well-structured Markdown document. The document should be a practical guide for our business development team.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be tailored to the specific challenges and opportunities of entering a new geographic market.
*   The recommendations should be actionable and results-oriented.
*   The tone should be strategic, persuasive, and professional.

## Tips

For business leaders looking to expand their business into new territories through strategic alliances.
