# Create a Content Marketing Strategy with a Hub-and-Spoke Model

Develop a comprehensive hub-and-spoke content strategy to build topical authority, boost organic traffic, and generate B2B SaaS leads.

Variables: company_name, industry, hub_topic_idea

## Prompt

I am the content marketing manager for {{company_name}}, a B2B SaaS company in the {{industry}} space. Our current content marketing efforts are ad-hoc and not driving significant organic traffic or leads. I want to implement a more strategic approach using the hub-and-spoke model.

Act as a content strategy expert. Your task is to help me develop a hub-and-spoke content marketing strategy for the next quarter.

The process should be:

1.  **Identify a 'Hub' Topic:**
    *   Help me choose a broad, high-value topic (the "hub") that is central to our business and has high search volume. This topic should be something we want to be known for. For our business, a potential hub topic could be {{hub_topic_idea}}.
2.  **Brainstorm 'Spoke' Topics:**
    *   For the chosen hub topic, brainstorm a list of 8-12 more specific "spoke" topics. These spokes should be sub-topics that are related to the hub and address specific questions or keywords that our target audience is searching for.
3.  **Content Format for Hub and Spokes:**
    *   **Hub Content:** The hub should be a substantial piece of "pillar" content, like a long-form ultimate guide, an ebook, or a webinar.
    *   **Spoke Content:** The spokes can be shorter pieces of content, like blog posts, infographics, or short videos, that dive deeper into the specific sub-topics.
4.  **Internal Linking Strategy:**
    *   Explain the importance of internal linking in the hub-and-spoke model. Each spoke should link back to the hub, and the hub should link out to all the spokes. This builds topical authority.
5.  **Content Promotion Plan:**
    *   Suggest a plan for promoting both the hub and the spoke content to maximize their reach and impact.

**Output Format:**

Please generate a content strategy plan in Markdown. The plan should clearly outline the hub topic, the list of spoke topics (with suggested titles), the content formats, and the promotion plan.

**Constraints:**

*   The chosen hub topic must be strategic and relevant to our business.
*   The spoke topics should cover the hub topic comprehensively.
*   The plan should be actionable and easy for a content team to execute.

## Tips

The hub-and-spoke model is a powerful way to build topical authority and drive SEO results. This prompt provides a clear framework for implementing it.
