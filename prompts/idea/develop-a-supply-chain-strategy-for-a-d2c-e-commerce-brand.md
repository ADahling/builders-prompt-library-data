# Develop a Supply Chain Strategy for a D2C E-commerce Brand

Create a scalable, end-to-end supply chain strategy for a D2C brand to optimize sourcing, inventory, 3PL fulfillment, and logistics costs.

Variables: brand_name, product_type

## Prompt

I am the founder of a growing D2C e-commerce brand, {{brand_name}}, that sells {{product_type}}. As we scale, our supply chain is becoming more complex and costly. I need to develop a more strategic approach to our supply chain management.

Act as a supply chain and logistics consultant. Your task is to create a supply chain strategy for my business.

The strategy should cover the entire supply chain, from sourcing to delivery:

1.  **Sourcing & Procurement:**
    *   Should we source from a single supplier or multiple suppliers? What are the pros and cons?
    *   How can we negotiate better terms with our suppliers?

2.  **Inventory Management:**
    *   What is the right inventory management strategy for us? (e.g., Just-in-Time, Economic Order Quantity).
    *   How can we use inventory management software to avoid stockouts and reduce carrying costs?

3.  **Warehousing & Fulfillment:**
    *   Should we manage our own warehouse or outsource to a third-party logistics (3PL) provider? What are the key factors to consider in this decision?
    *   If we use a 3PL, what are the key criteria for selecting a partner?

4.  **Shipping & Logistics:**
    *   What is the optimal shipping strategy to balance cost and delivery speed for our customers?
    *   Should we offer free shipping? If so, how do we make it financially viable?

5.  **Technology & Data:**
    *   What are the key technologies we should invest in to improve our supply chain visibility and efficiency? (e.g., Warehouse Management System, Transportation Management System).

**Output Format:**

Please generate the supply chain strategy as a detailed report in Markdown. The report should provide clear recommendations and frameworks for making these key decisions.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be tailored to a D2C e-commerce business.
*   The recommendations should be practical and scalable.
*   The tone should be analytical, strategic, and cost-conscious.

## Tips

As e-commerce businesses scale, supply chain becomes a critical competitive advantage. This prompt provides a strategic framework for getting it right.
