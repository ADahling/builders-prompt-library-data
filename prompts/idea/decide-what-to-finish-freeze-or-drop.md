# Decide What to Finish, Freeze, or Drop

Audit and declutter your project backlog by ruthlessly categorizing tasks to prioritize completion, pause low-value work, or cut sunk costs.

## Prompt

Current active projects: [list them]. Decide:

FINISH: Close enough to complete. What specifically needs to happen?
FREEZE: Pause for Q1. What state to leave in for easy resume?
DROP: Consciously abandon. Sunk cost vs potential return?

For each: rationale, dependencies affected, communication needed.
Priority order for FINISH items.

Be ruthless. Half-finished projects cost more than they're worth.
