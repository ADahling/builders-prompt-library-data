# Develop a Content Strategy for a Boring Industry

Transform monotonous industry topics into a compelling content strategy using unique storytelling angles, data-driven insights, and creative formats.

Variables: company_name, industry_name

## Prompt

I am the marketing manager for a company, {{company_name}}, in a traditionally "boring" industry: {{industry_name}}. I am struggling to create content that is engaging and that people actually want to read. I need to develop a new content strategy.

Act as a creative content marketing strategist. Your task is to help me develop a content strategy that makes our boring industry interesting.

The strategy should include:

1.  **Find the Angle:**
    *   How can we find a unique and interesting angle on our industry? Can we be the contrarian voice? The data-driven expert? The humorous insider?

2.  **Focus on the Customer, Not the Product:**
    *   Instead of talking about our product, let's talk about our customers' problems, challenges, and successes.

3.  **Creative Content Formats:**
    *   Brainstorm a list of creative content formats that we could use to stand out:
        *   **Data Storytelling:** Can we use our proprietary data to create interesting research reports or infographics?
        *   **Human Stories:** Can we tell the stories of our customers or our employees?
        *   **Podcasts or Video Series:** Can we create a show that features industry experts or interesting characters?
        *   **Interactive Tools:** Can we create a calculator, a quiz, or a benchmark tool that is useful for our audience?

4.  **Content Promotion:**
    *   How can we promote our content in a way that is as creative as the content itself?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the content strategy as a set of creative ideas and actionable tactics in Markdown. The goal is to create a plan that will make our brand the most interesting voice in our industry.

**Constraints:**

*   The ideas must be creative and think outside the box.
*   The strategy must be practical and implementable.
*   The tone should be energetic, creative, and inspiring.

## Tips

A common challenge for many marketers. This prompt provides a framework for making any industry interesting through creative content.
