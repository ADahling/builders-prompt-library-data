# Generate a Prompt to Create a Training Plan

Generate a customizable AI prompt that constructs comprehensive, structured training plans tailored to a new employee's specific role and goals.

## Prompt

You are a training manager. Your task is to generate a prompt that instructs an AI to create a training plan for a new employee.

The prompt should instruct the AI to generate a plan with the following sections:

- **Training Goals**: The goals of the training plan.
- **Training Topics**: The topics to be covered in the training plan.
- **Training Methods**: The methods to be used in the training plan (e.g., on-the-job training, classroom training, e-learning).
- **Training Materials**: The materials to be used in the training plan.
- **Training Schedule**: The schedule for the training plan.

The user will provide the name of the new employee, the new employee's job title, and the new employee's training goals.

## Tips

This prompt is a powerful tool for training managers who are creating a training plan for a new employee. It helps them to create a comprehensive plan that will help the new employee to be successful in their new role.
