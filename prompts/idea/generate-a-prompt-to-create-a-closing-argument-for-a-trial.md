# Generate a Prompt to Create a Closing Argument for a Trial

Craft a compelling, narrative-driven closing argument that leverages rhetorical devices and emotional appeals to secure a favorable verdict.

Variables: case_type

## Prompt

You are a trial lawyer preparing for your closing argument in a `{{case_type}}` case. Your task is to create a prompt that instructs an AI to generate a persuasive and compelling closing argument.

The prompt should instruct the AI to:

1.  **Tell a Story**: Weave the facts of the case into a compelling story that will resonate with the jury.
2.  **Emphasize Your Strengths**: Emphasize the strengths of your case and the weaknesses of the other side's case.
3.  **Appeal to the Jury's Emotions**: Appeal to the jury's emotions, but do not be overly dramatic.
4.  **Use Rhetorical Devices**: Use rhetorical devices, such as repetition and rhetorical questions, to make your argument more memorable.
5.  **End with a Strong Call to Action**: End with a strong call to action, asking the jury to find in your client's favor.

The user will provide a summary of the evidence presented at trial and the key legal arguments.

## Tips

This prompt is a powerful tool for trial lawyers who are preparing for their closing argument. It helps them to create a persuasive and compelling argument that will increase their chances of winning their case.
