# Diagnose Why Teams Feel Slow

Get a diagnosis of team bottlenecks across process, decisions, tools, and skills, plus three priority fixes for the quarter.

## Prompt

My team feels slow. Diagnose:

1. Process Bottlenecks: Where do things sit waiting?
2. Decision Bottlenecks: Which decisions take too long? Still necessary?
3. Communication Overhead: Time in meetings/Slack/email vs work. What to cut?
4. Tool Friction: Helping or hindering? Manual steps to automate?
5. Clarity: Does everyone know what to work on and why?
6. Skill Gaps: Learning curve or genuinely hard?
7. Top 3 Fixes: Biggest speed impact this quarter.

Based on: [team size, work type, symptoms].
