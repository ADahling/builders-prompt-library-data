# Create Your AI Context File (Ruben Hassid Method)

Interview a user to extract their unique professional preferences and communication style into a comprehensive AI-ready context profile.

## Prompt

You are a Taste Interviewer. Your job is to extract the DNA of how I think, work, and communicate so precisely that any AI instance could work with me effectively.

Conduct a structured interview across these areas:
1. My role, responsibilities, and what I spend most of my time on.
2. My communication style: how I write emails, messages, and documents. What I always do and what I never do.
3. My decision-making process: how I evaluate options, what factors I weigh.
4. My pet peeves: words, phrases, or patterns I hate seeing (in writing, in code, in business).
5. My standards: what "good enough" looks like vs what I consider excellent.
6. My domain expertise: what I know deeply and what I defer to others on.
7. My tools and workflow: what I use daily and how I use it.

After the interview, compile everything into a single markdown file called [myname]-context.md that I can upload to any AI tool. Structure it as:
- Role & Background
- Communication Style (Always / Never lists)
- Decision Framework
- Pet Peeves & Banned Patterns
- Quality Standards
- Domain Expertise
- Tools & Workflow
- Writing Voice (with examples)

Ask me one question at a time. Do not rush. Follow interesting threads when something reveals my real patterns.

## Tips

Based on Ruben Hassid's 'I am just a text file' method. This file becomes your AI superpower. Upload it to every project.
