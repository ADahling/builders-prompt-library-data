# Create a Business Case for a New Project

Generate a structured, data-driven business case template to secure project funding and leadership buy-in through rigorous financial analysis.

Variables: your_role, company_name, project_name, project_benefit

## Prompt

I am a {{your_role}} at {{company_name}}. I have an idea for a new project, {{project_name}}, which I believe will {{project_benefit}}. I need to get buy-in and funding for this project from my leadership team. To do this, I need to create a compelling business case.

Act as a senior business analyst. Your task is to help me create a comprehensive and persuasive business case document.

The business case should include the following sections:

1.  **Executive Summary:**
    *   A concise overview of the problem, the proposed solution, and the expected outcomes. This should be the last thing you write, but the first thing in the document.

2.  **Problem Statement:**
    *   Clearly define the business problem or opportunity that this project will address. Use data to quantify the problem where possible.

3.  **Proposed Solution:**
    *   Describe your proposed project in detail. What will be delivered? What are the key features or activities?

4.  **Alignment with Strategic Goals:**
    *   Explain how this project aligns with the company's overall strategic objectives.

5.  **Cost-Benefit Analysis:**
    *   **Costs:** Estimate the one-time and recurring costs of the project (e.g., software, hardware, personnel, training).
    *   **Benefits:** Estimate the tangible (e.g., increased revenue, cost savings) and intangible (e.g., improved customer satisfaction, enhanced brand reputation) benefits of the project. Quantify the benefits in financial terms where possible.
    *   **ROI:** Calculate the projected Return on Investment (ROI) or Net Present Value (NPV) of the project.

6.  **Project Plan & Timeline:**
    *   Provide a high-level project plan with key milestones and a projected timeline.

7.  **Risk Assessment:**
    *   Identify the potential risks associated with the project and suggest mitigation strategies.

**Output Format:**

Please generate a business case template in Markdown. The template should have clear headings for each section and should include prompts and questions to guide me in filling it out.

**Constraints:**

*   The business case must be structured, data-driven, and persuasive.
*   The financial analysis should be sound.
*   The tone should be professional, objective, and confident.

## Tips

A fundamental skill for anyone who wants to get their ideas approved and funded within a larger organization.
