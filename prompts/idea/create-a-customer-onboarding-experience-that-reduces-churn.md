# Create a Customer Onboarding Experience That Reduces Churn

Develop a comprehensive 90-day B2B SaaS onboarding playbook combining automated messaging, in-app guidance, and human touchpoints to curb churn.

Variables: product_name

## Prompt

I am the Head of Customer Success for a B2B SaaS product, {{product_name}}. We have a high churn rate in the first 90 days, which tells me that our customer onboarding is failing. I need to redesign our customer onboarding experience to ensure that new customers are successful and see the value of our product quickly.

Act as a customer onboarding expert. Your task is to create a new customer onboarding plan for the first 90 days.

The plan should be a multi-channel experience that includes:

1.  **The Welcome Email Series:**
    *   Design a series of 5-7 automated emails to be sent to new customers in their first few weeks. The emails should welcome them, guide them through the key setup steps, and share best practices.

2.  **In-App Onboarding:**
    *   How can we use in-app guided tours, checklists, and contextual help to guide new users through the product?
    *   The goal is to get them to their "aha!" moment as quickly as possible.

3.  **Human Touchpoints:**
    *   What is the role of our customer success managers (CSMs) in the onboarding process? 
    *   Should we have a kickoff call? A 30-day check-in? A 90-day business review?

4.  **Educational Content:**
    *   What educational content do we need to create to support onboarding? (e.g., getting started guides, video tutorials, webinars).

5.  **Measuring Onboarding Success:**
    *   How will we know if our new onboarding is working? Define a set of key onboarding metrics to track (e.g., time to first value, feature adoption rate, 90-day retention rate).

**Output Format:**

Please generate the customer onboarding plan as a detailed, day-by-day plan in Markdown. The plan should be a complete playbook for our customer success team.

**Constraints:**

*   The onboarding experience must be customer-centric and focused on their desired outcomes.
*   The plan must be a mix of automated and human touchpoints.
*   The tone should be helpful, proactive, and welcoming.

## Tips

Onboarding is arguably the most important part of the customer lifecycle. This prompt provides a framework for designing a world-class onboarding experience.
