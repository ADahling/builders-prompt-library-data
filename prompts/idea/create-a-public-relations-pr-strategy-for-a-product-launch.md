# Create a Public Relations (PR) Strategy for a Product Launch

Draft a comprehensive, narrative-driven PR launch plan including media lists, pitches, press releases, and a strategic outreach timeline.

Variables: company_name, product_name, launch_date

## Prompt

I am the Head of Communications at {{company_name}}. We are launching a new product, {{product_name}}, on {{launch_date}}. I need to create a public relations (PR) strategy to generate media coverage and buzz for the launch.

Act as a PR expert from a top tech PR agency. Your task is to create a PR strategy for our product launch.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **The Story:**
    *   What is the story we are trying to tell? It's not just about the product; it's about the problem we are solving and why it matters.

2.  **The Media List:**
    *   Who are the key journalists, publications, and influencers we want to reach? Create a targeted media list.

3.  **The Pitch:**
    *   Craft a compelling and personalized email pitch to send to the journalists on our media list. The pitch should be short, punchy, and newsworthy.

4.  **The Press Release:**
    *   Write a professional press release for the launch.

5.  **The Media Kit:**
    *   What should be in our media kit? (e.g., press release, company backgrounder, executive bios, product screenshots, logo).

6.  **The Embargo/Exclusive Strategy:**
    *   Should we offer an exclusive story to a single, top-tier publication? Or should we brief a wider group of journalists under embargo?

7.  **The Timeline:**
    *   A detailed timeline for our PR outreach, starting from a few weeks before the launch to the day of the launch and the follow-up.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the PR strategy as a detailed plan in Markdown. The plan should be a complete guide to executing a successful product launch PR campaign.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be focused on telling a compelling story, not just on blasting out a press release.
*   The pitch must be tailored to the interests of the journalists we are targeting.
*   The tone should be professional, persuasive, and newsworthy.

## Tips

PR can be a powerful way to build credibility and drive awareness for a new product. This prompt provides a framework for a strategic PR launch.
