# Develop a Personal Finance Plan

Generate a personalized, comprehensive financial roadmap covering budgeting, debt reduction, retirement planning, and investment strategies.

Variables: age, income, financial_goal, list_of_debts, retirement_account_type

## Prompt

I am a {{age}}-year-old professional with an annual income of {{income}}. I want to get my personal finances in order and create a plan to achieve my financial goals. My primary financial goal is to {{financial_goal}}.

Act as a certified financial planner (CFP). Your task is to help me create a personal financial plan.

The plan should cover:

1.  **Budgeting & Cash Flow:**
    *   Help me create a monthly budget to track my income and expenses. What budgeting method should I use? (e.g., the 50/30/20 rule).

2.  **Debt Management:**
    *   I have the following debts: {{list_of_debts}}. What is the best strategy for paying off my debt? (e.g., the debt snowball or the debt avalanche method).

3.  **Emergency Fund:**
    *   How much should I have in my emergency fund? (e.g., 3-6 months of living expenses). Where should I keep it?

4.  **Retirement Savings:**
    *   How much should I be saving for retirement? 
    *   I have a {{retirement_account_type}} (e.g., 401(k), IRA). What should I be investing in within that account? (e.g., low-cost index funds).

5.  **Investing for Other Goals:**
    *   How should I invest for my other financial goals? (e.g., a down payment on a house).

6.  **Insurance:**
    *   What types of insurance do I need? (e.g., health, life, disability).

**Output Format:**

Please generate the personal financial plan as a detailed and actionable guide in Markdown. The plan should be a step-by-step guide to improving my financial health.

**Constraints:**

*   The advice must be sound and based on established financial planning principles.
*   The plan must be personalized to my specific situation and goals.
*   The tone should be that of a trusted and knowledgeable financial advisor.

## Tips

A great prompt for anyone who wants to take control of their personal finances. It provides a comprehensive and actionable plan.
