# Create a Pitch Deck for a D2C Brand

Generate a data-driven, narrative-led seed round pitch deck outline to help D2C founders secure venture capital funding.

Variables: brand_name, product_type, traction_details

## Prompt

I am the founder of a new direct-to-consumer (D2C) brand, {{brand_name}}, that sells {{product_type}}. We have early traction ({{traction_details}}) and are looking to raise a seed round of funding. I need to create a compelling pitch deck.

Act as a VC who specializes in consumer brands. Your task is to create a pitch deck for my D2C brand.

The pitch deck should tell a powerful story about our brand and should include the following slides:

1.  **The Vision:**
    *   A big, inspiring vision for our brand.

2.  **The Problem:**
    *   What is the problem with the current market that we are solving?

3.  **Our Solution:**
    *   Our product and our brand as the solution.

4.  **The Product:**
    *   Beautiful photos and a detailed description of our product. What makes it special?

5.  **The Brand:**
    *   What does our brand stand for? Who is our target customer?

6.  **The Market:**
    *   An analysis of the market size and trends.

7.  **Traction:**
    *   A slide that showcases our early traction. This is the most important slide. It should include key metrics like revenue, customer growth, and customer love (e.g., NPS, reviews).

8.  **Business Model:**
    *   Our unit economics (COGS, contribution margin) and our customer lifetime value (LTV).

9.  **Go-to-Market Strategy:**
    *   How are we acquiring customers? What is our customer acquisition cost (CAC)?

10. **The Team:**
    *   Why are we the right team to build this brand?

11. **The Ask & Use of Funds:**
    *   How much are we raising and how will we use the money?

**Output Format:**

Please generate a detailed outline for the pitch deck in Markdown. For each slide, provide guidance on the content, visuals, and narrative.

**Constraints:**

*   The pitch deck must be visually beautiful and tell a strong brand story.
*   The deck must be data-driven, especially the traction and unit economics slides.
*   The tone should be passionate, ambitious, and credible.

## Tips

Pitching a D2C brand is different from pitching a SaaS company. This prompt provides a template for a D2C pitch deck that focuses on brand, product, and traction.
