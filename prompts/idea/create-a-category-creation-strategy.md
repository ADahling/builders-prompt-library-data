# Create a Category Creation Strategy

Develops a comprehensive category creation strategy and manifesto to define, launch, and dominate an entirely new market landscape.

Variables: startup_name, product_name

## Prompt

I am the founder of a startup, {{startup_name}}, that has developed a truly innovative new product, {{product_name}}. Our product is so different that it doesn't fit into any existing market category. I believe our biggest challenge and opportunity is not to compete in an existing market, but to create a new category.

Act as a category creation expert (in the style of the authors of "Play Bigger"). Your task is to create a strategy for designing and dominating a new market category.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **Define the Category:**
    *   Help me craft a name for our new category. The name should be descriptive, memorable, and position us as the leader.
    *   Write a "Point of View" (POV) that explains why the old way of doing things is no longer good enough and why this new category is the future.

2.  **The Category Blueprint:**
    *   Create a blueprint for the category that defines its boundaries, its core principles, and its ecosystem.

3.  **Product-Category Fit:**
    *   How do we ensure our product is the best possible embodiment of the new category? The product must solve the problem that the category is organized around.

4.  **Company-Category Fit:**
    *   How do we align our entire company (our culture, our marketing, our sales) around the mission of creating and dominating this new category?

5.  **The Lightning Strike:**
    *   Design a "lightning strike" event to launch the new category to the world. This is not just a product launch; it's a coordinated effort to evangelize the new category and our leadership position in it. This could involve a book, a major research report, a conference, and a PR blitz.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the category creation strategy as a detailed and inspiring Markdown document. The document should be a manifesto that can rally our team and the market around our new category.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be bold and visionary.
*   The advice must be practical and based on the principles of successful category creation.
*   The tone should be that of a revolutionary leader who is changing the world.

## Tips

Category creation is the ultimate offensive strategy. This prompt provides a playbook for companies that are looking to create and dominate a new market.
