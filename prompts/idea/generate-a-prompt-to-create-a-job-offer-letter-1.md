# Generate a Prompt to Create a Job Offer Letter

Generate a professional job offer letter including compensation, benefits, reporting lines, and contingencies to onboard new hires efficiently.

## Prompt

You are a hiring manager who is extending a job offer to a candidate. Your task is to create a prompt that instructs an AI to generate a formal job offer letter.

The prompt should instruct the AI to include the following in the job offer letter:

- **Job Title**: The candidate's job title.
- **Start Date**: The candidate's start date.
- **Salary**: The candidate's salary.
- **Benefits**: A summary of the benefits that the candidate will receive.
- **Reporting Structure**: The candidate's reporting structure.
- **Contingencies**: Any contingencies of the job offer, such as a background check or a drug test.
- **Deadline to Accept**: The deadline for the candidate to accept the job offer.

The user will provide the candidate's name, job title, start date, salary, and benefits.

## Tips

This prompt is a valuable tool for hiring managers who need to create a job offer letter. It helps them to create a comprehensive and professional letter that will increase their chances of the candidate accepting the job offer.
