# Estimate Time to Business Impact

Estimate when an initiative will actually show results: implementation phases, the adoption curve, and when to expect measurable impact.

## Prompt

Considering initiative: [describe]. Estimate when it shows results:

1. Implementation Timeline: Realistic, broken into phases.
2. Adoption Curve: How long until consistent usage?
3. Leading Indicators: Early signals within 2-4 weeks.
4. Lagging Indicators: When measurable business impact?
5. Best / Realistic / Worst Case scenarios.
6. Total Investment: Money, time, opportunity cost before payoff.
7. Break-Even: When return exceeds investment?
8. Kill Criteria: When to abandon if results aren't showing?

Be realistic, not optimistic.
