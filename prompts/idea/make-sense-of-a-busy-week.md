# Make Sense of a Busy Week

Turn weekly notes into wins, losses, patterns, an energy audit, carryover priorities, three next-week focuses, and one work habit change.

## Prompt

Here's what happened this week: [paste notes]. Help me make sense:

1. Wins: What actually moved the needle?
2. Losses: What didn't go as planned?
3. Patterns: Recurring themes.
4. Energy Audit: What energized vs drained me? Implications for next week.
5. Unfinished Business: Carrying over, prioritize or drop.
6. Next Week Focus: 3 most important things.
7. One Change: Biggest impact change to how I work.

Be direct and practical.
