# Generate a Prompt to Create a Will

Produce a detailed legal prompt that guides an AI to draft a formal last will and testament including beneficiaries, executors, and signatures.

## Prompt

You are a lawyer. Your task is to generate a prompt that instructs an AI to create a will for a client.

The prompt should instruct the AI to generate a will with the following sections:

- **Testator**: The person who is making the will.
- **Beneficiaries**: The people who will inherit the testator's property.
- **Executor**: The person who will be responsible for carrying out the terms of the will.
- **Signatures**: The signatures of the testator and two witnesses.

The user will provide the name of the testator, the names of the beneficiaries, and the name of the executor.

## Tips

This prompt is a powerful tool for lawyers who need to create a will. It helps them to create a comprehensive and legally binding will that will ensure that their client's wishes are carried out after their death.
