# Develop a Crisis Management and Communication Plan

Generate a comprehensive crisis management and communication template with response protocols, stakeholder messaging, and assessment tools.

Variables: company_name, industry

## Prompt

I am the CEO of {{company_name}}, a company in the {{industry}} industry. I want to be prepared for a potential crisis that could damage our reputation, finances, or operations. I need to develop a comprehensive crisis management and communication plan.

Act as a seasoned crisis management expert. Your task is to create a crisis management plan template that I can adapt for my company.

The plan should include the following sections:

1.  **Crisis Management Team:**
    *   Define the roles and responsibilities of the core crisis management team (e.g., Crisis Leader, Communications Lead, Legal Lead, Operations Lead).
2.  **Crisis Identification and Assessment:**
    *   Create a framework for identifying potential crisis scenarios (e.g., product recall, data breach, executive scandal, natural disaster).
    *   Develop a crisis assessment matrix to determine the severity and potential impact of a crisis.
3.  **Crisis Response Protocol:**
    *   Outline the initial steps to be taken within the first hour of a crisis being declared (the "Golden Hour").
    *   Establish a clear chain of command and decision-making process.
4.  **Crisis Communication Plan:**
    *   **Stakeholder Communication:** Identify all key stakeholders (employees, customers, investors, media, regulators) and the key messages for each.
    *   **Holding Statements:** Prepare pre-approved holding statements for different types of crises that can be released immediately to show that we are aware of the situation and are taking action.
    *   **Spokesperson:** Designate and train official company spokespeople.
    *   **Channel Strategy:** Define which communication channels will be used to disseminate information (e.g., press releases, social media, company website, internal emails).
5.  **Post-Crisis Review:**
    *   Outline a process for conducting a post-crisis review to identify lessons learned and improve the crisis management plan.

**Output Format:**

Please generate a detailed crisis management plan template in Markdown. The template should be easy to customize for my specific company.

**Constraints:**

*   The plan must be comprehensive and cover all critical aspects of crisis management.
*   The advice should be practical and actionable in a high-pressure situation.
*   The tone should be calm, authoritative, and proactive.

An essential document for any responsible organization. This prompt helps create a robust plan to navigate a crisis effectively.
