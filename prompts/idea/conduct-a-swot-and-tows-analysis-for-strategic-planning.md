# Conduct a SWOT and TOWS Analysis for Strategic Planning

Plan annual company strategy through guided SWOT questions, then use a TOWS matrix to brainstorm strategic options based on your answers.

Variables: company_name, industry

## Prompt

As the strategy lead for {{company_name}}, a company in the {{industry}} sector, I am preparing for our annual strategic planning process. I need to conduct a comprehensive SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) analysis and then use it to generate strategic options via a TOWS matrix.

Act as a strategy consultant. Your task is to guide me through this two-part process.

**Part 1: SWOT Analysis**

First, help me brainstorm the components of our SWOT analysis. For each of the four areas, ask me a series of questions to uncover the key factors. I will provide the answers.

*   **Strengths (Internal):** What are we good at? What unique assets do we have? (e.g., strong brand, talented team, proprietary technology).
*   **Weaknesses (Internal):** Where do we need to improve? What are we lacking? (e.g., high costs, outdated technology, lack of brand awareness).
*   **Opportunities (External):** What market trends can we take advantage of? (e.g., growing market, new technology, changing customer preferences).
*   **Threats (External):** What external factors could harm our business? (e.g., new competitors, economic downturn, changing regulations).

**Part 2: TOWS Matrix for Strategy Generation**

Once we have the SWOT analysis, guide me in creating a TOWS matrix to formulate strategic options. For each of the four strategic combinations, help me brainstorm specific actions:

*   **Strengths-Opportunities (SO - Maxi-Maxi):** How can we use our strengths to maximize opportunities? (e.g., "Launch our new product in the growing Asian market").
*   **Weaknesses-Opportunities (WO - Mini-Maxi):** How can we overcome our weaknesses by taking advantage of opportunities? (e.g., "Acquire a small tech company to fill our technology gap").
*   **Strengths-Threats (ST - Maxi-Mini):** How can we use our strengths to minimize threats? (e.g., "Leverage our strong brand to defend against new, low-cost competitors").
*   **Weaknesses-Threats (WT - Mini-Mini):** How can we minimize our weaknesses and avoid threats? (This is a defensive strategy, e.g., "Divest from our unprofitable business unit").

**Output Format:**

Please structure your response as an interactive workshop. First, you will ask me the questions for the SWOT analysis. After I respond, you will then generate the TOWS matrix with the brainstormed strategic options, presented in a clear Markdown table.

**Constraints:**

*   The process must be collaborative, with the AI acting as a facilitator.
*   The final output should be a set of concrete, actionable strategic initiatives.
*   The tone should be analytical, structured, and insightful.

## Tips

This prompt takes the classic SWOT analysis a step further by using the TOWS matrix to make it actionable and strategic.
