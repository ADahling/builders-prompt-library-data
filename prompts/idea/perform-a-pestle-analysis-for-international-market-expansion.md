# Perform a PESTLE Analysis for International Market Expansion

Evaluate macro-environmental risks and opportunities in a target foreign market to inform strategic international expansion decisions.

Variables: company_name, company_type, country_name

## Prompt

I am the international strategy manager for {{company_name}}, a {{company_type}} looking to expand into {{country_name}}. Before we make a final decision, I need to conduct a thorough PESTLE (Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental) analysis to understand the macro-environmental factors in this new market.

Act as an international business analyst. Your task is to conduct a detailed PESTLE analysis for {{country_name}}.

For each of the six PESTLE factors, you need to research and provide a detailed analysis of the key trends and their potential impact on our business. You should cover:

*   **Political:**
    *   Government stability and political climate.
    *   Trade policies, tariffs, and tax regulations.
    *   Potential for political unrest or instability.
*   **Economic:**
    *   Economic growth rate (GDP), inflation, and interest rates.
    *   Exchange rates and currency stability.
    *   Consumer spending power and income distribution.
*   **Social:**
    *   Demographics, population growth, and age distribution.
    *   Cultural norms, values, and consumer lifestyles.
    *   Language, education levels, and social mobility.
*   **Technological:**
    *   Level of technological adoption and infrastructure (e.g., internet penetration, mobile connectivity).
    *   Innovation ecosystem and government support for R&D.
    *   Automation and AI trends in the local industry.
*   **Legal:**
    *   Labor laws and employment regulations.
    *   Consumer protection laws and data privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR equivalent).
    *   Intellectual property laws.
*   **Environmental:**
    *   Environmental regulations and sustainability standards.
    *   Climate change and its potential impact on supply chains or operations.
    *   Consumer attitudes towards green products and corporate social responsibility.

**Output Format:**

Please generate a comprehensive report in Markdown. The report should have a clear section for each of the six PESTLE factors. At the end of the report, provide a summary of the key opportunities and threats for our business in {{country_name}} and a final recommendation on whether to proceed with the expansion.

**Constraints:**

*   The analysis must be based on up-to-date, credible sources (you can cite your sources if possible).
*   The insights must be specific to {{country_name}} and relevant to our business.
*   The final recommendation should be well-reasoned and based on the analysis.

## Tips

A research-intensive prompt that requires the AI to act as a country-specific analyst, perfect for companies considering international expansion.
