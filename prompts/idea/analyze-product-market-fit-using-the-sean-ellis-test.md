# Analyze Product-Market Fit Using the Sean Ellis Test

Plan a Sean Ellis product-market fit survey with question wording, delivery methods, audience selection, and guidance on the 40% benchmark.

Variables: startup_name, product_name, number_of_users

## Prompt

As the founder of a startup, {{startup_name}}, that has recently launched a new product, {{product_name}}, I need to assess our product-market fit (PMF). We have acquired our first {{number_of_users}} users, and I want to use a quantitative method to measure our PMF.

I need you to act as a growth hacking expert and guide me through the process of using the Sean Ellis test to measure product-market fit. Your guidance should be a step-by-step plan that I can execute with my team.

The plan should include:

1.  **Survey Design:**
    *   Provide the exact wording for the one-question survey: "How would you feel if you could no longer use our product?" with the multiple-choice answers: "Very disappointed," "Somewhat disappointed," or "Not disappointed."
    *   Suggest a few follow-up open-ended questions to gather qualitative feedback, such as "What is the main benefit you receive from our product?" and "How could we improve our product for you?"
2.  **Survey Implementation:**
    *   Recommend the best way to deliver the survey to our users (e.g., in-app pop-up, email survey).
    *   Suggest the target audience for the survey (e.g., users who have been active in the last two weeks).
3.  **Data Analysis & Interpretation:**
    *   Explain the 40% benchmark: if over 40% of users say they would be "Very disappointed," it's a strong indicator of product-market fit.
    *   Describe how to analyze the qualitative feedback from the open-ended questions to identify our core value proposition and areas for improvement.
4.  **Action Plan:**
    *   Based on the results, what are the next steps? If we have PMF, how do we double down on growth? If we don't have PMF, how do we iterate on the product to get there?

**Output Format:**

Please generate a detailed plan in Markdown format. The plan should be a practical guide that I can share with my team.

**Constraints:**

*   The plan should be easy to understand for a non-technical founder.
*   The advice should be actionable and focused on helping us make data-driven decisions.
*   The tone should be encouraging and expert.
