# Create a Board Deck for a Quarterly Board Meeting

Generate a comprehensive Markdown template for a Series A board deck to facilitate strategic discussions and transparent financial reporting.

Variables: startup_name

## Prompt

I am the CEO of a Series A startup, {{startup_name}}. I need to prepare the deck for our upcoming quarterly board meeting. I want the deck to be clear, concise, and facilitate a strategic discussion with my board members.

Act as an experienced CEO who has run many board meetings. Your task is to create a template for a quarterly board deck.

The template should have the following slides:

1.  **Title Slide:** (Company Name, Q[X] YYYY Board Meeting)
2.  **Agenda:** (What will be covered in the meeting)
3.  **CEO's Opening:** (A high-level summary of the quarter - the good, the bad, and the ugly. What is the #1 thing you want to discuss in this meeting?)
4.  **Financials:**
    *   A summary of the P&L for the quarter vs. plan.
    *   A cash flow statement and runway projection.
5.  **SaaS Metrics Dashboard:**
    *   A one-page dashboard of our key SaaS metrics (MRR, ARR, Churn, LTV, CAC, etc.) with trends over time.
6.  **Sales & Marketing Review:**
    *   A review of the sales and marketing funnel for the quarter. What worked? What didn't?
7.  **Product & Engineering Review:**
    *   A review of the product roadmap. What did we ship? What is the impact?
    *   A summary of key engineering metrics (e.g., uptime, performance).
8.  **Team:**
    *   An update on key hires, departures, and overall team morale.
9.  **Strategic Deep Dive:**
    *   A deep dive into one or two key strategic topics that you want to discuss with the board (e.g., a new pricing proposal, a competitive threat, a potential partnership).
10. **The Ask:**
    *   What specific help or advice do you need from the board?
11. **Appendix:**
    *   Detailed financial statements and other supporting data.

**Output Format:**

Please generate a detailed board deck template in Markdown. For each slide, provide guidance on what content to include and how to present it. The goal is to create a deck that tells a story and facilitates a productive conversation.

**Constraints:**

*   The deck should be concise. The goal is to have a conversation, not to read slides.
*   The data should be presented clearly and visually where possible.
*   The tone should be transparent, strategic, and forward-looking.

## Tips

A well-run board meeting is a huge asset for a CEO. This prompt provides a template for creating a board deck that drives a strategic conversation.
