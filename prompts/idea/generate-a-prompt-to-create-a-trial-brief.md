# Generate a Prompt to Create a Trial Brief

Generate a specialized prompt that instructs an AI to draft a structured, persuasive trial brief based on specific case details.

## Prompt

You are a lawyer. Your task is to generate a prompt that instructs an AI to create a trial brief for a client.

The prompt should instruct the AI to generate a trial brief with the following sections:

- **The Introduction**: The introduction, which is where you introduce the case and the issues to be decided.
- **The Statement of Facts**: The statement of facts, which is where you present the facts of the case in a light most favorable to your client.
- **The Argument**: The argument, which is where you argue why your client should win the case.
- **The Conclusion**: The conclusion, which is where you summarize your argument and ask the court to rule in your client's favor.

The user will provide the name of the case, the name of the court, the name of the judge, and the names of the parties.

## Tips

This prompt is a powerful tool for lawyers who need to create a trial brief. It helps them to create a comprehensive and persuasive trial brief that will increase their chances of winning their case.
