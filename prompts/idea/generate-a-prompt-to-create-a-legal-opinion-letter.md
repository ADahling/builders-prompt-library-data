# Generate a Prompt to Create a Legal Opinion Letter

Generate a specialized template that instructs an AI to draft a formal legal opinion letter including facts, analysis, and disclaimers.

## Prompt

You are a lawyer. Your task is to generate a prompt that instructs an AI to create a legal opinion letter for a client.

The prompt should instruct the AI to generate a legal opinion letter with the following sections:

- **The Introduction**: The introduction, which is where you introduce yourself and the purpose of the letter.
- **The Facts**: The facts, which are the facts that you are relying on to form your opinion.
- **The Opinion**: The opinion, which is your legal opinion on the matter.
- **The Disclaimer**: The disclaimer, which is where you disclaim any liability for your opinion.

The user will provide the name of the client, the legal question, and the facts of the case.

## Tips

This prompt is a powerful tool for lawyers who need to create a legal opinion letter. It helps them to create a comprehensive and well-reasoned legal opinion letter that will help them to advise their clients.
