# Develop a Pricing Page That Converts

Generate a high-conversion B2B SaaS pricing page blueprint using CRO principles, detailed wireframes, and persuasive messaging strategies.

Variables: product_name, tier_1, tier_2, tier_3

## Prompt

I am the product marketing manager for a B2B SaaS product, {{product_name}}. We have a tiered pricing model ({{tier_1}}, {{tier_2}}, {{tier_3}}), but our pricing page is not converting well. I need to redesign it to be clearer, more persuasive, and more effective at driving sign-ups.

Act as a conversion rate optimization (CRO) expert and a UX/UI designer. Your task is to provide a detailed blueprint for a high-converting pricing page.

The blueprint should cover:

1.  **Page Structure & Layout:**
    *   Provide a wireframe or a detailed structural outline of the pricing page. This should include the key sections in order: headline, social proof, pricing grid, FAQ, etc.

2.  **The Pricing Grid:**
    *   How should we design the pricing grid to make it easy for users to compare the different tiers?
    *   Should we highlight a "most popular" plan? How?
    *   Should we offer a monthly/annual toggle? What is the best practice for displaying the savings for an annual plan?

3.  **Feature Comparison:**
    *   How do we present the features in each tier without overwhelming the user? Should we have a detailed feature comparison table? If so, how should it be designed?

4.  **Copywriting & Messaging:**
    *   Craft a compelling headline and sub-headline for the pricing page.
    *   For each pricing tier, suggest a name and a short description that speaks to the target user for that tier.
    *   What should the call-to-action (CTA) buttons say? (e.g., "Start Free Trial" vs. "Buy Now").

5.  **Trust & Social Proof:**
    *   Where should we place customer testimonials, logos of well-known customers, or security badges to build trust?

6.  **The FAQ Section:**
    *   What are the most common questions users have about pricing? Provide a list of 5-7 questions to include in the FAQ section to overcome objections.

**Output Format:**

Please generate a detailed blueprint in Markdown. The blueprint should be a mix of design recommendations, copywriting suggestions, and strategic advice. It should be a complete guide to creating a best-in-class pricing page.

**Constraints:**

*   The advice must be based on proven CRO principles and UX best practices.
*   The recommendations must be specific and actionable.
*   The tone should be persuasive, data-driven, and user-centric.

## Tips

The pricing page is one of the most critical pages on any SaaS website. This prompt provides a comprehensive guide to optimizing it for conversions.
