# Generate a Prompt to Create an Email Marketing Campaign

Construct an AI prompt that generates a complete multi-stage email marketing sequence including audience analysis, copy, and calls to action.

## Prompt

You are an email marketing manager. Your task is to generate a prompt that instructs an AI to create an email marketing campaign for a new product.

The prompt should instruct the AI to generate a campaign with the following components:

- **Target Audience**: A description of the target audience for the campaign.
- **Email Sequence**: A sequence of emails to be sent to the target audience.
- **Email Copy**: The copy for each email in the sequence.
- **Call to Action**: The call to action for each email in the sequence.

The user will provide the name of the product, a description of the product, and the target audience.

## Tips

This prompt is a powerful tool for email marketing managers who are planning a new campaign. It helps them to create a comprehensive plan that will increase their chances of success.
