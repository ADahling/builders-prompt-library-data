# Develop a Change Management Plan Using Kotter's 8-Step Model

Generate a comprehensive, eight-stage change management strategy using Kotter’s model to overcome resistance and ensure organizational buy-in.

Variables: company_name, type_of_change, number_of_employees

## Prompt

I am the leader of a major change initiative at my company, {{company_name}}. We are implementing a new {{type_of_change}}, which will impact {{number_of_employees}} employees across the organization. I anticipate resistance to this change and need to develop a structured change management plan to ensure a smooth transition.

Act as an experienced change management consultant. Your task is to guide me in creating a change management plan based on John Kotter's 8-Step Model for Leading Change.

For each of Kotter's 8 steps, you should:

1.  **Explain the Step:** Briefly describe the purpose of the step.
2.  **Provide Actionable Recommendations:** Suggest specific actions that I and my leadership team can take to execute this step effectively within our organization.

**Kotter's 8 Steps:**

1.  **Create a Sense of Urgency:** (How do we make people feel that the change is necessary right now?)
2.  **Build a Guiding Coalition:** (Who are the key people we need to get on board to lead the change?)
3.  **Form a Strategic Vision and Initiatives:** (What is our vision for the future state? How can we communicate it clearly and concisely?)
4.  **Enlist a Volunteer Army:** (How do we empower a broad base of employees to get involved and drive the change?)
5.  **Enable Action by Removing Barriers:** (What obstacles—like outdated processes or resistant managers—do we need to remove?)
6.  **Generate Short-Term Wins:** (How can we create and celebrate visible, short-term successes to build momentum?)
7.  **Sustain Acceleration:** (How do we build on the momentum of short-term wins and tackle bigger challenges?)
8.  **Institute Change:** (How do we anchor the new ways of working into the company culture?)

**Output Format:**

Please generate a detailed change management plan in Markdown. The plan should be a step-by-step guide that I can use to lead my organization through this transition.

**Constraints:**

*   The recommendations must be practical and tailored to a corporate environment.
*   The plan should be comprehensive and cover all aspects of leading a major change initiative.
*   The tone should be authoritative, empathetic, and strategic.

## Tips

A classic and robust framework for managing organizational change, this prompt provides a clear structure for leaders to follow.
