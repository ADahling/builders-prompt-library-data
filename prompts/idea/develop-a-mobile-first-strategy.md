# Develop a Mobile-First Strategy

Architect a comprehensive mobile-first strategy including technical platform selection, UX design principles, and a phased implementation roadmap.

Variables: company_name

## Prompt

I am the Head of Digital at {{company_name}}, a company whose customers are increasingly interacting with us on their mobile devices. Our current website is not optimized for mobile, and our mobile app has limited functionality. I need to develop a comprehensive mobile-first strategy.

Act as a mobile strategy consultant. Your task is to create a mobile-first strategy for my company.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **The Case for Mobile-First:**
    *   A brief analysis of our customer behavior on mobile vs. desktop. Why is a mobile-first strategy critical for our business?

2.  **Responsive Web vs. Mobile App vs. Progressive Web App (PWA):**
    *   Explain the pros and cons of each approach.
    *   Recommend the right mobile strategy for us. Should we focus on a responsive website, a native mobile app, a PWA, or a combination?

3.  **Mobile User Experience (UX):**
    *   What are the key principles of mobile-first design? (e.g., simplicity, clear navigation, large touch targets).
    *   How do we design a user experience that is optimized for the small screen and the mobile context?

4.  **Mobile-Specific Features:**
    *   What features should we build that take advantage of the unique capabilities of mobile devices? (e.g., location services, push notifications, camera).

5.  **Mobile Marketing:**
    *   How do we acquire and engage users on mobile? (e.g., App Store Optimization, mobile advertising, SMS marketing).

6.  **The Roadmap:**
    *   A high-level roadmap for implementing our new mobile-first strategy.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the mobile-first strategy as a detailed and structured report in Markdown. The report should be a complete guide to transforming our business to be mobile-first.

**Constraints:**

*   The recommendation for responsive vs. app vs. PWA must be well-justified.
*   The strategy must be customer-centric and focused on the mobile user experience.
*   The tone should be strategic, forward-looking, and practical.

## Tips

In today's world, every company needs a mobile strategy. This prompt provides a framework for developing a comprehensive mobile-first approach.
