# Create a Stakeholder Communication Plan for a Controversial Public Project

Generate a strategic, multi-channel communication plan to manage public sentiment, mitigate controversy, and build trust for sensitive projects.

Variables: project_name

## Prompt

I am the communications director for a government agency responsible for a new public infrastructure project: {{project_name}}. The project is controversial and has attracted significant public attention and opposition from community groups and environmental organizations.

Act as a crisis communications and public relations expert. Your task is to develop a robust stakeholder communication plan to manage public perception, address concerns, and build trust.

The plan must include:

1.  **Stakeholder Mapping & Analysis:**
    *   Identify all key stakeholder groups (e.g., local residents, business owners, elected officials, media, opposition groups).
    *   For each group, analyze their primary concerns, level of influence, and current sentiment towards the project.
2.  **Core Messaging & Narrative:**
    *   Develop a clear and consistent core narrative for the project that highlights its benefits and addresses the main points of controversy head-on.
    *   Craft key messages tailored to each stakeholder group.
    *   Prepare a Q&A document that anticipates and provides transparent answers to the toughest questions.
3.  **Communication Channels & Tactics:**
    *   Recommend a multi-channel communication strategy, including:
        *   **Public Meetings & Town Halls:** How to structure them for productive dialogue.
        *   **Media Relations:** Proactive outreach to journalists, press releases, and media briefing materials.
        *   **Digital & Social Media:** A content plan for the project website and social media channels to provide updates and counter misinformation.
        *   **Direct Outreach:** A plan for one-on-one briefings with key influencers and community leaders.
4.  **Crisis Response Protocol:**
    *   Outline a protocol for responding to negative press, protests, or other crisis situations. Who is the designated spokesperson? What is the approval process for public statements?

**Output Format:**

Please generate a comprehensive communication plan in Markdown. The plan should be detailed, strategic, and ready to be implemented by a communications team.

**Constraints:**

*   The plan must demonstrate a sophisticated understanding of public relations and crisis management.
*   The tone should be proactive, transparent, and empathetic to stakeholder concerns.
*   The strategies must be ethical and aimed at fostering genuine dialogue, not just spinning the narrative.
