# ACT AS — Give the AI a Specific Persona

Set up a task-specific AI persona with a role, audience, experience, priorities, and frustrations to shape app-building decisions.

## Prompt

For this task, act as a [ROLE] who:
- Works at [TYPE OF COMPANY] serving [SPECIFIC AUDIENCE]
- Has [X] years of experience with [SPECIFIC TECHNOLOGIES]
- Cares most about [PRIORITIES: security, performance, simplicity, UX]
- Has specific pain points: [LIST 2-3 REAL FRUSTRATIONS]

Example:
"Act as a solo developer who just launched their first SaaS on Lovable. You have 47 users, no funding, and you need to add Stripe payments without breaking anything. You're paranoid about security because you read that article about vibe-coded apps getting hacked. You have 3 hours to implement this."

This context shapes EVERY decision: what to build, how to build it, what to skip, and what to prioritize. Without it, the AI builds for a generic enterprise company.

## Tips

From Reddit r/lovable — specificity beats instructions. A persona with pain points produces better code than a list of requirements. Source: reddit.com/r/lovable/comments/1px6vpg
