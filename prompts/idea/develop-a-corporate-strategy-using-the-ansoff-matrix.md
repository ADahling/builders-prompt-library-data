# Develop a Corporate Strategy Using the Ansoff Matrix

Facilitate a strategic planning session to identify growth opportunities across the four quadrants of the Ansoff Matrix.

Variables: company_name, industry, core_products, primary_market

## Prompt

I am the CEO of {{company_name}}, an established company in the {{industry}} industry. We are looking to define our growth strategy for the next 3-5 years. I want to use the Ansoff Matrix framework to explore our strategic options systematically.

Act as a senior corporate strategist. Your task is to facilitate a strategic planning session for my executive team using the Ansoff Matrix. Our current core products are {{core_products}} and our primary market is {{primary_market}}.

For each of the four quadrants of the Ansoff Matrix, I need you to:

1.  **Explain the Quadrant:** Clearly define the strategic option (Market Penetration, Product Development, Market Development, Diversification).
2.  **Ask Probing Questions:** Pose a series of strategic questions to my team to brainstorm specific initiatives within that quadrant.
3.  **Provide Examples:** Give relevant examples of companies that have successfully executed a strategy in that quadrant.

**The Four Quadrants:**

*   **Market Penetration (Existing Products, Existing Markets):**
    *   *Questions:* How can we increase our market share with our current products? Can we increase usage frequency or purchase value among our existing customers?
*   **Product Development (New Products, Existing Markets):**
    *   *Questions:* What new products or services can we develop for our existing customer base? Are there any customer needs that we are not currently meeting?
*   **Market Development (Existing Products, New Markets):**
    *   *Questions:* Can we sell our existing products to new customer segments or in new geographic regions? How can we adapt our marketing and sales for these new markets?
*   **Diversification (New Products, New Markets):**
    *   *Questions:* What new products could we create for new markets? This is the riskiest strategy, so what are the potential synergies with our current business? (Related vs. Unrelated Diversification).

**Output Format:**

Please structure your response as a detailed agenda and guide for a strategic offsite meeting. The output should be a Markdown document that I can share with my leadership team to prepare for the session.

**Constraints:**

*   The questions must be thought-provoking and designed to stimulate strategic thinking.
*   The examples should be relevant and illustrative.
*   The final output should be a practical tool for facilitating a high-level strategy discussion.

## Tips

This prompt uses a classic strategy framework to help leaders think systematically about growth opportunities.
