# Pressure-Test a Plan Before Executing

Stress-test a plan before you commit to it: critical path dependencies, missing resources, and the failure modes you have not accounted for.

## Prompt

Here is my plan: [paste]. Pressure-test it:

1. Dependencies: What must happen first? Critical path.
2. Resource Check: People, tools, budget, access needed but not yet available.
3. Risk Assessment: Top 5 risks with likelihood, impact, mitigation.
4. Quick Wins: Items completable in 48 hours for momentum.
5. Delegation Map: What to delegate vs only I can do.
6. Decision Log: Decisions needed before execution. Flag ambiguity.
7. Week 1 Sprint: Day-by-day plan for first week.

Be blunt about what's unrealistic.
