# Develop a Sales Compensation Plan for an AE Team

Design a comprehensive B2B SaaS sales compensation plan including OTE structures, tiered quotas, and performance incentives for AEs.

Variables: company_name, number_of_AEs

## Prompt

I am the new VP of Sales at a B2B SaaS company, {{company_name}}. I need to design a new sales compensation plan for our team of {{number_of_AEs}} Account Executives (AEs) for the upcoming fiscal year. The goal is to motivate the team to drive new business and align their incentives with the company's revenue goals.

Act as a sales compensation consultant. Your task is to create a comprehensive and motivating sales compensation plan.

The plan should include:

1.  **On-Target Earnings (OTE):**
    *   Help me determine the appropriate OTE for our AEs, considering their experience level and our industry benchmarks. The OTE should be a mix of base salary and variable commission.
    *   What is a typical split between base and variable for a SaaS AE? (e.g., 50/50, 60/40).

2.  **Quota & Commission Rate:**
    *   How do we set a realistic but challenging annual quota for each AE?
    *   What should be the commission rate on new Annual Contract Value (ACV)? Should the rate be a flat percentage or tiered?

3.  **Accelerators & Bonuses:**
    *   How can we use accelerators (i.e., higher commission rates for exceeding quota) to reward top performers?
    *   Should we include any bonuses for specific behaviors, such as signing multi-year deals or selling a new product?

4.  **Clawbacks & Decelerators:**
    *   Should there be any clawbacks for deals that churn early?
    *   Should there be decelerators for AEs who are performing well below quota?

5.  **The Plan Document:**
    *   Generate a clear and concise sales compensation plan document that can be shared with the AEs. The document should explain all the components of the plan and include examples of how the commission is calculated.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the sales compensation plan as a detailed Markdown document. The document should be a complete guide that I can use to roll out the new plan to my team.

**Constraints:**

*   The plan must be fair, motivating, and easy to understand.
*   The mechanics of the plan should be clearly explained with examples.
*   The tone should be professional, transparent, and motivating.

## Tips

A well-designed sales comp plan is a powerful lever for growth. This prompt provides a structured way to create one.
