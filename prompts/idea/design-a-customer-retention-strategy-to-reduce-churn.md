# Design a Customer Retention Strategy to Reduce Churn

Plan a B2B SaaS retention strategy with customer health scoring, onboarding, training, proactive outreach, and feedback collection.

Variables: company_name, churn_rate, product_description, customer_profile

## Prompt

As the Head of Customer Success for a B2B SaaS company, {{company_name}}, I am concerned about our customer churn rate, which is currently at {{churn_rate}}% per month. Our product is a {{product_description}}, and our customers are {{customer_profile}}.

I need you to act as a customer success consultant and design a comprehensive customer retention strategy to reduce our churn rate. The strategy should be data-driven and focus on proactive measures.

The strategy should include the following initiatives:

1.  **Customer Health Scoring:**
    *   Define a customer health score model. What are the key metrics and signals we should track to predict churn risk? (e.g., product usage, support tickets, NPS scores).
    *   How should we use the health score to trigger proactive outreach?
2.  **Onboarding & Training:**
    *   How can we improve our customer onboarding process to ensure users experience the value of our product quickly?
    *   Suggest a plan for ongoing customer training and education (e.g., webinars, tutorials, office hours).
3.  **Proactive Communication & Engagement:**
    *   What is a good cadence for checking in with customers?
    *   What kind of content should we share to keep them engaged and informed about new features?
4.  **Feedback & Feature Requests:**
    *   How can we systematically collect customer feedback and feature requests?
    *   How should we close the loop with customers to let them know we are listening?
5.  **At-Risk Customer Playbook:**
    *   Create a playbook for our customer success managers to follow when a customer is identified as at-risk of churning.
    *   What are the steps to take to re-engage the customer and address their issues?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the customer retention strategy as a detailed report in Markdown. For each initiative, provide a clear description, actionable steps, and the expected impact on churn.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be tailored to a B2B SaaS business.
*   The recommendations should be practical and implementable for a customer success team.
*   The tone should be empathetic, strategic, and focused on delivering customer value.

## Tips

This prompt is for experienced managers who need a structured approach to tackling a high churn rate.
