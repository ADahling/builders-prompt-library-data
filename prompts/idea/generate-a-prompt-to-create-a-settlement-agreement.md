# Generate a Prompt to Create a Settlement Agreement

Get an AI prompt for drafting a client settlement agreement covering parties, settlement amount, release of claims, confidentiality, and signatures.

## Prompt

You are a lawyer. Your task is to generate a prompt that instructs an AI to create a settlement agreement for a client.

The prompt should instruct the AI to generate a settlement agreement with the following sections:

- **The Parties**: The parties to the settlement agreement.
- **The Settlement Amount**: The amount of money that will be paid to settle the dispute.
- **The Release of Claims**: A release of all claims that the parties have against each other.
- **The Confidentiality Clause**: A clause that requires the parties to keep the terms of the settlement agreement confidential.
- **The Signatures**: The signatures of the parties.

The user will provide the names of the parties, the settlement amount, and the terms of the release of claims.

## Tips

This prompt is a powerful tool for lawyers who need to create a settlement agreement. It helps them to create a comprehensive and legally binding settlement agreement that will resolve their client's dispute.
