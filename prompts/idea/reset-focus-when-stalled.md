# Reset Focus When Stalled

Work out why a project has stalled: an honest account of real progress, the precise bottleneck, and the next concrete move.

## Prompt

I feel stuck on [describe]. Help reset:

1. Honest Assessment: What's actually been accomplished vs what it feels like?
2. Bottleneck: What specific thing is blocking? Name it precisely.
3. Minimum Viable Next Step: Smallest action in 30 minutes to create momentum.
4. Energy Audit: Stuck because hard, unclear, boring, or scary? Solution differs.
5. Environment Check: Time of day, location, tools, distractions working against me?
6. Reframe: If advising someone else, what would I tell them?
7. Commitment: One specific thing to complete today with deadline.

No judgment. Just clarity.
