# Develop a Product Roadmap for a Cybersecurity Training Platform

Plan a cybersecurity training roadmap with module sequencing, professional feedback, and adjustments for evolving threats and regulations.

Variables: security_professionals

## Prompt

Develop a detailed product roadmap for a cybersecurity training platform aimed at {{security_professionals}} seeking advanced skills.

Context:
- The platform provides interactive training modules and simulations to enhance cybersecurity expertise.
- Aligning roadmap milestones with emerging threats and industry certification requirements is crucial for platform relevance.

Provide:
1. Structured approach to sequencing cybersecurity training modules and assessments.
2. Strategies for integrating feedback from security professionals to enhance training content and tools.
3. Methods for adapting the roadmap in response to evolving cybersecurity threats and regulatory changes.

## Tips

Use this prompt to guide the development of a comprehensive cybersecurity training platform that meets the diverse needs of security professionals.
