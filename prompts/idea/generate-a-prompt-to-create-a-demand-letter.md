# Generate a Prompt to Create a Demand Letter

Create a demand letter generator that details payment requirements, contact information, and legal threats to recover debts for clients.

## Prompt

You are a lawyer. Your task is to generate a prompt that instructs an AI to create a demand letter for a client.

The prompt should instruct the AI to generate a demand letter with the following sections:

- **Your Contact Information**: Your name, address, phone number, and email address.
- **The Recipient's Contact Information**: The name, address, phone number, and email address of the person or company that you are sending the letter to.
- **The Amount of Money Owed**: The amount of money that the recipient owes your client.
- **A Demand for Payment**: A demand that the recipient pay the amount of money owed.
- **A Threat of Legal Action**: A threat of legal action if the recipient does not pay the amount of money owed.
- **Your Signature**: Your electronic signature.

The user will provide their contact information, the recipient's contact information, and the amount of money that the recipient owes their client.

## Tips

This prompt is a powerful tool for lawyers who need to create a demand letter. It helps them to create a comprehensive and legally binding demand letter that will increase their chances of getting their client paid.
