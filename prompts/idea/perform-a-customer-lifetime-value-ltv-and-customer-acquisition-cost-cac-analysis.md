# Perform a Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Analysis

Calculate and analyze LTV:CAC ratios to evaluate SaaS unit economics and determine strategic growth or optimization opportunities.

Variables: business_name

## Prompt

I am the founder of a subscription-based business, {{business_name}}. I need to understand the fundamental economics of my business to ensure we are building a sustainable company. I need to calculate and analyze our Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).

Act as a SaaS finance expert. I will provide you with the necessary data:

*   **Average Revenue Per Account (ARPA):** (per month)
*   **Gross Margin:** (%)
*   **Customer Churn Rate:** (per month)
*   **Total Sales & Marketing Spend:** (for a specific period)
*   **New Customers Acquired:** (in that same period)

Your task is to:

1.  **Calculate LTV:**
    *   Provide the formula for LTV: (ARPA * Gross Margin) / Customer Churn Rate.
    *   Calculate our LTV based on the data I provide.

2.  **Calculate CAC:**
    *   Provide the formula for CAC: Total Sales & Marketing Spend / New Customers Acquired.
    *   Calculate our CAC based on the data I provide.

3.  **Analyze the LTV:CAC Ratio:**
    *   Calculate the LTV:CAC ratio.
    *   Explain what this ratio means. What is a healthy LTV:CAC ratio for a SaaS business? (A common benchmark is 3:1 or higher).
    *   Interpret our LTV:CAC ratio. Is our business model sustainable? Are we spending too much or too little to acquire customers?

4.  **Provide Strategic Recommendations:**
    *   Based on the analysis, what are your recommendations? 
    *   If the ratio is too low, what are the levers we can pull to improve it? (e.g., increase prices, reduce churn, improve marketing efficiency).
    *   If the ratio is very high, does this mean we should be investing more aggressively in growth?

**Output Format:**

Please structure your response as a concise financial analysis memo. The memo should clearly explain the calculations and provide actionable strategic insights.

**Constraints:**

*   The formulas and calculations must be accurate.
*   The interpretation must be insightful and go beyond the numbers.
*   The recommendations must be practical and strategic.

## Tips

LTV:CAC is the god metric of many subscription businesses. This prompt helps users calculate it correctly and understand its strategic implications.
