# Create a Term Sheet for a Seed Round Financing

Generate a founder-friendly seed round term sheet using specific startup data and plain-English legal explanations to facilitate negotiation.

Variables: startup_name

## Prompt

I am the founder of an early-stage startup, {{startup_name}}. We have received interest from a lead investor for our seed funding round. The investor has asked me to prepare a draft of the term sheet.

Act as an experienced startup lawyer. Your task is to generate a founder-friendly seed round term sheet. The term sheet should be comprehensive but also easy to understand.

The term sheet should include all the standard sections:

1.  **Offering Terms:**
    *   **Issuer:** {{startup_name}}, Inc.
    *   **Amount of Financing:** (e.g., $1,500,000)
    *   **Type of Security:** (e.g., Series Seed Preferred Stock)
    *   **Pre-Money Valuation:** (e.g., $8,500,000)
    *   **Price Per Share:**

2.  **Terms of the Preferred Stock:**
    *   **Liquidation Preference:** (e.g., 1x, non-participating)
    *   **Conversion Rights:** (e.g., optional conversion, automatic conversion on IPO)
    *   **Voting Rights:** (e.g., voting as a single class with common stock)
    *   **Protective Provisions:** (List the key corporate actions that would require the approval of the preferred stockholders).

3.  **Board of Directors:**
    *   Specify the composition of the board post-financing (e.g., 2 common, 1 preferred).

4.  **Pro-Rata Rights:**
    *   Granting major investors the right to participate in future financing rounds.

5.  **Information Rights:**
    *   The company's obligation to provide financial statements to major investors.

6.  **No-Shop Agreement:**
    *   A clause preventing the company from soliciting other offers for a specified period.

**Output Format:**

Please generate a complete term sheet in Markdown. For each clause, you can add a brief, plain-English explanation of what it means and why it's included. This will help me understand the document I am creating.

**Constraints:**

*   The term sheet should be founder-friendly but still commercially reasonable.
*   The language should be clear and avoid excessive legal jargon where possible.
*   The final output should be a professional document that I can share with our potential investor.

## Tips

A term sheet is a critical legal document. This prompt helps founders understand and create a standard, founder-friendly version for a seed round.
