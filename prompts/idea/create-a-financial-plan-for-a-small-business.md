# Create a Financial Plan for a Small Business

Generate a comprehensive 12-month financial plan including startup costs, cash flow, and P&L statements to secure small business funding.

Variables: business_type, business_name

## Prompt

I am starting a new small business, a {{business_type}} called {{business_name}}. I need to create a financial plan to secure a small business loan and to guide my decisions in the first year of operation.

Act as a small business accountant. Your task is to help me create a 12-month financial plan.

The plan should include:

1.  **Startup Costs:**
    *   A detailed list of all the one-time costs required to start the business (e.g., equipment, licenses, legal fees, initial inventory).

2.  **Sales Forecast:**
    *   A month-by-month forecast of your sales revenue for the first year.

3.  **Expense Budget:**
    *   A month-by-month budget for all your operating expenses, including:
        *   **Fixed Costs:** (e.g., rent, salaries, insurance).
        *   **Variable Costs:** (e.g., cost of goods sold, marketing spend).

4.  **Projected Profit & Loss (P&L) Statement:**
    *   A month-by-month P&L statement showing your projected revenue, expenses, and profit/loss.

5.  **Projected Cash Flow Statement:**
    *   A month-by-month cash flow statement showing the flow of cash in and out of your business. This is critical for managing your cash balance and avoiding a cash crunch.

6.  **Break-Even Analysis:**
    *   A calculation of your break-even point - the level of sales you need to achieve to cover all your costs.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the financial plan as a set of interconnected Markdown tables. The plan should be easy for a non-financial person to understand. For each section, provide a brief explanation of what it is and why it's important.

**Constraints:**

*   The financial plan must be realistic and based on sound assumptions.
*   The formulas and calculations must be clearly explained.
*   The final output should be a professional document that can be included in a business plan or loan application.

## Tips

A solid financial plan is the foundation of any successful business. This prompt provides a template for creating one.
