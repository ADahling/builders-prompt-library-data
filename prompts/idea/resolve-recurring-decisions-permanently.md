# Resolve Recurring Decisions Permanently

Get a root cause analysis, decision framework, policy, and team message for recurring decisions, with exception rules and review triggers.

## Prompt

These decisions keep resurfacing: [describe]. Resolve permanently:

1. Root Cause: Why does this keep coming back?
2. Decision Framework: Simple decision tree or criteria anyone can use without escalating.
3. Policy Draft: Clear, concise policy statement.
4. Communication: Draft message to share with team.
5. Exception Handling: When exceptions are allowed and who approves.
6. Review Trigger: When to revisit (quarterly, after X events).

Goal: stop relitigating the same thing.
