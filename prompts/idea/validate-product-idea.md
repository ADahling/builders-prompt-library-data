# Validate a product idea

Get a product idea decision brief with risks, assumptions, interview questions, a small evidence test, and a go, revise, or stop recommendation.

## Prompt

Act as a product strategist. Evaluate {{product_idea}} for {{target_user}}. Identify the core problem, current alternatives, urgency signals, risks, and the smallest evidence test. Return a decision brief with assumptions, evidence needed, interview questions, and a go, revise, or stop recommendation.
