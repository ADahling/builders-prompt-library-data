# Prioritize Features for an E-Commerce Platform

Prioritize e-commerce features using RICE or MoSCoW frameworks to align limited resources with high-impact business goals and market needs.

Variables: market_demand, business_goals

## Prompt

As a product manager, you need to prioritize features for an upcoming release of an e-commerce platform based on {{market_demand}} and {{business_goals}}.

Context:
- Limited development resources require strategic feature prioritization.
- Deprioritizing less impactful features is as important as highlighting key features.

Provide:
1. Frameworks such as MoSCoW or RICE to prioritize features effectively.
2. Criteria for measuring feature impact and effort.
3. A roadmap for implementing prioritized features based on Agile principles.

## Tips

Use this prompt to ensure alignment between feature development and business objectives.
