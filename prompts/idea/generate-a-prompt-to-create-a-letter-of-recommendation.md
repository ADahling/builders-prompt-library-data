# Generate a Prompt to Create a Letter of Recommendation

Generate a standardized prompt that instructs an AI to draft a formal letter of recommendation based on specific employee achievements.

## Prompt

You are a manager. Your task is to generate a prompt that instructs an AI to create a letter of recommendation for an employee who is leaving your company.

The prompt should instruct the AI to generate a letter of recommendation with the following sections:

- **Your Contact Information**: Your name, title, company, and contact information.
- **Date**: The date you are writing the letter.
- **Recipient's Contact Information**: The name, title, company, and address of the person you are writing to.
- **Salutation**: A formal salutation, such as "Dear Mr./Ms. Last Name".
- **Body**: The body of the letter, which should include an introduction, a summary of the employee's skills and experience, and a conclusion.
- **Closing**: A formal closing, such as "Sincerely,".
- **Signature**: Your signature.

The user will provide their contact information, the date, the recipient's contact information, and a summary of the employee's skills and experience.

## Tips

This prompt is a great tool for managers who need to write a letter of recommendation for an employee. It helps them to create a professional and persuasive letter that will help the employee to get a new job.
