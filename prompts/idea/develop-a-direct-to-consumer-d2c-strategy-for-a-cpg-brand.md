# Develop a Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) Strategy for a CPG Brand

Create a comprehensive D2C transition roadmap for CPG brands that balances platform selection, marketing, and retail partner harmony.

Variables: brand_name

## Prompt

I am the brand manager for {{brand_name}}, a traditional CPG brand that has always sold our products through retail partners. I want to develop a direct-to-consumer (D2C) strategy to build a direct relationship with our customers and create a new revenue stream.

Act as a D2C e-commerce consultant. Your task is to create a D2C strategy for my brand.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **The Business Case for D2C:**
    *   Why should we go D2C? What are the potential benefits (e.g., higher margins, direct customer data) and risks (e.g., channel conflict with our retail partners)?

2.  **The E-commerce Platform:**
    *   What e-commerce platform should we use for our D2C store? (e.g., Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento). What are the pros and cons of each?

3.  **The D2C Product Offering:**
    *   Should we sell our entire product line online, or should we offer exclusive products or bundles that are only available on our D2C site?

4.  **Marketing & Customer Acquisition:**
    *   How will we drive traffic to our new D2C store? Recommend a digital marketing strategy, including SEO, content marketing, social media, and paid advertising.

5.  **Fulfillment & Logistics:**
    *   How will we handle warehousing, picking, packing, and shipping? Should we build our own fulfillment capabilities or outsource to a 3PL?

6.  **Managing Channel Conflict:**
    *   How do we launch a D2C channel without alienating our existing retail partners? Suggest strategies to minimize channel conflict.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the D2C strategy as a detailed and structured report in Markdown. The report should be a complete business plan for our new D2C venture.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be tailored to a traditional CPG brand that is new to D2C.
*   The plan must address the critical issue of channel conflict.
*   The tone should be strategic, practical, and customer-focused.

## Tips

For many CPG brands, D2C is the future. This prompt provides a comprehensive guide to making the transition.
