# Create a Business Plan for a SaaS Startup

Generate a comprehensive, VC-ready B2B SaaS business plan template designed to secure pre-seed funding through data-driven strategic sections.

Variables: startup_name

## Prompt

I am the founder of a new B2B SaaS startup, {{startup_name}}. I am in the process of writing a business plan to raise a pre-seed round of funding.

Act as a venture capitalist who reviews hundreds of business plans. Your task is to create a template for a SaaS business plan that will get a VC's attention.

The business plan should be a concise and compelling document (no more than 15-20 pages) and should include the following sections:

1.  **Executive Summary:**
    *   A one-page summary of the entire business plan.

2.  **The Problem:**
    *   A deep and insightful description of the problem that our target customer is facing.

3.  **The Solution:**
    *   Our product as the solution to that problem. How is it 10x better than the current alternatives?

4.  **The Product:**
    *   A more detailed description of our product, its key features, and our product roadmap.

5.  **The Market:**
    *   A bottoms-up analysis of the market size (TAM, SAM, SOM).

6.  **The Go-to-Market Strategy:**
    *   How will we acquire our first customers? What is our long-term GTM strategy? (e.g., PLG, sales-led).

7.  **The Business Model:**
    *   Our pricing and packaging. Our projected LTV and CAC.

8.  **The Team:**
    *   Why are we the right team to solve this problem?

9.  **The Financial Plan:**
    *   A 3-year financial projection (P&L and cash flow).

10. **The Ask:**
    *   How much are we raising and what are the key milestones we will achieve with the funding?

**Output Format:**

Please generate a detailed business plan template in Markdown. The template should have clear headings for each section and should include prompts and questions to guide me in filling it out.

**Constraints:**

*   The business plan must be concise, data-driven, and tell a compelling story.
*   The financial projections must be realistic and based on a clear set of assumptions.
*   The tone should be ambitious, confident, and credible.

## Tips

A great business plan is a critical tool for any startup founder. This prompt provides a template for a modern SaaS business plan.
