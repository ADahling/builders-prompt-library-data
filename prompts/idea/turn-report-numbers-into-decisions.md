# Turn Report Numbers Into Decisions

Read a report and produce decisions: the three headlines, the real trends, the anomalies worth chasing, and what to do next.

## Prompt

Here is a report: [paste numbers]. Turn into decisions:

1. Headlines: 3 most important things these numbers say.
2. Trends: Improving, declining, flat? Over what timeframe?
3. Anomalies: Anything unexpected needing investigation?
4. So What: For each headline, business implication and action.
5. Decisions: 2-3 specific decisions for this week.
6. Missing Data: What additional info needed for better decisions?
7. Executive Summary: 5-sentence version for leadership.

Don't describe numbers back. Tell me what they mean.
