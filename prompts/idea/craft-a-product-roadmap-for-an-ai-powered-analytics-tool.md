# Craft a Product Roadmap for an AI-Powered Analytics Tool

Develop a strategic product roadmap for an AI analytics tool by aligning feature development with market trends, user feedback, and competitive shifts.

Variables: industry_sector

## Prompt

You are responsible for crafting a product roadmap for an AI-powered analytics tool that aims to revolutionize {{industry_sector}}.

Context:
- The tool leverages machine learning algorithms to provide advanced data insights.
- Aligning feature releases with market trends and technological advancements is key for success.

Provide:
1. Structured approach to creating a comprehensive product roadmap.
2. Strategies for incorporating user feedback and market research into the roadmap.
3. Methods for adapting the roadmap based on evolving business needs and competitor analysis.

## Tips

Use this prompt to effectively communicate the product vision and milestone timeline to stakeholders.
