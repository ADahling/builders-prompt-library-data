# Conduct a Comprehensive Competitive Analysis

Generate a structured competitive analysis report detailing pricing, features, and market gaps to inform B2B SaaS product strategy.

Variables: product_name, target_audience, solve_a_problem, competitor_1, competitor_2, competitor_3

## Prompt

As a Product Manager for a new B2B SaaS platform, {{product_name}}, I need to conduct a thorough competitive analysis. Our product helps {{target_audience}} to {{solve_a_problem}}. We have identified our main competitors as {{competitor_1}}, {{competitor_2}}, and {{competitor_3}}.

I need you to act as a senior market analyst and generate a detailed competitive analysis report. The report should cover the following areas for each competitor:

*   **Product Offering:** Analyze their core features, functionality, and unique selling propositions (USPs).
*   **Pricing Strategy:** Detail their pricing tiers, subscription models, and any free trials or freemium offerings.
*   **Target Market:** Describe their primary customer segments and go-to-market strategy.
*   **Marketing & Sales:** Analyze their online presence, content marketing, social media strategy, and sales tactics.
*   **Strengths & Weaknesses:** Identify their key strengths and weaknesses based on the analysis above and customer reviews from sites like G2 and Capterra.

**Output Format:**

Please generate a detailed report in Markdown format. For each competitor, create a section with the following structure:

### [Competitor Name]

*   **Product Offering:**
*   **Pricing Strategy:**
*   **Target Market:**
*   **Marketing & Sales:**
*   **Strengths:**
*   **Weaknesses:**

After analyzing each competitor, provide a summary section that includes:

*   **Competitive Landscape Overview:** A high-level summary of the market and the competitive environment.
*   **Opportunities for {{product_name}}:** Identify gaps in the market and areas where our product can differentiate itself.
*   **Strategic Recommendations:** Suggest actionable strategies for product development, marketing, and sales to effectively compete in the market.

**Constraints:**

*   The analysis must be based on publicly available information.
*   The tone should be objective, data-driven, and strategic.
*   The final report should be comprehensive and provide actionable insights.

## Tips

This prompt is for users who have already identified their main competitors and need a structured framework for a deep-dive analysis.
