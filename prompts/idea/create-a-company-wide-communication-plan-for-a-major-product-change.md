# Create a Company-Wide Communication Plan for a Major Product Change

Generate a comprehensive, multi-channel communication strategy and timeline to manage internal and external stakeholder expectations during product shifts.

Variables: company_name, product_name, description_of_change

## Prompt

I am the Head of Product at {{company_name}}. We are about to make a major change to our product, {{product_name}}. We are {{description_of_change}}. This change will impact all of our customers and all of our internal teams. I need to create a comprehensive communication plan to manage this change.

Act as a communications expert. Your task is to create a communication plan for this major product change.

The plan should be a multi-channel, multi-audience plan that covers:

1.  **Internal Communication:**
    *   How and when will we communicate the change to our internal teams (sales, marketing, support, success)?
    *   What training do we need to provide to each team to prepare them for the change?

2.  **Customer Communication:**
    *   How will we communicate the change to our existing customers? The communication needs to be segmented by customer type.
    *   What channels will we use? (e.g., email, in-app messages, blog post, webinar).
    *   Craft the key messages for the customer communication. The messaging should be clear, transparent, and focus on the benefits of the change.

3.  **External Communication (PR & Marketing):**
    *   How will we communicate the change to the market, the press, and potential new customers?
    *   Should we issue a press release? Write a blog post?

4.  **The Timeline:**
    *   A detailed timeline for all the communication activities, starting from a few weeks before the launch to the day of the launch and beyond.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the communication plan as a detailed timeline and checklist in Markdown. The plan should be a complete guide to managing the communication for a major product change.

**Constraints:**

*   The communication must be proactive, transparent, and empathetic to the concerns of customers and employees.
*   The plan must be highly coordinated across all departments.
*   The tone should be confident, clear, and consistent.

## Tips

Communication is key to the success of any major product change. This prompt provides a framework for a comprehensive communication plan.
