# Cascading OKRs Across an Entire Engineering Department

Develop a scalable, bi-directional OKR framework to align engineering teams through structured workshops, measurable KRs, and tracking.

Variables: company_name, number_of_engineers, number_of_teams

## Prompt

I am the VP of Engineering at {{company_name}}, a fast-growing tech company with {{number_of_engineers}} engineers organized into {{number_of_teams}} teams. Our company has just set its top-level OKRs for the year. My goal is to cascade these OKRs down to each engineering team and individual engineer in a way that ensures alignment and accountability.

Act as an engineering leadership coach and OKR expert. Your task is to create a comprehensive plan for cascading OKRs throughout the engineering department.

The plan should include:

1.  **The Cascading Process:**
    *   Describe a top-down and bottom-up process for setting OKRs at each level: Department -> Team -> Individual.
    *   Explain how to ensure vertical alignment (team OKRs contributing to department OKRs) and horizontal alignment (collaboration between teams).
2.  **OKR Setting Workshops:**
    *   Provide an agenda and facilitation guide for running OKR setting workshops with engineering managers and their teams.
    *   Include exercises to help teams brainstorm ambitious and measurable Key Results.
3.  **Crafting High-Quality Engineering OKRs:**
    *   Provide best practices and examples of well-written OKRs for different types of engineering teams (e.g., platform, product, SRE).
    *   Explain how to balance product-focused KRs (e.g., shipping features) with engineering-focused KRs (e.g., improving reliability, reducing tech debt).
4.  **Tracking and Scoring OKRs:**
    *   Recommend a process and tools for tracking progress on OKRs throughout the quarter.
    *   Explain how to score OKRs at the end of the quarter and facilitate a retrospective to learn and improve the process.

**Output Format:**

Please generate a detailed guide in Markdown. Include templates for OKR tracking spreadsheets and workshop agendas. The guide should be a practical playbook for engineering leaders.

**Constraints:**

*   The process must be scalable for a growing engineering organization.
*   The advice should be practical and avoid overly bureaucratic or complex systems.
*   The tone should be authoritative, clear, and motivating.

## Tips

Aimed at senior engineering leaders, this prompt tackles the complex challenge of implementing OKRs at scale in a technical organization.
