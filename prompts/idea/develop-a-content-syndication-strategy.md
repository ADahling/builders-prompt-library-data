# Develop a Content Syndication Strategy

Create a comprehensive content syndication plan to scale lead generation, select distribution channels, and design automated nurture flows.

Variables: company_name

## Prompt

I am the content marketing manager at {{company_name}}. We have a library of high-quality content (e.g., ebooks, whitepapers, webinars), but we are struggling to get it in front of a larger audience. I want to develop a content syndication strategy to expand our reach and generate more leads.

Act as a content marketing and demand generation expert. Your task is to create a content syndication strategy for my company.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **Goals & Objectives:**
    *   What are our primary goals for content syndication? (e.g., lead generation, brand awareness, thought leadership).

2.  **Choosing the Right Content:**
    *   Which of our existing content assets are best suited for syndication?

3.  **Syndication Channels:**
    *   Recommend a mix of paid and organic syndication channels:
        *   **Paid Syndication Networks:** (e.g., NetLine, Madison Logic, TechTarget). What are the pros and cons of these platforms?
        *   **Organic Syndication:** (e.g., republishing our content on platforms like Medium, LinkedIn Articles, or with industry publications). How do we do this without getting penalized by Google for duplicate content?

4.  **Lead Nurturing:**
    *   The leads we get from content syndication will likely be cold. Design a lead nurturing email campaign to warm up these leads and move them down the sales funnel.

5.  **Measuring ROI:**
    *   How will we measure the return on investment (ROI) of our content syndication efforts? What are the key metrics to track? (e.g., cost per lead (CPL), lead-to-opportunity conversion rate).

**Output Format:**

Please generate the content syndication strategy as a detailed plan in Markdown. The plan should be a practical guide for a content marketer looking to expand their reach.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be realistic for a company with a specific budget (you can ask me for the budget).
*   The recommendations should be actionable and results-oriented.
*   The tone should be strategic, data-driven, and practical.

## Tips

Content syndication can be a powerful way to get more mileage out of your content. This prompt provides a strategic approach to doing it effectively.
