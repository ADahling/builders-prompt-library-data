# Develop a Market Research Plan for a New Mobile App

Create a comprehensive, budget-friendly market research plan to validate a mobile app concept through targeted user insights and analysis.

Variables: app_name, app_description, target_audience

## Prompt

I am the founder of a startup developing a new mobile app, {{app_name}}, which {{app_description}}. Our target audience is {{target_audience}}. Before we invest heavily in development, we need to conduct thorough market research to validate our idea and identify our target users' needs.

Act as a market research consultant and create a detailed market research plan for me. The plan should be comprehensive and actionable for a startup with a limited budget.

The plan should include the following sections:

1.  **Research Objectives:** Clearly define the key questions we need to answer with our research. (e.g., Is there a real need for this app? Who are our ideal users? What features are most important to them?)
2.  **Research Methodology:** Recommend a mix of primary and secondary research methods. For each method, explain why it's suitable and what kind of insights we can expect to gain.
    *   **Secondary Research:** Suggest sources for industry reports, competitor analysis, and market trends.
    *   **Primary Research:** Outline a plan for conducting surveys, user interviews, and focus groups. Provide sample questions for the surveys and interviews.
3.  **Target Audience & Segmentation:** Describe how to identify and recruit participants for our primary research. Suggest criteria for segmenting our target audience.
4.  **Timeline & Budget:** Propose a realistic timeline for executing the research plan (e.g., in weeks). Provide a sample budget, including any recommended tools or services (e.g., for surveys or user testing).
5.  **Data Analysis & Reporting:** Explain how to analyze the data collected from the research and how to structure the final research report.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the market research plan in a well-structured Markdown document. Use headings, subheadings, and bullet points to make the plan easy to read and follow.

**Constraints:**

*   The recommended methods and tools should be cost-effective for a startup.
*   The plan should be practical and provide step-by-step guidance.
*   The tone should be professional, supportive, and expert.

## Tips

This prompt helps users create a structured plan for validating their business idea through market research.
