# Create a Sales Playbook for a New Product Launch

Generate a comprehensive, actionable sales playbook including ICPs, competitive battle cards, and objection handling for a new product launch.

Variables: company_name, product_name, launch_quarter

## Prompt

I am the VP of Sales at {{company_name}}. We are launching a new product, {{product_name}}, in {{launch_quarter}}. I need to create a sales playbook to equip our sales team to sell this new product effectively from day one.

Act as a sales enablement expert. Your task is to create a comprehensive sales playbook for our new product.

The playbook should be a detailed guide that includes:

1.  **Product Overview:**
    *   A clear and concise description of the new product, its features, and its benefits.

2.  **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & Personas:**
    *   Who is the target customer for this new product? What are their pain points and needs?

3.  **Value Proposition & Messaging:**
    *   What is the unique value proposition of the new product?
    *   Provide key messaging points and an elevator pitch.

4.  **Competitive Landscape:**
    *   Who are the main competitors? How does our new product differentiate itself?
    *   Provide battle cards for the top 2-3 competitors.

5.  **The Sales Process:**
    *   Outline the recommended sales process for this product, from prospecting to closing.

6.  **Discovery & Qualification:**
    *   Provide a list of discovery questions to ask potential customers to uncover their needs and qualify them.

7.  **Objection Handling:**
    *   What are the common objections we can expect to hear from customers? Provide a guide for how to handle these objections.

8.  **Pricing & Packaging:**
    *   A clear explanation of the pricing and packaging for the new product.

9.  **Case Studies & Success Stories:**
    *   (Placeholder for) customer success stories and case studies that the sales team can use as social proof.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the sales playbook as a well-structured Markdown document. The playbook should be a practical and comprehensive resource for our sales team.

**Constraints:**

*   The playbook must be easy to read and use.
*   The content must be actionable and directly relevant to the sales process.
*   The tone should be clear, confident, and persuasive.

## Tips

A sales playbook is essential for aligning a sales team and enabling them to sell a new product effectively. This prompt provides a complete template.
