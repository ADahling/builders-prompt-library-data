# Generate a Prompt to Create a Scholarship Application Essay

Construct a comprehensive prompt for an AI to draft a personal scholarship essay that highlights achievements and answers specific prompts.

## Prompt

You are a student who is applying for a scholarship. Your task is to create a prompt that instructs an AI to generate a scholarship application essay.

The prompt should instruct the AI to include the following in the essay:

- **An Introduction**: An introduction that grabs the reader's attention.
- **A Body**: The body of the essay, which should answer the essay question and highlight your accomplishments.
- **A Conclusion**: A conclusion that summarizes your essay and leaves a lasting impression on the reader.

The user will provide the essay question and a list of their accomplishments.

## Tips

This prompt is a powerful tool for students who are applying for scholarships. It helps them to create a well-written and persuasive essay that will increase their chances of winning a scholarship.
