# Develop a Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Strategy

Construct an integrated CSR strategy that aligns social impact initiatives with company mission to drive employee engagement and brand authenticity.

Variables: company_name, company_type, company_mission

## Prompt

I am the Head of Strategy at {{company_name}}, a {{company_type}} company. We want to develop a meaningful Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) strategy that is authentic to our brand, engages our employees, and creates a positive impact. Our company mission is to {{company_mission}}.

Act as a CSR consultant. Your task is to help me develop a CSR strategy that is integrated with our business, not just a separate "feel-good" initiative.

The strategy should include:

1.  **CSR Vision & Mission:**
    *   Help me craft a CSR vision statement that aligns with our overall company mission.
2.  **Focus Areas:**
    *   Based on our industry and company values, recommend 2-3 CSR focus areas where we can have the most significant impact. Examples could include:
        *   **Environmental Sustainability:** (e.g., reducing our carbon footprint, using sustainable materials).
        *   **Community Engagement:** (e.g., employee volunteering, supporting local charities).
        *   **Ethical Sourcing:** (e.g., ensuring fair labor practices in our supply chain).
        *   **Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI):** (e.g., creating a more inclusive workplace and supporting underrepresented communities).
3.  **Signature Initiatives:**
    *   For each focus area, brainstorm a "signature initiative" – a flagship program that will be the cornerstone of our CSR efforts.
4.  **Goals & Metrics:**
    *   Define specific, measurable goals for our CSR program. How will we measure our impact and success?
5.  **Employee Engagement:**
    *   Suggest ways to engage our employees in our CSR initiatives and make them proud to work for our company.
6.  **Reporting & Communication:**
    *   Outline a plan for communicating our CSR efforts to our customers, investors, and the public. This could include an annual CSR report.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the CSR strategy as a well-structured Markdown document. The document should be a compelling and actionable plan that I can present to our leadership team.

**Constraints:**

*   The CSR strategy must be authentic and avoid "greenwashing."
*   The initiatives should be realistic for a company of our size and resources.
*   The tone should be inspiring, strategic, and purpose-driven.

## Tips

This prompt helps companies develop a CSR strategy that is more than just a PR exercise, but a genuine commitment to making a positive impact.
