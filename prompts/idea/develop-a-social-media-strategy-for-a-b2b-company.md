# Develop a Social Media Strategy for a B2B Company

Create a comprehensive B2B social media strategy to boost brand authority, drive lead generation, and empower employee advocacy.

Variables: company_name, product_or_service, target_audience

## Prompt

I am the marketing manager for a B2B company, {{company_name}}, that sells {{product_or_service}} to {{target_audience}}. Our social media presence is weak and not generating any real business results. I need to develop a new social media strategy.

Act as a B2B social media marketing expert. Your task is to create a social media strategy for my company.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **Goals & Objectives:**
    *   What are our primary goals for social media? (e.g., brand awareness, lead generation, thought leadership, customer engagement).

2.  **Platform Strategy:**
    *   Which social media platforms should we focus on? For a B2B company, this is likely to be LinkedIn, but what about Twitter or other platforms?
    *   What is the purpose of each platform in our strategy?

3.  **Content Strategy:**
    *   What kind of content should we post on each platform? This should be a mix of:
        *   **Original Content:** (e.g., blog posts, case studies, industry insights).
        *   **Curated Content:** (e.g., sharing relevant articles from other sources).
        *   **Company Culture Content:** (e.g., highlighting our employees and our company culture).
    *   Provide a sample content calendar for one week.

4.  **Employee Advocacy:**
    *   How can we encourage our employees (especially our executives and sales team) to be active on social media and share our content?

5.  **Engagement & Community Management:**
    *   How will we engage with our audience, respond to comments, and participate in industry conversations?

6.  **Measurement & ROI:**
    *   How will we measure the success of our social media efforts? What are the key metrics to track?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the social media strategy as a detailed and structured plan in Markdown. The plan should be a practical guide for a B2B marketer.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be tailored to a B2B audience and the platforms they use.
*   The content strategy must be focused on providing value and building thought leadership, not just on self-promotion.
*   The tone should be professional, strategic, and results-oriented.

## Tips

Social media can be a powerful channel for B2B companies, but it requires a different approach than B2C. This prompt provides a framework for a strategic B2B social media plan.
