# Weekly Focus Planner

Turn a weekly task list into ranked priorities, a daily schedule, delegation suggestions, and decline messages that account for energy levels.

## Prompt

What's on my plate this week: [dump everything]. Focus:

1. Must-Do: 3 things that absolutely must get done. No more than 3.
2. Should-Do: 3-5 important but won't cause crisis if they slip.
3. Could-Do: Everything else. Only if Must/Should complete.
4. Block Schedule: Day-by-day for Must-Do items.
5. Delegate: What could someone else handle? Who?
6. Decline/Defer: What to say no to? Draft the message.
7. Energy Management: Hard tasks when fresh, easy when low energy.

Be direct. Cut fluff. Protect my time.
