# Brainstorm Business Model Innovations Using the 'Freemium' Pattern

Guide a workshop to transition a business to a freemium model by defining free value, premium tiers, conversion paths, and financial projections.

Variables: product_name, product_description, current_business_model, quota_example

## Prompt

I am the founder of {{product_name}}, a {{product_description}}. We are currently using a traditional {{current_business_model}}. I want to explore how we could innovate our business model by implementing a Freemium strategy to accelerate user acquisition and build a strong user base before upselling to premium features.

Act as a business model innovation consultant. Your task is to guide me in designing a viable Freemium model for my product.

Your guidance should cover:

1.  **Freemium Strategy Fundamentals:**
    *   Explain the core principles of a successful Freemium model. What are the key success factors and common pitfalls?
2.  **Defining the Free Offering:**
    *   Help me decide what features to include in the free version of {{product_name}}. The free offering should be valuable enough to attract a large number of users, but limited enough to create a strong incentive to upgrade.
    *   Suggest different ways to limit the free version, such as by features, usage quotas (e.g., {{quota_example}}), or number of users.
3.  **Designing the Premium Offering:**
    *   What advanced features or benefits should be reserved for the paid, premium version? These should be features that our most valuable customers are willing to pay for.
4.  **The Upgrade Path:**
    *   How will we encourage free users to upgrade to the premium version? Brainstorm ideas for in-app messaging, email campaigns, and other tactics to drive conversions.
5.  **Financial Modeling:**
    *   Discuss the key metrics for a Freemium model, such as conversion rate (free to paid) and ARPU (Average Revenue Per User). Help me build a simple financial model to project the potential revenue from this new model.

**Output Format:**

Please generate your recommendations as a structured workshop agenda in Markdown. The agenda should guide a brainstorming session with my team to design our new Freemium business model.

**Constraints:**

*   The advice must be tailored to my specific product and target market.
*   The Freemium model design should be sustainable and not just a way to give away free stuff.
*   The tone should be creative, strategic, and practical.
