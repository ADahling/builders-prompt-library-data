# Create a Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Plan

Generate a comprehensive, actionable Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery plan template covering impact analysis, risk, and communications.

Variables: company_name, company_type

## Prompt

I am the COO of {{company_name}}, a {{company_type}} company. I need to ensure that our business can continue to operate in the event of a major disruption, such as a natural disaster, a cyber-attack, or a pandemic. I need to create a Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery (BCDR) plan.

Act as a BCDR consultant. Your task is to create a BCDR plan template that I can adapt for my company.

The plan should include:

1.  **Business Impact Analysis (BIA):**
    *   Guide me in conducting a BIA to identify our critical business processes and the potential impact of a disruption to each process.
    *   For each process, define the Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO).

2.  **Risk Assessment:**
    *   Identify the potential threats and vulnerabilities that could disrupt our business.

3.  **Business Continuity Plan (BCP):**
    *   For each critical business process, outline the procedures to be followed to ensure it can continue to operate during a disruption. This should cover:
        *   **People:** How will employees work if they can't access the office?
        *   **Processes:** What are the manual workarounds for key processes?
        *   **Technology:** What are the alternative technology solutions?

4.  **Disaster Recovery (DR) Plan:**
    *   This is the technology-focused part of the plan. It should outline the procedures for recovering our IT infrastructure and data after a disaster. This should include details on our data backup and recovery strategy.

5.  **Crisis Management & Communication:**
    *   A plan for communicating with employees, customers, and other stakeholders during a crisis.

6.  **Testing & Maintenance:**
    *   A schedule for regularly testing and updating the BCDR plan.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the BCDR plan template as a detailed and structured Markdown document. The template should be a comprehensive guide to preparing for and responding to a business disruption.

**Constraints:**

*   The plan must be comprehensive and cover all aspects of business continuity and disaster recovery.
*   The advice must be practical and actionable.
*   The tone should be thorough, prudent, and proactive.

## Tips

A BCDR plan is a critical document for any resilient organization. This prompt provides a template for creating a comprehensive plan.
