# Calculate and Interpret Key SaaS Quick Ratio

Get a quarterly SaaS Quick Ratio calculation from MRR data, an explanation of the formula, benchmark analysis, and growth recommendations.

Variables: company_name

## Prompt

As the Head of Finance for a Series B SaaS company, {{company_name}}, I need to assess the efficiency of our growth. I want to calculate and interpret our SaaS Quick Ratio to understand how predictably we can grow our revenue.

Act as a SaaS finance expert (like a CFO from a top-tier SaaS company). I will provide you with the MRR data for the last quarter: New MRR, Expansion MRR, and Churned MRR for each month.

Your task is to:

1.  **Explain the SaaS Quick Ratio:**
    *   Clearly define the SaaS Quick Ratio and its formula: (New MRR + Expansion MRR) / Churned MRR.
    *   Explain what the ratio measures and why it's a critical indicator of growth efficiency.
2.  **Calculate the Ratio:**
    *   Once I provide the data, calculate the SaaS Quick Ratio for the last quarter.
3.  **Interpret the Result:**
    *   Provide a detailed interpretation of the calculated ratio. What does this number say about the health of our business?
    *   Use the common benchmark for a healthy SaaS Quick Ratio (a ratio of 4 or higher is considered excellent). Explain where we stand relative to this benchmark.
4.  **Provide Strategic Recommendations:**
    *   Based on the result, what are your strategic recommendations? 
    *   If the ratio is low, what levers can we pull to improve it? (e.g., improve lead generation for New MRR, focus on upselling for Expansion MRR, or reduce churn).
    *   If the ratio is high, how can we capitalize on this strong growth efficiency?

**Output Format:**

Please structure your response as a concise memo to the executive team. Start by explaining the concept, then present the calculation and your interpretation, and conclude with actionable recommendations.

**Constraints:**

*   The explanation must be clear for a non-finance audience.
*   The interpretation must be insightful and go beyond a superficial reading of the number.
*   The recommendations must be strategic and directly linked to the SaaS Quick Ratio result.

## Tips

This prompt focuses on a specific, powerful SaaS metric and requires the AI to act as a strategic financial advisor.
