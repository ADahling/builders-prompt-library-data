# Develop a Customer Acquisition Strategy for a D2C Brand

Generate a data-driven, multi-channel growth plan tailored for D2C startups to scale traffic and sales during their first six months.

Variables: brand_name, products, target_audience, launch_month

## Prompt

I am the founder of a new direct-to-consumer (D2C) brand, {{brand_name}}, that sells {{products}}. Our target audience is {{target_audience}}. We are launching our online store in {{launch_month}} and need to develop a customer acquisition strategy to drive traffic and sales.

Act as a growth marketing expert. Your task is to create a comprehensive customer acquisition strategy for our first six months post-launch. The strategy should focus on a mix of organic and paid channels.

The strategy should include detailed recommendations for the following areas:

1.  **Content Marketing:**
    *   What type of content should we create (e.g., blog posts, videos, guides)?
    *   What topics should we focus on to attract our target audience?
    *   Provide 5 blog post ideas with catchy titles.
2.  **Search Engine Optimization (SEO):**
    *   What are the top 10 keywords we should target?
    *   What are the key on-page and off-page SEO activities we should focus on?
3.  **Social Media Marketing:**
    *   Which social media platforms should we prioritize (e.g., Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest)?
    *   What is our content strategy for each platform?
    *   Suggest a plan for collaborating with influencers in our niche.
4.  **Paid Advertising:**
    *   Recommend a budget allocation for paid channels (e.g., Google Ads, Facebook/Instagram Ads).
    *   Describe the target audience and ad creatives for our first campaign.
5.  **Email Marketing:**
    *   How can we build our email list from day one?
    *   What should be our welcome email series for new subscribers?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the customer acquisition strategy as a detailed Markdown document. Use headings, subheadings, and bullet points to organize the information.

**Constraints:**

*   The recommended strategies should be practical for a new D2C brand with a limited budget.
*   The advice should be actionable and provide specific examples.
*   The tone should be creative, strategic, and results-oriented.

## Tips

A great prompt for entrepreneurs who need a holistic plan for acquiring their first customers.
