# Create a Pitch Deck for a Non-Profit Organization

Generate a narrative-driven pitch deck outline that leverages emotional storytelling and impact data to secure funding for non-profit goals.

Variables: non_profit_name, mission, campaign_goal

## Prompt

I am the Executive Director of a non-profit organization, {{non_profit_name}}. Our mission is to {{mission}}. We are launching a major fundraising campaign to {{campaign_goal}}. I need to create a compelling pitch deck to present to potential donors and foundations.

Act as a non-profit fundraising consultant. Your task is to create a pitch deck for our fundraising campaign.

The pitch deck should tell a powerful story and should include the following slides:

1.  **The Problem:**
    *   A slide that clearly and emotionally communicates the problem we are trying to solve. Use data and a personal story to make the problem real and urgent.

2.  **Our Mission & Vision:**
    *   A clear statement of our organization's mission and our vision for a better future.

3.  **Our Solution:**
    *   A description of our programs and how they address the problem. Explain our unique approach.

4.  **Our Impact:**
    *   A slide that showcases our past success and impact. Use key metrics, testimonials, and success stories to demonstrate our effectiveness.

5.  **The Opportunity:**
    *   A description of our new fundraising campaign. What specific project or program will the funds support?

6.  **Our Goal & Budget:**
    *   A clear statement of our fundraising goal and a high-level budget showing how the funds will be used.

7.  **The Ask:**
    *   A specific request for a donation. This could be a tiered set of giving levels (e.g., "$100 provides X, $500 provides Y").

8.  **Our Team:**
    *   A slide introducing our leadership team and board of directors to build credibility and trust.

9.  **Call to Action:**
    *   A final slide with a clear call to action and our contact information.

**Output Format:**

Please generate a detailed outline for the pitch deck in Markdown. For each slide, provide guidance on the content, visuals, and narrative.

**Constraints:**

*   The pitch deck must be emotionally resonant and tell a compelling story.
*   The deck must be clear, concise, and professional.
*   The tone should be passionate, credible, and inspiring.

## Tips

A great pitch deck is just as important for a non-profit as it is for a startup. This prompt provides a template for creating a powerful fundraising pitch.
