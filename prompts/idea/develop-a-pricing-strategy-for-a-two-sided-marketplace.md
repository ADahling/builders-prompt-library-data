# Develop a Pricing Strategy for a Two-Sided Marketplace

Design a comprehensive pricing strategy for a two-sided marketplace to balance supply and demand while maximizing sustainable revenue.

Variables: marketplace_name, side_A, side_B

## Prompt

I am the founder of a two-sided marketplace, {{marketplace_name}}, that connects {{side_A}} with {{side_B}}. I need to develop a pricing strategy that will attract both sides of the marketplace and create a sustainable business model.

Act as a marketplace pricing expert. Your task is to help me design a pricing strategy for my marketplace.

Your guidance should cover:

1.  **Who to Charge?**
    *   Should we charge the supply side ({{side_A}}), the demand side ({{side_B}}), or both? What are the pros and cons of each approach?
    *   This is the classic "chicken and egg" problem. Which side is harder to acquire? We should generally subsidize the harder side.

2.  **Pricing Models:**
    *   Recommend a pricing model. Discuss the pros and cons of different models for a marketplace:
        *   **Commission/Take Rate:** Charging a percentage of each transaction.
        *   **Listing Fees:** Charging to list a product or service.
        *   **Subscription Fees:** Charging a recurring fee for access to the marketplace.
        *   **Freemium:** Offering a basic version for free and charging for premium features (e.g., better visibility).

3.  **The Take Rate:**
    *   If we choose a commission model, what should our take rate be? How does this compare to other marketplaces in our industry?

4.  **Dynamic Pricing:**
    *   Should we consider dynamic pricing (i.e., changing prices based on supply and demand)?

5.  **Getting the First Users:**
    *   What pricing strategy should we use to attract our first 1,000 users on both sides of the marketplace? Should we waive our fees for early adopters?

**Output Format:**

Please generate your recommendations as a detailed pricing strategy document in Markdown. The document should be a well-reasoned and persuasive guide to this critical set of decisions.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be tailored to the specific dynamics of our two-sided marketplace.
*   The recommendations must be practical and consider the need to solve the chicken and egg problem.
*   The tone should be strategic, analytical, and expert.

## Tips

Pricing is one of the most complex and important decisions for a marketplace founder. This prompt provides a structured way to think through the options.
