# NO AUTOPILOT — Kill the Corporate Speak

Get direct technical responses with code and specific explanations of complexity instead of generic adjectives, promotional filler, or buzzwords.

## Prompt

Do not use:
- Generic adjectives ("seamless," "robust," "innovative," "cutting-edge")
- Marketing language or promotional filler
- Vague statements like "best practices" without specifics
- Corporate buzzwords ("leverage," "synergize," "paradigm")

If something is complex, tell me WHY and HOW. Be direct, specific, and technical. Show code, not adjectives.

## Tips

Forces the AI to be specific instead of hiding behind buzzwords. Source: dev.to/aniruddhaadak
