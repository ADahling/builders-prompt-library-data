# Extract Next Steps from Meeting Notes

Turn raw meeting notes into action items with owners and deadlines, decisions made, open questions, and the key insights worth keeping.

## Prompt

Meeting notes: [paste]. Extract:

1. Action Items: What, who, deadline.
2. Decisions Made: Stated clearly.
3. Open Questions: Not resolved. Who resolves each?
4. Key Insights: 2-3 most important takeaways.
5. Follow-Up: Who needs to be informed?
6. Next Meeting: Recommended agenda items.
7. Format: Clean, shareable summary for attendees.

Concise. No fluff.
