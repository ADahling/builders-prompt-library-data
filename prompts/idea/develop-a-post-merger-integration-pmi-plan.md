# Develop a Post-Merger Integration (PMI) Plan

Generate a structured 100-day post-merger integration plan that synchronizes functional workstreams, tracks synergies, and manages culture.

Variables: acquiring_company, acquired_company, strategic_goal

## Prompt

I am the leader of the post-merger integration (PMI) team for a recent acquisition. My company, {{acquiring_company}}, has just acquired {{acquired_company}}. The goal of the acquisition is to {{strategic_goal}}. I need to develop a comprehensive PMI plan to ensure a successful integration and realize the intended synergies.

Act as a PMI expert. Your task is to create a detailed 100-day PMI plan template.

The plan should be organized by functional workstream and should cover:

1.  **Integration Governance:**
    *   Define the structure of the integration team, including the Integration Management Office (IMO) and functional workstream leads.
    *   Establish a regular meeting cadence and reporting process.
2.  **Functional Workstream Plans:**
    For each of the following functional areas, outline the key integration activities, milestones, and risks for the first 100 days:
    *   **Go-to-Market (Sales & Marketing):** (e.g., integrating sales teams, aligning branding and messaging, managing customer communication).
    *   **Product & Technology:** (e.g., creating a joint product roadmap, integrating technology stacks, managing engineering teams).
    *   **People & Culture:** (e.g., communicating with employees, aligning compensation and benefits, managing cultural integration).
    *   **Finance & Legal:** (e.g., integrating financial reporting, consolidating legal entities, ensuring compliance).
    *   **Operations:** (e.g., integrating supply chains, facilities, and IT systems).
3.  **Synergy Tracking:**
    *   Create a framework for tracking both cost and revenue synergies. Who is responsible for each synergy target?
4.  **Communication Plan:**
    *   Develop a communication plan for all stakeholders (employees, customers, partners, investors) to keep them informed and manage uncertainty.

**Output Format:**

Please generate a detailed PMI plan template in Markdown. Use tables to outline the activities and milestones for each workstream. The template should be a comprehensive playbook for managing a complex post-merger integration.

**Constraints:**

*   The plan must be highly structured and detailed.
*   The advice should be based on best practices for post-merger integration.
*   The tone should be organized, proactive, and focused on execution.

## Tips

M&A is notoriously difficult to get right. This prompt provides a structured and comprehensive plan to maximize the chances of a successful integration.
