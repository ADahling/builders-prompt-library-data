# Define a Balanced Scorecard of KPIs for a Subscription Box Business

Establish a holistic balanced scorecard of subscription-specific KPIs to track financial health, customer loyalty, and operational efficiency.

Variables: brand_name, niche

## Prompt

I am the founder of {{brand_name}}, a subscription box service for {{niche}}. I need to move beyond vanity metrics and establish a balanced scorecard of Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to manage the health and growth of my business holistically.

Act as a seasoned e-commerce and subscription business consultant. Your task is to help me create a balanced scorecard that covers four key perspectives: Financial, Customer, Internal Business Processes, and Learning & Growth.

For each perspective, you should:

1.  **Explain the Perspective:** Briefly describe the importance of this perspective for a subscription business.
2.  **Recommend 3-4 KPIs:** For each perspective, recommend specific, measurable, and relevant KPIs. For each KPI, explain:
    *   **What it is:** A clear definition of the metric.
    *   **How to calculate it:** The formula for the KPI.
    *   **Why it matters:** The business insight that this KPI provides.
    *   **Target/Benchmark:** A suggested target or industry benchmark to aim for.

**Example KPIs to consider:**

*   **Financial:** Customer Lifetime Value (LTV), Average Order Value (AOV), Contribution Margin.
*   **Customer:** Net Promoter Score (NPS), Churn Rate, Customer Retention Cost.
*   **Internal Processes:** Order Fulfillment Time, Inventory Turnover, Website Conversion Rate.
*   **Learning & Growth:** Employee Satisfaction, Number of New Product SKUs Launched, Time to Market for New Boxes.

**Output Format:**

Please generate the balanced scorecard as a well-structured Markdown document. Use a table format to present the KPIs for each perspective.

**Constraints:**

*   The KPIs must be highly relevant to a subscription box business model.
*   The explanations must be clear and concise.
*   The scorecard should provide a holistic view of the business, not just financial performance.

## Tips

This prompt helps business owners create a more sophisticated and balanced view of their performance using the classic Balanced Scorecard framework.
