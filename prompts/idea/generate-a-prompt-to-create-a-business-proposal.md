# Generate a Prompt to Create a Business Proposal

Get an AI prompt for drafting a client business proposal with an executive summary, problem statement, solution, pricing, and company details.

## Prompt

You are a sales manager. Your task is to generate a prompt that instructs an AI to create a business proposal for a potential client.

The prompt should instruct the AI to generate a business proposal with the following sections:

- **Executive Summary**: A brief overview of the business proposal.
- **Problem Statement**: A statement of the problem that the potential client is facing.
- **Proposed Solution**: A description of the solution that you are proposing to the potential client.
- **Pricing**: The pricing for your solution.
- **Company Information**: Information about your company.

The user will provide the name of the potential client, a description of the problem that the potential client is facing, and the pricing for your solution.

## Tips

This prompt is a powerful tool for sales managers who need to create a business proposal. It helps them to create a comprehensive and persuasive business proposal that will increase their chances of winning new business.
