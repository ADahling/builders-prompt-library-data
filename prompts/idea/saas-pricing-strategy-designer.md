# SaaS Pricing Strategy Designer

Develop a comprehensive, data-driven SaaS pricing model and tier structure to optimize revenue, user adoption, and enterprise scalability.

Variables: product_name, product_description, target_market, mau, current_pricing, competitor_pricing, cost_per_user

## Prompt

You are a SaaS pricing strategist. Design a pricing strategy for {{product_name}}, a {{product_description}} targeting {{target_market}}.

Current data:
- Monthly active users: {{mau}}
- Current pricing: {{current_pricing}}
- Competitors: {{competitor_pricing}}
- Cost per user: {{cost_per_user}}

Design:
1. **Pricing Model**: Freemium, free trial, usage-based, seat-based, or hybrid? Justify
2. **Tier Structure**: 3-4 tiers with specific features, limits, and prices
3. **Value Metric**: What unit should pricing be based on? (users, API calls, storage, etc.)
4. **Free Tier Strategy**: What to include to drive adoption without cannibalizing paid
5. **Enterprise Tier**: Custom pricing triggers, minimum contract value
6. **Annual Discount**: Optimal discount percentage to maximize cash flow
7. **Expansion Revenue**: Upsell triggers, usage alerts, upgrade prompts
8. **Pricing Page Copy**: Actual tier names, descriptions, and feature comparison table
9. **A/B Test Plan**: What to test first (price points, tier names, feature allocation)

## Tips

Pricing is the most impactful lever for SaaS growth. A 1% improvement in pricing yields 11% profit improvement.
