# Turn Customer Feedback Into Actions

Turn customer feedback into counted themes, sentiment, product and messaging actions, quick wins, and an effort-versus-impact priority matrix.

## Prompt

Customer feedback: [paste from surveys, reviews, tickets]. Actions:

1. Themes: Group into 5-7 themes with mention counts.
2. Sentiment: Per theme (positive/mixed/negative).
3. Urgency: Which cause churn or prevent purchase?
4. Product Actions: Specific changes for top themes.
5. Sales Actions: Messaging/positioning shifts.
6. Support Actions: Common questions solvable with better docs/UI.
7. Quick Wins: 3 things this week for loudest feedback.
8. Priority Matrix: Effort vs impact.

Be specific. "Improve UX" is not an action. "Add progress indicator to upload flow" is.
