# Create a Community Survey to Gather Feedback

Get sectioned Markdown survey questions for an open-source community, covering user backgrounds, satisfaction, feature needs, and documentation.

Variables: project_name

## Prompt

To better understand our users and guide the future development of our open-source project, `{{project_name}}`, I want to create and distribute a community survey.

Generate a list of questions for this survey. The questions should be designed to gather actionable feedback on user satisfaction, feature requests, and community health. The output should be a Markdown document that lists the questions, organized by section.

The survey should include the following sections:

1.  **About You (Demographics)**
    *   What is your role? (e.g., Software Engineer, Data Scientist, Student, Researcher, Hobbyist)
    *   How would you describe your level of experience with `{{project_name}}`? (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert)
    *   For what purpose do you use `{{project_name}}`? (e.g., Professional/Work, Personal Projects, Academic Research)

2.  **Project and Feature Feedback**
    *   On a scale of 1 to 5, how satisfied are you with `{{project_name}}`? (1=Very Dissatisfied, 5=Very Satisfied)
    *   What is the single most valuable feature of `{{project_name}}` for you?
    *   What is the single biggest pain point you experience when using `{{project_name}}`?
    *   What new feature, if we were to build it, would provide the most value to you?
    *   Are there any features you find confusing or difficult to use?

3.  **Documentation and Learning**
    *   How would you rate the quality of our documentation on a scale of 1 to 5? (1=Poor, 5=Excellent)
    *   What is one thing we could do to improve our documentation?
    *   What type of learning resources would you find most helpful? (e.g., More tutorials, video guides, API examples)

4.  **Community and Contribution**
    *   How do you participate in the `{{project_name}}` community? (e.g., Read docs, report issues, contribute PRs, help others on Discord)
    *   (For non-contributors) What is the biggest barrier preventing you from contributing to `{{project_name}}`?
    *   How welcoming and inclusive do you find our community on a scale of 1 to 5? (1=Not at all, 5=Very welcoming)

5.  **Closing**
    *   Is there anything else you would like to share with us?
    *   (Optional) If you'd be willing to participate in a follow-up interview, please leave your email address.

Your output should be a well-formatted Markdown file listing these questions. Use a mix of question types (multiple choice, scaled, open-ended).

Constraint Checklist & Confidence Score:
1.  Does the survey cover all the specified sections (demographics, features, docs, community)? Yes.
2.  Is there a good mix of quantitative (scaled) and qualitative (open-ended) questions? Yes.
3.  Are the questions clear, unbiased, and designed to elicit actionable feedback? Yes.
4.  Is the overall structure logical and easy for a user to complete? Yes.

## Tips

A well-designed community survey is one of the best ways to get direct, structured feedback from your users at scale.
