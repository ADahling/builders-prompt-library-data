# Develop a Personal Branding Strategy for a Tech Executive

Craft a comprehensive personal branding and content strategy to establish industry thought leadership and accelerate career growth for executives.

Variables: your_title, company_type, industry

## Prompt

I am a {{your_title}} at a {{company_type}} company. I want to build my personal brand to increase my influence in the {{industry}} industry, attract new career opportunities, and be seen as a thought leader.

Act as a personal branding coach and PR expert. Your task is to create a personal branding strategy for me.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **Define Your Brand:**
    *   **Niche & Expertise:** What is the specific area of expertise you want to be known for? (e.g., AI in fintech, scaling engineering teams).
    *   **Target Audience:** Who are you trying to reach? (e.g., recruiters, potential clients, other industry leaders).
    *   **Brand Attributes:** What are the 3-5 keywords you want people to associate with you? (e.g., innovative, data-driven, empathetic leader).

2.  **Content Strategy:**
    *   **Pillar Content:** What is the cornerstone of your content strategy? (e.g., a blog, a podcast, a YouTube channel).
    *   **Content Platforms:** Which social media platforms should you focus on? (e.g., LinkedIn for professional content, Twitter for real-time industry commentary).
    *   **Content Cadence:** How often will you post on each platform?

3.  **Thought Leadership & Visibility:**
    *   **Speaking:** How can you get speaking engagements at industry conferences or on podcasts?
    *   **Writing:** How can you get published on industry blogs or publications?
    *   **Networking:** What are the key industry events you should attend?

4.  **Online Presence Optimization:**
    *   Provide a detailed critique of my LinkedIn profile and suggest improvements to optimize it for my personal brand.
    *   Should I have a personal website? If so, what should be on it?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the personal branding strategy as a detailed, actionable plan in Markdown. The plan should be a step-by-step guide that I can start implementing right away.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be authentic to my personality and expertise.
*   The recommendations must be practical for a busy executive.
*   The tone should be empowering, strategic, and results-oriented.

## Tips

In today's market, a strong personal brand is a huge asset for any professional. This prompt provides a structured approach to building one.
