# Develop a Pricing Strategy for a New Hardware Product

Generate a comprehensive hardware pricing strategy that balances unit economics, retail margins, and recurring revenue models to maximize profit.

Variables: product_name, bom_cost

## Prompt

I am the founder of a startup that has developed a new piece of consumer hardware, {{product_name}}. The product has a Bill of Materials (BOM) cost of {{bom_cost}} per unit. I need to develop a pricing strategy for this new product.

Act as a hardware pricing expert. Your task is to help me develop a pricing strategy for my new hardware product.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **Pricing Objectives:**
    *   What is our primary pricing objective? (e.g., maximize profit, maximize market share, communicate a premium brand image).

2.  **Pricing Methodologies:**
    *   Help me choose a pricing methodology. Discuss the pros and cons of:
        *   **Cost-Plus Pricing:** Adding a standard markup to our BOM cost.
        *   **Value-Based Pricing:** Pricing based on the perceived value to the customer.
        *   **Competitive Pricing:** Pricing based on what our competitors are charging.

3.  **The Retail Price:**
    *   Based on the chosen methodology, what should be the Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price (MSRP) for our product?

4.  **Channel Margins:**
    *   If we are selling our product through retail partners, what is the typical channel margin we need to provide to distributors and retailers?

5.  **Pricing for a Product Line:**
    *   Should we have a single product or a product line with a "good, better, best" pricing structure?

6.  **The Business Model:**
    *   Should we consider a business model other than a one-time hardware sale? For example, could we have a recurring revenue component, such as a subscription for a related software service?

**Output Format:**

Please generate your recommendations as a detailed pricing strategy document in Markdown. The document should be a well-reasoned and persuasive guide to this critical set of decisions.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be tailored to a hardware product and the economics of manufacturing.
*   The recommendations must be commercially sound and consider the entire value chain.
*   The tone should be strategic, analytical, and expert.

## Tips

Hardware pricing is a different beast than software pricing. This prompt provides a framework for a comprehensive hardware pricing strategy.
