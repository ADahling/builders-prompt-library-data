# Develop a Scenario Planning Exercise for Strategic Uncertainty

Design a comprehensive workshop guide to build scenario matrices, narrative futures, and robust strategic responses for navigating industry uncertainty.

Variables: company_name, industry, source_of_uncertainty_1, source_of_uncertainty_2

## Prompt

I am the Head of Strategy at {{company_name}}. Our industry, {{industry}}, is facing a high degree of uncertainty due to {{source_of_uncertainty_1}} and {{source_of_uncertainty_2}}. I want to run a scenario planning workshop with my leadership team to prepare for a range of possible futures.

Act as a futurist and strategy consultant. Your task is to design a scenario planning workshop for my team.

The workshop design should follow these steps:

1.  **Identify Key Uncertainties:**
    *   Guide the team in identifying the top 2-3 most critical and uncertain forces that will shape the future of our industry.

2.  **Define the Scenario Matrix:**
    *   Use the top two uncertainties as the axes of a 2x2 matrix. For each axis, define two plausible extreme outcomes. This will create four distinct future scenarios.

3.  **Flesh out the Scenarios:**
    *   For each of the four quadrants in the matrix, create a rich, narrative description of that future world. Give each scenario a memorable name (e.g., "The Great Slowdown," "A New Frontier"). What would it be like to operate in that world in 5-10 years?

4.  **Implications and Options:**
    *   For each scenario, facilitate a discussion on:
        *   **Implications:** What are the biggest threats and opportunities for our company in this scenario?
        *   **Strategic Options:** What strategic moves could we make to succeed in this scenario?

5.  **Identify Robust Strategies & Leading Indicators:**
    *   What strategic initiatives would be beneficial across multiple scenarios? (These are our "robust strategies").
    *   What are the leading indicators we should monitor to see which scenario is beginning to unfold?

**Output Format:**

Please generate a detailed facilitator's guide for the scenario planning workshop in Markdown. The guide should include the agenda, the exercises for each step, and probing questions to ask the team.

**Constraints:**

*   The process must be designed to stretch the team's thinking and challenge their assumptions about the future.
*   The scenarios must be plausible, internally consistent, and structurally different from each other.
*   The focus should be on developing strategic agility, not on predicting the future.

## Tips

Scenario planning is a powerful tool for navigating deep uncertainty. This prompt provides a structured process for running a workshop.
