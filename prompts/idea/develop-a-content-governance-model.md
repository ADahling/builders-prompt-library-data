# Develop a Content Governance Model

Establish a comprehensive content governance framework to standardize workflows, define roles, and ensure brand consistency across large teams.

Variables: organization_name

## Prompt

I am the Head of Content at a large organization, {{organization_name}}. We have dozens of people across different departments creating content for our website, blog, and social media channels. The result is inconsistent messaging, variable quality, and a chaotic user experience. I need to create a content governance model to bring order to this chaos.

Act as a content strategy and governance expert. Your task is to create a content governance model for my organization.

The model should include:

1.  **Content Strategy & Standards:**
    *   A clear definition of our content strategy, including our target audience, key messages, and content pillars.
    *   A set of content standards, including a style guide, a voice and tone guide, and SEO best practices.

2.  **Roles & Responsibilities:**
    *   Define the roles and responsibilities for content creation, review, and approval. This could be a RACI (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed) matrix.
    *   Who has the final say on what gets published?

3.  **Workflows & Processes:**
    *   Design a clear workflow for the entire content lifecycle, from idea generation to publication and archiving.
    *   What tools will we use to manage this workflow? (e.g., a project management tool, a content calendar).

4.  **Performance Measurement:**
    *   How will we measure the performance of our content? Define a set of key content marketing KPIs.

5.  **Training & Enablement:**
    *   How will we train everyone in the organization on our new content governance model?

**Output Format:**

Please generate the content governance model as a detailed and structured Markdown document. The document should be a formal policy that can be adopted by our organization.

**Constraints:**

*   The model must be comprehensive and cover all aspects of content governance.
*   The model must be practical and implementable in a large, complex organization.
*   The tone should be authoritative, clear, and collaborative.

## Tips

Content governance is a critical but often overlooked aspect of content marketing, especially in large organizations. This prompt provides a framework for creating a robust governance model.
