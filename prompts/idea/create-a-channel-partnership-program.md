# Create a Channel Partnership Program

Draft a comprehensive B2B SaaS channel partnership playbook covering recruitment, tiered incentives, onboarding, and conflict resolution.

Variables: company_name

## Prompt

I am the Head of Partnerships at a B2B SaaS company, {{company_name}}. We want to build a channel partnership program to accelerate our sales growth. We want to recruit and enable other companies (like marketing agencies, consultants, and VARs) to sell our product.

Act as a channel sales expert. Your task is to create a comprehensive plan for building a successful channel partnership program.

The plan should include:

1.  **Ideal Partner Profile (IPP):**
    *   Who are our ideal channel partners? What characteristics do they have? (e.g., industry focus, customer base, technical expertise).

2.  **Partner Program Tiers & Benefits:**
    *   Design a tiered partner program (e.g., Registered, Silver, Gold) with increasing benefits and requirements for each tier.
    *   What benefits will we offer our partners? (e.g., referral fees, reseller margins, marketing development funds (MDF), co-marketing opportunities, dedicated channel manager).

3.  **Partner Recruitment & Onboarding:**
    *   How will we find and recruit our ideal partners?
    *   Create a 30-day onboarding plan for new partners to ensure they are trained and equipped to sell our product effectively.

4.  **Partner Enablement:**
    *   What sales and marketing materials do we need to provide to our partners? (e.g., sales decks, data sheets, case studies, co-brandable marketing assets).
    *   How will we provide ongoing training and support?

5.  **Partner Portal:**
    *   What functionality should our partner portal have? (e.g., deal registration, lead sharing, content library, training modules).

6.  **Channel Rules of Engagement:**
    *   How do we avoid channel conflict between our direct sales team and our partners?

**Output Format:**

Please generate a detailed channel partnership program plan in Markdown. The plan should be a complete playbook for launching and managing our program.

**Constraints:**

*   The program must be attractive to potential partners and profitable for our company.
*   The plan must be highly structured and actionable.
*   The tone should be strategic, collaborative, and results-oriented.

## Tips

Building a channel program is a significant strategic initiative. This prompt provides a comprehensive guide to doing it right.
