# Develop a Product-Led Growth (PLG) Strategy

Engineer a comprehensive Product-Led Growth strategy covering onboarding, monetization models, and PQL definitions to drive organic SaaS scaling.

Variables: startup_name

## Prompt

I am the founder of a B2B SaaS startup, {{startup_name}}. I want to build a business where the product itself is the primary driver of customer acquisition, conversion, and expansion. I want to adopt a Product-Led Growth (PLG) strategy.

Act as a PLG expert (in the style of experts from OpenView or Wes Bush). Your task is to create a PLG strategy for my startup.

The strategy should cover:

1.  **The PLG Flywheel:**
    *   How do we design a product experience that encourages users to discover value, share the product with others, and upgrade to paid plans?

2.  **Freemium vs. Free Trial:**
    *   Should we offer a freemium plan or a time-based free trial? What are the pros and cons of each for our business?

3.  **The "Aha!" Moment:**
    *   What is the "aha!" moment for our product - the moment where users experience the core value? How do we design our onboarding to get users to this moment as quickly as possible?

4.  **Product-Qualified Leads (PQLs):**
    *   How do we define a Product-Qualified Lead (PQL)? What are the in-product behaviors that indicate a user or a team is ready for a sales conversation?

5.  **The Role of Sales in a PLG Model:**
    *   If the product is doing the selling, what is the role of the sales team? Should we have a traditional sales team, or a team of "product specialists" who assist users who have already experienced the product?

6.  **Pricing & Packaging:**
    *   How should we design our pricing and packaging to support a PLG model? (e.g., a free plan, a self-serve paid plan, and an enterprise plan).

**Output Format:**

Please generate the PLG strategy as a detailed plan in Markdown. The plan should be a complete guide to building a successful product-led business.

**Constraints:**

*   The strategy must be deeply rooted in the product experience.
*   The advice must be practical and actionable for a startup.
*   The tone should be modern, product-centric, and growth-oriented.

## Tips

PLG is the dominant GTM motion for modern SaaS companies. This prompt provides a comprehensive guide to implementing a PLG strategy.
