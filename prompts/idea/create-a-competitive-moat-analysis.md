# Create a Competitive Moat Analysis

Evaluate a company's long-term competitive durability by analyzing intangible assets, switching costs, network effects, and cost advantages.

Variables: company_name, industry

## Prompt

I am a long-term investor analyzing a company, {{company_name}}, in the {{industry}} industry. I want to determine if this company has a durable competitive advantage, or a "moat," that will protect it from competition and allow it to earn high returns on capital for a long time.

Act as a seasoned investment analyst in the style of Warren Buffett or Pat Dorsey. Your task is to conduct a competitive moat analysis for {{company_name}}.

Your analysis should evaluate the company's potential moat based on the four primary sources of competitive advantage:

1.  **Intangible Assets:**
    *   Does the company have a strong brand that allows it to charge a premium price? 
    *   Does it have patents or regulatory licenses that protect it from competition?

2.  **Switching Costs:**
    *   Is it difficult, costly, or time-consuming for customers to switch from this company's product to a competitor's product? (e.g., due to data migration costs, retraining, or integration with other systems).

3.  **Network Effects:**
    *   Does the value of the company's product or service increase as more people use it? (e.g., social networks, marketplaces).

4.  **Cost Advantages:**
    *   Does the company have a structural cost advantage over its competitors? This could be due to:
        *   **Scale:** (e.g., lower per-unit costs from high volume).
        *   **Process:** (e.g., a unique and more efficient way of doing things).
        *   **Location:** (e.g., a favorable geographic position).

**Output Format:**

Please generate a detailed analysis in Markdown. For each of the four sources of a moat, provide your assessment of whether {{company_name}} has this advantage and the strength of that advantage (e.g., wide, narrow, or no moat). Conclude with an overall assessment of the company's competitive position and the durability of its moat.

**Constraints:**

*   The analysis must be based on deep, qualitative reasoning, not just financial numbers.
*   The assessment must be objective and consider both the strengths and weaknesses of the company's competitive position.
*   The tone should be that of a skeptical, long-term-oriented business analyst.

## Tips

A classic framework from value investing. This prompt helps users think deeply about the sustainability of a company's competitive advantage.
