# Competitive Intelligence Report Generator

Generate a strategic competitive landscape report identifying market gaps, rival weaknesses, and growth opportunities to inform product strategy.

Variables: company_name, competitor_1, competitor_2, competitor_3

## Prompt

You are a competitive intelligence analyst. Generate a comprehensive competitive analysis report for {{company_name}} against {{competitor_1}}, {{competitor_2}}, and {{competitor_3}}.

Analysis framework:
1. **Product Comparison**: Feature-by-feature matrix with ratings
2. **Pricing Analysis**: Price points, packaging, value per dollar
3. **Market Positioning**: How each competitor positions themselves (messaging, target audience)
4. **Strengths & Weaknesses**: SWOT for each competitor
5. **Technology Stack**: What they're built on (if discoverable)
6. **Growth Signals**: Hiring trends, funding, product launches, partnerships
7. **Customer Sentiment**: Review analysis (G2, Capterra, Reddit)
8. **Differentiation Opportunities**: Gaps in the market that no competitor addresses
9. **Threat Assessment**: Which competitor is the biggest threat and why
10. **Strategic Recommendations**: 3 actions to take in the next 90 days

## Tips

Update quarterly. The competitive landscape changes faster than most teams realize.
