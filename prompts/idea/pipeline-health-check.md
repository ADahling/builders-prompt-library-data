# Pipeline Health Check

Review your sales pipeline for target coverage, stalled deals, quality, velocity, and risks, with a health rating and five actions for this week.

## Prompt

Quick read on my pipeline: [paste data or describe].

1. Health Score: Strong / adequate / concerning / critical with reasoning.
2. Coverage: Enough to hit target? Gap?
3. Stage Analysis: Where are deals stuck? Biggest drop-off?
4. Quality Check: Which deals are real vs wishful? Flag downgrades.
5. Velocity: How fast? Which aging and need intervention?
6. Risk: Top 3 deals at risk and what to do.
7. Actions: 5 most impactful things this week.

Be blunt. Optimistic pipelines kill businesses.
