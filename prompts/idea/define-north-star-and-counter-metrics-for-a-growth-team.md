# Define North Star and Counter Metrics for a Growth Team

Establish a strategic growth framework by defining a North Star Metric, identifying protective counter metrics, and mapping an actionable KPI tree.

Variables: company_name, company_description, product_name, business_model

## Prompt

I am the leader of the growth team at {{company_name}}, a {{company_description}}. We are struggling to align our efforts and measure our impact effectively. We need to define a North Star Metric and a set of counter metrics to guide our work.

Act as a growth strategy consultant. Your task is to help me define a North Star Metric and a set of counter metrics for our growth team. Our product is {{product_name}}, and our business model is {{business_model}}.

Your guidance should cover:

1.  **North Star Metric Definition:**
    *   Explain the concept of a North Star Metric (NSM) and why it's important.
    *   Help me brainstorm potential NSM candidates for our business. The NSM should represent customer value and be a leading indicator of revenue.
    *   Recommend a single, clear NSM for our team to focus on and justify your choice.
2.  **Counter Metrics:**
    *   Explain the importance of counter metrics to ensure we are not optimizing for the NSM at the expense of other important aspects of the business.
    *   For our chosen NSM, recommend 2-3 counter metrics that we should track. For example, if our NSM is "Weekly Active Users," a counter metric could be "Churn Rate" or "Customer Support Tickets."
3.  **KPI Tree:**
    *   Create a KPI tree that shows how our NSM breaks down into smaller, more actionable input metrics that the team can influence directly.
    *   For each input metric, suggest a team or individual who would be responsible for it.

**Output Format:**

Please generate your recommendations in a structured Markdown document. The document should be a clear and persuasive argument for the chosen North Star Metric and counter metrics.

**Constraints:**

*   The chosen North Star Metric must be appropriate for our business model and product.
*   The recommendations should be practical and help my team focus on what matters most.
*   The tone should be expert, data-driven, and strategic.

## Tips

Defining a North Star Metric is a high-leverage activity. This prompt helps users think through this critical process in a structured way.
